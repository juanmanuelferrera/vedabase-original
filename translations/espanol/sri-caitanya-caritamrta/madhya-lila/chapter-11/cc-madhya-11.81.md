### CC Madhya 11.81

> prabhura sevā karite purī ājñā dila
> ataeva prabhu iṅhāke nikaṭe rākhila

> prabhura sevā karite purī ājñā dila
> ataeva prabhu iṅhāke nikaṭe rākhila

*prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sevā*—el servicio; *karite*—para realizar; *purī*—Īśvara Purī; *ājñā dila*—ordenó; *ataeva*—por consiguiente; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *iṅhāke*—a él; *nikaṭe*—a Su lado; *rākhila*—mantuvo.

**«Īśvara Purī le ordenó a Govinda que sirviera a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. De este modo, el Señor lo mantiene a Su lado».**
