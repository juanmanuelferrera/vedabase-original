### CC Madhya 25.179

> tapana miśra, raghunātha, mahārāṣṭrīya brāhmaṇa
> candraśekhara, kīrtanīyā-paramānanda,-pañca jana

*tapana miśra*—Tapana Miśra; *raghunātha*—Raghunātha; *mahārāṣṭrīya brāhmaṇa*—el *brāhmaṇa* mahārāṣṭriano; *candraśekhara*—Candraśekhara; *kīrtanīyā-paramānanda*—Paramānanda, quien solía realizar kīrtana; *pañca jana*—estas cinco personas.

**Esos cinco devotos eran Tapana Miśra, Raghunātha, el *brāhmaṇa* mahārāṣṭriano, Candraśekhara y Paramānanda Kīrtanīyā.**
