### CC Antya 1.56

> 'advaita nityānanda, tomarā dui-jane
> prabhu kahe--rūpe kṛpā kara kāya-mane

*advaita*—Advaita Ācārya; *nityānanda*—Nityānanda Prabhu; *tomarā dui-jane*—Ustedes dos; *prabhu kahe*—el Señor Caitanya Mahāprabhu dice; *rūpe*—a Rūpa Gosvāmī; *kṛpā*—misericordia; *kara*—muestren; *kāya-mane*—de todo corazón.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu dijo a Advaita Ācārya y a Nityānanda Prabhu: «Ambos debéis mostrar vuestra misericordia de todo corazón a Rūpa Gosvāmī.»**
