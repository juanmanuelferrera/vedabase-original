### CC Adi 17.292

> rādhāra viśuddha-bhāvera acintya prabhāva
> ye kṛṣṇere karāilā dvi-bhuja-svabhāva

*rādhāra*—de Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; *viśuddha*—purificado; *bhāvera*—del éxtasis; *acintya*—inconcebible; *prabhāva*—influencia; *ye*—que; *kṛṣṇere*—a Kṛṣṇa; *karāilā*—obligó; *dvi-bhuja*—de dos brazos; *svabhāva*—forma original.

**La influencia del éxtasis puro de Rādhārāṇī es tan inconcebiblemente grande que obligó a Kṛṣṇa a volver a Su forma original de dos brazos.**
