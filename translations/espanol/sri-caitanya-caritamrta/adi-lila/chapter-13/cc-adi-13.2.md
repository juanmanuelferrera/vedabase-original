### CC Adi 13.2

> jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya gauracandra
> jayādvaitacandra jaya jaya nityānanda

*jaya jaya*—toda gloria; *śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya*—al Señor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *gaura-candra*—al Señor Gauracandra; *jaya ādvaita-candra*—toda gloria a Advaita Ācārya; *jaya jaya*—toda gloria a; *nityānanda*—al Señor Nityānanda Prabhu.

**¡Toda gloria a Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu! ¡Toda gloria a Advaitacandra! ¡Toda gloria al Señor Nityānanda Prabhu!**
