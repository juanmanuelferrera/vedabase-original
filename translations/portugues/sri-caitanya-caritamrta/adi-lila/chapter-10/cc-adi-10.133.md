### CC Adi 10.133

> রামানন্দ রায়, পট্টনায়ক গোপীনাথ ।\
> কলানিধি, সুধানিধি, নায়ক বাণীনাথ ॥১৩৩॥

rāmānanda rāya, paṭṭanāyaka gopīnātha

kalānidhi, sudhānidhi, nāyaka vāṇīnātha

*rāmānanda rāya*—de nome Rāmānanda Rāya; *paṭṭanāyaka gopīnātha*—de nome Paṭṭanāyaka Gopīnātha; *kalānidhi*—de nome Kalānidhi; *sudhānidhi*—de nome Sudhānidhi; *nāyaka vāṇīnātha*—de nome Nāyaka Vāṇīnātha.

**Os cinco filhos de Bhavānanda Rāya eram Rāmānanda Rāya, Paṭṭanāyaka Gopīnātha, Kalānidhi, Sudhānidhi e Nāyaka Vāṇīnātha.**
