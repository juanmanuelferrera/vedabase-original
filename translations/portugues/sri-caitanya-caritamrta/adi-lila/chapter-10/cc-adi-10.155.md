### CC Adi 10.155

> রঘুনাথ বাল্যে কৈল প্রভুর সেবন ।
> উচ্ছিষ্ট-মার্জন আর পাদ-সম্বাহন ॥১৫৫॥

raghunātha bālye kaila prabhura sevana

ucchiṣṭa-mārjana āra pāda-saṁvāhana

*raghunātha*—Raghunātha, o filho de Tapana Miśra; *bālye*—em sua infância; *kaila*—fez; *prabhura*—do Senhor Caitanya; *sevana*—prestar serviço; *ucchiṣṭa-mārjana*—lavar a louça; *āra*—e; *pāda-saṁvāhana*—massagear os pés.

**Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu hospedou-Se na casa de Tapana Miśra, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, que então era um menino, lavava Seus pratos e massageava Suas pernas.**
