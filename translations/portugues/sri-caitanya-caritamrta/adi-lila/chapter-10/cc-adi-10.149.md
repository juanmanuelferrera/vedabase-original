### CC Adi 10.149

> সিঙ্গাভট্ট, কামাভট্ট, দন্তুর শিবানন্দ ।\
> গৌড়ে পূর্ব ভৃত্য প্রভুর প্রিয় কমলানন্দ ॥১৪৯॥

siṅgābhaṭṭa, kāmābhaṭṭa, dantura śivānanda

gauḍe pūrva bhṛtya prabhura priya kamalānanda

*siṅgābhaṭṭa*—de nome Siṅgābhaṭṭa; *kāmābhaṭṭa*—de nome Kāmābhaṭṭa; *dantura śivānanda*—de nome Dantura Śivānanda; *gauḍe*—em Bengala; *pūrva*—anteriormente; *bhṛtya*—servo; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *priya*—muito querido; *kamalānanda*—de nome Kamalānanda.

**Siṅgābhaṭṭa era o trigésimo primeiro, Kāmābhaṭṭa o trigésimo segundo, Śivānanda o trigésimo terceiro e Kamalānanda o trigésimo quarto. Todos eles serviram outrora Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu em Bengala, mas, mais tarde, esses servos deixaram Bengala para viver com o Senhor em Jagannātha Purī.**
