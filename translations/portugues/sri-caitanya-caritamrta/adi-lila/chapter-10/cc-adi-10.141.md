### CC Adi 10.141

> অঙ্গসেবা গোবিন্দেরে দিলেন ঈশ্বর ।
> জগন্নাথ দেখিতে চলেন আগে কাশীশ্বর ॥১৪১॥

aṅga-sevā govindere dilena īśvara

jagannātha dekhite calena āge kāśīśvara

*aṅga-sevā*—cuidar do corpo; *govindere*—a Govinda; *dilena*—Ele deu; *īśvara*—a Suprema Personalidade de Deus; *jagannātha*—a Deidade de Jagannātha; *dekhite*—ao ir visitar; *calena*—ia; *āge*—à frente; *kāśīśvara*—de nome Kāśīśvara.

**Govinda cuidava do corpo de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, ao passo que Kāśīśvara ia à frente do Senhor quando Ele ia ver Jagannātha no templo.**
