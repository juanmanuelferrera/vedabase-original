### CC Adi 10.123

> কেবল নীলাচলে প্রভুর যে যে ভক্তগণ ।
> সংক্ষেপে করিয়ে কিছু সে সব কথন ॥১২৩॥

kevala nīlācale prabhura ye ye bhakta-gaṇa

saṅkṣepe kariye kichu se saba kathana

*kevala*—somente; *nīlācale*—em Jagannātha Purī; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *ye ye*—todos aqueles; *bhakta-gaṇa*—devotos; *saṅkṣepe*—em breve; *kariye*—faço; *kichu*—alguma; *se saba*—todos aqueles; *kathana*—narração.

**Permita-me descrever brevemente alguns dos devotos do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu em Jagannātha Purī.**
