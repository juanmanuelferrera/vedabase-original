### CC Adi 10.68

> প্রভু যাঁর নিত্য লয় থোড়-মোচা-ফল ।\
> যাঁর ফুটা-লৌহপাত্রে প্রভু পিলা জল ॥৬৮॥

prabhu yāṅra nitya laya thoḍa-mocā-phala

yāṅra phuṭā-lauhapātre prabhu pilā jala

*prabhu*—o Senhor; *yāṅra*—de quem; *nitya*—diariamente; *laya*—toma; *thoḍa*—a polpa da bananeira; *mocā*—as flores da bananeira; *phala*—os frutos da bananeira; *yāṅra*—de quem; *phuṭā*—quebrado; *lauha-pātre*—no pote de ferro; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *pilā*—bebeu; *jala*—água.

**Todos os dias, o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu, brincando, tomava de Śrīdhara frutos, flores e polpa, e bebia de seu pote de ferro quebrado.**
