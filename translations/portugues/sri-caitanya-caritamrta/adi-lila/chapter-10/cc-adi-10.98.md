### CC Adi 10.98

> অন্ন-জল ত্যাগ কৈল অন্য-কথন ।
> পল দুই-তিন মাঠা করেন ভক্ষণ ॥৯৮॥

anna-jala tyāga kaila anya-kathana

pala dui-tina māṭhā karena bhakṣaṇa

*anna-jala*—alimento e bebida; *tyāga*—renúncia; *kaila*—fez; *anya-kathana*—falar de outras coisas; *pala dui-tina*—umas poucas gotas de; *māṭhā*—leite talhado; *karena*—faz; *bhakṣaṇa*—comer.

**Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī gradualmente abandonou todo alimento e toda bebida, exceto algumas gotas de soro de leite.**
