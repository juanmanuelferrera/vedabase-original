### CC Adi 10.118

> রামদাস, মাধব, আর বাসুদেব ঘোষ ।\
> প্রভু-সঙ্গে রহে গোবিন্দ পাইয়া সন্তোষ ॥১১৮॥

rāmadāsa, mādhava, āra vāsudeva ghoṣa

prabhu-saṅge rahe govinda pāiyā santoṣa

*rāmadāsa*—de nome Rāmadāsa; *mādhava*—de nome Mādhava; *āra*—e; *vāsudeva ghoṣa*—de nome Vāsudeva Ghoṣa; *prabhu-saṅge*—na companhia do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rahe*—permaneceu; *govinda*—de nome Govinda; *pāiyā*—sentindo; *santoṣa*—grande satisfação.

**Esses três eram Rāmadāsa, Mādhava Ghoṣa e Vāsudeva Ghoṣa. Govinda Ghoṣa, contudo, permaneceu com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu em Jagannātha Purī e, assim, sentiu grande satisfação.**
