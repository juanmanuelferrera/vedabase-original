### CC Adi 13.2

jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya gauracandra
jayādvaitacandra jaya jaya nityānanda

*jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya*—ao Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *gaura-candra*—ao Senhor Gauracandra; *jaya ādvaita-candra*—todas as glórias a Advaita Ācārya; *jaya jaya*—todas as glórias a; *nityānanda*—ao Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu.

**Todas as glórias a Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu! Todas as glórias a Advaitacandra! Todas as glórias ao Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu!**
