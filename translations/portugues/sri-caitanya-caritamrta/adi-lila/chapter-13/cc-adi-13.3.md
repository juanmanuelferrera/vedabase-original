### CC Adi 13.3

jaya jaya gadādhara jaya śrīnivāsa
jaya mukunda vāsudeva jaya haridāsa

*jaya jaya gadādhara*—todas as glórias a Gadādhara Prabhu; *jaya śrīnivāsa*—todas as glórias a Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura; *jaya mukunda*—todas as glórias a Mukunda; *vāsudeva*—todas as glórias a Vāsudeva; *jaya haridāsa*—todas as glórias a Haridāsa Ṭhākura.

**Todas as glórias a Gadādhara Prabhu! Todas as glórias a Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura! Todas as glórias a Mukunda Prabhu e Vāsudeva Prabhu! Todas as glórias a Haridāsa Ṭhākura!**
