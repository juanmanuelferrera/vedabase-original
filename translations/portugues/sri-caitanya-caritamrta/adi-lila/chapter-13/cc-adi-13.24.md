### CC Adi 13.24

> অতএব ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলে নারীগণ ।\
> দেখিতে আইসে যেবা সর্ব বন্ধুজন ॥২৪॥

ataeva 'hari' 'hari' bale nārīgaṇa

dekhite āise yebā sarva bandhu jana

*ataeva*—portanto; *hari hari*—o santo nome do Senhor; *bale*—cantam; *nārī-gaṇa*—todas as senhoras; *dekhite*—para ver; *āise*—elas vêm; *yebā*—quem quer que; *sarva*—todos; *bandhu-jana*—amigos.

**Todas as senhoras amigas que vinham ver a criança cantavam os santos nomes, "Hari, Hari!", assim que a criança chorava.**
