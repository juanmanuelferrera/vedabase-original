### CC Adi 13.23

> বাল্যভাব ছলে প্রভু করেন ক্রন্দন ।
> ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরি’ নাম শুনি’ রহয়ে রোদন ॥২৩॥

bālya-bhāva chale prabhu karena krandana

'kṛṣṇa' 'hari' nāma śuni' rahaye rodana

*bālya-bhāva chale*—como se em Seu estado de infância; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *karena*—faz; *krandana*—chorar; *kṛṣṇa*—Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *hari*—Senhor Hari; *nāma*—nomes; *śuni'*—ouvindo; *rahaye*—cessa; *rodana*—o choro.

**Em Sua infância, quando o Senhor chorava, parava imediatamente ao ouvir os santos nomes Kṛṣṇa e Hari.**
