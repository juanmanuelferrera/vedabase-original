### CC Adi 13.10

> চব্বিশ বৎসর প্রভু কৈল গৃহবাস ।
> নিরন্তর কৈল কৃষ্ণ-কীর্তন-বিলাস ॥১০॥

cabbiśa vatsara prabhu kaila gṛha-vāsa
nirantara kaila kṛṣṇa-kīrtana-vilāsa

*cabbiśa*—vinte e quatro; *vatsara*—anos; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *kaila*—fez; *gṛha-vāsa*—residir em casa; *nirantara*—sempre; *kaila*—fez; *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*—o cantar do mantra Hare Kṛṣṇa; *vilāsa*—passatempos.

**Durante vinte e quatro anos, o Senhor Caitanya viveu no gṛhastha-āśrama [vida doméstica], sempre ocupando-Se nos passatempos do movimento Hare Kṛṣṇa.**
