### CC Adi 9.22

> সেই দুইস্কন্ধে বহু শাখা উপজিল ।
> তার উপশাখাগণে জগৎ ছাইল ॥২২॥

sei dui-skandhe bahu śākhā upajila
tāra upaśākhā-gaṇe jagat chāila

*sei*—aqueles; *dui-skandhe*—nos dois troncos; *bahu*—muitos; *śākhā*—ramos; *upajila*—cresceram; *tāra*—destes; *upaśākhā-gaṇe*—os sub-ramos; *jagat*—o mundo inteiro; *chāila*—cobriram.

**Desses dois troncos cresceram muitos ramos e sub-ramos que cobriram o mundo inteiro.**
