### CC Adi 9.7

> প্রভু কহে, আমি ‘বিশ্বম্ভর’ নাম ধরি ।
> নাম সার্থক হয়, যদি প্রেমে বিশ্ব ভরি ॥৭॥

prabhu kahe, āmi 'viśvambhara' nāma dhari
nāma sārthaka haya, yadi preme viśva bhari

*prabhu kahe*—o Senhor disse; *āmi*—Eu; *viśvambhara*—Viśvambhara; *nāma*—nome; *dhari*—aceito; *nāma*—o nome; *sārthaka*—completo; *haya*—torna-se; *yadi*—se; *preme*—com amor a Deus; *viśva*—o universo inteiro; *bhari*—encher.

**O Senhor Caitanya pensou: "Meu nome é Viśvambhara, 'aquele que mantém o universo inteiro'. Seu significado se realizará se Eu puder encher o universo inteiro com amor a Deus."**
