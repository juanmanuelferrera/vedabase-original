### CC Adi 3.82

> উপপুরাণেহ শুনি শ্রীকৃষ্ণবচন ।
> কৃপা করি ব্যাস প্রতি করিয়াছেন কথন ॥৮২॥

upa-purāṇeha śuni śrī-kṛṣṇa-vacana
kṛpā kari vyāsa prati kariyāchena kathana

*upa-purāṇeha*—nos Upapurāṇas; *śuni*—ouvimos; *śrī-kṛṣṇa-vacana*—as palavras do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *kṛpā kari*—tendo misericórdia; *vyāsa prati*—para com Vyāsadeva; *kariyāchena*—Ele fez; *kathana*—falando.

**Nos Upapurāṇas, ouvimos Śrī Kṛṣṇa mostrar Sua misericórdia a Vyāsadeva ao falar-lhe da seguinte maneira.**
