### CC Adi 3.13

> যথেষ্ট বিহরি’ কৃষ্ণ করে অন্তর্ধান ।\
> অন্তর্ধান করি’ মনে করে অনুমান ॥১৩॥

yatheṣṭa vihari' kṛṣṇa kare antardhāna
antardhāna kari' mane kare anumāna

*yathā-iṣṭa*—tanto quanto Ele deseja; *vihari'*—desfrutando; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *kare*—faz; *antardhāna*—desaparecimento; *antardhāna kari'*—desaparecendo; *mane*—na mente; *kare*—Ele faz; *anumāna*—consideração.
