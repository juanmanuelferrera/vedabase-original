### CC Adi 8.70

> আচার্যগোসাঞির শিষ্য — চক্রবর্তী শিবানন্দ ।
> নিরবধি তাঁর চিত্তে শ্রীচৈতন্য-নিত্যানন্দ ॥৭০॥

ācārya-gosāñira śiṣya--cakravartī śivānanda
niravadhi tāṅra citte caitanya-nityānanda

*ācārya-gosāñira*—de Ācārya Gosāñi; *śiṣya*—o discípulo; *cakravartī śivānanda*—chamado Śivānanda Cakravartī; *niravadhi*—sempre; *tāṅra*—seu; *citte*—no coração; *caitanya-nityānanda*—o Senhor Caitanya e Nityānanda estão situados.

**Entre os discípulos de Ananta Ācārya estava Śivānanda Cakravartī, em cujo coração o Senhor Caitanya e Nityānanda habitavam constantemente.**
