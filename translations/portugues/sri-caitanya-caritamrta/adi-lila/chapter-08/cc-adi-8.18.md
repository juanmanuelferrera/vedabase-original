### CC Adi 8.18

> কৃষ্ণ যদি ছুটে ভক্তে ভুক্তি মুক্তি দিয়া ।
> কভু প্রেমভক্তি না দেন রাখেন লুকাইয়া ॥১৮॥

kṛṣṇa yadi chuṭe bhakte bhukti mukti diyā

kabhu prema-bhakti nā dena rākhena lukāiyā

*kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *yadi*—evidentemente; *chuṭe*—afasta-se; *bhakte*—ao devoto; *bhukti*—gozo material; *mukti*—liberação; *diyā*—oferecendo; *kabhu*—em momento algum; *prema-bhakti*—amor a Deus; *nā*—não; *dena*—dá; *rākhena*—mantém; *lukāiyā*—oculto.

**Se um devoto deseja gratificação material dos sentidos ou liberação por parte do Senhor, Kṛṣṇa imediatamente as concede, mas o serviço devocional puro Ele mantém oculto.**
