### CC Adi 16.87

> শুনিয়া প্রভুর ব্যাখ্যা দিগ্বিজয়ী বিস্মিত ।\
> মুখে না নিঃসরে বাক্য, প্রতিভা স্তম্ভিত ॥৮৭॥

śuniyā prabhura vyākhyā digvijayī vismita

mukhe nā niḥsare vākya, pratibhā stambhita

*śuniyā*—ao ouvir; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *vyākhyā*—explicação; *dig-vijayī*—o campeão; *vismita*—maravilhado; *mukhe*—na boca; *nā*—não; *niḥsare*—saíam; *vākya*—palavras; *pratibhā*—engenho; *stambhita*—paralisado.

**Após ouvir a explicação do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu, o poeta campeão ficou tomado de assombro. Com sua perspicácia atordoada, ele não pôde dizer nada.**
