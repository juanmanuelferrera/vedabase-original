### CC Adi 16.47

> প্রভু কহেন, — কহি, যদি না করহ রোষ ।
> কহ তোমার এই শ্লোকে কিবা আছে দোষ ॥৪৭॥

prabhu kahena,--kahi, yadi na karaha roṣa

kaha tomāra ei śloke kibā āche doṣa

*prabhu kahena*—o Senhor respondeu; *kahi*—deixa-Me dizer; *yadi*—se; *nā*—não; *karaha*—te tornas; *roṣa*—irado; *kaha*—por favor, dize-Me; *tomāra*—teu; *ei śloke*—neste verso; *kibā*—qual; *āche*—há; *doṣa*—falha.

**O Senhor disse: "Meu caro senhor, eu poderia dizer-te algo se não te irares. Podes explicar as falhas neste verso?"**
