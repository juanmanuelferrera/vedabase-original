### CC Adi 16.93

> ইহা শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু অতি বড় রঙ্গী ।\
> তাঁহার হৃদয় জানি’ কহে করি’ ভঙ্গী ॥৯৩॥

ihā śuni' mahāprabhu ati baḍa raṅgī

tāṅhāra hṛdaya jāni' kahe kari' bhaṅgī

*ihā śuni'*—ao ouvir isto; *mahāprabhu*—Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ati*—muito; *baḍa*—bastante; *raṅgī*—divertido; *tāṅhāra*—seu; *hṛdaya*—coração; *jāni'*—compreendendo; *kahe*—diz; *kari'*—fazendo; *bhaṅgī*—insinuação.

**Ao ouvir isto e compreender o coração do paṇḍita, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu respondeu de maneira jocosa.**
