### CC Adi 16.13

> তেঁহো তোমার সাধ্য-সাধন করিবে নিশ্চয় ।\
> সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর তেঁহো, — নাহিক সংশয় ॥১৩॥

teṅho tomāra sādhya-sādhana karibe niścaya
sākṣāt īśvara teṅho,--nāhika saṁśaya

*teṅho*—Ele; *tomāra*—teu; *sādhya*—objetivo da vida; *sādhana*—processo; *karibe*—fará; *niścaya*—averiguar; *sākṣāt*—direto; *īśvara*—o Senhor; *teṅho*—Ele é; *nāhika*—não há; *saṁśaya*—dúvida.

**"Porque Ele é o Senhor [īśvara]," disse-lhe o brāhmaṇa, "Ele pode, sem dúvida, dar-te a direção apropriada."**
