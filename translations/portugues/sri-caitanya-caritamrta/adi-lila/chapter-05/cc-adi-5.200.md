### CC Adi 5.200

> জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ, নিত্যানন্দ-রাম ।
> যাঁহার কৃপাতে পাইনু বৃন্দাবন-ধাম ॥২০০॥

jaya jaya nityānanda, nityānanda-rāma

yāṅhāra kṛpāte pāinu vṛndāvana-dhāma

*jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *nityānanda*—ao Senhor Nityānanda; *nityānanda-rāma*—ao Senhor Balarāma, que apareceu como Nityānanda; *yāṅhāra kṛpāte*—por cuja misericórdia; *pāinu*—obtive; *vṛndāvana-dhāma*—o abrigo de Vṛndāvana.

**Toda glória, toda glória ao Senhor Nityānanda Balarāma, por cuja misericórdia alcancei abrigo na morada transcendental de Vṛndāvana.**
