### CC Adi 5.150

> রামের চরিত্র সব, — দুঃখের কারণ ।
> স্বতন্ত্র লীলায় দুঃখ সহেন লক্ষ্মণ ॥১৫০॥

rāmera caritra saba,--duḥkhera kāraṇa
svatantra līlāya duḥkha sahena lakṣmaṇa

*rāmera caritra saba*—todas as atividades do Senhor Rāmacandra; *duḥkhera kāraṇa*—causas de sofrimento; *sva-tantra*—embora independente; *līlāya*—nos passatempos; *duḥkha*—sofrimento; *sahena lakṣmaṇa*—Lakṣmaṇa tolera.

**As atividades do Senhor Rāma eram repletas de sofrimento, mas Lakṣmaṇa, por Sua própria iniciativa, tolerava esse sofrimento.**
