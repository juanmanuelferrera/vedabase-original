### CC Adi 7.156

> লক্ষ লক্ষ লোক আইসে প্রভুকে দেখিতে ।\
> মহাভিড় হৈল দ্বারে, নারে প্রবেশিতে ॥১৫৬॥

lakṣa lakṣa loka āise prabhuke dekhite

mahā-bhiḍa haila dvāre, nāre praveśite

*lakṣa lakṣa*—centenas e milhares; *loka*—pessoas; *āise*—vinham; *prabhuke*—ao Senhor; *dekhite*—ver; *mahā-bhiḍa*—uma grande multidão; *haila*—aconteceu; *dvāre*—à porta; *nāre*—não podiam; *praveśite*—entrar.

**A multidão à porta de Sua residência era tão grande que chegava a centenas e milhares.**
