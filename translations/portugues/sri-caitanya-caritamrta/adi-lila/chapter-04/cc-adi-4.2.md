### CC Adi 4.2

> জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ।
> জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥২॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

*jaya jaya*—toda glória; *śrī-caitanya*—ao Senhor Caitanya; *jaya*—toda glória; *nityānanda*—ao Senhor Nityānanda; *jaya*—toda glória; *advaita-candra*—a Advaita Ācārya; *jaya*—toda glória; *gaura-bhakta-vṛnda*—aos devotos do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Toda glória ao Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Toda glória ao Senhor Nityānanda. Toda glória a Śrī Advaita Ācārya. E toda glória a todos os devotos do Senhor Caitanya.**
