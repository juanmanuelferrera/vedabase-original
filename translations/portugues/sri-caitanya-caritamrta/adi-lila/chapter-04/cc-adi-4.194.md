### CC Adi 4.194

> কিন্তু কৃষ্ণের সুখ হয় গোপী-রূপ-গুণে ।
> তাঁর সুখে সুখবৃদ্ধি হয়ে গোপীগণে ॥১৯৪॥

kintu kṛṣṇera sukha haya gopī-rūpa-guṇe
tāṅra sukhe sukha-vṛddhi haye gopī-gaṇe

*kintu*—mas; *kṛṣṇera*—do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *sukha*—a felicidade; *haya*—é; *gopī-rūpa-guṇe*—nas qualidades e na beleza das gopīs; *tāṅra*—d'Ele; *sukhe*—na felicidade; *sukha-vṛddhi*—aumento de felicidade; *haye*—há; *gopī-gaṇe*—nas gopīs.

**Kṛṣṇa, porém, obtém prazer da beleza e das boas qualidades das gopīs. E, quando as gopīs veem o prazer d'Ele, a alegria das gopīs aumenta.**
