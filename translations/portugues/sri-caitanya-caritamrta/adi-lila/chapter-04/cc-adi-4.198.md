### CC Adi 4.198

> গোপীপ্রেমে করে কৃষ্ণমাধুর্যের পুষ্টি ।
> মাধুর্যে বাঢ়ায় প্রেম হঞা মহাতুষ্টি ॥১৯৮॥

gopī-preme kare kṛṣṇa-mādhuryera puṣṭi

mādhurye bāḍhāya prema hañā mahā-tuṣṭi

*gopī-preme*—o amor das gopīs; *kare*—faz; *kṛṣṇa-mādhuryera*—da doçura do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *puṣṭi*—nutrição; *mādhurye*—a doçura; *bāḍhāya*—faz aumentar; *prema*—o amor; *hañā*—estando; *mahā-tuṣṭi*—imensamente satisfeitas.

**O amor das gopīs nutre a doçura do Senhor Kṛṣṇa. Essa doçura, por sua vez, faz aumentar o amor delas, pois elas ficam imensamente satisfeitas.**
