### CC Adi 4.92

> সর্ব-সৌন্দর্য-কান্তি বৈসয়ে যাঁহাতে ।\
> সর্বলক্ষ্মীগণের শোভা হয় যাঁহা হৈতে ॥৯২॥

sarva-saundarya-kānti vaisaye yāṅhāte
sarva-lakṣmī-gaṇera śobhā haya yāṅhā haite

*sarva-saundarya*—de toda a beleza; *kānti*—o esplendor; *vaisaye*—repousa; *yāṅhāte*—em quem; *sarva-lakṣmī-gaṇera*—de todas as deusas da fortuna; *śobhā*—o esplendor; *haya*—é; *yāṅhā haite*—de quem.

**A palavra "sarva-kānti" indica que toda a beleza e todo o brilho repousam em Seu corpo. Todas as lakṣmīs derivam d'Ela a sua beleza.**
