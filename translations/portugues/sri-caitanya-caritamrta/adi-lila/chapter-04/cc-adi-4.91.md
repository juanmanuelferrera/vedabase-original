### CC Adi 4.91

> কিম্বা, ‘সর্বলক্ষ্মী’ — কৃষ্ণের ষড়্বিধ ঐশ্বর্য ।\
> তাঁর অধিষ্ঠাত্রী শক্তি — সর্বশক্তিবর্য ॥৯১॥

kimvā, 'sarva-lakṣmī'--kṛṣṇera ṣaḍ-vidha aiśvarya
tāṅra adhiṣṭhātrī śakti--sarva-śakti-varya

*kimvā*—ou; *sarva-lakṣmī*—a palavra sarva-lakṣmī; *kṛṣṇera*—do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *ṣaṭ-vidha*—seis espécies; *aiśvarya*—opulências; *tāṅra*—d'Ele; *adhiṣṭhātrī*—principal; *śakti*—energia; *sarva-śakti*—de todas as energias; *varya*—a melhor.

**Ou então "sarva-lakṣmī" indica que Ela representa plenamente as seis opulências de Kṛṣṇa. Por isso, Ela é a energia suprema do Senhor Kṛṣṇa.**
