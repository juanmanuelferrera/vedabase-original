### CC Adi 4.144

> দর্পণাদ্যে দেখি’ যদি আপন মাধুরী ।\
> আস্বাদিতে হয় লোভ, আস্বাদিতে নারি ॥১৪৪॥

darpaṇādye dekhi' yadi āpana mādhurī
āsvādite haya lobha, āsvādite nāri

*darpaṇa-ādye*—começando num espelho; *dekhi'*—vendo; *yadi*—se; *āpana*—própria; *mādhurī*—doçura; *āsvādite*—saborear; *haya*—há; *lobha*—desejo; *āsvādite*—saborear; *nāri*—não sou capaz.

**"Se vejo Minha doçura num espelho, sinto-Me tentado a saboreá-la, mas, ainda assim, não posso fazê-lo.**
