### CC Adi 4.104

> অতি গূঢ় হেতু সেই ত্রিবিধ প্রকার ।\
> দামোদরস্বরূপ হৈতে যাহার প্রচার ॥১০৪॥

ati gūḍha hetu sei tri-vidha prakāra
dāmodara-svarūpa haite yāhāra pracāra

*ati*—muito; *gūḍha*—esotérica; *hetu*—razão; *sei*—essa; *tri-vidha*—três; *prakāra*—espécies; *dāmodara-svarūpa haite*—de Svarūpa Dāmodara; *yāhāra*—da qual; *pracāra*—a proclamação.

**Essa razão tão confidencial é tríplice. Svarūpa Dāmodara a revelou.**
