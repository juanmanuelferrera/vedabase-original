### CC Adi 11.18

> শ্রীমাধব ঘোষ — মুখ্য কীর্তনীয়াগণে ।
> নিত্যানন্দপ্রভু নৃত্য করে যাঁর গানে ॥১৮॥

śrī-mādhava ghoṣa--mukhya kīrtanīyā-gaṇe
nityānanda-prabhu nṛtya kare yāṅra gāne

*śrī-mādhava ghoṣa*—de nome Śrī Mādhava Ghoṣa; *mukhya*—principal; *kīrtanīyā-gaṇe*—entre os executantes do saṅkīrtana; *nityānanda-prabhu*—de nome Nityānanda Prabhu; *nṛtya*—dança; *kare*—faz; *yāṅra*—cujo; *gāne*—no canto.

**Śrī Mādhava Ghoṣa era um dos principais executantes de kīrtana. Enquanto ele cantava, Nityānanda Prabhu dançava.**
