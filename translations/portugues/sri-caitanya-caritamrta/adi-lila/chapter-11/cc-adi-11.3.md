### CC Adi 11.3

jaya jaya śrī-advaita, jaya nityānanda
jaya jaya mahāprabhura sarva-bhakta-vṛnda

*jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *śrī-advaita*—a Śrī Advaita Ācārya; *jaya*—todas as glórias; *nityānanda*—ao Senhor Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu; *jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *mahāprabhura*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sarva*—todos; *bhakta-vṛnda*—os devotos.

**Todas as glórias a Śrī Advaita Prabhu, a Nityānanda Prabhu e a todos os devotos do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu!**
