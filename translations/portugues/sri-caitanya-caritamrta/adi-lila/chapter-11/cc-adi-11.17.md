### CC Adi 11.17

> গদাধর দাস গোপীভাবে পূর্ণানন্দ ।
> যাঁর ঘরে দানকেলি কৈল নিত্যানন্দ ॥১৭॥

gadādhara dāsa gopībhāve pūrṇānanda
yāṅra ghare dānakeli kaila nityānanda

*gadādhara dāsa*—de nome Gadādhara dāsa; *gopī-bhāve*—no êxtase das gopīs; *pūrṇa-ānanda*—plenamente em bem-aventurança transcendental; *yāṅra ghare*—em cuja casa; *dāna-keli*—a representação do dānakeli-līlā; *kaila*—realizou; *nityānanda*—o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu.

**Śrīla Gadādhara dāsa estava sempre plenamente absorto no êxtase de uma gopī. Em sua casa, o Senhor Nityānanda encenou o drama Dānakeli.**
