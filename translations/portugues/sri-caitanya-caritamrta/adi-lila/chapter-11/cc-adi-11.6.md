### CC Adi 11.6

> মালাকারের ইচ্ছা-জলে বাড়ে শাখাগণ ।
> প্রেম-ফুল-ফলে ভরি’ ছাইল ভুবন ॥৬॥

mālākarera icchā jale bāḍe śākhā-gaṇa
prema-phula-phale bhari' chāila bhuvana

*mālā-kārera*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *icchā-jale*—pela água de Seu desejo; *bāḍe*—aumentam; *śākhā-gaṇa*—os ramos; *prema*—amor a Deus; *phula-phale*—com flores e frutos; *bhari'*—enchendo; *chāila*—cobriu; *bhuvana*—o mundo inteiro.

**Regados pelo desejo de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, esses ramos e subramos cresceram ilimitadamente e cobriram o mundo inteiro com frutos e flores.**
