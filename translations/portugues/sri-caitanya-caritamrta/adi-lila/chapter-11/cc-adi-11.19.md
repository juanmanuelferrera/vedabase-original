### CC Adi 11.19

> বাসুদেব গীতে করে প্রভুর বর্ণনে ।\
> কাষ্ঠ-পাষাণ দ্রবে যাহার শ্রবণে ॥১৯॥

vāsudeva gīte kare prabhura varṇane
kāṣṭha-pāṣāṇa drave yāhāra śravaṇe

*vāsudeva*—de nome Vāsudeva; *gīte*—enquanto cantava; *kare*—faz; *prabhura*—de Nityānanda Prabhu e Śrī Caitanya Prabhu; *varṇane*—na descrição; *kāṣṭha*—madeira; *pāṣāṇa*—pedra; *drave*—derretem; *yāhāra*—cujo; *śravaṇe*—ao ouvir.

**Quando Vāsudeva Ghoṣa executava kīrtana, descrevendo o Senhor Caitanya e Nityānanda, até mesmo a madeira e a pedra derretiam ao ouvi-lo.**
