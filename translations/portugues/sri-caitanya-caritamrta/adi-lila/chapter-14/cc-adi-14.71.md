### CC Adi 14.71

> চৈতন্য-চাপল্য দেখি’ প্রেমে সর্বজন ।
> শচী-জগন্নাথে দেখি’ দেন ওলাহন ॥৭১॥

caitanya-cāpalya dekhi' preme sarva jana

śacī-jagannāthe dekhi' dena olāhana

*caitanya*—o Senhor Caitanya; *cāpalya*—travessura; *dekhi'*—vendo; *preme*—por amor; *sarva jana*—todas as pessoas; *śacī*—diante de Śacīmātā; *jagannāthe*—e de Jagannātha Miśra; *dekhi'*—vendo-os; *dena*—faziam; *olāhana*—uma pequena repreensão.

**Quando os vizinhos viam o comportamento travesso do Senhor Caitanya, eles, por amor a Ele, apresentavam queixas a Śacīmātā e Jagannātha Miśra.**
