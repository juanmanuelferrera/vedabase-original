### CC Adi 14.65

> দুঁহা দেখি’ দুঁহার চিত্তে হইল উল্লাস ।\
> দেবপূজা ছলে কৈল দুঁহে পরকাশ ॥৬৫॥

duṅhā dekhi' duṅhāra citte ha-ila ullāsa

deva-pūjā chale kaila duṅhe parakāśa

*duṅhā*—ambos; *dekhi'*—vendo; *duṅhāra*—de ambos; *citte*—nas mentes; *ha-ila*—houve; *ullāsa*—prazer; *deva-pūjā*—adoração aos semideuses; *chale*—sob o pretexto de; *kaila*—houve; *duṅhe*—ambos; *parakāśa*—manifestação.

**Ambos sentiram prazer natural ao se verem e, sob o pretexto de adorar os semideuses, manifestaram seus sentimentos.**
