### CC Adi 14.60

> আনিয়া নৈবেদ্য তারা সম্মুখে ধরিল ।\
> খাইয়া নৈবেদ্য তারে ইষ্টবর দিল ॥৬০॥

āniyā naivedya tārā sammukhe dharila

khāiyā naivedya tāre iṣṭa-vara dila

*āniyā*—trazendo; *naivedya*—oferenda; *tārā*—todas elas; *sammukhe*—diante; *dharila*—puseram; *khāiyā*—comendo; *naivedya*—oferenda; *tāre*—a elas; *iṣṭa-vara*—a bênção desejada; *dila*—concedeu.

**As moças, então, trouxeram as oferendas diante do Senhor, que as comeu todas e abençoou as moças conforme a satisfação delas.**
