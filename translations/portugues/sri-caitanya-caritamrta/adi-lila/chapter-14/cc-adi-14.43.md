### CC Adi 14.43

> শুনি’ ক্রুদ্ধ হঞা প্রভু ঘর-ভিতর যাঞা ।
> ঘরে যত ভাণ্ড ছিল, ফেলিল ভাঙ্গিয়া ॥৪৩॥

śuni' kruddha hañā prabhu ghara-bhitara yāñā

ghare yata bhāṇḍa chila, phelila bhāṅgiyā

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *kruddha*—irado; *hañā*—ficando; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *ghara-bhitara*—dentro do quarto; *yāñā*—indo; *ghare*—no quarto; *yata*—todos; *bhāṇḍa*—potes; *chila*—havia; *phelila*—Ele os jogava; *bhāṅgiyā*—quebrando.

**Assim repreendido por Sua mãe, o Senhor ia irado a um quarto e quebrava todos os potes que ali houvesse.**
