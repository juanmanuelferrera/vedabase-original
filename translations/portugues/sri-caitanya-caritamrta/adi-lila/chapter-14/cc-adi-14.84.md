### CC Adi 14.84

rātre svapna dekhe,--eka āsi' brahmaṇa

miśrere kahaye kichu sa-roṣa vacana

*rātre*—à noite; *svapna dekhe*—sonhou; *eka*—um; *āsi'*—vindo; *brāhmana*—brāhmaṇa; *miśrere*—a Jagannātha Miśra; *kahaye*—falou; *kichu*—algo; *sa-roṣa*—com ira; *vacana*—palavras.
