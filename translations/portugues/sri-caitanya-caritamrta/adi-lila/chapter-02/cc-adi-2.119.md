### CC Adi 2.119

> চৈতন্যপ্রভুর মহিমা কহিবার তরে ।
> কৃষ্ণের মহিমা কহি করিয়া বিস্তারে ॥১১৯॥

caitanya-prabhura mahimā kahibāra tare

kṛṣṇera mahimā kahi kariyā vistāre

*caitanya-prabhura*—do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mahimā*—as glórias; *kahibāra tare*—com o propósito de falar; *kṛṣṇera*—do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *mahimā*—as glórias; *kahi*—eu falo; *kariyā*—fazendo; *vistāre*—em expansão.

**Apenas para enunciar as glórias de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, procurei descrever as glórias de Śrī Kṛṣṇa em detalhe.**
