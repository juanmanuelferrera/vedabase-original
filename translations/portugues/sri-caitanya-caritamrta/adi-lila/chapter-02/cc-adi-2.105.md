### CC Adi 2.105

> যদ্যপি ব্রহ্মাণ্ডগণের পুরুষ আশ্রয় ।\
> সেই পুরুষাদি সভার কৃষ্ণ মূলাশ্রয় ॥১০৫॥

yadyapi brahmāṇḍa-gaṇera puruṣa āśraya
sei puruṣādi sabhāra kṛṣṇa mūlāśraya

*yadyapi*—embora; *brahma-aṇḍa-gaṇera*—da multidão de universos; *puruṣa*—o puruṣa-avatāra; *āśraya*—o abrigo; *sei*—esse; *puruṣa-ādi*—dos puruṣa-avatāras, etc.; *sabhāra*—da assembleia; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *mūla-āśraya*—fonte original.

**"Embora os três puruṣas sejam o abrigo de todos os universos, o Senhor Kṛṣṇa é a fonte original dos puruṣas.**
