### CC Adi 17.289

> এত বলি নমস্করি’ গেলা গোপীগণ ।\
> হেনকালে রাধা আসি’ দিলা দরশন ॥২৮৯॥

eta bali namaskari' gelā gopī-gaṇa

hena-kāle rādhā āsi' dilā daraśana

*eta bali*—dizendo isto; *namaskari'*—oferecendo reverências; *gelā*—foram embora; *gopī-gaṇa*—todas as gopīs; *hena-kāle*—nesse momento; *rādhā*—Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; *āsi'*—chegando ali; *dilā*—deu; *daraśana*—audiência.

**Após dizerem isto e oferecerem reverências, todas as gopīs se dispersaram. Então Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī chegou e apareceu diante do Senhor Kṛṣṇa.**
