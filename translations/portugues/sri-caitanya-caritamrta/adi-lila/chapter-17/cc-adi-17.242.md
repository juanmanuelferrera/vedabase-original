### CC Adi 17.242

> কভু দুর্গা, লক্ষ্মী হয়, কভু বা চিচ্ছক্তি ।
> খাটে বসি’ ভক্তগণে দিলা প্রেমভক্তি ॥২৪২॥

kabhu durgā, lakṣmī haya, kabhu vā cic-chakti

khāṭe vasi' bhakta-gaṇe dilā prema-bhakti

*kabhu*—às vezes; *durgā*—o papel da deusa Durgā; *lakṣmī*—a deusa da fortuna; *haya*—é; *kabhu*—às vezes; *vā*—ou; *cit-śakti*—a potência espiritual; *khāṭe*—num catre; *vasi'*—sentado; *bhakta-gaṇe*—aos devotos; *dilā*—deu; *prema-bhakti*—amor a Deus.

**Às vezes, o Senhor assumia o papel da deusa Durgā, de Lakṣmī [a deusa da fortuna] ou da principal potência, Yogamāyā. Sentado num catre, Ele concedia amor a Deus a todos os devotos presentes.**
