### CC Adi 17.286

> চতুর্ভুজ মূর্তি ধরি’ আছেন বসিয়া ।
> কৃষ্ণ দেখি’ গোপী কহে নিকটে আসিয়া ॥২৮৬॥

catur-bhuja mūrti dhari' āchena vasiyā

kṛṣṇa dekhi' gopī kahe nikaṭe āsiyā

*catur-bhuja*—de quatro braços; *mūrti*—forma; *dhari'*—assumindo; *āchena*—estava; *vasiyā*—sentado; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *dekhi'*—vendo; *gopī*—as gopīs; *kahe*—dizem; *nikaṭe*—perto; *āsiyā*—chegando ali.

**Kṛṣṇa assumiu Sua forma de Nārāyaṇa de quatro braços e sentou-Se ali. Quando todas as gopīs chegaram, elas olharam para Ele e falaram o seguinte.**
