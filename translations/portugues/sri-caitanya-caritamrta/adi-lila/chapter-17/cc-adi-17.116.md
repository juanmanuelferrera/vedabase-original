### CC Adi 17.116

> নিত্যানন্দ-গোসাঞি প্রভুর আবেশ জানিল ।\
> গঙ্গাজল-পাত্র আনি’ সম্মুখে ধরিল ॥১১৬॥

nityānanda-gosāñi prabhura āveśa jānila
gaṅgā-jala-pātra āni' sammukhe dharila

*nityānanda-gosāñi*—o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *āveśa*—êxtase; *jānila*—pôde compreender; *gaṅgā-jala*—água do Ganges; *pātra*—vasilha; *āni'*—trazendo; *sammukhe*—diante; *dharila*—colocou-a.

**Nityānanda Prabhu Gosāñi, compreendendo o estado de êxtase de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, trouxe uma vasilha de água do Ganges como símbolo e a colocou diante d'Ele.**
