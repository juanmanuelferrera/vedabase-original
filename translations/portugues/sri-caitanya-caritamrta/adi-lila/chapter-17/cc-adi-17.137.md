### CC Adi 17.137

> পাছে সম্প্রদায়ে নৃত্য করে গৌরচন্দ্র ।\
> তাঁর সঙ্গে নাচি’ বুলে প্রভু নিত্যানন্দ ॥১৩৭॥

pāche sampradāye nṛtya kare gauracandra
tāṅra saṅge nāci' bule prabhu nityānanda

*pāche*—na retaguarda; *sampradāye*—no grupo; *nṛtya*—dançando; *kare*—faz; *gauracandra*—o Senhor Gaurāṅga; *tāṅra*—Sua; *saṅge*—junto com; *nāci'*—dançando; *bule*—move-se; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *nityānanda*—de nome Nityānanda.

**O próprio Senhor Gaurasundara dançava no grupo da retaguarda, e Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu movia-se acompanhando a dança do Senhor Caitanya.**
