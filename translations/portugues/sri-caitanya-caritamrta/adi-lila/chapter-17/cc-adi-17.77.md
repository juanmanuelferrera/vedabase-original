### CC Adi 17.77

> মুরারিকে কহে তুমি কৃষ্ণ বশ কৈলা ।\
> শুনিয়া মুরারি শ্লোক কহিতে লাগিলা ॥৭৭॥

murārike kahe tumi kṛṣṇa vaśa kailā

śuniyā murāri śloka kahite lāgilā

*murārike*—a Murāri; *kahe*—diz; *tumi*—tu; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *vaśa*—satisfeito; *kaila*—fizeste; *śuniyā*—ouvindo; *murāri*—de nome Murāri; *śloka*—verso; *kahite*—a falar; *lāgila*—começou.

**O Senhor Caitanya então louvou Murāri Gupta, dizendo: "Tu satisfizeste o Senhor Kṛṣṇa." Ao ouvir isso, Murāri Gupta citou um verso do *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*.**
