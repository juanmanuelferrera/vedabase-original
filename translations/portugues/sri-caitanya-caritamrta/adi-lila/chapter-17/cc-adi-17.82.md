### CC Adi 17.82

> শত দুই ফল প্রভু শীঘ্র পাড়াইল ।
> প্রক্ষালন করি’ কৃষ্ণে ভোগ লাগাইল ॥৮২॥

śata dui phala prabhu śīghra pāḍāila

prakṣālana kari' kṛṣṇe bhoga lāgāila

*śata*—cem; *dui*—dois; *phala*—frutos; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *śīghra*—logo; *pāḍāila*—mandou colher; *prakṣālana*—lavando; *kari'*—fazendo; *kṛṣṇe*—ao Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *bhoga*—oferenda; *lāgāila*—assim fez.

**O Senhor imediatamente colheu cerca de duzentos frutos e, após lavá-los, ofereceu-os a Kṛṣṇa para que os comesse.**
