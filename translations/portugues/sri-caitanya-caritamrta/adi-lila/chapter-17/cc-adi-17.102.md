### CC Adi 17.102

> প্রভু-সঙ্গে নৃত্য করে পরম উল্লাসে ।\
> প্রভু তারে প্রেম দিল, প্রেমরসে ভাসে ॥১০২॥

prabhu-saṅge nṛtya kare parama ullāse

prabhu tāre prema dila, prema-rase bhāse

*prabhu-saṅge*—junto com o Senhor; *nṛtya kare*—dançava; *parama*—muita; *ullāse*—em satisfação; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *tāre*—a ele; *prema*—amor a Deus; *dila*—outorgou; *prema-rase*—na doçura do amor a Deus; *bhāse*—começou a flutuar.

**Ele dançou com o Senhor porque foi favorecido com amor a Kṛṣṇa. Assim, flutuou nas doçuras do amor a Deus.**
