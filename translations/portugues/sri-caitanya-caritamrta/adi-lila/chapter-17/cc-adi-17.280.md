### CC Adi 17.280

> ইহা ছাড়ি’ কৃষ্ণ যদি হয় অন্যাকার ।\
> গোপিকার ভাব নাহি যায় নিকট তাহার ॥২৮০॥

ihā chāḍi' kṛṣṇa yadi haya anyākāra

gopikāra bhāva nāhi yāya nikaṭa tāhāra

*ihā*—esta; *chāḍi'*—abandonando; *kṛṣṇa*—Kṛṣṇa; *yadi*—se; *haya*—assume; *anya-ākāra*—outra forma; *gopikāra*—das gopīs; *bhāva*—o êxtase; *nāhi*—não; *yāya*—surge; *nikaṭa*—perto; *tāhāra*—dessa (forma).

**Se o Senhor Kṛṣṇa abandona esta forma original e assume outra forma de Viṣṇu, a proximidade dEle não pode evocar a disposição extática das gopīs.**
