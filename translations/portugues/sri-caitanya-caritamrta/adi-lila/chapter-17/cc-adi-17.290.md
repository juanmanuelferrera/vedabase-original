### CC Adi 17.290

> রাধা দেখি’ কৃষ্ণ তাঁরে হাস্য করিতে ।\
> সেই চতুর্ভুজ মূর্তি চাহেন রাখিতে ॥২৯০॥

rādhā dekhi' kṛṣṇa tāṅre hāsya karite

sei catur-bhuja mūrti cāhena rākhite

*rādhā*—Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; *dekhi'*—vendo; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *tāṅre*—a Ela; *hāsya*—brincar; *karite*—fazer; *sei*—aquela; *catur-bhuja*—de quatro braços; *mūrti*—forma; *cāhena*—quis; *rākhite*—manter.

**Quando o Senhor Kṛṣṇa viu Rādhārāṇī, Ele quis manter a forma de quatro braços para brincar com Ela.**
