### CC Adi 17.284

> দূর হৈতে কৃষ্ণে দেখি’ বলে গোপীগণ ।
> “এই দেখ কুঞ্জের ভিতর ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ।।” ॥ ২৮৪ ॥

dūra haite kṛṣṇe dekhi' bale gopī-gaṇa

"ei dekha kuñjera bhitara vrajendra-nandana"

*dūra haite*—de longe; *kṛṣṇe*—a Kṛṣṇa; *dekhi'*—vendo; *bale*—disseram; *gopī-gaṇa*—todas as gopīs; *ei dekha*—vejam aqui; *kuñjera*—o arbusto; *bhitara*—dentro; *vrajendra-nandana*—o filho de Nanda Mahārāja.

**"Vejam!", disseram as gopīs ao avistarem Kṛṣṇa de um lugar distante. "Aqui, dentro de um arbusto, está Kṛṣṇa, o filho de Nanda Mahārāja."**
