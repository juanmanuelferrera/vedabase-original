### CC Adi 17.248

> এক পড়ুয়া আইল প্রভুকে দেখিতে ।
> ‘গোপী’ ‘গোপী’ নাম শুনি’ লাগিল বলিতে ॥২৪৮॥

eka paḍuyā āila prabhuke dekhite

'gopī' 'gopī' nāma śuni' lāgila balite

*eka paḍuyā*—um estudante; *āila*—veio até ali; *prabhuke*—o Senhor; *dekhite*—para ver; *gopī gopī*—"Gopī, Gopī"; *nāma*—o nome; *śuni'*—ouvindo; *lāgila*—começou; *balite*—a dizer.

**Um estudante que veio ver o Senhor ficou pasmo de que o Senhor estivesse cantando "Gopī! Gopī!" Assim, ele falou o seguinte.**
