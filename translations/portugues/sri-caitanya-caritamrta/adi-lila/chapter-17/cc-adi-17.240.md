### CC Adi 17.240

> কহিতে, শুনিতে ঐছে প্রাতঃকাল হৈল ।
> প্রভু শ্রীবাসেরে তোষি’ আলিঙ্গন কৈল ॥২৪০॥

kahite, śunite aiche prātaḥ-kāla haila

prabhu śrīvāsere toṣi' āliṅgana kaila

*kahite*—falando; *śunite*—ouvindo; *aiche*—dessa maneira; *prātaḥ-kāla*—a manhã; *haila*—surgiu; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śrīvāsere*—a Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura; *toṣi'*—satisfazendo; *āliṅgana*—abraço; *kaila*—deu.

**Enquanto o Senhor assim pedia e Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura falava, a manhã surgiu, e o Senhor abraçou Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura e o satisfez.**
