### CC Adi 6.65-66

> কৃষ্ণের প্রেয়সী ব্রজে যত গোপীগণ ।
> যাঁর পদধূলি করে উদ্ধব প্রার্থন ॥৬৫॥
> যাঁ-সবার উপরে কৃষ্ণের প্রিয় নাহি আন ।
> তাঁহারা আপনাকে করে দাসী-অভিমান ॥৬৬॥

kṛṣṇera preyasī vraje yata gopī-gaṇa
yāṅra pada-dhūli kare uddhava prārthana
yāṅ-sabāra upare kṛṣṇera priya nāhi āna
tāṅhārā āpanāke kare dāsī-abhimāna

*kṛṣṇera*—do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *preyasī*—as moças amadas; *vraje*—em Vṛndāvana; *yata*—todas; *gopī-gaṇa*—as gopīs; *yāṅra*—de quem; *pada-dhūli*—a poeira dos pés; *kare*—faz; *uddhava*—de nome Uddhava; *prārthana*—desejando; *yāṅ-sabāra*—todas elas; *upare*—além; *kṛṣṇera*—do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *priya*—querido; *nāhi*—não há; *āna*—ninguém mais; *tāṅhārā*—todas elas; *āpanāke*—a si mesmas; *kare*—fazem; *dāsī-abhimāna*—a concepção de serem criadas.

**Mesmo as amadas namoradas do Senhor Kṛṣṇa em Vṛndāvana, as gopīs, a poeira de cujos pés era desejada por Śrī Uddhava e além das quais ninguém é mais querido a Kṛṣṇa, consideram-se criadas de Kṛṣṇa.**
