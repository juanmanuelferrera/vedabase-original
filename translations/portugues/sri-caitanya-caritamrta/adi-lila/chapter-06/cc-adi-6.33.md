### CC Adi 6.33

> অদ্বৈত-আচার্য — ঈশ্বরের অংশবর্য ।\
> তাঁর তত্ত্ব-নাম-গুণ, সকলি আশ্চর্য ॥৩৩॥

advaita-ācārya--īśvarera aṁśa-varya
tāṅra tattva-nāma-guṇa, sakali āścarya

*advaita-ācārya*—Advaita Ācārya Prabhu; *īśvarera*—do Senhor Supremo; *aṁśa-varya*—a parte principal; *tāṅra*—Seus; *tattva*—verdades; *nāma*—nomes; *guṇa*—atributos; *sakali*—todos; *āścarya*—maravilhosos.

**Śrī Advaita Ācārya é o membro principal do Senhor Supremo. Suas verdades, nomes e atributos são todos maravilhosos.**
