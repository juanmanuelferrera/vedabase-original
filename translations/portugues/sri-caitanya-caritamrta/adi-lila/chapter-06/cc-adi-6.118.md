### CC Adi 6.118

jaya jaya jaya śrī-advaita ācārya

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya, nityānanda ārya

*jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *jaya*—todas as glórias; *śrī-advaita ācārya*—a Śrī Advaita Ācārya; *jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *śrī-caitanya*—ao Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nityānanda*—ao Senhor Nityānanda; *ārya*—o superior.

**Todas as glórias, todas as glórias a Śrī Advaita Ācārya! Todas as glórias ao Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu e ao superior Senhor Nityānanda!**
