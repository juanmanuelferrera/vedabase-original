### CC Adi 6.18

> যদ্যপি সাংখ্য মানে, ‘প্রধান’ — কারণ ।
> জড় হইতে কভু নহে জগৎ-সৃজন ॥১৮॥

yadyapi sāṅkhya māne, 'pradhāna'--kāraṇa
jaḍa ha-ite kabhu nahe jagat-sṛjana

*yadyapi*—embora; *sāṅkhya*—a filosofia Sāṅkhya; *māne*—aceite; *pradhāna*—os ingredientes; *kāraṇa*—causa; *jaḍa ha-ite*—da matéria; *kabhu*—em momento algum; *nahe*—não há; *jagat-sṛjana*—a criação do mundo material.

**Embora a filosofia Sāṅkhya aceite que os ingredientes materiais são a causa, jamais a criação do mundo surge da matéria morta.**
