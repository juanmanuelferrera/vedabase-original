### CC Adi 12.69

> ক্রুদ্ধ হঞা স্কন্ধ তারে জল না সঞ্চারে ।
> জলাভাবে কৃশ শাখা শুকাইয়া মরে ॥৬৯॥

kruddha hañā skandha tāre jala nā sañcāre

jalābhāve kṛśa śākhā śukāiyā mare

*kruddha hañā*—estando irado; *skandha*—o tronco; *tāre*—sobre eles; *jala*—água; *nā*—não; *sañcāre*—aspergiu; *jala-abhāve*—por falta de água; *kṛśa*—mais magro; *śākhā*—ramo; *śukāiyā*—ressequido; *mare*—morreu.

**Assim, o Senhor Caitanya não aspergiu sobre eles a água de Sua misericórdia, e eles gradualmente murcharam e morreram.**
