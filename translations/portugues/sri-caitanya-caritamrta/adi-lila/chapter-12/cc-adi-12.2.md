### CC Adi 12.2

> জয় জয় মহাপ্রভু শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ।\
> জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ জয়াদ্বৈত ধন্য ॥২॥

jaya jaya mahāprabhu śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya
jaya jaya nityānanda jayādvaita dhanya

*jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *mahāprabhu*—a Mahāprabhu; *śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya*—de nome Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya; *jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *nityānanda*—ao Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *jaya advaita*—todas as glórias a Advaita Prabhu; *dhanya*—que são todos muito gloriosos.

**Todas as glórias a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu! Todas as glórias ao Senhor Nityānanda! Todas as glórias a Śrī Advaita Prabhu! Todos Eles são gloriosos.**
