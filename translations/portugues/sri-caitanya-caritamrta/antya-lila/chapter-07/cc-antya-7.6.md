### CC Antya 7.6

> মান্য করি’ প্রভু তারে নিকটে বসাইলা  ।
> বিনয় করিয়া ভট্ট কহিতে লাগিলা ॥৬॥

mānya kari' prabhu tāre nikaṭe vasāilā
vinaya kariyā bhaṭṭa kahite lāgilā

*mānya kari'*—com grande respeito; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāre*—a ele; *nikaṭe*—perto; *vasāilā*—sentou; *vinaya kariyā*—com grande humildade; *bhaṭṭa*—Vallabha Bhaṭṭa; *kahite lāgilā*—começou a falar.

**Com grande respeito, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu fez Vallabha Bhaṭṭa sentar-se perto de Si. Então, com grande humildade, Vallabha Bhaṭṭa começou a falar.**
