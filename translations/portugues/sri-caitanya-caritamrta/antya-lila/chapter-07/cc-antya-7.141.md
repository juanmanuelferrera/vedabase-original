### CC Antya 7.141

> স্বগণ-সহিত প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণ কৈলা ।\
> মহাপ্রভু তারে তবে প্রসন্ন হইলা ॥১৪১॥

svagaṇa-sahita prabhura nimantraṇa kailā

mahāprabhu tāre tabe prasanna ha-ilā

*sva-gaṇa-sahita*—com Seus associados; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nimantraṇa*—convite; *kailā*—fez; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāre*—com ele; *tabe*—então; *prasanna ha-ilā*—ficou muito satisfeito.

**Quando Vallabha Bhaṭṭa convidou Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e Seus associados, o Senhor ficou muito satisfeito com ele.**
