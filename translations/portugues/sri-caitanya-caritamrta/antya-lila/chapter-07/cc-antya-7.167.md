### CC Antya 7.167

> অভিমান-পঙ্ক ধুঞা ভট্টেরে শোধিলা ।\
> সেইদ্বারা আর সব লোকে শিখাইলা ॥১৬৭॥

abhimāna-paṅka dhuñā bhaṭṭere śodhilā

sei-dvārā āra saba loke śikhāilā

*abhimāna-paṅka*—a lama do falso orgulho; *dhuñā*—lavando; *bhaṭṭere śodhilā*—purificou Vallabha Bhaṭṭa; *sei-dvārā*—por isso; *āra saba*—todas as outras; *loke*—pessoas; *śikhāilā*—instruiu.

**O Senhor purificou Vallabha Bhaṭṭa, limpando-o da lama do falso orgulho. Com tais atividades, o Senhor também instruiu outros.**
