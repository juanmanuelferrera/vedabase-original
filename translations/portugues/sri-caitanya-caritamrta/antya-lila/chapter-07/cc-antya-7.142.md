### CC Antya 7.142

> জগদানন্দ-পণ্ডিতের শুদ্ধ গাঢ় ভাব ।
> সত্যভামা-প্রায় প্রেম ‘বাম্য-স্বভাব’ ॥১৪২॥

jagadānanda-paṇḍitera śuddha gāḍha bhāva

satyabhāmā-prāya prema 'vāmya-svabhāva'

*jagadānanda-paṇḍitera*—de Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; *śuddha*—puro; *gāḍha*—profundo; *bhāva*—amor extático; *satyabhāmā-prāya*—como Satyabhāmā; *prema*—seu amor pelo Senhor; *vāmya-svabhāva*—natureza inclinada à contenda.

**O puro amor extático de Jagadānanda Paṇḍita por Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu era muito profundo. Pode ser comparado ao amor de Satyabhāmā, que sempre disputava com o Senhor Kṛṣṇa.**
