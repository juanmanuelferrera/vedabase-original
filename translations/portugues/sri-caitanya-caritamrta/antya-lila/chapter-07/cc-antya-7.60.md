### CC Antya 7.60

> তবে ভট্ট কহে বহু বিনয় বচন ।\
> বহু দৈন্য করি’ প্রভুরে কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ॥৬০॥

tabe bhaṭṭa kahe bahu vinaya vacana

bahu dainya kari' prabhure kaila nimantraṇa

*tabe*—em seguida; *bhaṭṭa kahe*—Vallabha Bhaṭṭa disse; *bahu*—muito; *vinaya*—humildes; *vacana*—palavras; *bahu dainya kari'*—com toda a humildade; *prabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kaila nimantraṇa*—convidou para jantar.

**Em seguida, com grande submissão e humildade, Vallabha Bhaṭṭa convidou Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu para jantar em sua casa.**
