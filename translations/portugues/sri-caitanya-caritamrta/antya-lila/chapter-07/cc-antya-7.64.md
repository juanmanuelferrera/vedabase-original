### CC Antya 7.64

> পরমানন্দ পুরী-সঙ্গে সন্ন্যাসীর গণ ।
> একদিকে বৈসে সব করিতে ভোজন ॥৬৪॥

paramānanda purī-saṅge sannyāsīra gaṇa

eka-dike vaise saba karite bhojana

*paramānanda purī-saṅge*—com Paramānanda Purī; *sannyāsīra gaṇa*—todos os associados sannyāsīs de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *eka-dike*—de um lado; *vaise*—sentaram-se; *saba*—todos; *karite bhojana*—para aceitar o prasāda.

**Todos os associados sannyāsīs de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, encabeçados por Paramānanda Purī, sentaram-se de um lado e assim participaram do prasāda.**
