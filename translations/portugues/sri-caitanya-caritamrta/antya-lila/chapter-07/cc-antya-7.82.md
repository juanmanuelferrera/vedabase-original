### CC Antya 7.82

> প্রভু কহে, — “ভাগবতার্থ বুঝিতে না পারি ।\
> ভাগবতার্থ শুনিতে আমি নহি অধিকারী ॥৮২॥

prabhu kahe,--"bhāgavatārtha bujhite nā pāri

bhāgavatārtha śunite āmi nahi adhikārī

*prabhu kahe*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu respondeu; *bhāgavata-artha*—o significado do Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; *bujhite nā pāri*—não consigo compreender; *bhāgavata-artha*—o significado do Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; *śunite*—ouvir; *āmi nahi adhikārī*—não sou a pessoa apropriada.

**O Senhor respondeu: "Não compreendo o significado do *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*. De fato, não sou pessoa adequada para ouvir seu significado.**
