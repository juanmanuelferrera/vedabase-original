### CC Antya 7.56

> প্রভুর মুখে বৈষ্ণবতা শুনিয়া সবার ।\
> ভট্টের ইচ্ছা হৈল তাঁ-সবারে দেখিবার ॥৫৬॥

prabhura mukhe vaiṣṇavatā śuniyā sabāra

bhaṭṭera icchā haila tāṅ-sabāre dekhibāra

*prabhura mukhe*—da boca de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *vaiṣṇavatā*—o padrão do vaiṣṇavismo; *śuniyā sabāra*—ouvindo acerca de todos os devotos; *bhaṭṭera*—de Vallabha Bhaṭṭa; *icchā*—desejo; *haila*—surgiu; *tāṅ-sabāre*—todos eles; *dekhibāra*—de ver.

**Quando Vallabha Bhaṭṭa ouviu da boca de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu acerca do vaiṣṇavismo puro de todos esses devotos, surgiu nele imediatamente o desejo de vê-los.**
