### CC Antya 7.70

> প্রসাদ পায় বৈষ্ণবগণ বলে, ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ।\
> হরি হরি ধ্বনি উঠে সব ব্রহ্মাণ্ড ভরি’ ॥৭০॥

prasāda pāya vaiṣṇava-gaṇa bale, 'hari' 'hari'

hari hari dhvani uṭhe saba brahmāṇḍa bhari'

*prasāda*—o prasāda; *pāya*—aceitam; *vaiṣṇava-gaṇa*—todos os vaiṣṇavas; *bale*—cantam; *hari hari*—Hari, Hari; *hari hari dhvani*—a vibração de Hari, Hari; *uṭhe*—ergue-se; *saba brahmāṇḍa*—o universo inteiro; *bhari'*—enchendo.

**Aceitando o prasāda, todos os vaiṣṇavas cantavam os santos nomes Hari, Hari. A crescente vibração do santo nome de Hari encheu o universo inteiro.**
