### CC Antya 7.103

> একদিন ভট্ট পুছিল আচার্যেরে ।\
> “জীব-‘প্রকৃতি’ ‘পতি’ করি’ মানয়ে কৃষ্ণেরে ॥১০৩॥

eka-dina bhaṭṭa puchila ācāryere

"jīva-'prakṛti' 'pati' kari' mānaye kṛṣṇere

*eka-dina*—certo dia; *bhaṭṭa*—Vallabha Bhaṭṭa; *puchila ācāryere*—perguntou a Advaita Ācārya; *jīva*—a entidade viva; *prakṛti*—fêmea; *pati*—esposo; *kari'*—como; *mānaye kṛṣṇere*—aceita Kṛṣṇa.

**Certo dia, Vallabha Bhaṭṭa disse a Advaita Ācārya: "Toda entidade viva é fêmea [prakṛti] e considera Kṛṣṇa seu esposo [pati].**
