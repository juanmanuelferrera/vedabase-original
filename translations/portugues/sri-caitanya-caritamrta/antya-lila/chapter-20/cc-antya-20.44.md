### CC Antya 20.44

> ঈর্ষ্যা, উৎকণ্ঠা, দৈন্য, প্রৌঢ়ি, বিনয় ।
> এত ভাব এক-ঠাঞি করিল উদয় ॥৪৪॥

īrṣyā, utkaṇṭhā, dainya, prauḍhi, vinaya

eta bhāva eka-ṭhāñi karila udaya

*īrṣyā*—inveja; *utkaṇṭhā*—ânsia; *dainya*—humildade; *prauḍhi*—fervor; *vinaya*—súplica; *eta bhāva*—todos esses êxtases transcendentais; *eka-ṭhāñi*—num só lugar; *karila udaya*—despertaram.

**Os sintomas extáticos de inveja, grande ânsia, humildade, fervor e súplica manifestaram-se todos ao mesmo tempo.**
