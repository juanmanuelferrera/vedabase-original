### CC Antya 9.61

> ভবানন্দ-রায়ের গোষ্ঠী করে রাজবিষয় ।\
> নানা-প্রকারে করে তারা রাজদ্রব্য ব্যয় ॥৬১॥

bhavānanda-rāyera goṣṭhī kare rāja-viṣaya

nānā-prakāre kare tārā rāja-dravya vyaya

*bhavānanda-rāyera*—de Bhavānanda Rāya; *goṣṭhī*—a família; *kare*—ocupa-se; *rāja-viṣaya*—no serviço do governo; *nānā-prakāre*—de várias maneiras; *kare*—fazem; *tārā*—todos eles; *rāja-dravya vyaya*—gastando as receitas do governo.

**"Todos os membros da família de Bhavānanda Rāya estão ocupados no serviço do governo, mas gastam as receitas do governo de várias maneiras.**
