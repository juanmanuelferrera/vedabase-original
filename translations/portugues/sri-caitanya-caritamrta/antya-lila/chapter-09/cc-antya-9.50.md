### CC Antya 9.50

> তুমি যাই’ কর তাহাঁ সর্ব সমাধান ।
> দ্রব্য যৈছে আইসে, আর রহে তার প্রাণ ।।” ॥ ৫০ ॥

tumi yāi' kara tāhāṅ sarva samādhāna

dravya yaiche āise, āra rahe tāra prāṇa"

*tumi*—tu; *yāi'*—indo; *kara*—faze; *tāhāṅ*—lá; *sarva samādhāna*—todos os ajustes; *dravya*—os bens; *yaiche*—de modo que; *āise*—venham; *āra*—e; *rahe*—permaneça; *tāra*—sua; *prāṇa*—vida.

**"Vai até lá e ajusta tudo. Quero apenas o pagamento, não a sua vida."**
