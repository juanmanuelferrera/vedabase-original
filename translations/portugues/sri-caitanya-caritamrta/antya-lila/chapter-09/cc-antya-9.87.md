### CC Antya 9.87

> শুনিয়া ক্ষোভিত হৈল মহাপ্রভুর মন ।
> ক্রোধে গোপীনাথে কৈলা বহুত ভর্ৎসন ॥৮৭॥

śuniyā kṣobhita haila mahāprabhura mana

krodhe gopīnāthe kailā bahuta bhartsana

*śuniyā*—ouvindo; *kṣobhita haila*—ficou agitada; *mahāprabhura mana*—a mente de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *krodhe*—em fúria; *gopīnāthe*—a Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka; *kailā*—fez; *bahuta bhartsana*—muita repreensão.

**"Ao ouvir isto, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ficou extremamente pesaroso de coração e, em fúria, repreendeu Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka.**
