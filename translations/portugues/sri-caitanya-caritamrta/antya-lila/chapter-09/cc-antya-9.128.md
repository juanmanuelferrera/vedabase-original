### CC Antya 9.128

> পঞ্চপুত্র-সহিতে আসি’ পড়িলা চরণে ।
> উঠাঞা প্রভু তাঁরে কৈলা আলিঙ্গনে ॥১২৮॥

pañca-putra-sahite āsi' paḍilā caraṇe

uṭhāñā prabhu tāṅre kailā āliṅgane

*pañca-putra-sahite*—com cinco filhos; *āsi'*—vindo; *paḍilā caraṇe*—prostrou-se aos pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *uṭhāñā*—levantando-o; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kailā āliṅgane*—abraçou.

**Bhavānanda Rāya, juntamente com seus cinco filhos, prostrou-se aos pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, que o levantou e o abraçou.**
