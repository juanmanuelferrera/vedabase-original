### CC Antya 9.25

> তারে নিন্দা করি’ কহে সগর্ব বচনে  ।
> রাজা কৃপা করে তাতে ভয় নাহি মানে ॥২৫॥

tāre nindā kari' kahe sagarva vacane

rājā kṛpā kare tāte bhaya nāhi māne

*tāre*—a ele; *nindā kari'*—criticando; *kahe*—disse; *sa-garva vacane*—palavras cheias de orgulho; *rājā*—o rei; *kṛpā kare*—era muito bondoso com ele; *tāte*—por isso; *bhaya nāhi māne*—ele não tinha medo.

**"Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka criticou o príncipe. Ele não temia o príncipe, pois o rei era muito bondoso para com ele.**
