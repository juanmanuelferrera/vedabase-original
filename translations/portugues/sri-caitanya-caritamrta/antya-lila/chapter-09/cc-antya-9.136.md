### CC Antya 9.136

> লোকে চমৎকার মোর এ সব দেখিয়া ।
> প্রশংসে তোমার কৃপা-মহিমা গাঞা ॥১৩৬॥

loke camatkāra mora e saba dekhiyā

praśaṁse tomāra kṛpā-mahimā gāñā

*loke*—entre as pessoas; *camatkāra*—grande assombro; *mora*—meus; *e saba*—todos estes; *dekhiyā*—ao ver; *praśaṁse*—elas glorificam; *tomāra*—Tua; *kṛpā*—da misericórdia; *mahimā*—a grandeza; *gāñā*—cantando.

**"Atônita com os meus acontecimentos, a população está glorificando a grandeza de Tua misericórdia.**
