### CC Antya 9.53

> ‘ক্রমে ক্রমে দিমু, আর যত কিছু পারি  ।
> অবিচারে প্রাণ লহ, — কি বলিতে পারি ?’ ৫৩ ॥৫৩॥

'krame krame dimu, āra yata kichu pāri

avicāre prāṇa laha,--ki balite pāri?'

*krame krame*—gradualmente; *dimu*—pagarei; *āra*—mais; *yata*—tanto quanto; *kichu*—qualquer coisa; *pāri*—eu puder; *avicāre*—sem consideração; *prāṇa laha*—tirais a minha vida; *ki balite pāri*—que posso dizer.

**"Pagarei o restante gradualmente, conforme eu puder. Sem consideração alguma, contudo, íeis tirar a minha vida. Que posso dizer?"**
