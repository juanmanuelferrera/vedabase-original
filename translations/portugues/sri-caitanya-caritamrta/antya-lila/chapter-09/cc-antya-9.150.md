### CC Antya 9.150

> কাশীমিশ্রে না সাধিল, রাজারে না সাধিল ।
> উদ্যোগ বিনা মহাপ্রভু এত ফল দিল ॥১৫০॥

kāśī-miśre nā sādhila, rājāre nā sādhila

udyoga vinā mahāprabhu eta phala dila

*kāśī-miśre*—Kāśī Miśra; *nā sādhila*—Ele não pediu; *rājāre*—o rei; *nā sādhila*—Ele não pediu; *udyoga vinā*—sem esforço; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *eta*—tal; *phala*—resultado; *dila*—deu.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu deu tanto a Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka sem fazer diretamente pedidos a Kāśī Miśra ou ao rei.**
