### CC Antya 9.81

> প্রতাপরুদ্রের এক আছয়ে নিয়মে  ।
> যত দিন রহে তেঁহ শ্রীপুরুষোত্তমে ॥৮১॥

pratāparudrera eka āchaye niyame

yata dina rahe teṅha śrī-puruṣottame

*pratāparudrera*—do rei Pratāparudra; *eka*—um; *āchaye*—há; *niyame*—um dever regular; *yata dina*—enquanto; *rahe*—permanecia; *teṅha*—ele; *śrī-puruṣottame*—em Jagannātha Purī.

**Enquanto o rei Pratāparudra permanecia em sua capital, Puruṣottama, ele cumpria um dever regular.**
