### CC Antya 9.56

> সে কহে — “বাণীনাথ নির্ভয়ে লয় কৃষ্ণনাম ।\
> ‘হরে কৃষ্ণ, হরে কৃষ্ণ’ কহে অবিশ্রাম ॥৫৬॥

se kahe--"vāṇīnātha nirbhaye laya kṛṣṇa-nāma

'hare kṛṣṇa, hare kṛṣṇa' kahe aviśrāma

*se kahe*—ele respondeu; *vāṇīnātha*—Vāṇīnātha; *nirbhaye*—sem medo; *laya kṛṣṇa-nāma*—cantava o mahā-mantra Hare Kṛṣṇa; *hare kṛṣṇa, hare kṛṣṇa*—Hare Kṛṣṇa, Hare Kṛṣṇa; *kahe aviśrāma*—cantava incessantemente.

**O mensageiro respondeu: "Ele estava cantando destemida e incessantemente o mahā-mantra: Hare Kṛṣṇa, Hare Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa, Hare Hare, Hare Rāma, Hare Rāma, Rāma Rāma, Hare Hare.**
