### CC Antya 9.12

> প্রভুর দর্শনে সব লোক প্রেমে ভাসে ।
> এইমত যায় প্রভুর রাত্রি-দিবসে ॥১২॥

prabhura darśane saba loka preme bhāse
ei-mata yāya prabhura rātri-divase

*prabhura darśane*—ao ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *saba loka*—todas as pessoas; *preme bhāse*—ficavam inundadas de amor extático; *ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *yāya*—passam; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rātri-divase*—noite e dia.

**Toda sorte de pessoas vinha ver o Senhor e, ao vê-Lo, ficavam dominadas de amor extático por Kṛṣṇa. Dessa maneira, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu passava Seus dias e Suas noites.**
