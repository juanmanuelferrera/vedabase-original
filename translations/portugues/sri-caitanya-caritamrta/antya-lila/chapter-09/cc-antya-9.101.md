### CC Antya 9.101

> মিশ্র কহে, “কৌড়ি ছাড়িবা, — নহে প্রভুর মনে ।\
> কৌড়ি ছাড়িলে প্রভু কদাচিৎ দুঃখ মানে ।।” ॥ ১০১ ॥

miśra kahe, "kauḍi chāḍibā,--nahe prabhura mane

kauḍi chāḍile prabhu kadācit duḥkha māne"

*miśra kahe*—Kāśī Miśra disse; *kauḍi chāḍibā*—dispensarás todas as dívidas; *nahe*—não é; *prabhura mane*—o pensamento de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kauḍi chāḍile*—se dispensares todas as dívidas; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kadācit*—certamente; *duḥkha māne*—ficará pesaroso.

**Kāśī Miśra disse: "Dispensar Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka de todas as suas dívidas não tornará o Senhor feliz, pois essa não é Sua intenção."**
