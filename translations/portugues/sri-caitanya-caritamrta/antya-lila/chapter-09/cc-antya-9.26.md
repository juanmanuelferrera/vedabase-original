### CC Antya 9.26

> ‘আমার ঘোড়া গ্রীবা না ফিরায় ঊর্ধ্বে নাহি চায়  ।
> তাতে ঘোড়ার মূল্য ঘাটি করিতে না যুয়ায় ।।’ ২৬ ॥২৬॥

'āmāra ghoḍā grīvā nā phirāya ūrdhve nāhi cāya

tāte ghoḍāra mūlya ghāṭi karite nā yuyāya'

*āmāra ghoḍā*—meus cavalos; *grīvā*—o pescoço; *nā phirāya*—não viram; *ūrdhve*—para cima; *nāhi cāya*—não olham; *tāte*—por causa disso; *ghoḍāra mūlya*—o preço do cavalo; *ghāṭi karite*—reduzir; *nā yuyāya*—não é apropriado.

**"Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka disse: 'Meus cavalos nunca viram o pescoço nem olham para cima. Portanto, o preço deles não deve ser reduzido.'**
