### CC Antya 9.9

> সপ্তদীপে নবখণ্ডে বৈসে যত জন ।\
> নানা-বেশে আসি’ করে প্রভুর দরশন ॥৯॥

sapta-dvīpe nava-khaṇḍe vaise yata jana
nānā-veśe āsi' kare prabhura daraśana

*sapta-dvīpe*—em sete ilhas; *nava-khaṇḍe*—em nove khaṇḍas; *vaise*—residem; *yata jana*—todas as pessoas; *nānā-veśe*—em diferentes trajes; *āsi'*—vindo; *kare prabhura daraśana*—visitavam Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Vestidas de diferentes maneiras, pessoas das sete ilhas e dos nove khaṇḍas visitavam Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
