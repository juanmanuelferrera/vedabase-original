### CC Antya 8.40

> প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণে লাগে কৌড়ি চারি পণ ।\
> কভু কাশীশ্বর, গোবিন্দ খান তিন জন ॥৪০॥

prabhura nimantraṇe lāge kauḍi cāri paṇa

kabhu kāśīśvara, govinda khāna tina jana

*prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nimantraṇe*—para o convite; *lāge*—é necessário; *kauḍi cāri paṇa*—quatro vezes oitenta pequenos búzios; *kabhu kāśīśvara*—às vezes Kāśīśvara; *govinda*—o servo pessoal de Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *khāna*—comem; *tina jana*—três pessoas.

**Convidar Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu custava 320 kauḍis [pequenos búzios]. Isso fornecia almoço para três pessoas, incluindo Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e, às vezes, Kāśīśvara e Govinda.**
