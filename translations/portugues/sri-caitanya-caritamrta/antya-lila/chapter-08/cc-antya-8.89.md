### CC Antya 8.89

> ভোজ্যান্ন বিপ্র যদি নিমন্ত্রণ করে ।
> কিছু ‘প্রসাদ’ আনে, কিছু পাক করে ঘরে ॥৮৯॥

bhojyānna vipra yadi nimantraṇa kare

kichu 'prasāda' āne, kichu pāka kare ghare

*bhojya-anna vipra*—um brāhmaṇa em cuja casa se podia aceitar um convite; *yadi*—se; *nimantraṇa kare*—convida; *kichu*—algum; *prasāda*—prasāda; *āne*—traz; *kichu*—algum; *pāka kare*—cozinha; *ghare*—em casa.

**Quando um brāhmaṇa em cuja casa se podia aceitar um convite O convidava, esse brāhmaṇa comprava parte do prasāda e cozinhava o restante em casa.**
