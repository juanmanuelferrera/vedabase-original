### CC Antya 8.45

> এই নিন্দা করি’ কহে সর্বলোক-স্থানে ।
> প্রভুরে দেখিতেহ অবশ্য আইসে প্রতিদিনে ॥৪৫॥

ei nindā kari' kahe sarva-loka-sthāne

prabhure dekhiteha avaśya āise prati-dine

*ei nindā*—esta crítica; *kari'*—fazendo; *kahe*—fala; *sarva-loka-sthāne*—a todos; *prabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhiteha*—para ver, ainda; *avaśya*—certamente; *āise*—vem; *prati-dine*—diariamente.

**Dessa maneira, Rāmacandra Purī blasfemava contra Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu diante de todos; não obstante, ele vinha regularmente ver o Senhor todos os dias.**
