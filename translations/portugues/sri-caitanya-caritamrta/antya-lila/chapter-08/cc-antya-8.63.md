### CC Antya 8.63

> প্রণাম করি’ প্রভু কৈলা চরণ বন্দন ।
> প্রভুরে কহয়ে কিছু হাসিয়া বচন ॥৬৩॥

praṇāma kari' prabhu kailā caraṇa vandana

prabhure kahaye kichu hāsiyā vacana

*praṇāma kari'*—oferecendo reverências; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kailā caraṇa vandana*—prestou respeito a seus pés; *prabhure*—a Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kahaye*—ele diz; *kichu*—algumas; *hāsiyā*—sorrindo; *vacana*—palavras.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ofereceu Suas reverências a Rāmacandra Purī, adorando seus pés. Então Rāmacandra Purī sorriu e falou ao Senhor.**
