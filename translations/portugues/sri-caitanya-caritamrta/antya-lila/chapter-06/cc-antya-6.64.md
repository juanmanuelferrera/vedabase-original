### CC Antya 6.64

> শুনি’ পণ্ডিত ভট্টাচার্য যত বিপ্র আইলা ।
> মান্য করি’ প্রভু সবারে উপরে বসাইলা ॥৬৪॥

śuni' paṇḍita bhaṭṭācārya yata vipra āilā

mānya kari' prabhu sabāre upare vasāilā

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *paṇḍita bhaṭṭācārya*—eruditos e sacerdotes letrados; *yata*—todos; *vipra*—brāhmaṇas; *āilā*—vieram; *mānya kari'*—prestando honra; *prabhu*—o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *sabāre*—a todos eles; *upare vasāilā*—fez sentar-se no alto.

**Ao ouvirem falar do festival, toda sorte de eruditos letrados, brāhmaṇas e sacerdotes dirigiram-se ao local. O Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu honrou-os e fê-los sentar-se na plataforma elevada com Ele.**
