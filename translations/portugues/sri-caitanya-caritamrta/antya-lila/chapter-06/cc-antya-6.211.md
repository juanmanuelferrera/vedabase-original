### CC Antya 6.211

> রঘুনাথ সমুদ্রে যাঞা স্নান করিলা ।
> জগন্নাথ দেখি’ পুনঃ গোবিন্দ-পাশ আইলা ॥২১১॥

raghunātha samudre yāñā snāna karilā

jagannātha dekhi' punaḥ govinda-pāśa āilā

*raghunātha*—Raghunātha dāsa; *samudre*—ao mar; *yāñā*—indo; *snāna karilā*—banhou-se; *jagannātha dekhi'*—após ver o Senhor Jagannātha; *punaḥ*—novamente; *govinda-pāśa āilā*—veio a Govinda.

**Raghunātha dāsa banhou-se no mar e viu o Senhor Jagannātha. Depois, retornou a Govinda, o servo pessoal de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
