### CC Antya 6.103

> মহাপ্রভু তাঁর নৃত্য করেন দরশন ।\
> সবে নিত্যানন্দ দেখে, না দেখে অন্যজন ॥১০৩॥

mahāprabhu tāṅra nṛtya karena daraśana

sabe nityānanda dekhe, nā dekhe anya-jana

*mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅra*—Sua; *nṛtya*—dança; *karena daraśana*—vê; *sabe*—somente; *nityānanda dekhe*—Nityānanda Prabhu vê; *nā dekhe*—não veem; *anya-jana*—os outros.

**O Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu observava a dança do Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu. Nityānanda Prabhu podia ver isso, mas os outros não podiam.**
