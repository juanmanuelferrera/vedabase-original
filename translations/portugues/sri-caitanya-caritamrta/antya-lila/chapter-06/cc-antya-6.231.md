### CC Antya 6.231

> প্রভুর আগে স্বরূপ নিবেদিলা আর দিনে  ।\
> রঘুনাথ নিবেদয় প্রভুর চরণে ॥২৩১॥

prabhura āge svarūpa nivedilā āra dine

raghunātha nivedaya prabhura caraṇe

*prabhura āge*—diante de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *svarūpa*—Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; *nivedilā*—submeteu; *āra dine*—no dia seguinte; *raghunātha nivedaya*—Raghunātha dāsa pergunta; *prabhura caraṇe*—aos pés de lótus do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**No dia seguinte, Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī submeteu ao Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu: "Raghunātha dāsa tem isto a dizer a Vossos pés de lótus.**
