### CC Antya 6.41

> চৈতন্যচন্দ্রের কৃপা হঞাছে ইঁহারে  ।\
> চৈতন্যচন্দ্রের ‘বাতুল’ কে রাখিতে পারে ?” ৪১ ॥৪১॥

caitanya-candrera kṛpā hañāche iṅhāre

caitanya-candrera 'bātula' ke rākhite pāre?"

*caitanya-candrera*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kṛpā*—misericórdia; *hañāche iṅhāre*—foi-lhe concedida; *caitanya-candrera*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bātula*—louco; *ke*—quem; *rākhite pāre*—pode manter.

**"O Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu concedeu-lhe plenamente Sua misericórdia. Quem pode manter em casa tamanho louco de Caitanya-candra?"**
