### CC Antya 6.173

> গ্রামে-গ্রামের পথ ছাড়ি’ যায় বনে বনে ।\
> কায়মনোবাক্যে চিন্তে চৈতন্য-চরণে ॥১৭৩॥

grāme-grāmera patha chāḍi' yāya vane vane

kāya-mano-vākye cinte caitanya-caraṇe

*grāme-grāmera*—de aldeia em aldeia; *patha*—o caminho geral; *chāḍi'*—abandonando; *yāya*—vai; *vane vane*—pelas selvas; *kāya-manaḥ-vākye*—com o corpo, a mente e as palavras; *cinte*—pensa; *caitanya caraṇe*—nos pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Abandonando o caminho geral de aldeia em aldeia, ele atravessou as selvas, pensando de todo o coração e alma nos pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
