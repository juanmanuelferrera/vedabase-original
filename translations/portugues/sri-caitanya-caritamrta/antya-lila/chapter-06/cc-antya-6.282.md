### CC Antya 6.282

> গোবিন্দ-পাশ শুনি’ প্রভু পুছেন স্বরূপেরে ।
> ‘রঘু ভিক্ষা লাগি’ ঠাড় কেনে নহে সিংহদ্বারে ?’ ॥২৮২॥

govinda-pāśa śuni' prabhu puchena svarūpere

'raghu bhikṣā lāgi' ṭhāḍa kene nahe siṁha-dvāre'?

*govinda-pāśa*—de Govinda; *śuni'*—ouvindo; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *puchena svarūpere*—indagou de Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; *raghu*—Raghunātha dāsa; *bhikṣā lāgi'*—para mendigar; *ṭhāḍa kene nahe*—por que não se posta; *siṁha-dvāre*—no portão Siṁha-dvāra.

**Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ouviu essa notícia de Govinda, indagou de Svarūpa Dāmodara: "Por que Raghunātha dāsa não mais se posta no portão Siṁha-dvāra para mendigar esmolas?"**
