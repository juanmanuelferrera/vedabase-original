### CC Antya 6.179

> সেই-সঙ্গে রঘুনাথ গেল পলাঞা  ।\
> দশ জন যাহ, তারে আনহ ধরিয়া ।।” ১৭৯ ॥১৭৯॥

sei-saṅge raghunātha gela palāñā

daśa jana yāha, tāre ānaha dhariyā"

*sei-saṅge*—com eles; *raghunātha*—Raghunātha dāsa; *gela palāñā*—fugiu; *daśa jana*—dez homens; *yāha*—ide; *tāre*—a ele; *ānaha*—trazei; *dhariyā*—capturando.

**"Raghunātha dāsa fugiu com eles. Dez homens devem ir imediatamente capturá-lo e trazê-lo de volta."**
