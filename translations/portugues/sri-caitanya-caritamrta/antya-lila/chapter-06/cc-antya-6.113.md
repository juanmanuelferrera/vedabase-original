### CC Antya 6.113

> প্রতিদিন মহাপ্রভু করেন ভোজন ।
> মধ্যে মধ্যে প্রভু তাঁরে দেন দরশন ॥১১৩॥

prati-dina mahāprabhu karena bhojana

madhye madhye prabhu tāṅre dena daraśana

*prati-dina*—diariamente; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *karena bhojana*—come; *madhye madhye*—às vezes; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *dena daraśana*—concede Sua audiência.

**Todos os dias, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu comia na casa de Rāghava Paṇḍita. Às vezes, Ele concedia a Rāghava Paṇḍita a oportunidade de vê-Lo.**
