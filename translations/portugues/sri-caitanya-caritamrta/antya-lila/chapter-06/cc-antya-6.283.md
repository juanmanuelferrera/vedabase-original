### CC Antya 6.283

> স্বরূপ কহে, — “সিংহদ্বারে দুঃখ অনুভবিয়া ।\
> ছত্রে মাগি’ খায় মধ্যাহ্নকালে গিয়া ।।” ॥ ২৮৩ ॥

svarūpa kahe,--"siṁha-dvāre duḥkha anubhaviyā

chatre māgi' khāya madhyāhna-kāle giyā"

*svarūpa kahe*—Svarūpa Dāmodara respondeu; *siṁha-dvāre*—no portão Siṁha-dvāra; *duḥkha anubhaviyā*—sentindo-se infeliz; *chatre*—no posto de caridade; *māgi'*—mendigando; *khāya*—ele come; *madhyāhna-kāle*—ao meio-dia; *giyā*—indo.

**Svarūpa Dāmodara respondeu: "Raghunātha dāsa sentia-se infeliz postando-se no Siṁha-dvāra. Por isso, ele agora vai ao meio-dia mendigar esmolas no posto de caridade."**
