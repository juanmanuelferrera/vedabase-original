### CC Antya 6.100

> এই ত’ কহিলুঁ নিত্যানন্দের বিহার ।\
> ‘চিড়া-দধি-মহোৎসব’-নামে খ্যাতি যার ॥১০০॥

ei ta' kahiluṅ nityānandera vihāra

'ciḍā-dadhi-mahotsava'-nāme khyāti yāra

*ei ta'*—dessa maneira; *kahiluṅ*—descrevi; *nityānandera vihāra*—os passatempos do Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *ciḍā-dadhi-mahotsava*—o festival de comer arroz batido e iogurte; *nāme*—de nome; *khyāti*—a fama; *yāra*—do qual.

**Dessa maneira, descrevi os passatempos do Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu relacionados com o célebre festival de arroz batido e iogurte.**
