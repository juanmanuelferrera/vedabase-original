### CC Antya 6.22

> মারিতে আনয়ে যদি দেখে রঘুনাথে ।
> মন ফিরি’ যায়, তবে না পারে মারিতে ॥২২॥

mārite ānaye yadi dekhe raghunāthe

mana phiri' yāya, tabe nā pāre mārite

*mārite*—para espancar; *ānaye*—traz; *yadi*—quando; *dekhe*—vê; *raghunāthe*—Raghunātha dāsa; *mana*—sua mente; *phiri' yāya*—transforma-se; *tabe*—nesse momento; *nā pāre mārite*—não conseguia espancar.

**O caudhurī queria espancá-lo, mas, assim que via o rosto de Raghunātha, sua mente se transformava e ele não conseguia espancá-lo.**
