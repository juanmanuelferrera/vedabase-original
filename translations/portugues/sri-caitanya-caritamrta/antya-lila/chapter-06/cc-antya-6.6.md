### CC Antya 6.6

> রামানন্দের কৃষ্ণকথা, স্বরূপের গান ।\
> বিরহ-বেদনায় প্রভুর রাখয়ে পরাণ ॥৬॥

rāmānandera kṛṣṇa-kathā, svarūpera gāna
viraha-vedanāya prabhura rākhaye parāṇa

*rāmānandera*—de Rāmānanda Rāya; *kṛṣṇa-kathā*—as conversas sobre o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *svarūpera gāna*—os cantos de Svarūpa Dāmodara; *viraha-vedanāya*—no momento das pungências da separação; *prabhura*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rākhaye*—mantêm; *parāṇa*—a vida.

**Quando o Senhor sentia agudamente as dores da separação de Kṛṣṇa, somente as conversas de Śrī Rāma-nanda Rāya sobre Kṛṣṇa e os doces cantos de Svarūpa Dāmodara O mantinham vivo.**
