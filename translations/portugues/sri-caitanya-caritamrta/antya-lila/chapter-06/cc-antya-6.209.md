### CC Antya 6.209

> এত বলি’ প্রভু মধ্যাহ্ন করিতে উঠিলা ।
> রঘুনাথ-দাস সব ভক্তেরে মিলিলা ॥২০৯॥

eta bali' prabhu madhyāhna karite uṭhilā

raghunātha-dāsa saba bhaktere mililā

*eta bali'*—após dizer isto; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *madhyāhna karite*—para realizar Seus deveres do meio-dia; *uṭhilā*—levantou-se; *raghunātha-dāsa*—Raghunātha dāsa; *saba*—todos; *bhaktere*—os devotos; *mililā*—encontrou.

**Após dizer isto, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu levantou-se e foi realizar Seus deveres do meio-dia, e Raghunātha encontrou-se com todos os devotos presentes.**
