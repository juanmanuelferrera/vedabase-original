### CC Antya 6.165

> রঘুনাথে কহে, — “তারে করহ সাধন ।
> সেবা যেন করে, আর নাহিক ব্রাহ্মণ ।।” ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

raghunāthe kahe,--"tāre karaha sādhana

sevā yena kare, āra nāhika brāhmaṇa"

*raghunāthe kahe*—ele disse a Raghunātha dāsa; *tāre*—a ele; *karaha sādhana*—induze a aceitar o serviço; *sevā*—serviço; *yena*—que; *kare*—ele faz; *āra*—outro; *nāhika*—não há; *brāhmaṇa*—brāhmaṇa.

**Yadunandana Ācārya pediu a Raghunātha dāsa: "Por favor, induze o brāhmaṇa a retomar o serviço, pois não há outro brāhmaṇa para fazê-lo."**
