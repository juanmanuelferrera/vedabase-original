### CC Antya 6.249

> সে মনুষ্য শিবানন্দ-সেনেরে পুছিল  ।\
> “মহাপ্রভুর স্থানে এক ‘বৈরাগী’ দেখিল ॥২৪৯॥

se manuṣya śivānanda-senere puchila

"mahāprabhura sthāne eka 'vairāgī' dekhila

*se manuṣya*—aquele mensageiro; *śivānanda-senere*—a Śivānanda Sena; *puchila*—perguntou; *mahāprabhura sthāne*—no lugar de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *eka vairāgī*—uma pessoa na ordem renunciada; *dekhila*—viste.

**Aquele homem perguntou a Śivānanda Sena: "Viste alguém na ordem renunciada na residência de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu?**
