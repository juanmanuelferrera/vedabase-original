### CC Antya 6.48

> প্রভু বোলায়, তেঁহো নিকটে না করে গমন ।
> আকর্ষিয়া তাঁর মাথে প্রভু ধরিলা চরণ ॥৪৮॥

prabhu bolāya, teṅho nikaṭe nā kare gamana

ākarṣiyā tāṅra māthe prabhu dharilā caraṇa

*prabhu bolāya*—o Senhor chama; *teṅho*—ele; *nikaṭe*—para perto; *nā kare gamana*—não vem; *ākarṣiyā*—trazendo-o para perto; *tāṅra māthe*—sobre sua cabeça; *prabhu*—Nityānanda Prabhu; *dharilā caraṇa*—colocou Seus pés.

**O Senhor o chamou, mas Raghunātha dāsa não se aproximou Dele. Então, o Senhor agarrou-o à força e colocou Seus pés de lótus sobre a cabeça de Raghunātha dāsa.**
