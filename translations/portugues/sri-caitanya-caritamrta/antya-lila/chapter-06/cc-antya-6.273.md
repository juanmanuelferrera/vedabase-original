### CC Antya 6.273

> ‘রঘু কেনে আমায় নিম়ন্ত্রণ ছাড়ি’ দিল ?’ স্বরূপ কহে, — “মনে কিছু বিচার করিল ॥২৭৩॥

'raghu kene āmāya nimantraṇa chāḍi' dila?'

svarūpa kahe,--"mane kichu vicāra karila

*raghu*—Raghunātha dāsa; *kene*—por que; *āmāya*—a Mim; *nimantraṇa*—convite; *chāḍi' dila*—parou de fazer; *svarūpa kahe*—Svarūpa Dāmodara respondeu; *mane*—em sua mente; *kichu*—algo; *vicāra karila*—ele refletiu.

**O Senhor perguntou: "Por que Raghunātha dāsa parou de Me convidar?" Svarūpa Dāmodara respondeu: "Ele deve ter reconsiderado algo em sua mente.**
