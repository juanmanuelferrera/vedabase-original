### CC Antya 6.157

> হেনকালে গৌড়দেশের সব ভক্তগণ ।
> প্রভুরে দেখিতে নীলাচলে করিলা গমন ॥১৫৭॥

hena-kāle gauḍa-deśera saba bhakta-gaṇa

prabhure dekhite nīlācale karilā gamana

*hena-kāle*—nesse momento; *gauḍa-deśera*—de Bengala; *saba*—todos; *bhakta-gaṇa*—os devotos; *prabhure*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhite*—para ver; *nīlācale*—a Jagannātha Purī; *karilā gamana*—foram.

**Nesse momento, todos os devotos de Bengala dirigiam-se a Jagannātha Purī para ver o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
