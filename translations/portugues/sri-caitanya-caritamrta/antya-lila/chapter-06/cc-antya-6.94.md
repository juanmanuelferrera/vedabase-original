### CC Antya 6.94

> ভোজন করি’ নিত্যানন্দ আচমন কৈলা ।\
> চারি কুণ্ডীর অবশেষ রঘুন়াথে দিলা ॥৯৪॥

bhojana kari' nityānanda ācamana kailā

cāri kuṇḍīra avaśeṣa raghunāthe dilā

*bhojana kari'*—após terminar de comer; *nityānanda*—Nityānanda Prabhu; *ācamana kailā*—lavando as mãos e a boca; *cāri kuṇḍīra*—dos quatro potes; *avaśeṣa*—o que restava; *raghunāthe dilā*—entregou a Raghunātha dāsa.

**Após o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu terminar de comer, Ele lavou as mãos e a boca e deu a Raghunātha dāsa o alimento que restava nos quatro potes.**
