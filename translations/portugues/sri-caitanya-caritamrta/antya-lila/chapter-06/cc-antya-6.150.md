### CC Antya 6.150

> “প্রভুর সঙ্গে যত মহান্ত, ভৃত্য, আশ্রিত জন  ।
> পূজিতে চাহিয়ে আমি সবার চরণ ॥১৫০॥

"prabhura saṅge yata mahānta, bhṛtya āśrita jana

pūjite cāhiye āmi sabāra caraṇa

*prabhura saṅge*—com o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *yata*—todos; *mahānta*—grandes devotos; *bhṛtya*—servos; *āśrita jana*—pessoas subordinadas; *pūjite*—adorar; *cāhiye*—desejo; *āmi*—eu; *sabāra caraṇa*—os pés de lótus de todos eles.

**"Desejo dar dinheiro", disse ele, "apenas para adorar os pés de lótus de todos os grandes devotos, servos e subordinados do Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu.**
