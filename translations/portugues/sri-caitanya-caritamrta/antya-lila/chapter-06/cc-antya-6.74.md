### CC Antya 6.74

> প্রভু কহে, — “এ-দ্রব্য দিনে করিয়ে ভোজন ।
> রাত্র্যে তোমার ঘরে প্রসাদ করিমু ভক্ষণ ॥৭৪॥

prabhu kahe,--"e-dravya dine kariye bhojana

rātrye tomāra ghare prasāda karimu bhakṣaṇa

*prabhu kahe*—o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu disse; *e-dravya*—este alimento; *dine*—durante o dia; *kariye bhojana*—deixa-Me comer; *rātrye*—à noite; *tomāra ghare*—em tua casa; *prasāda*—o prasāda; *karimu bhakṣaṇa*—comerei.

**O Senhor Nityānanda respondeu: "Deixa-Me comer todo este alimento aqui durante o dia, e à noite comerei em tua casa.**
