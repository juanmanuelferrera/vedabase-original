### CC Antya 6.147

> তাঁরে নিষেধিলা, — “প্রভুরে এবে না কহিবা ।
> নিজ-ঘরে যাবেন যবে তবে নিবেদিবা ।।” ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

tāṅre niṣedhilā,--"prabhure ebe nā kahibā

nija-ghare yābena yabe tabe nivedibā"

*tāṅre*—a ele; *niṣedhilā*—proibiu; *prabhure*—ao Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *ebe*—agora; *nā kahibā*—não fale; *nija-ghare*—para o Seu lar; *yābena*—regressará; *yabe*—quando; *tabe*—então; *nivedibā*—por favor, informe-O.

**Raghunātha dāsa advertiu o tesoureiro: "Não fale disto ao Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu agora, mas, quando Ele regressar ao lar, por favor, informe-O sobre esta oferenda."**
