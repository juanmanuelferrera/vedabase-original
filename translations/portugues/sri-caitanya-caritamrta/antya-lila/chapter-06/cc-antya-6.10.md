### CC Antya 6.10

> পূর্বে যৈছে রাধার ললিতা সহায়-প্রধান ।
> তৈছে স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি রাখে মহাপ্রভুর প্রাণ ॥১০॥

pūrve yaiche rādhāra lalitā sahāya-pradhāna
taiche svarūpa-gosāñi rākhe mahāprabhura prāṇa

*pūrve*—anteriormente; *yaiche*—assim como; *rādhāra*—de Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; *lalitā*—Sua companheira de nome Lalitā; *sahāya-pradhāna*—a melhor auxiliar; *taiche*—de modo semelhante; *svarūpa-gosāñi*—Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; *rākhe*—mantém; *mahāprabhura prāṇa*—a vida de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Anteriormente, quando Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī sentia as pungências de separação de Kṛṣṇa, Sua companheira constante Lalitā mantinha-A viva, auxiliando-A de muitas maneiras. De modo semelhante, quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu sentia as emoções de Rādhārāṇī, Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī O ajudava a manter Sua vida.**
