### CC Antya 6.245

> রঘুনাথ-দাস যবে সবারে মিলিলা ।
> অদ্বৈত-আচার্য তাঁরে বহু কৃপা কৈলা ॥২৪৫॥

raghunātha-dāsa yabe sabāre mililā

advaita-ācārya tāṅre bahu kṛpā kailā

*raghunātha-dāsa*—Raghunātha dāsa; *yabe*—quando; *sabāre mililā*—encontrou-se com todos os devotos; *advaita-ācārya*—Advaita Ācārya; *tāṅre*—a ele; *bahu*—muita; *kṛpā*—misericórdia; *kailā*—fez.

**Quando Raghunātha dāsa encontrou-se com todos os devotos, Advaita Ācārya demonstrou-lhe grande misericórdia.**
