### CC Antya 6.221

> প্রভুরে গোবিন্দ কহে, — “রঘুনাথ ‘প্রসাদ’ না লয় ।\
> রাত্র্যে সিংহদ্বারে খাড়া হঞা মাগি’ খায় ।।” ॥ ২২১ ॥

prabhure govinda kahe,--"raghunātha 'prasāda' nā laya

rātrye siṁha-dvāre khāḍā hañā māgi' khāya"

*prabhure*—ao Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *govinda kahe*—Govinda disse; *raghunātha*—Raghunātha dāsa; *prasāda nā laya*—não aceita prasāda; *rātrye*—à noite; *siṁha-dvāre*—junto ao portão Siṁha-dvāra; *khāḍā hañā*—de pé; *māgi'*—mendigando; *khāya*—ele come.

**Govinda disse a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu: "Raghunātha dāsa já não aceita prasāda aqui. Agora ele permanece de pé junto ao Siṁha-dvāra, onde mendiga algumas esmolas para comer."**
