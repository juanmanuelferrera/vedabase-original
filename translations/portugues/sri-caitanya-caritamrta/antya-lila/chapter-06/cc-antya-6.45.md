### CC Antya 6.45

> তলে উপরে বহুভক্ত হঞাছে বেষ্টিত ।
> দেখি’ প্রভুর প্রভাব রঘুনাথ — বিস্মিত ॥৪৫॥

tale upare bahu-bhakta hañāche veṣṭita

dekhi' prabhura prabhāva raghunātha--vismita

*tale*—a superfície; *upare*—sobre; *bahu-bhakta*—muitos devotos; *hañāche veṣṭita*—Ele estava rodeado; *dekhi'*—vendo; *prabhura prabhāva*—a influência de Nityānanda Prabhu; *raghunātha*—Raghunātha dāsa; *vismita*—assombrado.

**Muitos devotos sentavam-se no chão ao Seu redor. Ao ver a influência de Nityānanda Prabhu, Raghunātha dāsa ficou assombrado.**
