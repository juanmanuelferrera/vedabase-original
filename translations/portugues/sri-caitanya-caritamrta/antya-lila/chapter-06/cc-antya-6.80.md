### CC Antya 6.80

> হাসি’ মহাপ্রভু আর এক গ্রাস লঞা ।
> তাঁর মুখে দিয়া খাওয়ায় হাসিয়া হাসিয়া ॥৮০॥

hāsi' mahāprabhu āra eka grāsa lañā

tāṅra mukhe diyā khāoyāya hāsiyā hāsiyā

*hāsi'*—sorrindo; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āra*—outro; *eka grāsa*—um bocado; *lañā*—pegando; *tāṅra mukhe*—na boca do Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *diyā*—colocando; *khāoyāya*—faz comer; *hāsiyā hāsiyā*—rindo.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, também sorrindo, pegava um bocado de alimento, colocava-o na boca de Nityānanda e ria enquanto fazia o Senhor Nityānanda comê-lo.**
