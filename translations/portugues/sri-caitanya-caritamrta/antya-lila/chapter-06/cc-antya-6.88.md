### CC Antya 6.88

> নিত্যানন্দ মহাপ্রভু — কৃপালু, উদার ।\
> রঘুনাথের ভাগ্যে এত কৈলা অঙ্গীকার ॥৮৮॥

nityānanda mahāprabhu--kṛpālu, udāra

raghunāthera bhāgye eta kailā aṅgīkāra

*nityānanda mahāprabhu*—o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu e o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kṛpālu*—misericordiosos; *udāra*—generosos; *raghunāthera bhāgye*—pela grande fortuna de Raghunātha dāsa; *eta*—tudo isto; *kailā aṅgīkāra*—Eles aceitaram.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu são extremamente misericordiosos e generosos. Foi pela boa fortuna de Raghunātha dāsa que Eles aceitaram todos esses tratos.**
