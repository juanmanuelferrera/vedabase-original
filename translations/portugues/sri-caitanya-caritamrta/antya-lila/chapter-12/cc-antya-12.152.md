### CC Antya 12.152

> জগদানন্দে-প্রভুতে প্রেম চলে এইমতে ।\
> সত্যভামা-কৃষ্ণে যৈছে শুনি ভাগবতে ॥১৫২॥

jagadānande-prabhute prema cale ei-mate

satyabhāmā-kṛṣṇe yaiche śuni bhāgavate

*jagadānande-prabhute*—entre Jagadānanda Paṇḍita e o Senhor; *prema*—afeição; *cale*—prossegue; *ei-mate*—dessa maneira; *satyabhāmā-kṛṣṇe*—entre Satyabhāmā e Kṛṣṇa; *yaiche*—como; *śuni*—aprendemos; *bhāgavate*—no Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.

**Os afetuosos intercâmbios de amor entre Jagadānanda Paṇḍita e o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu prosseguiram dessa maneira, exatamente como os intercâmbios entre Satyabhāmā e o Senhor Kṛṣṇa relatados no *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*.**
