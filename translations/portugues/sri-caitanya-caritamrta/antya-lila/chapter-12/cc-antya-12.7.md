### CC Antya 12.7

> এথা গৌড়দেশে প্রভুর যত ভক্তগণ ।\
> প্রভু দেখিবারে সবে করিলা গমন ॥৭॥

ethā gauḍa-deśe prabhura yata bhakta-gaṇa
prabhu dekhibāre sabe karilā gamana

*ethā*—por outro lado; *gauḍa-deśe*—em Bengala; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yata*—todos; *bhakta-gaṇa*—os devotos; *prabhu dekhibāre*—para ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sabe*—todos; *karilā gamana*—partiram.

**Enquanto isso, todos os devotos partiram de seus lares em Bengala para ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
