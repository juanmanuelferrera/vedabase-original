### CC Antya 12.120

> তৈল ভাঙ্গি’ সেই পথে নিজ-ঘর গিয়া ।\
> শুইয়া রহিলা ঘরে কপাট খিলিয়া ॥১২০॥

taila bhāṅgi' sei pathe nija-ghara giyā

śuiyā rahilā ghare kapāṭa khiliyā

*taila bhāṅgi'*—quebrando o pote de óleo; *sei*—ele; *pathe*—pelo caminho; *nija-ghara*—a seu quarto; *giyā*—indo; *śuiyā rahilā*—deitou-se; *ghare*—no quarto; *kapāṭa*—a porta; *khiliyā*—trancando.

**Após quebrar o jarro, Jagadānanda Paṇḍita regressou a seus aposentos, trancou a porta e deitou-se.**
