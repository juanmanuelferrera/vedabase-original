### CC Antya 12.144

> প্রভু কহেন, — “গোবিন্দ, তুমি ইহাঁই রহিবা ।\
> পণ্ডিত ভোজন কৈলে, আমারে কহিবা ।।” ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

prabhu kahena,--"govinda, tumi ihāṅi rahibā

paṇḍita bhojana kaile, āmāre kahibā"

*prabhu kahena*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse; *govinda*—Govinda; *tumi*—você; *ihāṅi rahibā*—ficará aqui; *paṇḍita*—Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; *bhojana kaile*—depois que tiver tomado sua refeição; *āmāre kahibā*—você deve avisar-Me.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu então disse a Govinda: "Permaneça aqui. Quando o Paṇḍita tiver tomado seu alimento, venha avisar-Me."**
