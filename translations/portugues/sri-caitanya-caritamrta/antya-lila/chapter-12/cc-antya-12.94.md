### CC Antya 12.94

> নিমাঞি খাঞাছে, — ঐছে হয় মোর মন ।\
> পাছে জ্ঞান হয়, — মুঞি দেখিনু ‘স্বপন’ ।।” ॥ ৯৪ ॥

nimāñi khāñāche,--aiche haya mora mana

pāche jñāna haya,--muñi dekhinu 'svapana' "

*nimāñi khāñāche*—Nimāi comeu; *aiche*—assim; *haya*—é; *mora*—minha; *mana*—mente; *pāche*—depois; *jñāna haya*—penso; *muñi*—eu; *dekhinu svapana*—vi um sonho.

**"Às vezes penso que Nimāi as comeu, mas depois penso que estava apenas sonhando."**
