### CC Antya 12.83

> নিজ-কৃপাগুণে প্রভু বান্ধিলা সবারে ।\
> মহাপ্রভুর কৃপা-ঋণ কে শোধিতে পারে ? ॥ ৮৩ ॥

nija-kṛpā-guṇe prabhu bāndhilā sabāre

mahāprabhura kṛpā-ṛṇa ke śodhite pāre?

*nija*—próprio; *kṛpā-guṇe*—pelo atributo da misericórdia; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bāndhilā*—prendeu; *sabāre*—a todos; *mahāprabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kṛpā-ṛṇa*—dívida pela misericórdia; *ke*—quem; *śodhite pāre*—pode saldar.

**O Senhor prendeu a todos por Sua misericórdia transcendental. Quem pode saldar sua dívida pela misericórdia de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu?**
