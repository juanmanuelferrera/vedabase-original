### CC Antya 12.80

> তবে প্রভু সবাকারে প্রবোধ করিয়া ।\
> সবারে বিদায় দিলা সুস্থির হঞা ॥৮০॥

tabe prabhu sabākāre prabodha kariyā

sabāre vidāya dilā susthira hañā

*tabe*—depois disso; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sabākāre*—a todos eles; *prabodha kariyā*—acalmando; *sabāre*—a cada um deles; *vidāya dilā*—despediu-se; *su-sthira hañā*—estando numa condição serena.

**Então, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu acalmou serenamente a todos e despediu-se de cada um deles.**
