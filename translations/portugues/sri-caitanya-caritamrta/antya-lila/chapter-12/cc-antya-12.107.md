### CC Antya 12.107

> এক-কলস সুগন্ধি তৈল গৌড়েতে করিয়া ।
> ইঁহা আনিয়াছে বহু যতন করিয়া ।।” ॥ ১০৭ ॥

eka-kalasa sugandhi taila gauḍete kariyā

ihāṅ āniyāche bahu yatana kariyā"

*eka-kalasa*—um grande jarro cheio; *su-gandhi taila*—óleo perfumado; *gauḍete kariyā*—fabricando em Bengala; *ihāṅ*—aqui; *āniyāche*—trouxe; *bahu yatana kariyā*—com grande cuidado.

**"Ele preparou um grande jarro dele em Bengala e, com grande cuidado, trouxe-o até aqui."**
