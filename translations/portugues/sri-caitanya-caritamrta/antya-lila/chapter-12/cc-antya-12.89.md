### CC Antya 12.89

> জগদানন্দে পাঞা মাতা আনন্দিত মনে ।
> তেঁহো প্রভুর কথা কহে, শুনে রাত্রি-দিনে ॥৮৯॥

jagadānande pāñā mātā ānandita mane

teṅho prabhura kathā kahe, śune rātri-dine

*jagadānande*—Jagadānanda; *pāñā*—obtendo; *mātā*—Śacīmātā; *ānandita mane*—em grande satisfação; *teṅho*—ele; *prabhura kathā*—os passatempos de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kahe*—narra; *śune*—escuta; *rātri-dine*—dia e noite.

**A vinda de Jagadānanda agradou imensamente à mãe Śacī. Enquanto ele falava do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu, ela escutava dia e noite.**
