### CC Antya 12.112

> শুনি’ প্রভু কহে কিছু সক্রোধ বচন ।
> মর্দনিয়া এক রাখ করিতে মর্দন ! ॥ ১১২ ॥

śuni' prabhu kahe kichu sakrodha vacana

mardaniyā eka rākha karite mardana!

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kahe*—diz; *kichu*—algumas; *sa-krodha vacana*—palavras de ira; *mardaniyā*—massagista; *eka*—um; *rākha*—mantém; *karite mardana*—para dar massagens.

**Quando o Senhor ouviu isso, disse com ira: "Por que não manter um massagista para Me massagear?**
