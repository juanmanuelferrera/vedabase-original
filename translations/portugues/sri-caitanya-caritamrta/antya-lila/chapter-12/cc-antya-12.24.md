### CC Antya 12.24

> এত বলি’ প্রভু-পাশে গেলা শিবানন্দ ।
> উঠি’ তাঁরে লাথি মাইলা প্রভু নিত্যানন্দ ॥২৪॥

eta bali' prabhu-pāśe gelā śivānanda

uṭhi' tāṅre lāthi māilā prabhu nityānanda

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *prabhu-pāśe*—a Nityānanda Prabhu; *gelā*—foi; *śivānanda*—Śivānanda Sena; *uṭhi'*—levantando-se; *tāṅre*—nele; *lāthi māilā*—deu um pontapé; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *nityānanda*—Nityānanda.

**Após dizer isso, Śivānanda Sena dirigiu-se a Nityānanda Prabhu, que então se levantou e lhe deu um pontapé.**
