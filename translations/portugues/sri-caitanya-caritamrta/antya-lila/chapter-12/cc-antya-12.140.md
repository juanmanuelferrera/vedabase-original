### CC Antya 12.140

> তবে মহাপ্রভু উঠি’ কৈলা আচমন ।
> পণ্ডিত আনিল, মুখবাস, মাল্য, চন্দন ॥১৪০॥

tabe mahāprabhu uṭhi' kailā ācamana

paṇḍita ānila, mukhavāsa, mālya, candana

*tabe*—nesse momento; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *uṭhi'*—levantando-Se; *kailā ācamana*—lavou as mãos e a boca; *paṇḍita*—Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; *ānila*—trouxe; *mukha-vāsa*—especiarias; *mālya*—uma guirlanda de flores; *candana*—polpa de sândalo.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu levantou-Se e lavou as mãos e a boca, enquanto Jagadānanda Paṇḍita trazia especiarias, uma guirlanda e polpa de sândalo.**
