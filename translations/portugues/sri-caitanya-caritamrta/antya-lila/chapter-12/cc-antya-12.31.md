### CC Antya 12.31

> শুনি’ নিত্যানন্দপ্রভুর আনন্দিত মন ।
> উঠি’ শিবানন্দে কৈলা প্রেম-আলিঙ্গন ॥৩১॥

śuni' nityānanda-prabhura ānandita mana

uṭhi' śivānande kailā prema-āliṅgana

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *nityānanda-prabhura*—do Senhor Nityānanda; *ānandita*—muito satisfeita; *mana*—a mente; *uṭhi'*—levantando-se; *śivānande*—em Śivānanda Sena; *kailā*—realizou; *prema*—em amor; *āliṅgana*—um abraço.

**Quando o Senhor Nityānanda ouviu isso, ficou muito feliz. Ele se levantou e abraçou Śivānanda Sena com grande amor.**
