### CC Antya 12.105

> তবে প্রভু-ঠাঞি গোবিন্দ কৈল নিবেদন  ।\
> “জগদানন্দ চন্দনাদি-তৈল আনিয়াছেন ॥১০৫॥

tabe prabhu-ṭhāñi govinda kaila nivedana

'jagadānanda candanādi-taila āniyāchena

*tabe*—em seguida; *prabhu-ṭhāñi*—perante o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *govinda*—Govinda; *kaila nivedana*—submeteu; *jagadānanda*—Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; *candana-ādi-taila*—óleo de sândalo perfumado; *āniyāchena*—trouxe.

**Govinda, por conseguinte, disse a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu: "Jagadānanda Paṇḍita trouxe um pouco de óleo de sândalo perfumado.**
