### CC Antya 12.138

> কিছু বলিতে নারেন প্রভু, খায়েন তরাসে ।
> না খাইলে জগদানন্দ করিবে উপবাসে ॥১৩৮॥

kichu balite nārena prabhu, khāyena tarāse

nā khāile jagadānanda karibe upavāse

*kichu*—nada; *balite nārena*—não podia dizer; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *khāyena*—come; *tarāse*—por temor; *nā khāile*—se Ele não comesse; *jagadānanda*—Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; *karibe upavāse*—jejuaria.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu não ousava proibi-lo de alimentá-Lo mais. Apenas continuava comendo, temeroso de que Jagadānanda jejuasse caso Ele parasse.**
