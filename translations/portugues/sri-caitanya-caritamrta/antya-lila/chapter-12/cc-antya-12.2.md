### CC Antya 12.2

> জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় দয়াময় ।
> জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ কৃপাসিন্ধু জয় ॥২॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya dayāmaya
jaya jaya nityānanda kṛpā-sindhu jaya

*jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *śrī-caitanya*—ao Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jaya*—todas as glórias; *dayā-maya*—todo-misericordioso; *jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *nityānanda*—a Nityānanda Prabhu; *kṛpā-sindhu*—o oceano de misericórdia; *jaya*—todas as glórias.

**Todas as glórias a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, que é todo-misericordioso! Todas as glórias a Nityānanda Prabhu, que é um oceano de misericórdia!**
