### CC Antya 12.53

> শিবানন্দের ‘প্রকৃতি’, পুত্র — যাবৎ এথায়  ।\
> আমার অবশেষ-পাত্র তারা যেন পায় ।।” ৫৩ ॥৫৩॥

"śivānandera 'prakṛti', putra--yāvat ethāya

āmāra avaśeṣa-pātra tārā yena pāya"

*śivānandera*—de Śivānanda Sena; *prakṛti*—a esposa; *putra*—os filhos; *yāvat*—enquanto; *ethāya*—aqui; *āmāra*—de Minha; *avaśeṣa-pātra*—prato dos restos de alimento; *tārā*—todos eles; *yena*—devem; *pāya*—receber.

**"Enquanto a esposa e os filhos de Śivānanda Sena permanecerem em Jagannātha Purī", disse Ele, "devem receber os restos do Meu alimento."**
