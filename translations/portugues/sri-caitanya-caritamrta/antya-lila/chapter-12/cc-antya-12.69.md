### CC Antya 12.69

> নিত্যানন্দে আজ্ঞা দিলুঁ গৌড়েতে রহিতে ।\
> আজ্ঞা লঙ্ঘি’ আইলা, কি পারি বলিতে ? ॥ ৬৯ ॥

nityānande ājñā diluṅ gauḍete rahite

ājñā laṅghi' āilā, ki pāri balite?

*nityānande*—a Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu; *ājñā diluṅ*—Eu ordenei; *gauḍete rahite*—permanecer em Bengala; *ājñā laṅghi'*—transgredindo Minha ordem; *āilā*—Ele veio; *ki*—o que; *pāri balite*—posso dizer.

**"Ordenei a Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu que não deixasse Bengala, mas Ele transgrediu Minha ordem e veio ver-Me. O que posso dizer?**
