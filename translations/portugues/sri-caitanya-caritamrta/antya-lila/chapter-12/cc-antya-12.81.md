### CC Antya 12.81

> নিত্যানন্দে কহিলা — “তুমি না আসিহ বারবার ।\
> তথাই আমার সঙ্গ হইবে তোমার ।।” ॥ ৮১ ॥

nityānande kahilā--"tumi nā āsiha bāra-bāra

tathāi āmāra saṅga ha-ibe tomāra"

*nityānande*—a Nityānanda Prabhu; *kahilā*—disse; *tumi*—Tu; *nā āsiha*—não venhas; *bāra-bāra*—repetidamente; *tathāi*—lá (em Bengala); *āmāra*—Minha; *saṅga*—associação; *ha-ibe*—haverá; *tomāra*—Tua.

**O Senhor aconselhou especificamente Nityānanda Prabhu: "Não deves vir aqui repetidamente. Terás Minha associação em Bengala."**
