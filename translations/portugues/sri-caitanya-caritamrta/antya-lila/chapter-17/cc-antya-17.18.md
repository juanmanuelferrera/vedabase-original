### CC Antya 17.18

> গাভী সব চৌদিকে শুঁকে প্রভুর শ্রীঅঙ্গ ।
> দূর কৈলে নাহি ছাড়ে প্রভুর শ্রীঅঙ্গ-সঙ্গ ॥১৮॥

gābhī saba caudike śuṅke prabhura śrī-aṅga

dūra kaile nāhi chāḍe prabhura śrī-aṅga-saṅga

*gābhī*—vacas; *saba*—todas; *cau-dike*—ao redor; *śuṅke*—farejam; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śrī-aṅga*—o corpo transcendental; *dūra kaile*—se são afastadas; *nāhi chāḍe*—não abandonam; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śrī-aṅga-saṅga*—a associação com o corpo transcendental.

**Todas as vacas ao redor do Senhor farejavam Seu corpo transcendental. Quando os devotos tentavam contê-las, elas se recusavam a abandonar a associação com o corpo transcendental de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
