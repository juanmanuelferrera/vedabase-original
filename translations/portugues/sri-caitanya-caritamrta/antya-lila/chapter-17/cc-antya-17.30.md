### CC Antya 17.30

> স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি প্রভুর ভাব জানিয়া ।
> ভাগবতের শ্লোক পড়ে মধুর করিয়া ॥৩০॥

svarūpa-gosāñi prabhura bhāva jāniyā

bhāgavatera śloka paḍe madhura kariyā

*svarūpa-gosāñi*—Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosāñi; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhāva*—a emoção; *jāniyā*—compreendendo; *bhāgavatera*—do Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; *śloka*—um verso; *paḍe*—recita; *madhura kariyā*—com voz doce.

**Compreendendo as emoções extáticas de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Svarūpa Dāmodara, com voz doce, recitou o seguinte verso do *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*.**
