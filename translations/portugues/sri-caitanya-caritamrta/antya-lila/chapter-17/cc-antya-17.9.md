### CC Antya 17.9

> গম্ভীরার দ্বারে গোবিন্দ করিলা শয়ন ।
> সবরাত্রি প্রভু করেন উচ্চসঙ্কীর্তন ॥৯॥

gambhīrāra dvāre govinda karilā śayana
saba-rātri prabhu karena ucca-saṅkīrtana

*gambhīrāra*—do quarto de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dvāre*—à porta; *govinda*—Seu servo pessoal; *karilā śayana*—deitou-se; *saba-rātri*—a noite toda; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *karena*—realiza; *ucca-saṅkīrtana*—canto em voz alta.

**O servo pessoal de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Govinda, deitou-se à porta de Seu quarto, e o Senhor cantou o mahā-mantra Hare Kṛṣṇa em voz muito alta durante toda a noite.**
