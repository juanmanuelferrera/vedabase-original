### CC Antya 17.14

> তবে স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি সঙ্গে লঞা ভক্তগণ ।
> দেউটি জ্বালিয়া করেন প্রভুর অন্বেষণ ॥১৪॥

tabe svarūpa-gosāñi saṅge lañā bhakta-gaṇa
deuṭi jvāliyā karena prabhura anveṣaṇa

*tabe*—em seguida; *svarūpa-gosāñi*—Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; *saṅge*—consigo; *lañā*—levando; *bhakta-gaṇa*—os devotos; *deuṭi*—tocha; *jvāliyā*—acendendo; *karena*—faz; *prabhura*—por Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *anveṣaṇa*—busca.

**Então Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī acendeu uma tocha e saiu com todos os devotos em busca de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
