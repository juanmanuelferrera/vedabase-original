### CC Antya 17.32

> শুনি’ প্রভু গোপীভাবে আবিষ্ট হইলা ।
> ভাগবতের শ্লোকের অর্থ করিতে লাগিলা ॥৩২॥

śuni' prabhu gopī-bhāve āviṣṭa ha-ilā

bhāgavatera ślokera artha karite lāgilā

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *gopī-bhāve*—na emoção das gopīs; *āviṣṭa ha-ilā*—ficou dominado; *bhāgavatera*—do Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; *ślokera*—do verso; *artha*—o significado; *karite lāgilā*—começou a explicar.

**Ao ouvir este verso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, dominado pelo êxtase das gopīs, começou a explicá-lo.**
