### CC Antya 17.67

> অদ্ভুত নিগূঢ় প্রেমের মাধুর্য-মহিমা ।
> আপনি আস্বাদি’ প্রভু দেখাইলা সীমা ॥৬৭॥

adbhuta nigūḍha premera mādhurya-mahimā

āpani āsvādi' prabhu dekhāilā sīmā

*adbhuta*—maravilhoso; *nigūḍha*—profundo; *premera*—do amor extático por Kṛṣṇa; *mādhurya-mahimā*—as glórias da doçura; *āpani*—pessoalmente; *āsvādi'*—saboreando; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhāilā*—mostrou; *sīmā*—o limite extremo.

**O amor extático por Kṛṣṇa é maravilhosamente profundo. Saboreando pessoalmente a gloriosa doçura desse amor, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu mostrou-nos o seu limite extremo.**
