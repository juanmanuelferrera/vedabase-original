### CC Antya 17.7

> মধ্যে মধ্যে আপনে প্রভু শ্লোক পড়িয়া ।\
> শ্লোকের অর্থ করেন প্রভু বিলাপ করিয়া ॥৭॥

madhye madhye āpane prabhu śloka paḍiyā
ślokera artha karena prabhu vilāpa kariyā

*madhye madhye*—a intervalos; *āpane*—pessoalmente; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śloka*—um verso; *paḍiyā*—recitando; *ślokera*—do verso; *artha*—significado; *karena*—dá; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *vilāpa kariyā*—lamentando-se.

**A intervalos, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu também recitava um verso. Então, em grande lamentação, Ele o explicava.**
