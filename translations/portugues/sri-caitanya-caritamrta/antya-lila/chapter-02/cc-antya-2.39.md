### CC Antya 2.39

> মহাপ্রভু তারে দেখি’ বড় কৃপা কৈলা ।\
> মাস-দুই তেঁহো প্রভুর নিকটে রহিলা ॥৩৯॥

mahāprabhu tāre dekhi' baḍa kṛpā kailā

māsa-dui teṅho prabhura nikaṭe rahilā

*mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāre*—a ele; *dekhi'*—vendo; *baḍa kṛpā kailā*—concedeu grande misericórdia; *māsa-dui*—por dois meses; *teṅho*—Śrīkānta Sena; *prabhura nikaṭe*—perto de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rahilā*—permaneceu.

**Ao ver Śrīkānta Sena, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu concedeu-lhe misericórdia imotivada. Śrīkānta Sena permaneceu perto de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu por cerca de dois meses em Jagannātha Purī.**
