### CC Antya 2.107

> তাঁর ঠাঞি তণ্ডুল মাগি’ আনিল হরিদাস ।\
> তণ্ডুল দেখি’ আচার্যের অধিক উল্লাস ॥১০৭॥

tāṅra ṭhāñi taṇḍula māgi' ānila haridāsa

taṇḍula dekhi' ācāryera adhika ullāsa

*tāṅra ṭhāñi*—dela; *taṇḍula māgi'*—mendigando arroz; *ānila haridāsa*—Haridāsa trouxe; *taṇḍula dekhi'*—vendo o arroz; *ācāryera*—de Bhagavān Ācārya; *adhika ullāsa*—grandíssima satisfação.

**Após mendigar o arroz a ela, Haridāsa Júnior trouxe-o a Bhagavān Ācārya, que ficou muito satisfeito ao ver sua qualidade.**
