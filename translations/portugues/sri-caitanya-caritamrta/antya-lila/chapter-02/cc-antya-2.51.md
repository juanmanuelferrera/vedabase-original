### CC Antya 2.51

> শুনি’ ব্রহ্মচারী কহে, — ‘করহ সন্তোষে ।\
> আমি ত’ আনিব তাঁরে তৃতীয় দিবসে ।।” ॥ ৫১ ॥

śuni' brahmacārī kahe,--'karaha santoṣe

āmi ta' āniba tāṅre tṛtīya divase'

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *brahmacārī*—Nṛsiṁhānanda Brahmacārī; *kahe*—disse; *karaha santoṣe*—ficai satisfeitos; *āmi*—eu; *ta'*—certamente; *āniba*—trarei; *tāṅre*—a Ele (Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu); *tṛtīya divase*—no terceiro dia.

**Ao ouvir isso, Nṛsiṁhānanda Brahmacārī respondeu: "Por favor, ficai satisfeitos. Asseguro-vos que O trarei aqui daqui a três dias."**
