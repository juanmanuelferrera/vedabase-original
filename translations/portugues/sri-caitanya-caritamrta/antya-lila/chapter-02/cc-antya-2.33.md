### CC Antya 2.33

> এইমত মহাপ্রভুর অচিন্ত্য প্রভাব ।\
> এবে শুন প্রভুর যৈছে হয় ‘আবির্ভাব ।।’ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhura acintya prabhāva

ebe śuna prabhura yaiche haya 'āvirbhāva'

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *mahāprabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *acintya prabhāva*—influência inconcebível; *ebe*—agora; *śuna*—ouve; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yaiche*—de que modo; *haya*—há; *āvirbhāva*—aparecimento.

**Dessa maneira, deve-se compreender as inconcebíveis potências de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Agora, por favor, ouve como ocorre Seu aparecimento [āvirbhāva].**
