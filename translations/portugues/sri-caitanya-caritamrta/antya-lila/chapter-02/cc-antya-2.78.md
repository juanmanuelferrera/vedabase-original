### CC Antya 2.78

> শুনি’ ভক্তগণ মনে আশ্চর্য মানিল ।\
> শিবানন্দের মনে তবে প্রত্যয় জন্মিল ॥৭৮॥

śuni' bhakta-gaṇa mane āścarya mānila

śivānandera mane tabe pratyaya janmila

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *bhakta-gaṇa*—todos os devotos; *mane*—na mente; *āścarya mānila*—sentiram assombro; *śivānandera*—de Śivānanda Sena; *mane*—na mente; *tabe*—diante disso; *pratyaya janmila*—surgiu a convicção.

**Ao ouvirem isso, todos os devotos ficaram assombrados, e Śivānanda convenceu-se de que o incidente era verdadeiro.**
