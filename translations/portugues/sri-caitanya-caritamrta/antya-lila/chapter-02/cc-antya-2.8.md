### CC Antya 2.8

> গৌড়-দেশের ভক্তগণ প্রত্যব্দ আসিয়া ।
> পুনঃ গৌড়দেশে যায় প্রভুরে মিলিয়া ॥৮॥

gauḍa-deśera bhakta-gaṇa pratyabda āsiyā
punaḥ gauḍa-deśe yāya prabhure miliyā

*gauḍa-deśera*—de Bengala; *bhakta-gaṇa*—devotos; *prati-abda*—todos os anos; *āsiyā*—vindo; *punaḥ*—novamente; *gauḍa-deśe*—a Bengala; *yāya*—retornam; *prabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *miliyā*—após encontrar-se com.

**Todos os anos, os devotos de Bengala iam a Jagannātha Purī para encontrar-se com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e, após o encontro, retornavam a Bengala.**
