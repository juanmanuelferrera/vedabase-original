### CC Antya 2.60

> জগন্নাথের ভিন্ন ভোগ পৃথক্ বাড়িল  ।\
> চৈতন্য প্রভুর লাগি’ আর ভোগ কৈল ॥৬০॥

jagannāthera bhinna bhoga pṛthak bāḍila

caitanya prabhura lāgi' āra bhoga kaila

*jagannāthera*—do Senhor Jagannātha; *bhinna*—separadas; *bhoga*—oferendas; *pṛthak*—separadamente; *bāḍila*—dispôs; *caitanya prabhura lāgi'*—para Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āra*—outras; *bhoga*—oferendas de alimento; *kaila*—fez.

**Depois de terminar de cozinhar, ele trouxe pratos separados para Jagannātha e Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
