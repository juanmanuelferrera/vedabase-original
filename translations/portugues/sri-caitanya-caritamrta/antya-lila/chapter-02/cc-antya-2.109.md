### CC Antya 2.109

> মধ্যাহ্নে আসিয়া প্রভু ভোজনে বসিলা  ।
> শাল্যন্ন দেখি’ প্রভু আচার্যে পুছিলা ॥১০৯॥

madhyāhne āsiyā prabhu bhojane vasilā

śālyanna dekhi' prabhu ācārye puchilā

*madhyāhne*—ao meio-dia; *āsiyā*—vindo; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhojane vasilā*—sentou-se para comer; *śāli-anna*—o arroz de fina qualidade; *dekhi'*—vendo; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ācārye puchilā*—perguntou a Bhagavān Ācārya.

**Ao meio-dia, quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu veio comer as oferendas de Bhagavān Ācārya, Ele primeiramente apreciou o arroz fino e, portanto, questionou-o.**
