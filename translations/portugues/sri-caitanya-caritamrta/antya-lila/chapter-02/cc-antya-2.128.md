### CC Antya 2.128

> আর দিন সবে পরমানন্দপুরী-স্থানে ।\
> ‘প্রভুকে প্রসন্ন কর’ — কৈলা নিবেদনে ॥১২৮॥

āra dina sabe paramānanda-purī-sthāne

'prabhuke prasanna kara'--kailā nivedane

*āra dina*—no dia seguinte; *sabe*—todos os devotos; *paramānanda-purī-sthāne*—ao lugar de Paramānanda Purī; *prabhuke*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *prasanna kara*—por favor, tentai apaziguar; *kailā nivedane*—submeteram.

**No dia seguinte, todos os devotos foram a Śrī Paramānanda Purī e pediram-lhe que apaziguasse o Senhor.**
