### CC Antya 2.149

> গন্ধর্ব-দেহে গান করেন অন্তর্ধানে ।\
> রাত্র্যে প্রভুরে শুনায় গীত, অন্যে নাহি জানে ॥১৪৯॥

gandharva-dehe gāna karena antardhāne

rātrye prabhure śunāya gīta, anye nāhi jāne

*gandharva-dehe*—no corpo de um Gandharva; *gāna karena*—ele canta; *antardhāne*—sem ser visto; *rātrye*—à noite; *prabhure*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śunāya gīta*—ele cantava; *anye*—outros; *nāhi jāne*—não podiam compreender.

**Num corpo espiritual semelhante ao de um Gandharva, Haridāsa Júnior, embora invisível, cantava à noite para que Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ouvisse. Ninguém, porém, exceto o Senhor, sabia disso.**
