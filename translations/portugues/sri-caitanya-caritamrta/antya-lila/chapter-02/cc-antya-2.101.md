### CC Antya 2.101

> একদিন আচার্য প্রভুরে কৈলা নিমন্ত্রণ ।
> ঘরে ভাত করি’ করে বিবিধ ব্যঞ্জন ॥১০১॥

eka-dina ācārya prabhure kailā nimantraṇa

ghare bhāta kari' kare vividha vyañjana

*eka-dina*—certo dia; *ācārya*—Bhagavān Ācārya; *prabhure*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kailā nimantraṇa*—fez um convite para jantar; *ghare*—em casa; *bhāta kari'*—cozinhando arroz; *kare*—prepara; *vividha vyañjana*—variedades de preparações de hortaliças.

**Certo dia, Bhagavān Ācārya convidou Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu para jantar em sua casa. Assim, ele estava preparando arroz e vários tipos de hortaliças.**
