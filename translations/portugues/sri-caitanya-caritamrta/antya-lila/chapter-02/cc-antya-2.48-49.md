### CC Antya 2.48-49

> আচম্বিতে নৃসিংহানন্দ তাহাঁই আইলা ।\
> দুঁহে তাঁরে মিলি’ তবে স্থানে বসাইলা ॥৪৮॥\
> দুঁহে দুঃখী দেখি’ তবে কহে নৃসিংহানন্দ ।\
> ‘তোমা দুহাঁকারে কেনে দেখি নিরানন্দ ?’ ৪৯ ॥৪৯॥

ācambite nṛsiṁhānanda tāhāṅi āilā

duṅhe tāṅre mili' tabe sthāne vasāilā

duṅhe duḥkhī dekhi' tabe kahe nṛsiṁhānanda

'tomā duṅhākāre kene dekhi nirānanda?'

*ācambite*—de repente; *nṛsiṁhānanda*—Nṛsiṁhānanda; *tāhāṅi āilā*—chegou ali; *duṅhe*—Śivānanda e Jagadānanda; *tāṅre*—a ele; *mili'*—encontrando; *tabe*—então; *sthāne vasāilā*—fizeram-no sentar; *duṅhe*—ambos; *duḥkhī*—infelizes; *dekhi'*—vendo; *tabe*—então; *kahe nṛsiṁhānanda*—Nṛsiṁhānanda começou a falar; *tomā duṅhākāre*—vós dois; *kene*—por que; *dekhi*—vejo; *nirānanda*—infelizes.

**De repente Nṛsiṁhānanda chegou, e Jagadānanda e Śivānanda providenciaram para que ele se sentasse junto a eles. Vendo ambos tão infelizes, Nṛsiṁhānanda perguntou: "Por que vejo vós dois tão abatidos?"**
