### CC Antya 2.150

> একদিন মহাপ্রভু পুছিলা ভক্তগণে ।
> ‘হরিদাস কাঁহা? তারে আনহ এখানে’ ॥১৫০॥

eka-dina mahāprabhu puchilā bhakta-gaṇe

'haridāsa kāṅhā? tāre ānaha ekhāne'

*eka-dina*—certo dia; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *puchilā bhakta-gaṇe*—perguntou aos devotos; *haridāsa kāṅhā*—onde está Haridāsa; *tāre*—a ele; *ānaha ekhāne*—trazei aqui.

**Certo dia, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu perguntou aos devotos: "Onde está Haridāsa? Agora podeis trazê-lo aqui."**
