### CC Antya 2.113

> ‘আজি হৈতে এই মোর আজ্ঞা পালিবা  ।\
> ছোট হরিদাসে ইহাঁ আসিতে না দিবা ।।’ ১১৩ ॥১১৩॥

'āji haite ei mora ājñā pālibā

choṭa haridāse ihāṅ āsite nā dibā'

*āji haite*—de hoje em diante; *ei*—esta; *mora*—Minha; *ājñā*—ordem; *pālibā*—deves cumprir; *choṭa haridāse*—Choṭa Haridāsa; *ihāṅ*—aqui; *āsite*—vir; *nā dibā*—não permitas.

**"De hoje em diante, não permitas que Choṭa Haridāsa venha aqui."**
