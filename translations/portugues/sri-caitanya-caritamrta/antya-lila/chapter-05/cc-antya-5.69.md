### CC Antya 5.69

> প্রভুর চরণ বন্দে উল্লসিত-মনে ।
> প্রভু কহে, — ‘কৃষ্ণকথা হইল শ্রবণে’ ? ॥ ৬৯ ॥

prabhura caraṇa vande ullasita-mane

prabhu kahe,--'kṛṣṇa-kathā ha-ila śravaṇe'?

*prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *caraṇa*—os pés de lótus; *vande*—adora; *ullasita-mane*—com grande felicidade; *prabhu kahe*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu diz; *kṛṣṇa-kathā*—conversas sobre Kṛṣṇa; *ha-ila śravaṇe*—ouviste.

**Com grande felicidade, ele adorou os pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. O Senhor indagou: "Ouviste tópicos sobre Kṛṣṇa?"**
