### CC Antya 5.68

> ঘরে গিয়া মিশ্র কৈল স্নান, ভোজন ।
> সন্ধ্যাকালে দেখিতে আইল প্রভুর চরণ ॥৬৮॥

ghare giyā miśra kaila snāna, bhojana

sandhyā-kāle dekhite āila prabhura caraṇa

*ghare giyā*—regressando ao lar; *miśra*—Pradyumna Miśra; *kaila*—realizou; *snāna*—banho; *bhojana*—refeição; *sandhyā-kāle*—ao anoitecer; *dekhite*—ver; *āila*—veio; *prabhura caraṇa*—os pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Após regressar ao lar, Pradyumna Miśra banhou-se e fez sua refeição. Ao anoitecer, veio ver os pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
