### CC Antya 5.34

> তবে মিশ্র রামানন্দের বৃত্তান্ত কহিলা  ।
> শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু তবে কহিতে লাগিলা ॥৩৪॥

tabe miśra rāmānandera vṛttānta kahilā

śuni' mahāprabhu tabe kahite lāgilā

*tabe*—então; *miśra*—Pradyumna Miśra; *rāmānandera*—de Śrī Rāmānanda Rāya; *vṛttānta kahilā*—descreveu as atividades; *śuni'*—ouvindo; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tabe*—então; *kahite lāgilā*—começou a falar.

**Pradyumna Miśra descreveu então as atividades de Śrī Rāmānanda Rāya. Após ouvir sobre essas atividades, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu começou a falar.**
