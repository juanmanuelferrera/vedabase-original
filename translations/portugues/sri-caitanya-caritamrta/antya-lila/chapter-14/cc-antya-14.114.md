### CC Antya 14.114

> নিপট্ট-বাহ্য হইলে প্রভু দুঁহারে বন্দিলা ।
> মহাপ্রভুরে দুইজন প্রেমালিঙ্গন কৈলা ॥১১৪॥

nipaṭṭa-bāhya ha-ile prabhu duṅhāre vandilā

mahāprabhure dui-jana premāliṅgana kailā

*nipaṭṭa-bāhya*—consciência externa completa; *ha-ile*—quando houve; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *duṅhāre*—a ambos; *vandilā*—ofereceu orações; *mahāprabhure*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dui-jana*—ambas as pessoas; *prema-āliṅgana kailā*—abraçaram com amor e afeto.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu retornou à plena consciência externa e imediatamente lhes ofereceu orações. Então esses dois senhores idosos abraçaram o Senhor com amorosa afeição.**
