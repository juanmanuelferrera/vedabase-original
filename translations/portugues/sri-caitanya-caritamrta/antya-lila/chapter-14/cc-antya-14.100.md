### CC Antya 14.100

> উচ্চ সঙ্কীর্তন করে প্রভুর শ্রবণে ।\
> শীতল জলে করে প্রভুর অঙ্গ সম্মার্জনে ॥১০০॥

ucca saṅkīrtana kare prabhura śravaṇe

śītala jale kare prabhura aṅga sammārjane

*ucca*—em voz alta; *saṅkīrtana*—cantar do mantra Hare Kṛṣṇa; *kare*—fazem; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śravaṇe*—ao alcance dos ouvidos; *śītala*—fria; *jale*—com água; *kare*—fazem; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *aṅga*—o corpo; *sammārjane*—lavar.

**Os devotos cantaram o mantra Hare Kṛṣṇa em voz alta perto de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e lavaram Seu corpo com água fria.**
