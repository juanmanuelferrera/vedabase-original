### CC Antya 14.59

> সব রাত্রি মহাপ্রভু করে জাগরণ ।
> উচ্চ করি’ কহে কৃষ্ণনামসঙ্কীর্তন ॥৫৯॥

saba rātri mahāprabhu kare jāgaraṇa

ucca kari' kahe kṛṣṇa-nāma-saṅkīrtana

*saba rātri*—a noite inteira; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kare*—faz; *jāgaraṇa*—permanecer sem dormir; *ucca kari'*—muito alto; *kahe kṛṣṇa-nāma-saṅkīrtana*—canta o santo nome de Kṛṣṇa.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu permaneceu acordado durante toda a noite, cantando o mantra Hare Kṛṣṇa em voz muito alta.**
