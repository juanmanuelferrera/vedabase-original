### CC Antya 14.2

> জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ।\
> জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র ভক্তগণ-প্রাণ ॥২॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya svayaṁ bhagavān
jaya jaya gauracandra bhakta-gaṇa-prāṇa

*jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *śrī-caitanya*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *svayam bhagavān*—a própria Personalidade de Deus; *jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *gaura-candra*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhakta-gaṇa-prāṇa*—a vida e a alma dos devotos.

**Todas as glórias a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, a Suprema Personalidade de Deus! Todas as glórias ao Senhor Gauracandra, a vida e a alma de Seus devotos!**
