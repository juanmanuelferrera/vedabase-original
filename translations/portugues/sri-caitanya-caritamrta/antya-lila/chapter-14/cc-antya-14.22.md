### CC Antya 14.22

> দেহাভ্যাসে নিত্যকৃত্য করি’ সমাপন ।
> কালে যাই’ কৈলা জগন্নাথ দরশন ॥২২॥

dehābhyāse nitya-kṛtya kari' samāpana

kāle yāi' kailā jagannātha daraśana

*deha-abhyāse*—por hábito; *nitya-kṛtya*—os deveres diários; *kari' samāpana*—concluindo; *kāle*—na hora apropriada; *yāi'*—indo; *kailā*—realizou; *jagannātha daraśana*—ver o Senhor Jagannātha.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu realizou Seus habituais deveres diários e, na hora de costume, foi ver o Senhor Jagannātha no templo.**
