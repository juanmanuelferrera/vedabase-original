### CC Antya 14.121

> এবে প্রভু যত কৈলা অলৌকিক-লীলা ।\
> কে বর্ণিতে পারে সেই মহাপ্রভুর খেলা ? ॥ ১২১ ॥

ebe prabhu yata kailā alaukika-līlā

ke varṇite pāre sei mahāprabhura khelā?

*ebe*—agora; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yata*—tudo o que; *kailā*—realizou; *alaukika-līlā*—passatempos incomuns; *ke*—quem; *varṇite pāre*—pode descrever; *sei*—eles; *mahāprabhura khelā*—a brincadeira de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Quem pode descrever apropriadamente todos os passatempos incomuns de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu? Todos eles são simplesmente Sua brincadeira.**
