### CC Antya 14.106

> ইহাঁ হৈতে আজি মুই গেনু গোবর্ধনে ।
> দেখোঁ, — যদি কৃষ্ণ করেন গোধন-চারণে ॥১০৬॥

ihāṅ haite āji mui genu govardhane

dekhoṅ,--yadi kṛṣṇa karena godhana-cāraṇe

*ihāṅ haite*—daqui; *āji*—hoje; *mui*—Eu; *genu*—fui; *govardhane*—à Colina de Govardhana; *dekhoṅ*—Eu estava procurando; *yadi*—se; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *karena*—faz; *godhana-cāraṇe*—pastoreio das vacas.

**"Hoje fui daqui à Colina de Govardhana para descobrir se Kṛṣṇa estava ali pastoreando Suas vacas.**
