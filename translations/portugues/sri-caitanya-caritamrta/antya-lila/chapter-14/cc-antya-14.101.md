### CC Antya 14.101

> এইমত বহুবার কীর্তন করিতে ।\
> ‘হরিবোল’ বলি’ প্রভু উঠে আচম্বিতে ॥১০১॥

ei-mata bahu-bāra kīrtana karite

'hari-bola' bali' prabhu uṭhe ācambite

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *bahu-bāra*—por muito tempo; *kīrtana karite*—cantando; *hari-bola bali'*—proferindo Hari bol; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *uṭhe*—levanta-se; *ācambite*—de repente.

**Depois que os devotos haviam cantado por muito tempo, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu levantou-se de repente, exclamando: "Hari bol!"**
