### CC Antya 14.80

> এই ত’ কহিলুঁ প্রভুর অদ্ভুত বিকার ।
> যাহার শ্রবণে লোকে লাগে চমৎকার ॥৮০॥

ei ta' kahiluṅ prabhura adbhuta vikāra

yāhāra śravaṇe loke lāge camatkāra

*ei ta'*—assim; *kahiluṅ*—descrevi; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *adbhuta vikāra*—as incomuns transformações do corpo; *yāhāra śravaṇe*—ao ouvir as quais; *loke*—as pessoas; *lāge*—sentem; *camatkāra*—assombro.

**Descrevi, assim, as incomuns transformações do corpo de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Quando as pessoas ouvem falar disso, ficam grandemente assombradas.**
