### CC Antya 14.56

> স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি করে কৃষ্ণলীলা গান ।\
> দুই জনে কিছু কৈলা প্রভুর বাহ্য জ্ঞান ॥৫৬॥

svarūpa-gosāñi kare kṛṣṇa-līlā gāna

dui jane kichu kailā prabhura bāhya jñāna

*svarūpa-gosāñi*—Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosāñi; *kare*—faz; *kṛṣṇa-līlā*—dos passatempos de Kṛṣṇa; *gāna*—canto; *dui jane*—ambos; *kichu*—em parte; *kailā*—reavivaram; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bāhya jñāna*—a consciência externa.

**Rāmānanda Rāya recitou versos do Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, e Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī cantou sobre os passatempos de Kṛṣṇa. Dessa maneira, eles trouxeram Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu de volta à consciência externa.**
