### CC Antya 14.84

> একদিন মহাপ্রভু সমুদ্রে যাইতে ।\
> ‘চটক’-পর্বত দেখিলেন আচম্বিতে ॥৮৪॥

eka-dina mahāprabhu samudre yāite

'caṭaka'-parvata dekhilena ācambite

*eka-dina*—certo dia; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *samudre yāite*—enquanto ia ao mar; *caṭaka-parvata*—a duna de areia Caṭaka-parvata; *dekhilena*—viu; *ācambite*—de repente.

**Certo dia, enquanto Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu Se dirigia ao mar para banhar-Se, Ele subitamente avistou uma duna de areia chamada Caṭaka-parvata.**
