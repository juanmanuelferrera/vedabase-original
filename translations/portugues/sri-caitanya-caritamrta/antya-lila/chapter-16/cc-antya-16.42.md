### CC Antya 16.42

> সেই গাড়ে করেন প্রভু পাদ-প্রক্ষালনে ।
> তবে করিবারে যায় ঈশ্বর-দরশনে ॥৪২॥

sei gāḍe karena prabhu pāda-prakṣālane

tabe karibāre yāya īśvara-daraśane

*sei gāḍe*—naquela vala; *karena*—faz; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *pāda-prakṣālane*—lavar os pés; *tabe*—em seguida; *karibāre*—para fazer; *yāya*—Ele vai; *īśvara-daraśane*—visitar o Senhor Jagannātha.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu lavava Seus pés nessa vala e, em seguida, entrava no templo para ver o Senhor Jagannātha.**
