### CC Antya 16.3

> এইমত মহাপ্রভু রহেন নীলাচলে ।\
> ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে সদা প্রেম-বিহ্বলে ॥৩॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu rahena nīlācale
bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge sadā prema-vihvale

*ei-mata*—desta maneira; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rahena*—permanece; *nīlācale*—em Jagannātha Purī; *bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge*—na companhia dos devotos; *sadā*—sempre; *prema-vihvale*—imerso no amor extático.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu permaneceu, portanto, em Jagannātha Purī, na companhia de Seus devotos, sempre imerso no amoroso serviço devocional extático.**
