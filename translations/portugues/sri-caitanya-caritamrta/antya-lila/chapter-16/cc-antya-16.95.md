### CC Antya 16.95

> এই বুদ্ধ্যে মহাপ্রভুর প্রেমাবেশ হৈল ।\
> জগন্নাথের সেবক দেখি’ সম্বরণ কৈল ॥৯৫॥

ei buddhye mahāprabhura premāveśa haila

jagannāthera sevaka dekhi' samvaraṇa kaila

*ei buddhye*—por esta compreensão; *mahāprabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *prema-āveśa*—emoção extática; *haila*—houve; *jagannāthera*—do Senhor Jagannātha; *sevaka*—os servos; *dekhi'*—vendo; *samvaraṇa kaila*—conteve-Se.

**Compreendendo isso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu sentiu uma emoção de amor extático por Kṛṣṇa, mas, ao ver os servos do Senhor Jagannātha, conteve-Se.**
