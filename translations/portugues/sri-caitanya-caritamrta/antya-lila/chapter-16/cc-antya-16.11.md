### CC Antya 16.11

> তাঁর ঠাঞি শেষ-পাত্র লয়েন মাগিয়া ।\
> কাহাঁ না পায়, তবে রহে লুকাঞা ॥১১॥

tāṅra ṭhāñi śeṣa-pātra layena māgiyā
kāhāṅ nā pāya, tabe rahe lukāñā

*tāṅra ṭhāñi*—deles; *śeṣa-pātra*—os pratos de restos; *layena*—toma; *māgiyā*—mendigando; *kāhāṅ*—onde; *nā pāya*—não consegue; *tabe*—então; *rahe*—permanece; *lukāñā*—escondendo-se.

**Ele mendigava restos do alimento de tais vaiṣṇavas e, se não recebia nenhum, escondia-se.**
