### CC Antya 16.56

> মহাপ্রভুর ইঙ্গিত গোবিন্দ সব জানে ।\
> কালিদাসেরে দিল প্রভুর শেষপাত্র-দানে ॥৫৬॥

mahāprabhura iṅgita govinda saba jāne

kālidāsere dila prabhura śeṣa-pātra-dāne

*mahāprabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *iṅgita*—indicações; *govinda*—Seu servo pessoal; *saba*—todas; *jāne*—conhece; *kālidāsere*—a Kālidāsa; *dila*—entregou; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śeṣa-pātra*—os restos do alimento; *dāne*—oferecimento.

**Govinda compreendeu todas as indicações de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Por isso, ele imediatamente entregou os restos do alimento de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu a Kālidāsa.**
