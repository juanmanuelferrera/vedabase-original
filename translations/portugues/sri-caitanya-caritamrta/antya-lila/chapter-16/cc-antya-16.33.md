### CC Antya 16.33

> ঝড়ু-ঠাকুর ঘর যাই’ দেখি’ আম্রফল ।\
> মানসেই কৃষ্ণচন্দ্রে অর্পিলা সকল ॥৩৩॥

jhaḍu-ṭhākura ghara yāi' dekhi' āmra-phala

mānasei kṛṣṇa-candre arpilā sakala

*jhaḍu-ṭhākura*—Jhaḍu Ṭhākura; *ghara yāi'*—regressando ao lar; *dekhi' āmra-phala*—vendo as mangas; *mānasei*—dentro de sua mente; *kṛṣṇa-candre*—a Kṛṣṇa; *arpilā*—ofereceu; *sakala*—todas.

**Ao regressar ao lar, Jhaḍu Ṭhākura viu as mangas que Kālidāsa havia presenteado. Dentro de sua mente, ofereceu-as a Kṛṣṇa-candra.**
