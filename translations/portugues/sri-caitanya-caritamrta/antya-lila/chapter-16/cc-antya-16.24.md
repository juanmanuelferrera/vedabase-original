### CC Antya 16.24

> তবে কালিদাস শ্লোক পড়ি’ শুনাইল ।
> শুনি’ ঝড়ু-ঠাকুরের বড় সুখ হইল ॥২৪॥

tabe kālidāsa śloka paḍi' śunāila

śuni' jhaḍu-ṭhākurera baḍa sukha ha-ila

*tabe*—em seguida; *kālidāsa*—Kālidāsa; *śloka*—versos; *paḍi'*—recitando; *śunāila*—fez ouvir; *śuni'*—ouvindo; *jhaḍu-ṭhākurera*—de Jhaḍu Ṭhākura; *baḍa*—muito grande; *sukha*—felicidade; *ha-ila*—houve.

**Kālidāsa recitou então alguns versos, que Jhaḍu Ṭhākura ficou muito feliz em ouvir.**
