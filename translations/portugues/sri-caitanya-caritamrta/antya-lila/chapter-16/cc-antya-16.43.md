### CC Antya 16.43

> গোবিন্দেরে মহাপ্রভু কৈরাছে নিয়ম ।\
> ‘মোর পাদজল যেন না লয় কোন জন’ ॥৪৩॥

govindere mahāprabhu kairāche niyama

'mora pāda-jala yena nā laya kona jana'

*govindere*—a Govinda; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kairāche*—deu; *niyama*—um princípio regulador; *mora*—Minha; *pāda-jala*—água do banho dos pés; *yena*—que; *nā laya*—não tome; *kona jana*—ninguém.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ordenou a Seu servo pessoal Govinda que ninguém tomasse a água que lavara Seus pés.**
