### CC Antya 16.73

> আর দিন কহেন প্রভু, — ‘পড়, পুরীদাস ।’
> এই শ্লোক করি’ তেঁহো করিলা প্রকাশ ॥৭৩॥

āra dina kahena prabhu,--'paḍa, purīdāsa'

ei śloka kari' teṅho karilā prakāśa

*āra dina*—outro dia; *kahena prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse; *paḍa*—recita; *purīdāsa*—Purīdāsa; *ei*—este; *śloka*—verso; *kari'*—compondo; *teṅho*—ele; *karilā prakāśa*—manifestou.

**Outro dia, quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse ao menino: "Recita, Meu querido Purīdāsa", o menino compôs o seguinte verso e o manifestou diante de todos.**
