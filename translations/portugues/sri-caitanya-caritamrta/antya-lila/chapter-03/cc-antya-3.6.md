### CC Antya 3.6

> বার বার নিষেধ করে ব্রাহ্মণকুমারে ।
> প্রভুরে না দেখিলে সেই রহিতে না পারে ॥৬॥

bāra bāra niṣedha kare brāhmaṇa-kumāre
prabhure nā dekhile sei rahite nā pāre

*bāra bāra*—repetidas vezes; *niṣedha kare*—proíbe; *brāhmaṇa-kumāre*—o filho do brāhmaṇa; *prabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nā dekhile*—sem ver; *sei*—aquele menino; *rahite nā pāre*—não conseguia ficar.

**Dāmodara Paṇḍita proibia repetidas vezes o filho do brāhmaṇa de visitar o Senhor, mas o menino não suportava ficar em casa sem ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
