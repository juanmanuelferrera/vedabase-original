### CC Antya 3.214

> বিপ্রের কুষ্ঠ শুনি’ হরিদাস মনে দুঃখী হৈলা ।
> বলাই-পুরোহিতে কহি’ শান্তিপুর আইলা ॥২১৪॥

viprera kuṣṭha śuni' haridāsa mane duḥkhī hailā

balāi-purohite kahi' śāntipura āilā

*viprera*—do brāhmaṇa; *kuṣṭha*—lepra; *śuni'*—ouvindo; *haridāsa*—Haridāsa Ṭhākura; *mane*—dentro da mente; *duḥkhī hailā*—ficou infeliz; *balāi-purohite*—a Balarāma Ācārya; *kahi'*—falando; *śāntipura āilā*—veio a Śāntipura.

**Haridāsa Ṭhākura ficou infeliz ao ouvir que o brāhmaṇa Gopāla Cakravartī fora atacado pela lepra. Assim, após informar Balarāma Ācārya, o sacerdote de Hiraṇya Majumadāra, ele foi a Śāntipura, o lar de Advaita Ācārya.**
