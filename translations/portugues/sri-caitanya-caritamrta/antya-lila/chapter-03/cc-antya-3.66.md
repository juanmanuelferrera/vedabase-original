### CC Antya 3.66

> শুনিয়া প্রভুর সুখ বাড়য়ে অন্তরে  ।\
> পুনরপি ভঙ্গী করি’ পুছয়ে তাঁহারে ॥৬৬॥

śuniyā prabhura sukha bāḍaye antare

punarapi bhaṅgī kari' puchaye tāṅhāre

*śuniyā*—ouvindo; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sukha*—felicidade; *bāḍaye*—aumentou; *antare*—dentro do coração; *punarapi*—ainda; *bhaṅgī kari'*—naturalmente; *puchaye tāṅhāre*—pergunta a Haridāsa Ṭhākura.

**Ao ouvir isso de Haridāsa Ṭhākura, a felicidade dentro de Seu coração aumentou, mas, naturalmente, Ele ainda continuou a indagar.**
