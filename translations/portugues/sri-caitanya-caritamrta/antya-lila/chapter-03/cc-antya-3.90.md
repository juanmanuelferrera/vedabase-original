### CC Antya 3.90

> মনের সন্তোষে তাঁরে কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ।\
> বাহ্যে প্রকাশিতে এ-সব করিলা বর্জন ॥৯০॥

manera santoṣe tāṅre kailā āliṅgana

bāhye prakāśite e-saba karilā varjana

*manera santoṣe*—com completa satisfação da mente; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kailā āliṅgana*—Ele abraçou; *bāhye*—externamente; *prakāśite*—revelar; *e-saba*—tudo isto; *karilā varjana*—Ele evitou.

**Grandemente satisfeito com as afirmações de Haridāsa Ṭhākura, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu o abraçou. Externamente, contudo, Ele evitou prosseguir com discussões sobre esses assuntos.**
