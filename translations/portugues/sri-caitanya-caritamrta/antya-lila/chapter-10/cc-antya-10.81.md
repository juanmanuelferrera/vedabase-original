### CC Antya 10.81

> সব লঞা প্রভু কৈলা প্রসাদ ভোজন ।
> সবারে বিদায় দিলা করিতে শয়ন ॥৮১॥

saba lañā prabhu kailā prasāda bhojana

sabāre vidāya dilā karite śayana

*saba lañā*—com todos eles; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kailā*—realizou; *prasāda bhojana*—tomou prasāda; *sabāre*—a todos; *vidāya dilā*—despediu-se; *karite śayana*—para descansarem.

**Então, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu tomou prasāda com todos eles e, em seguida, pediu-lhes que regressassem a seus aposentos e descansassem.**
