### CC Antya 10.59

> সাত-সম্প্রদায়ে নৃত্য করে সাত জন  ।\
> অদ্বৈত আচার্য, আর প্রভু-নিত্যানন্দ ॥৫৯॥

sāta-sampradāye nṛtya kare sāta jana

advaita ācārya, āra prabhu-nityānanda

*sāta-sampradāye*—nos sete grupos; *nṛtya kare*—dançavam; *sāta jana*—sete pessoas; *advaita ācārya*—Advaita Ācārya; *āra*—e; *prabhu-nityānanda*—o Senhor Nityānanda.

**Em cada um dos sete grupos havia um dançarino principal, como Advaita Ācārya e o Senhor Nityānanda.**
