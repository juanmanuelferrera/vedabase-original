### CC Antya 10.43

> সেইকালে মহাপ্রভু ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে ।
> নরেন্দ্রে আইলা দেখিতে জলকেলি-রঙ্গে ॥৪৩॥

sei-kāle mahāprabhu bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge

narendre āilā dekhite jala-keli-raṅge

*sei-kāle*—naquele momento; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge*—com Seus devotos; *narendre āilā*—chegou ao lago Narendra; *dekhite*—para ver; *jala-keli*—os passatempos na água; *raṅge*—em grande júbilo.

**Então, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu chegou com Seus associados pessoais para ver os jubilosos passatempos do Senhor Jagannātha no Narendra-sarovara.**
