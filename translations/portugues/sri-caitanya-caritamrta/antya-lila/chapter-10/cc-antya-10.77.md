### CC Antya 10.77

> তবে নিত্যানন্দ প্রভু সৃজিলা উপায় ।
> ক্রমে-ক্রমে কীর্তনীয়া রাখিল সবায় ॥৭৭॥

tabe nityānanda prabhu sṛjilā upāya

krame-krame kīrtanīyā rākhila sabāya

*tabe*—naquele momento; *nityānanda*—o Senhor Nityānanda; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *sṛjilā upāya*—arquitetou um meio; *krame-krame*—pouco a pouco; *kīrtanīyā*—os cantores; *rākhila*—fez parar; *sabāya*—a todos.

**Então, o Senhor Nityānanda encontrou um meio de encerrar o kīrtana. Gradualmente, fez com que todos os cantores parassem.**
