### CC Antya 10.79

> কোলাহল নাহি, প্রভুর কিছু বাহ্য হৈল ।
> তবে নিত্যানন্দ সবার শ্রম জানাইল ॥৭৯॥

kolāhala nāhi, prabhura kichu bāhya haila

tabe nityānanda sabāra śrama jānāila

*kolāhala*—som tumultuoso; *nāhi*—não havia; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kichu*—alguma; *bāhya*—consciência externa; *haila*—houve; *tabe*—naquele momento; *nityānanda*—o Senhor Nityānanda; *sabāra*—de todos eles; *śrama*—o cansaço; *jānāila*—informou.

**Quando já não havia som tumultuoso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu recobrou a consciência. Então, Nityānanda Prabhu informou-O do cansaço dos cantores e dançarinos.**
