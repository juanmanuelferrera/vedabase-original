### CC Antya 10.88

> গোবিন্দ কহে, — ‘করিতে চাহি পাদ-সম্বাহন’ ।\
> প্রভু কহে, — ‘কর বা না কর, যেই লয় তোমার মন’ ॥৮৮॥

govinda kahe,--'karite cāhi pāda-samvāhana'

prabhu kahe,--'kara vā nā kara, yei laya tomāra mana'

*govinda kahe*—Govinda disse; *karite*—realizar; *cāhi*—desejo; *pāda-samvāhana*—massagear Tuas pernas; *prabhu kahe*—o Senhor respondeu; *kara*—faze; *vā*—ou; *nā kara*—não o faças; *yei*—o que quer que; *laya tomāra mana*—decidas.

**Govinda pediu repetidamente: "Desejo massagear Tuas pernas", mas o Senhor disse: "Faze-o ou não o faças. Depende da tua vontade."**
