### CC Antya 10.108

> কেহ কোন প্রসাদ আনি’ দেয় গোবিন্দ-ঠাঞি ।
> ‘ইহা যেন অবশ্য ভক্ষণ করেন গোসাঞি’ ॥১০৮॥

keha kona prasāda āni' deya govinda-ṭhāñi

'ihā yena avaśya bhakṣaṇa karena gosāñi'

*keha*—alguém; *kona prasāda*—alguma variedade de prasāda; *āni'*—trazendo; *deya*—entrega; *govinda-ṭhāñi*—a Govinda; *ihā*—isto; *yena*—que; *avaśya*—certamente; *bhakṣaṇa karena*—come; *gosāñi*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Cada devoto trazia certo tipo de prasāda. Confiava-o a Govinda e lhe pedia: "Por favor, providencia que o Senhor com certeza coma este prasāda."**
