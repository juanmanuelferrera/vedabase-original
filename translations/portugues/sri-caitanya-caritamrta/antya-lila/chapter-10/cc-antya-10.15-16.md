### CC Antya 10.15-16

> আম্র-কাশন্দি, আদা-কাশন্দি ঝাল-কাশন্দি নাম ।\
> নেম্বু-আদা আম্রকোলি বিবিধ বিধান ॥১৫॥\
> আমিস্, আমখণ্ড, তৈলাম্র, আমসত্তা ।\
> যত্ন করি’ গুণ্ডা করি’ পুরাণ সুকুতা ॥১৬॥

āmra-kāśandi, ādā-kāśandi jhāla-kāśandi nāma
nembu-ādā āmra-koli vividha vidhāna
āmsi, āma-khaṇḍa, tailāmra, āma-sattā
yatna kari' guṇḍā kari' purāṇa sukutā

*āmra-kāśandi*—āmra-kāśandi; *ādā-kāśandi*—ādā-kāśandi; *jhāla-kāśandi*—jhāla-kāśandi; *nāma*—chamados; *nembu-ādā*—uma preparação feita com limão e gengibre; *āmra-koli*—āmra-koli; *vividha vidhāna*—diversas preparações; *āmsi*—āmsi; *āma-khaṇḍa*—āma-khaṇḍa; *tailāmra*—manga em óleo de mostarda; *āma-sattā*—āma-sattā; *yatna kari'*—com grande atenção; *guṇḍā kari'*—reduzindo a pó; *purāṇa sukutā*—vegetais amargos secos, tais como o melão-amargo.

**Estes são os nomes de alguns dos picles e condimentos contidos nos sacos de Rāghava Paṇḍita: āmra-kāśandi, ādā-kāśandi, jhāla-kāśandi, nembu-ādā, āmra-koli, āmsi, āma-khaṇḍa, tailāmra e āma-sattā. Com grande atenção, Damayantī também reduziu a pó vegetais amargos secos.**
