### CC Antya 10.102

> সঙ্ক্ষেপে কহিলুঁ এই পরিমুণ্ডা-নৃত্য ।
> অদ্যাপিহ গায় যাহা চৈতন্যের ভৃত্য ॥১০২॥

saṅkṣepe kahiluṅ ei pari-muṇḍā-nṛtya

adyāpiha gāya yāhā caitanyera bhṛtya

*saṅkṣepe*—em resumo; *kahiluṅ*—descrevi; *ei*—esta; *pari-muṇḍā-nṛtya*—a dança no salão de dança do templo de Jagannātha; *adyāpiha*—até hoje; *gāya*—cantam a respeito; *yāhā*—a qual; *caitanyera bhṛtya*—os servos de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Assim, descrevi brevemente a dança de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu no salão do templo de Jagannātha. Os servos de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu cantam a respeito dessa dança ainda hoje.**
