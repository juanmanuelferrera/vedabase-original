### CC Antya 10.61

> সাত-সম্প্রদায়ে প্রভু করেন ভ্রমণ ।
> ‘মোর সম্প্রদায়ে প্রভু’ — ঐছে সবার মন ॥৬১॥

sāta-sampradāye prabhu karena bhramaṇa

'mora sampradāye prabhu'--aiche sabāra mana

*sāta-sampradāye*—nos sete grupos; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *karena bhramaṇa*—perambula; *mora sampradāye prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu está em nosso grupo; *aiche*—dessa maneira; *sabāra mana*—todos pensavam.

**Enquanto Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu caminhava de um grupo a outro inspecionando-os, os homens de cada grupo pensavam: "O Senhor está dentro de nosso grupo."**
