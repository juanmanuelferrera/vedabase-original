### CC Antya 10.14

> নানা অপূর্ব ভক্ষ্যদ্রব্য প্রভুর যোগ্য ভোগ ।
> বৎসরেক প্রভু যাহা করেন উপযোগ ॥১৪॥

nānā apūrva bhakṣya-dravya prabhura yogya bhoga
vatsareka prabhu yāhā karena upayoga

*nānā*—diversos; *apūrva*—incomparáveis; *bhakṣya-dravya*—alimentos; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yogya bhoga*—perfeitamente adequados para comer; *vatsareka*—durante um ano; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yāhā*—os quais; *karena upayoga*—utiliza.

**Damayantī preparou variedades de alimentos incomparáveis, perfeitamente adequados para o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu comer. O Senhor comeu-os continuamente durante um ano.**
