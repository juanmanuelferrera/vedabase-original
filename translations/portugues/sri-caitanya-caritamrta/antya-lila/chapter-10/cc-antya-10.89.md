### CC Antya 10.89

> তবে গোবিন্দ বহির্বাস তাঁর উপরে দিয়া ।\
> ভিতর-ঘরে গেলা মহাপ্রভুরে লঙ্ঘিয়া ॥৮৯॥

tabe govinda bahirvāsa tāṅra upare diyā

bhitara-ghare gelā mahāprabhure laṅghiyā

*tabe*—então; *govinda*—Govinda; *bahirvāsa*—manto exterior; *tāṅra upare*—sobre Ele; *diyā*—estendendo; *bhitara-ghare*—no quarto; *gelā*—entrou; *mahāprabhure laṅghiyā*—passando por cima de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Então, Govinda estendeu o manto do Senhor sobre o corpo Dele e, dessa maneira, entrou no quarto passando por cima Dele.**
