### CC Antya 11.85

> প্রভু না খাইলে কেহ না করে ভোজন ।
> প্রভুরে সে দিনে কাশীমিশ্রের নিমন্ত্রণ ॥৮৫॥

prabhu nā khāile keha nā kare bhojana

prabhure se dine kāśī-miśrera nimantraṇa

*prabhu nā khāile*—enquanto o Senhor não comesse; *keha nā kare bhojana*—ninguém comeria; *prabhure*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *se dine*—naquele dia; *kāśī-miśrera*—de Kāśī Miśra; *nimantraṇa*—o convite.

**Todos os devotos que se sentavam não aceitavam o prasāda enquanto o Senhor não houvesse comido. Naquele dia, contudo, Kāśī Miśra havia estendido um convite ao Senhor.**
