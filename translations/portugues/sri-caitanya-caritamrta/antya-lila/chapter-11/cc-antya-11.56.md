### CC Antya 11.56

> ‘শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য’-শব্দ করিতে উচ্চারণ ।\
> নামের সহিত প্রাণ কৈল উৎক্রামণ ॥৫৬॥

'śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya' śabda karite uccāraṇa

nāmera sahita prāṇa kaila utkrāmaṇa

*śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya*—Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya; *śabda*—a vibração sonora; *karite uccāraṇa*—cantando; *nāmera sahita*—com o nome; *prāṇa*—a vida; *kaila utkrāmaṇa*—partiu.

**Enquanto cantava o santo nome de Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, ele exalou seu último suspiro de vida e deixou o corpo.**
