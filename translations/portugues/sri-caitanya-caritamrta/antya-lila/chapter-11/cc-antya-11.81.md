### CC Antya 11.81

> সব বৈষ্ণবে প্রভু বসাইলা সারি সারি ।
> আপনে পরিবেশে প্রভু লঞা জনা চারি ॥৮১॥

saba vaiṣṇave prabhu vasāilā sāri sāri

āpane pariveśe prabhu lañā janā cāri

*saba vaiṣṇave*—todos os vaiṣṇavas; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *vasāilā*—fez sentar; *sāri sāri*—em filas; *āpane*—pessoalmente; *pariveśe*—distribui; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *lañā*—tomando; *janā cāri*—quatro homens.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu fez todos os devotos sentarem-se em filas e pessoalmente começou a distribuir o prasāda, auxiliado por outros quatro homens.**
