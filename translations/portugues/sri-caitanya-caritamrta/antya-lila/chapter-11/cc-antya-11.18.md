### CC Antya 11.18

> গোবিন্দ কহে, — ‘উঠ আসি’ করহ ভোজন’ ।\
> হরিদাস কহে, — আজি করিমু লঙ্ঘন ॥১৮॥

govinda kahe,--'uṭha āsi' karaha bhojana'

haridāsa kahe,--āji karimu laṅghana

*govinda kahe*—Govinda disse; *uṭha*—por favor, levanta-te; *āsi'*—vindo; *karaha bhojana*—toma teu prasāda; *haridāsa kahe*—Haridāsa respondeu; *āji*—hoje; *karimu laṅghana*—observarei jejum.

**"Por favor, levanta-te e toma teu mahā-prasāda", disse Govinda. Haridāsa Ṭhākura respondeu: "Hoje observarei jejum.**
