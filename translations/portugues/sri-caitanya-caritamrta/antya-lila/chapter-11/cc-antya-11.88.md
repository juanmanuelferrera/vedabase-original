### CC Antya 11.88

> আকণ্ঠ পূরাঞা সবায় করাইলা ভোজন ।\
> দেহ’ দেহ’ বলি’ প্রভু বলেন বচন ॥৮৮॥

ākaṇṭha pūrāñā sabāya karāilā bhojana

deha' deha' bali' prabhu balena vacana

*ākaṇṭha pūrāñā*—enchendo até a garganta; *sabāya*—a todos; *karāilā bhojana*—fez comer; *deha' deha'*—deem-lhes mais, deem-lhes mais; *bali'*—dizendo; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *balena vacana*—falava.

**Todos ficaram empanturrados até a garganta, porque Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu não parava de dizer aos distribuidores: "Deem-lhes mais! Deem-lhes mais!"**
