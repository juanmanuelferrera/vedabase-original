### CC Antya 11.47

> প্রভু কহে, — ‘হরিদাস, কহ সমাচার’ ।
> হরিদাস কহে, — ‘প্রভু, যে কৃপা তোমার’ ॥৪৭॥

prabhu kahe,--'haridāsa, kaha samācāra'

haridāsa kahe,--'prabhu, ye kṛpā tomāra'

*prabhu kahe*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse; *haridāsa*—Meu querido Haridāsa; *kaha samācāra*—quais são as novidades; *haridāsa kahe*—Haridāsa respondeu; *prabhu*—meu Senhor; *ye*—qualquer que seja; *kṛpā*—misericórdia; *tomāra*—Tua.

**O Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu perguntou: "Meu querido Haridāsa, quais são as novidades?" Haridāsa Ṭhākura respondeu: "Meu Senhor, qualquer que seja a misericórdia que possas conceder-me."**
