### CC Antya 11.63

> আগে মহাপ্রভু চলেন নৃত্য করিতে করিতে ।
> পাছে নৃত্য করে বক্রেশ্বর ভক্তগণ-সাথে ॥৬৩॥

āge mahāprabhu calena nṛtya karite karite

pāche nṛtya kare vakreśvara bhakta-gaṇa-sāthe

*āge*—à frente; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *calena*—vai; *nṛtya*—dançando; *karite karite*—realizando; *pāche*—atrás; *nṛtya kare*—dança; *vakreśvara*—Vakreśvara; *bhakta-gaṇa-sāthe*—com os demais devotos.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu dançava à frente da procissão, e Vakreśvara Paṇḍita, juntamente com os demais devotos, cantava e dançava atrás Dele.**
