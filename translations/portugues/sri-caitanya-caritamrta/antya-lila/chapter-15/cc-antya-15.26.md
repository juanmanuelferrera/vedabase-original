### CC Antya 15.26

> সেই দুইজন প্রভুরে করে আশ্বাসন ।
> স্বরূপ গায়, রায় করে শ্লোক পঠন ॥২৬॥

sei dui-jana prabhure kare āśvāsana

svarūpa gāya, rāya kare śloka paṭhana

*sei*—aqueles; *dui-jana*—dois homens; *prabhure*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kare*—fazem; *āśvāsana*—apaziguamento; *svarūpa gāya*—Svarūpa Dāmodara canta; *rāya*—Rāmānanda Rāya; *kare*—faz; *śloka paṭhana*—recitação de versos.

**Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī cantava canções apropriadas, e Rāmānanda Rāya recitava versos adequados para realçar o êxtase do Senhor. Dessa maneira, conseguiam apaziguá-Lo.**
