### CC Antya 19.74

> তাঁর ভয়ে নারেন প্রভু বাহিরে যাইতে ।\
> তাঁর ভয়ে নারেন ভিত্ত্যে মুখাব্জ ঘষিতে ॥৭৪॥

tāṅra bhaye nārena prabhu bāhire yāite

tāṅra bhaye nārena bhittye mukhābja ghaṣite

*tāṅra bhaye*—por temor a ele; *nārena*—não era capaz; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bāhire yāite*—de sair; *tāṅra bhaye*—por temor a ele; *nārena*—não era capaz; *bhittye*—nas paredes; *mukha-abja ghaṣite*—de esfregar Seu rosto de lótus.

**Por temor a Śaṅkara, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu não podia nem sair de Seu aposento nem esfregar Seu rosto de lótus contra as paredes.**
