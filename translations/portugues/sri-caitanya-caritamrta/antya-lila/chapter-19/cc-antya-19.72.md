### CC Antya 19.72

> উঘাড়-অঙ্গে পড়িয়া শঙ্কর নিদ্রা যায় ।\
> প্রভু উঠি’ আপন-কাঁথা তাহারে জড়ায় ॥৭২॥

ughāḍa-aṅge paḍiyā śaṅkara nidrā yāya

prabhu uṭhi' āpana-kāṅthā tāhāre jaḍāya

*ughāḍa-aṅge*—sem nada cobrindo o corpo; *paḍiyā*—deitado; *śaṅkara*—Śaṅkara; *nidrā yāya*—dorme; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *uṭhi'*—levantando-se; *āpana-kāṅthā*—Sua própria coberta; *tāhāre jaḍāya*—envolve-o com ela.

**Ele ficava dormindo sem nada cobrindo seu corpo, e Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu Se levantava e o envolvia com Sua própria coberta.**
