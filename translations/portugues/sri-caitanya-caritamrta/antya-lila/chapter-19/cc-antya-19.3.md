### CC Antya 19.3

> এইমত মহাপ্রভু কৃষ্ণপ্রেমাবেশে ।
> উন্মাদ-প্রলাপ করে রাত্রি-দিবসে ॥৩॥

ei-mate mahāprabhu kṛṣṇa-premāveśe
unmāda-pralāpa kare rātri-divase

*ei-mate*—dessa maneira; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kṛṣṇa-prema-āveśe*—no amor emocional extático a Kṛṣṇa; *unmāda*—loucura; *pralāpa*—e fala insana; *kare*—realiza; *rātri-divase*—durante todo o dia e toda a noite.

**Assim, no êxtase do amor a Kṛṣṇa, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu comportava-se como um louco, falando de modo insano dia e noite.**
