### CC Antya 19.61

> দীপ জ্বালি’ ঘরে গেলা, দেখি’ প্রভুর মুখ ।
> স্বরূপ, গোবিন্দ দুঁহার হৈল বড় দুঃখ ॥৬১॥

dīpa jvāli' ghare gelā, dekhi' prabhura mukha

svarūpa, govinda duṅhāra haila baḍa duḥkha

*dīpa jvāli'*—acendendo a lamparina; *ghare*—no quarto; *gelā*—entraram; *dekhi'*—vendo; *prabhura mukha*—o rosto do Senhor; *svarūpa*—Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; *govinda*—e Govinda; *duṅhāra*—de ambos; *haila baḍa duḥkha*—houve grande tristeza.

**Acendendo uma lamparina, Svarūpa Dāmodara e Govinda entraram no quarto. Quando viram o rosto do Senhor, ambos ficaram tomados de tristeza.**
