### CC Antya 19.89

> নিরন্তর নাসায় পশে কৃষ্ণ-পরিমল ।\
> গন্ধ আস্বাদিতে প্রভু হইলা পাগল ॥৮৯॥

nirantara nāsāya paśe kṛṣṇa-parimala

gandha āsvādite prabhu ha-ilā pāgala

*nirantara*—incessantemente; *nāsāya*—nas narinas; *paśe*—entra; *kṛṣṇa-parimala*—o perfume do corpo de Kṛṣṇa; *gandha āsvādite*—saborear a fragrância; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ha-ilā pāgala*—enlouqueceu.

**O perfume do corpo de Kṛṣṇa, contudo, entrava incessantemente em Suas narinas, e o Senhor enlouqueceu de vontade de saboreá-lo.**
