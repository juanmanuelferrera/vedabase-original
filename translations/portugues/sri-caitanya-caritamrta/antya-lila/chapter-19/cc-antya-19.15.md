### CC Antya 19.15

> জগদানন্দ নদীয়া গিয়া মাতারে মিলিলা ।\
> প্রভুর যত নিবেদন, সকল কহিলা ॥১৫॥

jagadānanda nadīyā giyā mātāre mililā
prabhura yata nivedana, sakala kahilā

*jagadānanda*—Jagadānanda; *nadīyā*—a Navadvīpa; *giyā*—indo; *mātāre*—mãe Śacī; *mililā*—encontrou; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yata nivedana*—todo tipo de saudações; *sakala*—tudo; *kahilā*—ele relatou.

**Jagadānanda Paṇḍita retornou, então, a Nadia e, ao encontrar-se com Śacīmātā, transmitiu-lhe todas as saudações do Senhor.**
