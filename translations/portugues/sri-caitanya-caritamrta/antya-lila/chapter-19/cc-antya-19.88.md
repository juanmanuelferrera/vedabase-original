### CC Antya 19.88

> কৃষ্ণের শ্রীঅঙ্গগন্ধে ভরিছে উদ্যানে ।
> সেই গন্ধ পাঞা প্রভু হৈলা অচেতনে ॥৮৮॥

kṛṣṇera śrī-aṅga-gandhe bhariche udyāne

sei gandha pāñā prabhu hailā acetane

*kṛṣṇera*—do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *śrī-aṅga-gandhe*—o aroma do corpo transcendental; *bhariche*—enchia; *udyāne*—o jardim; *sei gandha pāñā*—sentindo aquele aroma; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *hailā*—ficou; *acetane*—inconsciente.

**O jardim inteiro estava impregnado do perfume do corpo transcendental do Senhor Śrī Kṛṣṇa. Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu o cheirou, Ele caiu inconsciente no mesmo instante.**
