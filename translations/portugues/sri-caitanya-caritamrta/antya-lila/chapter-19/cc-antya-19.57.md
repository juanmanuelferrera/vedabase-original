### CC Antya 19.57

> প্রেমাবেশে মহাপ্রভুর গর-গর মন ।
> নামসঙ্কীর্তন করি’ করেন জাগরণ ॥৫৭॥

premāveśe mahāprabhura gara-gara mana

nāma-saṅkīrtana kari' karena jāgaraṇa

*prema-āveśe*—em emoção grandemente extática; *mahāprabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *gara-gara mana*—a mente estava plenamente dominada; *nāma-saṅkīrtana kari'*—cantando o mantra Hare Kṛṣṇa; *karena*—faz; *jāgaraṇa*—vigília.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu permaneceu acordado a noite toda, cantando o mahā-mantra Hare Kṛṣṇa, com Sua mente dominada pelo êxtase espiritual.**
