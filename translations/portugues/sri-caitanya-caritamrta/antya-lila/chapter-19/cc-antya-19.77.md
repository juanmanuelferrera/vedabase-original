### CC Antya 19.77

> এইমত মহাপ্রভু রাত্রি-দিবসে ।
> প্রেমসিন্ধু-মগ্ন রহে, কভু ডুবে, ভাসে ॥৭৭॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu rātri-divase

prema-sindhu-magna rahe, kabhu ḍube, bhāse

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rātri-divase*—dia e noite; *prema-sindhu-magna rahe*—permanece imerso no oceano de amor por Kṛṣṇa; *kabhu ḍube*—às vezes afunda; *bhāse*—boia.

**Dessa maneira, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu permanecia imerso dia e noite num oceano de amor extático por Kṛṣṇa. Às vezes, Ele submergia, e às vezes boiava.**
