### CC Antya 19.73

> নিরন্তর ঘুমায় শঙ্কর শীঘ্র-চেতন ।
> বসি’ পাদ চাপি’ করে রাত্রি-জাগরণ ॥৭৩॥

nirantara ghumāya śaṅkara śīghra-cetana

vasi' pāda cāpi' kare rātri-jāgaraṇa

*nirantara*—incessantemente; *ghumāya*—dorme; *śaṅkara*—Śaṅkara; *śīghra*—muito rapidamente; *cetana*—despertando; *vasi'*—sentando-se; *pāda cāpi'*—massageando as pernas; *kare*—faz; *rātri-jāgaraṇa*—permanece acordado à noite.

**Śaṅkara Paṇḍita sempre adormecia, mas despertava rapidamente, sentava-se e começava de novo a massagear as pernas de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Dessa maneira, permanecia acordado a noite inteira.**
