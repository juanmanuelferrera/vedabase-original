### CC Antya 18.95

> চক্রবাক-মণ্ডল, পৃথক্ পৃথক্ যুগল,\
> জল হৈতে করিল উদ্গম ।\
> উঠিল পদ্মমণ্ডল, পৃথক্ পৃথক্ যুগল,\
> চক্রবাকে কৈল আচ্ছাদন ॥৯৫॥

cakravāka-maṇḍala, pṛthak pṛthak yugala,

jala haite karila udgama

uṭhila padma-maṇḍala, pṛthak pṛthak yugala,

cakravāke kaila ācchādana

*cakravāka-maṇḍala*—os globos das aves cakravāka; *pṛthak pṛthak*—separados; *yugala*—casais; *jala haite*—da água; *karila*—fizeram; *udgama*—aparecimento; *uṭhila*—ergueram-se; *padma-maṇḍala*—o círculo de flores de lótus; *pṛthak pṛthak*—separados; *yugala*—casais; *cakravāke*—as aves cakravāka; *kaila*—fizeram; *ācchādana*—cobertura.

**"Quando os seios erguidos das gopīs, que se assemelhavam aos corpos globulares das aves cakravāka, emergiam da água em casais separados, os lótus azulados das mãos de Kṛṣṇa erguiam-se para cobri-los.**
