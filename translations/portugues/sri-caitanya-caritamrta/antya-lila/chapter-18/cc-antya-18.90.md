### CC Antya 18.90

> যত গোপ-সুন্দরী, কৃষ্ণ তত রূপ ধরি’,
> সবার বস্ত্র করিলা হরণে ।
> যমুনা-জল নির্মল, অঙ্গ করে ঝলমল,
> সুখে কৃষ্ণ করে দরশনে ॥৯০॥

yata gopa-sundarī, kṛṣṇa tata rūpa dhari',

sabāra vastra karilā haraṇe

yamunā-jala nirmala, aṅga kare jhalamala,

sukhe kṛṣṇa kare daraśane

*yata*—tantas quantas; *gopa-sundarī*—belas gopīs; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *tata*—outras tantas; *rūpa*—formas; *dhari'*—assumindo; *sabāra*—de todas; *vastra*—roupas que as cobriam; *karilā haraṇe*—tirou; *yamunā-jala*—a água do Yamunā; *nirmala*—muito clara; *aṅga*—corpos; *kare jhalamala*—reluzem; *sukhe*—com felicidade; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *kare daraśane*—vê.

**"Kṛṣṇa expandiu-Se em tantas formas quantas eram as gopīs e então tirou todas as roupas que as cobriam. A água do rio Yamunā era cristalina, e Kṛṣṇa via com grande felicidade os reluzentes corpos das gopīs.**
