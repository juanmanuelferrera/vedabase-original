### CC Antya 18.82

> তীরে রহি’ দেখি আমি সখীগণ-সঙ্গে ।
> একসখী সখীগণে দেখায় সেই রঙ্গে ॥৮২॥

tīre rahi' dekhi āmi sakhī-gaṇa-saṅge

eka-sakhī sakhī-gaṇe dekhāya sei raṅge

*tīre*—na margem; *rahi'*—de pé; *dekhi*—vejo; *āmi*—Eu; *sakhī-gaṇa-saṅge*—com as gopīs; *eka-sakhī*—uma gopī; *sakhī-gaṇe*—às outras gopīs; *dekhāya*—mostra; *sei raṅge*—aquele passatempo.

**"Vi esse passatempo enquanto estava de pé na margem do Yamunā na companhia das gopīs. Uma gopī mostrava às outras gopīs os passatempos de Rādhā e Kṛṣṇa na água.**
