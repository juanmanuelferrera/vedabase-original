### CC Antya 18.99

> অতিশয়োক্তি, বিরোধাভাস, দুই অলঙ্কার প্রকাশ,
> করি’ কৃষ্ণ প্রকট দেখাইল ।
> যাহা করি’ আস্বাদন, আনন্দিত মোর মন,
> নেত্র-কর্ণ-যুগ্ম জুড়াইল ॥৯৯॥

atiśayokti, virodhābhāsa, dui alaṅkāra prakāśa,

kari' kṛṣṇa prakaṭa dekhāila

yāhā kari' āsvādana, ānandita mora mana,

netra-karṇa-yugma juḍāila

*atiśaya-ukti*—linguagem exagerada; *virodha-ābhāsa*—analogia incongruente; *dui alaṅkāra*—duas metáforas; *prakāśa*—manifestações; *kari'*—fazendo; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *prakaṭa*—exibiu; *dekhāila*—mostrou; *yāhā*—as quais; *kari' āsvādana*—saboreando; *ānandita*—satisfeita; *mora mana*—Minha mente; *netra-karṇa*—dos olhos e dos ouvidos; *yugma*—os pares; *juḍāila*—ficaram satisfeitos.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu continuou: "Em Seus passatempos, Kṛṣṇa exibiu os dois ornamentos da hipérbole e da analogia inversa. Saboreá-los trouxe alegria à Minha mente e satisfez plenamente Meus ouvidos e olhos.**
