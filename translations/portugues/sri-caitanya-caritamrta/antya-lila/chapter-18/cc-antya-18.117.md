### CC Antya 18.117

> প্রভু কহে, — “স্বপ্নে দেখি’ গেলাঙ বৃন্দাবনে ।\
> দেখি, — কৃষ্ণ রাস করেন গোপীগণ-সনে ॥১১৭॥

prabhu kahe,--"svapne dekhi' gelāṅa vṛndāvane

dekhi,--kṛṣṇa rāsa karena gopīgaṇa-sane

*prabhu kahe*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse; *svapne dekhi'*—sonhando; *gelāṅa vṛndāvane*—fui a Vṛndāvana; *dekhi*—vejo; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *rāsa karena*—realiza a dança da rāsa; *gopī-gaṇa-sane*—com as gopīs.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse: "Em Meu sonho, fui a Vṛndāvana, onde vi o Senhor Kṛṣṇa realizar a dança da rāsa com todas as gopīs.**
