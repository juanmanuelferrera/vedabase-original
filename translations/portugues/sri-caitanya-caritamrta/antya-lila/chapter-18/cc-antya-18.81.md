### CC Antya 18.81

> রাধিকাদি গোপীগণ-সঙ্গে একত্র মেলি’ ।
> যমুনার জলে মহারঙ্গে করেন কেলি ॥৮১॥

rādhikādi gopī-gaṇa-saṅge ekatra meli'

yamunāra jale mahā-raṅge karena keli

*rādhikā-ādi*—encabeçadas por Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; *gopī-gaṇa-saṅge*—com as gopīs; *ekatra meli'*—reunindo-se; *yamunāra*—do rio Yamunā; *jale*—na água; *mahā-raṅge*—em grande disposição esportiva; *karena keli*—realiza passatempos.

**"O Senhor Kṛṣṇa estava na água do Yamunā na companhia das gopīs, encabeçadas por Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī. Elas realizavam passatempos de maneira bastante esportiva.**
