### CC Antya 18.38

> চাহিয়ে বেড়াইতে ঐছে রাত্রি-শেষ হৈল ।\
> ‘অন্তর্ধান হইলা প্রভু’, — নিশ্চয় করিল ॥৩৮॥

cāhiye beḍāite aiche rātri-śeṣa haila

'antardhāna ha-ilā prabhu',--niścaya karila

*cāhiye*—procurando; *beḍāite*—perambulando; *aiche*—dessa maneira; *rātri-śeṣa haila*—a noite terminou; *antardhāna ha-ilā*—desapareceu; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *niścaya karila*—eles concluíram.

**Enquanto procuravam o Senhor, a noite terminou, e assim todos concluíram: "O Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu agora desapareceu."**
