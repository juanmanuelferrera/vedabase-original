### CC Antya 4.161

> শুনি’ সনাতন পায়ে ধরি’ প্রভুরে কহিল  ।
> “জগদানন্দের সৌভাগ্য আজি সে জানিল ॥১৬১॥

śuni' sanātana pāye dhari' prabhure kahila

'jagadānandera saubhāgya āji se jānila

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *sanātana*—Sanātana Gosvāmī; *pāye dhari'*—segurando os pés; *prabhure kahila*—começou a dizer a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jagadānandera*—de Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; *saubhāgya*—boa fortuna; *āji*—agora; *se*—essa; *jānila*—eu compreendo.

**Enquanto Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu repreendia Jagadānanda Paṇḍita dessa maneira, Sanātana Gosvāmī caiu aos pés do Senhor e disse: "Agora posso compreender a posição afortunada de Jagadānanda.**
