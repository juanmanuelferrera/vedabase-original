### CC Antya 4.40

> ‘রঘুনাথের পাদপদ্মে বেচিয়াছোঁ মাথা  ।\
> কাড়িতে না পারোঁ মাথা, পাঙ বড় ব্যথা ॥৪০॥

'raghunāthera pāda-padme veciyāchoṅ māthā

kāḍite nā pāroṅ māthā, pāṅa baḍa vyathā

*raghu-nāthera*—do Senhor Rāmacandra; *pāda-padme*—aos pés de lótus; *veciyāchoṅ māthā*—vendi minha cabeça; *kāḍite*—retirar; *nā pāroṅ*—sou incapaz; *māthā*—a cabeça; *pāṅa*—sinto; *baḍa vyathā*—demasiada dor.

**" 'Vendi minha cabeça aos pés de lótus do Senhor Rāmacandra. Não posso retirá-la. Isso seria por demais doloroso para mim.**
