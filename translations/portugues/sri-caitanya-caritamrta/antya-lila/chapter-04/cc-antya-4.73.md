### CC Antya 4.73

> সর্বজ্ঞ মহাপ্রভু নিষেধিলা মোরে  ।
> প্রভুর চরণ ধরি’ কহেন তাঁহারে ॥৭৩॥

sarvajña mahāprabhu niṣedhilā more

prabhura caraṇa dhari' kahena tāṅhāre

*sarva-jña*—que tudo conhece; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *niṣedhilā*—proibiu; *more*—a mim; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *caraṇa*—os pés; *dhari'*—tocando; *kahena tāṅhāre*—começou a falar-Lhe.

**Sanātana Gosvāmī concluiu: "O Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, que tudo conhece — passado, presente e futuro —, proibiu-me de cometer suicídio." Então, prostrou-se, tocando os pés de lótus do Senhor, e falou-Lhe da seguinte maneira.**
