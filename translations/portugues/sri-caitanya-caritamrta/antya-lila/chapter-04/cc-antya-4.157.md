### CC Antya 4.157

> এত শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু সরোষ-অন্তরে ।
> জগদানন্দে ক্রুদ্ধ হঞা করে তিরস্কারে ॥১৫৭॥

eta śuni' mahāprabhu saroṣa-antare

jagadānande kruddha hañā kare tiraṣkāre

*eta śuni'*—ouvindo isto; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sa-roṣa-antare*—com disposição irada; *jagadānande*—a Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; *kruddha hañā*—ficando muito irado; *kare tiraḥ-kāre*—repreende.

**Ao ouvir isto, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, com disposição irada, começou a repreender Jagadānanda Paṇḍita.**
