### CC Antya 4.212

> যে-যে-লীলা প্রভু পথে কৈলা যে-যে-স্থানে ।
> তাহা দেখি’ প্রেমাবেশ হয় সনাতনে ॥২১২॥

ye-ye-līlā prabhu pathe kailā ye-ye-sthāne

tāhā dekhi' premāveśa haya sanātane

*ye-ye*—quaisquer; *līlā*—passatempos; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *pathe*—pelo caminho; *kailā*—realizou; *ye-ye-sthāne*—em quaisquer lugares; *tāhā*—aqueles lugares; *dekhi'*—ao ver; *prema-āveśa*—amor extático; *haya*—há; *sanātane*—em Sanātana Gosvāmī.

**Assim que Sanātana Gosvāmī visitava um lugar onde Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu havia realizado Seus passatempos pelo caminho, ele se enchia imediatamente de amor extático.**
