### CC Antya 4.114

> দোলযাত্রা-আদি প্রভুর সঙ্গেতে দেখিল ।\
> দিনে-দিনে প্রভু-সঙ্গে আনন্দ বাড়িল ॥১১৪॥

dola-yātrā-ādi prabhura saṅgete dekhila

dine-dine prabhu-saṅge ānanda bāḍila

*dola-yātrā*—o festival de Dola-yātrā; *ādi*—e outros; *prabhura saṅgete*—com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhila*—ele viu; *dine-dine*—dia após dia; *prabhu-saṅge*—na companhia de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ānanda bāḍila*—seu prazer aumentou.

**Sanātana Gosvāmī observou a cerimônia de Dola-yātrā com o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Dessa maneira, seu prazer aumentou na companhia do Senhor.**
