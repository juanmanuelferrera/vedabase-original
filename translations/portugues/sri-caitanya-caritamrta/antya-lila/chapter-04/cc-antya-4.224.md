### CC Antya 4.224

> ‘উজ্জ্বলনীলমণি’-নাম গ্রন্থ কৈল আর  ।
> রাধাকৃষ্ণ-লীলারস তাহাঁ পাইয়ে পার ॥২২৪॥

'ujjvala-nīlamaṇi'-nāma grantha kaila āra

rādhā-kṛṣṇa-līlā-rasa tāhāṅ pāiye pāra

*ujjvala-nīla-maṇi*—Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi; *nāma*—de nome; *grantha*—escritura; *kaila*—compilou; *āra*—também; *rādhā-kṛṣṇa-līlā-rasa*—o rasa transcendental dos passatempos de Rādhā e Kṛṣṇa; *tāhāṅ*—ali; *pāiye*—obtemos; *pāra*—o limite extremo.

**Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī compilou também o livro chamado Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi, a partir do qual se podem compreender, em sua plenitude, os afazeres amorosos de Śrī Śrī Rādhā e Kṛṣṇa.**
