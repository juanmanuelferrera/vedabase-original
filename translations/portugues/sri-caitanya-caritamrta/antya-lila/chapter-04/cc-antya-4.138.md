### CC Antya 4.138

> নিষেধিতে প্রভু আলিঙ্গন করেন মোরে ।\
> মোর কণ্ডুরসা লাগে প্রভুর শরীরে ॥১৩৮॥

niṣedhite prabhu āliṅgana karena more

mora kaṇḍu-rasā lāge prabhura śarīre

*niṣedhite*—embora eu proíba; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āliṅgana*—abraçar; *karena*—faz; *more*—a mim; *mora kaṇḍu-rasā*—minhas feridas úmidas; *lāge*—tocam; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śarīre*—no corpo.

**"Embora eu O proíba de fazê-lo, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu mesmo assim me abraça, e por isso Seu corpo fica manchado com as secreções de minhas feridas com coceira.**
