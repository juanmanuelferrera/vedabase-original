### CC Antya 4.156

> জগদানন্দ-পণ্ডিতে আমি যুক্তি পুছিল ।\
> বৃন্দাবন যাইতে তেঁহ উপদেশ দিল ।।” ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

jagadānanda-paṇḍite āmi yukti puchila

vṛndāvana yāite teṅha upadeśa dila"

*jagadānanda-paṇḍite*—a Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; *āmi*—eu; *yukti*—conselho; *puchila*—consultei; *vṛndāvana yāite*—ir a Vṛndāvana; *teṅha*—ele; *upadeśa dila*—deu a instrução.

**"Consultei Jagadānanda Paṇḍita para conhecer sua opinião, e ele também me aconselhou a retornar a Vṛndāvana."**
