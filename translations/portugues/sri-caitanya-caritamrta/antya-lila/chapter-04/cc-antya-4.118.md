### CC Antya 4.118

> মধ্যাহ্নে সমুদ্র-বালু হঞাছে অগ্নি-সম ।
> সেইপথে সনাতন করিলা গমন ॥১১৮॥

madhyāhne samudra-vālu hañāche agni-sama

sei-pathe sanātana karilā gamana

*madhya-ahne*—ao meio-dia; *samudra-vālu*—a areia à beira-mar; *hañāche*—estava; *agni-sama*—tão quente quanto fogo; *sei-pathe*—por aquele caminho; *sanātana*—Sanātana Gosvāmī; *karilā gamana*—veio.

**Ao meio-dia, a areia da praia estava tão quente quanto fogo, mas Sanātana Gosvāmī veio por aquele caminho.**
