### CC Antya 4.199

> প্রভু কহে, — সনাতন, না মানিহ দুঃখ ।
> তোমার আলিঙ্গনে আমি পাই বড় সুখ ॥১৯৯॥

prabhu kahe,--"sanātana, nā māniha duḥkha

tomāra āliṅgane āmi pāi baḍa sukha

*prabhu kahe*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu continuou a falar; *sanātana*—Meu querido Sanātana; *nā māniha duḥkha*—não fiques infeliz; *tomāra āliṅgane*—ao abraçar-te; *āmi*—Eu; *pāi*—obtenho; *baḍa sukha*—grande felicidade.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu prosseguiu: "Meu querido Sanātana, não fiques aflito, pois, quando te abraço, na verdade obtenho grande prazer.**
