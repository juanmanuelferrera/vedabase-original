### CC Antya 4.106

> রথ-অগ্রে প্রভু তৈছে করিলা নর্তন ।\
> দেখি চমৎকার হৈল সনাতনের মন ॥১০৬॥

ratha-agre prabhu taiche karilā nartana

dekhi camatkāra haila sanātanera mana

*ratha-agre*—diante da carruagem; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *taiche*—de modo semelhante; *karilā nartana*—dançou; *dekhi*—vendo; *camatkāra haila*—ficou assombrada; *sanātanera mana*—a mente de Sanātana.

**Durante o festival de Ratha-yātrā, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu voltou a dançar diante da carruagem de Jagannātha. Quando Sanātana Gosvāmī viu isso, sua mente ficou assombrada.**
