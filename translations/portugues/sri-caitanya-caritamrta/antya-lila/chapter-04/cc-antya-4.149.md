### CC Antya 4.149

sanātana bhāgi' pāche karena gamana

balātkāre dhari, prabhu kailā āliṅgana

*sanātana*—Sanātana Gosvāmī; *bhāgi'*—fugindo; *pāche*—para trás; *karena gamana*—vai; *balātkāre*—à força; *dhari*—agarrando; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kailā āliṅgana*—abraçou.

**Sanātana Gosvāmī recuou, mas Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu o agarrou à força e o abraçou.**
