### CC Antya 4.11

> জগন্নাথ রথযাত্রায় হইবেন বাহির ।
> তাঁর রথ-চাকায় ছাড়িমু এই শরীর ॥১১॥

jagannātha ratha-yātrāya ha-ibena bāhira
tāṅra ratha-cākāya chāḍimu ei śarīra

*jagannātha ratha-yātrāya*—na ocasião do festival do carro do Senhor Jagannātha; *ha-ibena bāhira*—Ele sairá; *tāṅra*—Dele; *ratha-cākāya*—sob a roda do carro; *chāḍimu*—abandonarei; *ei śarīra*—este corpo.

**"Durante o festival Ratha-yātrā, quando o Senhor Jagannātha sair do templo, abandonarei este corpo sob a roda de Seu carro.**
