### CC Antya 4.33

> আমা-সবা-সঙ্গে কৃষ্ণকথা, ভাগবত শুনে ।\
> তাহার পরীক্ষা কৈলুঁ আমি-দুইজনে ॥৩৩॥

āmā-sabā-saṅge kṛṣṇa-kathā, bhāgavata śune

tāhāra parīkṣā kailuṅ āmi-dui-jane

*āmā-sabā*—todos nós; *saṅge*—com; *kṛṣṇa-kathā*—conversas sobre o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *bhāgavata śune*—ouve o Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; *tāhāra*—dele; *parīkṣā*—exame; *kailuṅ*—fizemos; *āmi-dui-jane*—nós dois.

**"Ele ouvia o Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam e conversas sobre o Senhor Kṛṣṇa conosco, e nós dois o examinamos.**
