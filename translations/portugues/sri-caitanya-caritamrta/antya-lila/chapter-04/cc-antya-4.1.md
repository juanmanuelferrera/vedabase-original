### CC Antya 4.1

> বৃন্দাবনাৎ পুনঃ প্রাপ্তং শ্রীগৌরঃ শ্রীসনাতনম্ ।
> দেহপাতাদবন্ স্নেহাৎ শুদ্ধং চক্রে পরীক্ষয়া ॥১॥

vṛndāvanāt punaḥ prāptaṁ
śrī-gauraḥ śrī-sanātanam
deha-pātād avan snehāt
śuddhaṁ cakre parīkṣayā

*vṛndāvanāt*—de Vṛndāvana; *punaḥ*—novamente; *prāptam*—recebido; *śrī-gauraḥ*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śrī-sanātanam*—Śrī Sanātana Gosvāmī; *deha-pātāt*—de abandonar seu corpo; *avan*—protegendo; *snehāt*—por afeição; *śuddham*—puro; *cakre*—tornou; *parīkṣayā*—por meio de exame.

**Quando Sanātana Gosvāmī retornou de Vṛndāvana, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, afetuosamente, salvou-o de sua determinação de cometer suicídio. Então, após colocá-lo à prova, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu purificou seu corpo.**
