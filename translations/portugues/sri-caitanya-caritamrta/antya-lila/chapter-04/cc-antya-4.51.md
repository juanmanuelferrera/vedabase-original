### CC Antya 4.51

> এইমত সনাতন রহে প্রভু-স্থানে ।
> জগন্নাথের চক্র দেখি’ করেন প্রণামে ॥৫১॥

ei-mata sanātana rahe prabhu-sthāne

jagannāthera cakra dekhi' karena praṇāme

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *sanātana*—Sanātana Gosvāmī; *rahe*—permanece; *prabhu-sthāne*—sob os cuidados de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jagannāthera*—do Senhor Jagannātha; *cakra*—a roda no topo do templo; *dekhi'*—vendo; *karena praṇāme*—oferece respeitosas reverências.

**Dessa maneira, Sanātana Gosvāmī permaneceu sob os cuidados de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Ele via a roda no pináculo do templo de Jagannātha e oferecia respeitosas reverências.**
