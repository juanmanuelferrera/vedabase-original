### CC Antya 4.169

> তোমারে উপদেশ করে, না যায় সহন ।\
> অতএব তারে আমি করিয়ে ভর্ৎসন ॥১৬৯॥

tomāre upadeśa kare, nā yāya sahana

ataeva tāre āmi kariye bhartsana

*tomāre*—a ti; *upadeśa kare*—aconselha; *nā yāya sahana*—não posso tolerar; *ataeva*—portanto; *tāre*—a ele; *āmi*—Eu; *kariye*—faço; *bhartsana*—repreensão.

**"O fato de Jagā te aconselhar é-Me intolerável. Por isso, Eu o repreendo.**
