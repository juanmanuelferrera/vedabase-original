### CC Antya 4.148

> অপরাধ-ভয়ে তেঁহ মিলিতে না আইল ।\
> মহাপ্রভু মিলিবারে সেই ঠাঞি গেল ॥১৪৮॥

aparādha-bhaye teṅha milite nā āila

mahāprabhu milibāre sei ṭhāñi gela

*aparādha-bhaye*—por temor de cometer ofensas; *teṅha*—Sanātana Gosvāmī; *milite*—para encontrar; *nā āila*—não se adiantou; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *milibāre*—para encontrar; *sei ṭhāñi*—a Sanātana Gosvāmī; *gela*—foi.

**Por temor de cometer ofensas, Sanātana Gosvāmī não se adiantou para encontrar Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. O Senhor, contudo, adiantou-se para encontrá-lo.**
