### CC Antya 4.7

> জগন্নাথে গেলে তাঁর দর্শন না পাইমু  ।\
> প্রভুর দর্শন সদা করিতে নারিমু ॥৭॥

jagannāthe gele tāṅra darśana nā pāimu
prabhura darśana sadā karite nārimu

*jagannāthe*—a Jagannātha Purī; *gele*—quando eu for; *tāṅra*—Sua; *darśana*—visita; *nā pāimu*—não conseguirei; *prabhura darśana*—ver o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sadā*—sempre; *karite*—fazer; *nārimu*—não serei capaz.

**"Quando eu for a Jagannātha Purī, não conseguirei ver o Senhor Jagannātha, nem poderei sempre ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
