### CC Antya 4.18

> হরিদাস কহে, — ‘সনাতন করে নমস্কার’ ।
> সনাতনে দেখি’ প্রভু হৈলা চমৎকার ॥১৮॥

haridāsa kahe,--'sanātana kare namaskāra'

sanātane dekhi' prabhu hailā camatkāra

*haridāsa kahe*—Haridāsa disse; *sanātana*—Sanātana Gosvāmī; *kare namaskāra*—está oferecendo suas reverências; *sanātane dekhi'*—vendo Sanātana Gosvāmī; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *hailā camatkāra*—ficou muito surpreso.

**Haridāsa Ṭhākura disse a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu: "Eis aqui Sanātana Gosvāmī, oferecendo suas reverências." Ao ver Sanātana Gosvāmī, o Senhor ficou grandemente surpreso.**
