### CC Antya 4.21

> বলাৎকারে প্রভু তাঁরে আলিঙ্গন কৈল ।
> কণ্ডুক্লেদ মহাপ্রভুর শ্রীঅঙ্গে লাগিল ॥২১॥

balātkāre prabhu tāṅre āliṅgana kaila

kaṇḍu-kleda mahāprabhura śrī-aṅge lāgila

*balātkāre*—à força; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *āliṅgana kaila*—abraçou; *kaṇḍu-kleda*—a umidade das feridas pruriginosas; *mahāprabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śrī*—transcendental; *aṅge*—corpo; *lāgila*—tocou.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, contudo, abraçou Sanātana Gosvāmī à força. Assim, a umidade que exsudava das feridas pruriginosas tocou o corpo transcendental de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
