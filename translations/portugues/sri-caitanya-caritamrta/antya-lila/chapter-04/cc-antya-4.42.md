### CC Antya 4.42

> রঘুনাথের পাদপদ্ম ছাড়ান না যায়  ।\
> ছাড়িবার মন হৈলে প্রাণ ফাটি’ যায় ।।’ ৪২ ॥৪২॥

raghunāthera pāda-padma chāḍāna nā yāya

chāḍibāra mana haile prāṇa phāṭi' yāya'

*raghu-nāthera*—do Senhor Raghunātha; *pāda-padma*—pés de lótus; *chāḍāna nā yāya*—é impossível abandonar; *chāḍibāra*—de abandonar; *mana haile*—quando penso; *prāṇa*—meu coração; *phāṭi' yāya*—parte-se.

**" 'É impossível para mim abandonar os pés de lótus do Senhor Raghunātha. Quando sequer penso em abandoná-los, meu coração se parte.'**
