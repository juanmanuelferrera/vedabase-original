### CC Antya 4.237

> এই ত’ কহিলুঁ পুনঃ সনাতন-সঙ্গমে ।
> প্রভুর আশয় জানি যাহার শ্রবণে ॥২৩৭॥

ei ta' kahiluṅ punaḥ sanātana-saṅgame

prabhura āśaya jāni yāhāra śravaṇe

*ei ta' kahiluṅ*—assim, descrevi; *punaḥ*—novamente; *sanātana-saṅgame*—o encontro com Sanātana Gosvāmī; *prabhura āśaya*—o desejo do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jāni*—posso compreender; *yāhāra śravaṇe*—ao ouvir o qual.

**Assim, descrevi o novo encontro do Senhor com Sanātana Gosvāmī. Ao ouvir isto, posso compreender o desejo do Senhor.**
