### CC Antya 4.53

> দিব্য প্রসাদ পায় নিত্য জগন্নাথ-মন্দিরে ।
> তাহা আনি’ নিত্য অবশ্য দেন দোঁহাকারে ॥৫৩॥

divya prasāda pāya nitya jagannātha-mandire

tāhā āni' nitya avaśya dena doṅhākāre

*divya*—de primeira classe; *prasāda*—prasāda; *pāya*—obtém; *nitya*—diariamente; *jagannātha-mandire*—no templo do Senhor Jagannātha; *tāhā āni'*—trazendo isso; *nitya*—diariamente; *avaśya*—certamente; *dena*—entrega; *doṅhākāre*—a ambos.

**As oferendas de prasāda no templo do Senhor Jagannātha eram da mais alta qualidade. Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu trazia esse prasāda e entregava-o a ambos os devotos.**
