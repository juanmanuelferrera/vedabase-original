### CC Antya 4.92

> তবে মহাপ্রভু করি’ দুঁহারে আলিঙ্গন ।
> ‘মধ্যাহ্ন’ করিতে উঠি’ করিলা গমন ॥৯২॥

tabe mahāprabhu kari' duṅhāre āliṅgana

'madhyāhna' karite uṭhi' karilā gamana

*tabe*—então; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kari' duṅhāre āliṅgana*—abraçando ambos; *madhya-ahna karite*—para cumprir Seus deveres do meio-dia; *uṭhi'*—levantando-se; *karilā gamana*—partiu.

**Assim, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu abraçou tanto Haridāsa Ṭhākura quanto Sanātana Gosvāmī e, em seguida, levantou-se e partiu para cumprir Seus deveres do meio-dia.**
