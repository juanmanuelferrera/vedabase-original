### CC Antya 4.50

> এত বলি’ মহাপ্রভু উঠিয়া চলিলা ।\
> গোবিন্দ-দ্বারায় দুঁহে প্রসাদ পাঠাইলা ॥৫০॥

eta bali' mahāprabhu uṭhiyā calilā

govinda-dvārāya duṅhe prasāda pāṭhāilā

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *uṭhiyā calilā*—levantou-se e partiu; *govinda-dvārāya*—por intermédio de Govinda; *duṅhe*—a ambos; *prasāda pāṭhāilā*—enviou prasāda.

**Tendo dito isto, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu levantou-se e partiu, e, por intermédio de Govinda, enviou-lhes prasāda para comerem.**
