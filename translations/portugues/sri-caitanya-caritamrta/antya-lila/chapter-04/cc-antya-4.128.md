### CC Antya 4.128

> শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু মনে সন্তোষ পাইলা  ।
> তুষ্ট হঞা তাঁরে কিছু কহিতে লাগিলা ॥১২৮॥

śuni' mahāprabhu mane santoṣa pāilā

tuṣṭa hañā tāṅre kichu kahite lāgilā

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mane*—na mente; *santoṣa pāilā*—ficou muito satisfeito; *tuṣṭa hañā*—estando contente; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kichu*—algo; *kahite lāgilā*—começou a falar.

**Tendo ouvido todos esses detalhes, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, grandemente satisfeito, falou da seguinte maneira.**
