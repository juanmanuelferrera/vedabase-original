### CC Antya 4.133

> এত বলি’ প্রভু তাঁরে আলিঙ্গন কৈল ।
> তাঁর কণ্ডুরসা প্রভুর শ্রীঅঙ্গে লাগিল ॥১৩৩॥

eta bali' prabhu tāṅre āliṅgana kaila

tāṅra kaṇḍu-rasā prabhura śrī-aṅge lāgila

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *āliṅgana kaila*—abraçou; *tāṅra*—dele; *kaṇḍu-rasā*—a secreção que escorria das feridas da coceira; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śrī-aṅge lāgila*—untou o corpo.

**Depois de dizer isto, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu abraçou Sanātana Gosvāmī, e a secreção que escorria das feridas pruriginosas no corpo de Sanātana untou o corpo do Senhor.**
