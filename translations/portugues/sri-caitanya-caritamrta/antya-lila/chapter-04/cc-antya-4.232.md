### CC Antya 4.232

> জীব-গোসাঞি গৌড় হৈতে মথুরা চলিলা ।
> নিত্যানন্দপ্রভু-ঠাঞি আজ্ঞা মাগিলা ॥২৩২॥

jīva-gosāñi gauḍa haite mathurā calilā

nityānanda-prabhu-ṭhāñi ājñā māgilā

*jīva-gosāñi*—Śrīpāda Jīva Gosvāmī; *gauḍa haite*—de Bengala; *mathurā calilā*—partiu para Mathurā; *nityānanda-prabhu-ṭhāñi*—a Śrīla Nityānanda Prabhu; *ājñā māgilā*—pediu permissão.

**Quando Jīva Gosvāmī quis ir de Bengala a Mathurā, ele pediu permissão a Śrīla Nityānanda Prabhu.**
