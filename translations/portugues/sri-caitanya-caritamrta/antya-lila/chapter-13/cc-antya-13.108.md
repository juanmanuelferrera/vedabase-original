### CC Antya 13.108

> পরম সন্তোষে প্রভু করেন ভোজন ।
> প্রভুর অবশিষ্ট-পাত্র ভট্টের ভক্ষণ ॥১০৮॥

parama santoṣe prabhu karena bhojana

prabhura avaśiṣṭa-pātra bhaṭṭera bhakṣaṇa

*parama santoṣe*—com grande satisfação; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *karena bhojana*—come; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *avaśiṣṭa-pātra*—o prato com os restos; *bhaṭṭera*—de Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa; *bhakṣaṇa*—o alimento.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu aceitava com grande satisfação todo o alimento que ele preparava. Depois que o Senhor ficava satisfeito, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa comia Seus restos.**
