### CC Antya 13.22

> ভিতরের ক্রোধ-দুঃখ প্রকাশ না কৈল ।\
> মথুরা যাইতে প্রভু-স্থানে আজ্ঞা মাগিল ॥২২॥

bhitarera krodha-duḥkha prakāśa nā kaila

mathurā yāite prabhu-sthāne ājñā māgila

*bhitarera*—interna; *krodha-duḥkha*—ira e infelicidade; *prakāśa nā kaila*—não revelou; *mathurā yāite*—de ir a Mathurā; *prabhu-sthāne*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ājñā māgila*—pediu permissão.

**Ora, ocultando sua ira e infelicidade, Jagadānanda Paṇḍita novamente pediu a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu permissão para ir a Mathurā.**
