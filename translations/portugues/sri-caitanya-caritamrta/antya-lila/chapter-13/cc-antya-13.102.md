### CC Antya 13.102

> মিশ্র আর শেখরের দণ্ডবৎ জানাইলা ।\
> মহাপ্রভু তাঁ-সবার বার্তা পুছিলা ॥১০২॥

miśra āra śekharera daṇḍavat jānāilā

mahāprabhu tāṅ-sabāra vārtā puchilā

*miśra*—de Tapana Miśra; *āra*—e; *śekharera*—de Candraśekhara; *daṇḍavat*—reverências; *jānāilā*—ele informou; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅ-sabāra*—de todos eles; *vārtā*—notícias; *puchilā*—perguntou.

**Raghunātha ofereceu respeitosas reverências a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu em nome de Tapana Miśra e Candraśekhara, e o Senhor também perguntou a respeito deles.**
