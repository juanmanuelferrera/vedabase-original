### CC Antya 13.106

> মধ্যে মধ্যে মহাপ্রভুর করেন নিমন্ত্রণ ।
> ঘর-ভাত করেন, আর বিবিধ ব্যঞ্জন ॥১০৬॥

madhye madhye mahāprabhura karena nimantraṇa

ghara-bhāta karena, āra vividha vyañjana

*madhye madhye*—de quando em quando; *mahāprabhura*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *karena nimantraṇa*—ele fazia convites; *ghara-bhāta karena*—ele cozinhava arroz em casa; *āra*—e; *vividha vyañjana*—vários tipos de hortaliças.

**De quando em quando, ele cozinhava arroz com vários tipos de hortaliças e convidava Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu para sua casa.**
