### CC Antya 13.109

> রামদাস যদি প্রথম প্রভুরে মিলিলা ।\
> মহাপ্রভু অধিক তাঁরে কৃপা না করিলা ॥১০৯॥

rāmadāsa yadi prathama prabhure mililā

mahāprabhu adhika tāṅre kṛpā nā karilā

*rāmadāsa*—o devoto Rāmadāsa Viśvāsa; *yadi*—quando; *prathama*—pela primeira vez; *prabhure mililā*—encontrou-se com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *adhika*—muita; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kṛpā*—misericórdia; *nā karilā*—não demonstrou.

**Quando Rāmadāsa Viśvāsa encontrou Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, o Senhor não lhe demonstrou nenhuma misericórdia especial, embora aquele fosse o primeiro encontro entre eles.**
