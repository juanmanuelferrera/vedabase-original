### CC Antya 13.3

> হেনমতে মহাপ্রভু জগদানন্দ-সঙ্গে ।
> নানামতে আস্বাদয় প্রেমের তরঙ্গে ॥৩॥

hena-mate mahāprabhu jagadānanda-saṅge
nānā-mate āsvādaya premera taraṅge

*hena-mate*—dessa maneira; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jagadānanda-saṅge*—na companhia de Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; *nānā-mate*—de várias maneiras; *āsvādaya*—saboreia; *premera taraṅge*—as ondas dos afetos amorosos espirituais.

**Na companhia de Jagadānanda Paṇḍita, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu saboreava diversas relações transcendentais de amor puro.**
