### CC Antya 13.14

> প্রভু কহেন, — “খাট এক আনহ পাড়িতে ।
> জগদানন্দ চাহে আমায় বিষয় ভুঞ্জাইতে ॥১৪॥

prabhu kahena,--"khāṭa eka ānaha pāḍite
jagadānanda cāhe āmāya viṣaya bhuñjāite

*prabhu kahena*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse; *khāṭa*—cama; *eka*—uma; *ānaha*—traze; *pāḍite*—para deitar; *jagadānanda*—Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; *cāhe*—quer; *āmāya*—a Mim; *viṣaya bhuñjāite*—fazer desfrutar de felicidade material.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu respondeu: "Deves trazer uma cama aqui para que Eu Me deite. Jagadānanda quer que Eu desfrute de felicidade material.**
