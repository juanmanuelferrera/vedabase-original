### CC Antya 13.94

> রঘুনাথ-ভট্টের সনে পথেতে মিলিলা ।
> ভট্টের ঝালি মাথে করি’ বহিয়া চলিলা ॥৯৪॥

raghunātha-bhaṭṭera sane pathete mililā

bhaṭṭera jhāli māthe kari' vahiyā calilā

*raghunātha-bhaṭṭera*—Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa; *sane*—com; *pathete*—no caminho; *mililā*—ele encontrou; *bhaṭṭera*—de Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa; *jhāli*—a bagagem; *māthe kari'*—tomando sobre a cabeça; *vahiyā calilā*—carregou.

**Quando encontrou Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa no caminho, ele tomou a bagagem de Raghunātha sobre a cabeça e a carregou.**
