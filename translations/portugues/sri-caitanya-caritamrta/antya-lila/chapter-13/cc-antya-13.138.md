### CC Antya 13.138

> যে এইসকল কথা শুনে শ্রদ্ধা করি’ ।\
> তাঁরে কৃষ্ণপ্রেমধন দেন গৌরহরি ॥১৩৮॥

ye ei-sakala kathā śune śraddhā kari'

tāṅre kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana dena gaurahari

*ye*—aquele que; *ei-sakala*—todos estes; *kathā*—tópicos; *śune*—ouve; *śraddhā kari'*—com fé e amor; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana*—a riqueza do amor extático ao Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *dena*—concede; *gaurahari*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu [Gaurahari] concede o amor extático a Kṛṣṇa a todo aquele que ouvir todos estes tópicos com fé e amor.**
