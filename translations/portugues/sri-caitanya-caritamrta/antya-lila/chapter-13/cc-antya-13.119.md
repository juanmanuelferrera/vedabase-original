### CC Antya 13.119

> পূর্ববৎ অষ্টমাস প্রভু-পাশ ছিলা  ।\
> অষ্টমাস রহি’ পুনঃ প্রভু আজ্ঞা দিলা ॥১১৯॥

pūrvavat aṣṭa-māsa prabhu-pāśa chilā

aṣṭa-māsa rahi' punaḥ prabhu ājñā dilā

*pūrva-vat*—como anteriormente; *aṣṭa-māsa*—por oito meses; *prabhu-pāśa chilā*—permaneceu com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *aṣṭa-māsa rahi'*—após permanecer por oito meses; *punaḥ*—de novo; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ājñā dilā*—ordenou-lhe.

**Como anteriormente, Raghunātha permaneceu continuamente com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu durante oito meses. Então, o Senhor deu-lhe a seguinte ordem.**
