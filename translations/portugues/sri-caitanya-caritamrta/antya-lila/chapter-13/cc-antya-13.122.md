### CC Antya 13.122

> এত বলি’ প্রভু তাঁরে আলিঙ্গন কৈলা ।\
> প্রভুর কৃপাতে কৃষ্ণপ্রেমে মত্ত হৈলা ॥১২২॥

eta bali' prabhu tāṅre āliṅgana kailā

prabhura kṛpāte kṛṣṇa-preme matta hailā

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa; *āliṅgana kailā*—abraçou; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kṛpāte*—pela misericórdia; *kṛṣṇa-preme*—em amor a Kṛṣṇa; *matta hailā*—tornou-se animado.

**Após dizer isso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu abraçou Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa e, pela misericórdia do Senhor, Raghunātha ficou animado com amor extático por Kṛṣṇa.**
