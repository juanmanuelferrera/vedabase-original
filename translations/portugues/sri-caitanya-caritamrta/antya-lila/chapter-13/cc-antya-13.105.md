### CC Antya 13.105

> এইমত প্রভু-সঙ্গে রহিলা অষ্টমাস ।
> দিনে দিনে প্রভুর কৃপায় বাড়য়ে উল্লাস ॥১০৫॥

ei-mata prabhu-saṅge rahilā aṣṭa-māsa

dine dine prabhura kṛpāya bāḍaye ullāsa

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *prabhu-saṅge*—junto com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rahilā*—permaneceu; *aṣṭa-māsa*—oito meses; *dine dine*—dia após dia; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kṛpāya*—pela misericórdia; *bāḍaye ullāsa*—sentia júbilo crescente.

**Assim, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa viveu continuamente com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu por oito meses e, pela misericórdia do Senhor, sentia felicidade transcendental crescente a cada dia.**
