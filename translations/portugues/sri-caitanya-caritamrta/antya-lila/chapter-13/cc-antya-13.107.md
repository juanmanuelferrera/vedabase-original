### CC Antya 13.107

> রঘুনাথ-ভট্ট — পাকে অতি সুনিপুণ ।\
> যেই রান্ধে, সেই হয় অমৃতের সম ॥১০৭॥

raghunātha-bhaṭṭa--pāke ati sunipuṇa

yei rāndhe, sei haya amṛtera sama

*raghunātha-bhaṭṭa*—Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa; *pāke*—em culinária; *ati su-nipuṇa*—muito perito; *yei rāndhe*—tudo o que cozinhava; *sei*—isso; *haya*—era; *amṛtera sama*—como néctar.

**Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa era um cozinheiro perito. Tudo o que preparava tinha sabor exatamente igual ao néctar.**
