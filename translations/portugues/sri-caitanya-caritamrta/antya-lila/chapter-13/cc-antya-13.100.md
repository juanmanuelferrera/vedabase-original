### CC Antya 13.100

> এইমতে রঘুনাথ আইলা নীলাচলে ।
> প্রভুর চরণে যাঞা মিলিলা কুতূহলে ॥১০০॥

ei-mate raghunātha āilā nīlācale

prabhura caraṇe yāñā mililā kutūhale

*ei-mate*—dessa maneira; *raghunātha*—Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa; *āilā*—chegou; *nīlācale*—a Jagannātha Purī; *prabhura caraṇe*—aos pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yāñā*—indo; *mililā*—encontrou-se; *kutūhale*—com grande deleite.

**Viajando dessa maneira, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa logo chegou a Jagannātha Purī. Ali, com grande deleite, ele se encontrou com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e caiu a Seus pés de lótus.**
