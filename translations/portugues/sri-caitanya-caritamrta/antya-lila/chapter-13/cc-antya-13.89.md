### CC Antya 13.89

> এথা তপনমিশ্র-পুত্র রঘুনাথ-ভট্টাচার্য ।\
> প্রভুরে দেখিতে চলিলা ছাড়ি’ সর্ব কার্য ॥৮৯॥

ethā tapana-miśra-putra raghunātha-bhaṭṭācārya

prabhure dekhite calilā chāḍi' sarva kārya

*ethā*—por outro lado; *tapana-miśra-putra*—o filho de Tapana Miśra; *raghunātha-bhaṭṭācārya*—Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa; *prabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhite*—para encontrar; *calilā*—pôs-se a caminho; *chāḍi'*—abandonando; *sarva kārya*—todos os deveres.

**Nesse ínterim, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭācārya, o filho de Tapana Miśra, abandonou todos os seus deveres e deixou seu lar, com a intenção de encontrar Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
