### CC Antya 1.60

> ইষ্টগোষ্ঠী দুঁহা সনে করি’ কতক্ষণ ।
> মধ্যাহ্ন করিতে প্রভু করিলা গমন ॥৬০॥

iṣṭa-goṣṭhī duṅhā sane kari' kata-kṣaṇa

madhyāhna karite prabhu karilā gamana

*iṣṭa-goṣṭhī*—conversa; *duṅhā sane*—com ambos, Rūpa Gosvāmī e Haridāsa; *kari'*—fazendo; *kata-kṣaṇa*—por algum tempo; *madhya-ahna karite*—para executar os deveres diários do meio-dia; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *karilā gamana*—deixou aquele lugar.

**Ele conversava por algum tempo com ambos e depois partia para realizar Seus deveres do meio-dia.**
