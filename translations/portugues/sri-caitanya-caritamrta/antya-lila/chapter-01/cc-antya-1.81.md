### CC Antya 1.81

> হেনকালে প্রভু আইলা তাঁহারে মিলিতে ।\
> চালে শ্লোক দেখি প্রভু লাগিলা পড়িতে ॥৮১॥

hena-kāle prabhu āilā tāṅhāre milite

cāle śloka dekhi prabhu lāgilā paḍite

*hena-kāle*—naquele momento; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āilā*—chegou ali; *tāṅhāre milite*—para encontrá-lo; *cāle*—no telhado de palha; *śloka*—verso; *dekhi*—vendo; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *lāgilā*—começou; *paḍite*—a ler.

**Naquele momento, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu foi ali para encontrá-lo e, ao ver a folha enfiada no telhado e ao ver o verso, começou a lê-lo.**
