### CC Antya 1.23

> চাহিয়া না পাইল কুক্কুর, লোক সব আইলা ।\
> দুঃখী হঞা শিবানন্দ উপবাস কৈলা ॥২৩॥

cāhiyā nā pāila kukkura, loka saba āilā

duḥkhī hañā śivānanda upavāsa kailā

*cāhiyā*—procurando; *nā*—não; *pāila*—encontraram; *kukkura*—o cão; *loka saba āilā*—todos os homens regressaram; *duḥkhī hañā*—estando infeliz; *śivānanda*—Śivānanda Sena; *upavāsa*—jejum; *kailā*—observou.

**Quando os homens regressaram sem êxito, Śivānanda Sena ficou muito infeliz e jejuou durante a noite.**
