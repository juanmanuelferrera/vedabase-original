### CC Antya 1.69

> “পৃথক্ নাটক করিতে সত্যভামা আজ্ঞা দিল ।\
> জানিলু, পৃথক্ নাটক করিতে প্রভু-আজ্ঞা হৈল ॥৬৯॥

"pṛthak nāṭaka karite satyabhāmā ājñā dila

jānilu, pṛthak nāṭaka karite prabhu-ājñā haila

*pṛthak nāṭaka*—dramas diferentes; *karite*—para escrever; *satyabhāmā*—de nome Satyabhāmā; *ājñā dila*—ordenou; *jānilu*—agora compreendo; *pṛthak nāṭaka*—dramas diferentes; *karite*—para escrever; *prabhu-ājñā*—a ordem do Senhor; *haila*—houve.

**"Satyabhāmā ordenou-me que escrevesse dois dramas distintos", pensou Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī. "Agora compreendo que esta ordem foi confirmada por Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
