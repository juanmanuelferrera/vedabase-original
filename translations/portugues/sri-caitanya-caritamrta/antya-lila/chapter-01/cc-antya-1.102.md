### CC Antya 1.102

> তবে মহাপ্রভু দুঁহে করি’ আলিঙ্গন ।\
> মধ্যাহ্ন করিতে সমুদ্রে করিলা গমন ॥১০২॥

tabe mahāprabhu duṅhe kari' āliṅgana

madhyāhna karite samudre karilā gamana

*tabe*—então; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *duṅhe*—a ambos, Rūpa Gosvāmī e Haridāsa Ṭhākura; *kari'*—fazendo; *āliṅgana*—abraço; *madhya-ahna karite*—para cumprir Seus deveres do meio-dia; *samudre*—à beira-mar; *karilā gamana*—foi.

**Assim, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu abraçou tanto Haridāsa quanto Rūpa Gosvāmī e partiu para a beira-mar a fim de cumprir Seus deveres do meio-dia.**
