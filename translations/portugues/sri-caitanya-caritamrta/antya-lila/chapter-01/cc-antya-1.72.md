### CC Antya 1.72

> রথযাত্রায় জগন্নাথ দর্শন করিলা ।
> রথ-অগ্রে প্রভুর নৃত্য-কীর্তন দেখিলা ॥৭২॥

ratha-yātrāya jagannātha darśana karilā

ratha-agre prabhura nṛtya-kīrtana dekhilā

*ratha-yātrāya*—durante a função do Ratha-yātrā; *jagannātha*—o Senhor Jagannātha; *darśana karilā*—ele viu; *ratha-agre*—à frente do ratha, ou quadriga; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nṛtya*—a dança; *kīrtana*—o canto; *dekhilā*—ele viu.

**Durante a cerimônia do Ratha-yātrā, Rūpa Gosvāmī viu o Senhor Jagannātha. Ele também viu o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu dançando e cantando à frente do ratha.**
