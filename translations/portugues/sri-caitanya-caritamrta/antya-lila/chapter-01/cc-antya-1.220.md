### CC Antya 1.220

> এত বলি’ প্রভু তাঁরে কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ।
> রূপ গোসাঞি শিরে ধরে প্রভুর চরণ ॥২২০॥

eta bali' prabhu tāṅre kailā āliṅgana

rūpa gosāñi śire dhare prabhura caraṇa

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a Rūpa Gosvāmī; *kailā āliṅgana*—abraçou; *rūpa gosāñi*—Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī; *śire*—sobre a cabeça; *dhare*—coloca; *prabhura caraṇa*—os pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Tendo assim falado, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu abraçou Rūpa Gosvāmī, que então colocou os pés de lótus do Senhor sobre sua cabeça.**
