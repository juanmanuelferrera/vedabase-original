### CC Antya 1.74

> পূর্বে সেই সব কথা করিয়াছি বর্ণন ।\
> তথাপি কহিয়ে কিছু সংক্ষেপে কথন ॥৭৪॥

pūrve sei saba kathā kariyāchi varṇana

tathāpi kahiye kichu saṅkṣepe kathana

*pūrve*—anteriormente; *sei*—estes; *saba*—todos; *kathā*—assuntos; *kariyāchi varṇana*—descrevi; *tathāpi*—ainda assim; *kahiye*—permita-me dizer; *kichu*—algo; *saṅkṣepe*—resumidamente; *kathana*—relato.

**Já descrevi todos esses incidentes, mas ainda desejo acrescentar brevemente algo mais.**
