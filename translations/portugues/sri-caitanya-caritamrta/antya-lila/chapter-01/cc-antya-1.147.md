### CC Antya 1.147

> রায় কহে, — “কহ দেখি ভাবের স্বভাব ?”\
> রূপ কহে, — “ঐছে হয় কৃষ্ণবিষয়ক ‘ভাব’ ।।” ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

rāya kahe,—"kaha dekhi bhāvera svabhāva?"

rūpa kahe,—"aiche haya kṛṣṇa-viṣayaka 'bhāva'"

*rāya kahe*—Rāmānanda Rāya diz; *kaha*—recita, por favor; *dekhi*—para que eu possa ver; *bhāvera svabhāva*—a característica do amor emocional; *rūpa kahe*—Rūpa Gosvāmī responde; *aiche*—tal; *haya*—é; *kṛṣṇa-viṣayaka*—referente a Kṛṣṇa; *bhāva*—amor emocional.

**Rāmānanda Rāya perguntou: "Quais são as características do amor emocional?" Rūpa Gosvāmī respondeu: "Esta é a natureza do amor emocional por Kṛṣṇa.**
