### CC Antya 1.29

> প্রসাদ নারিকেল-শস্য দেন ফেলাঞা ।
> ‘রাম’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরি’ কহ’ — বলেন হাসিয়া ॥২৯॥

prasāda nārikela-śasya dena phelāñā

'rāma' 'kṛṣṇa' 'hari' kaha'--balena hāsiyā

*prasāda*—alimento; *nārikela-śasya*—polpa de coco verde; *dena*—dá; *phelāñā*—jogando; *rāma*—o Senhor Rāmacandra; *kṛṣṇa*—Śrī Kṛṣṇa; *hari*—o santo nome de Hari; *kaha*—dize; *balena*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu diz; *hāsiyā*—sorrindo.

**Além disso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu jogava restos de polpa de coco verde ao cão. Sorrindo à Sua própria maneira, Ele dizia ao cão: "Canta os santos nomes Rāma, Kṛṣṇa e Hari."**
