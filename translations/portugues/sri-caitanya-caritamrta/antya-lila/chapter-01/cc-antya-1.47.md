### CC Antya 1.47

> ‘উপল-ভোগ’ দেখি’ হরিদাসেরে দেখিতে ।
> প্রতিদিন আইসেন, প্রভু আইলা আচম্বিতে ॥৪৭॥

'upala-bhoga' dekhi' haridāsere dekhite

pratidina āisena, prabhu āilā ācambite

*upala-bhoga*—a oferenda de alimento ao Senhor Jagannātha ao meio-dia; *dekhi'*—vendo; *hari-dāsere dekhite*—para ver Haridāsa Ṭhākura; *pratidina*—diariamente; *āisena*—vem; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āilā*—chegou lá; *ācambite*—de repente.

**Após ver a cerimônia de upala-bhoga no templo de Jagannātha, o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu regularmente vinha ver Haridāsa todos os dias. Assim, Ele chegou ali de repente.**
