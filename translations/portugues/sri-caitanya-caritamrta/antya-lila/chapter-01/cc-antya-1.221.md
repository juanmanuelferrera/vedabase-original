### CC Antya 1.221

> প্রভুর ভক্তগণ-পাশে বিদায় লইলা ।।
> পুনরপি গৌড়-পথে বৃন্দাবনে আইলা ॥২২১॥

prabhura bhakta-gaṇa-pāśe vidāya la-ilā

punarapi gauḍa-pathe vṛndāvane āilā

*prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhakta-gaṇa-pāśe*—dos devotos; *vidāya la-ilā*—despediu-se; *punarapi*—novamente; *gauḍa-pathe*—pelo caminho de Bengala; *vṛndāvane*—para Vṛndāvana; *āilā*—regressou.

**Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī despediu-se de todos os devotos de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e regressou a Vṛndāvana pelo caminho de Bengala.**
