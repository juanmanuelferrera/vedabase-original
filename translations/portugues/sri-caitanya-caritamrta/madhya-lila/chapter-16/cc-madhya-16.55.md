### CC Madhya 16.55

> আচার্য-গোসাঞি প্ৰভুর কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ।\
> তার মধ্যে কৈল যৈছে ঝড়-বরিষণ ॥৫৫॥

ācārya-gosāñi prabhura kaila nimantraṇa

tāra madhye kaila yaiche jhaḍa-variṣaṇa

*ācārya-gosāñi*—Advaita Ācārya; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kaila*—fez; *nimantraṇa*—convite; *tāra madhye*—dentro desse episódio; *kaila*—ocorreu; *yaiche*—assim como; *jhaḍa-variṣaṇa*—tempestade.

**Advaita Ācārya então estendeu um convite a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, e houve uma grande tempestade ligada a esse incidente.**
