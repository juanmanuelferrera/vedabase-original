### CC Madhya 16.100

> ‘কটকে’ আসিয়া কৈল ‘গোপাল’ দরশন ।\
> স্বপ্নেশ্বর-বিপ্র কৈল প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণ ॥১০০॥

'kaṭake' āsiyā kaila 'gopāla' daraśana

svapneśvara-vipra kaila prabhura nimantraṇa

*kaṭake*—à cidade de Kaṭaka; *āsiyā*—chegando; *kaila*—fez; *gopāla daraśana*—ver o Senhor Gopāla; *svapneśvara-vipra*—o brāhmaṇa chamado Svapneśvara; *kaila*—fez; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nimantraṇa*—convite.

**Após chegar à cidade de Kaṭaka, Ele visitou o templo de Gopāla, e um brāhmaṇa de lá chamado Svapneśvara convidou o Senhor para comer.**
