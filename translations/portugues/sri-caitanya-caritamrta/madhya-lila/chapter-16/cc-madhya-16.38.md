### CC Madhya 16.38

> আঠারনালাকে আইলা গোসাঞি শুনিয়া ।\
> দুইমালা পাঠাইলা গোবিন্দ-হাতে দিয়া ॥৩৮॥

āṭhāranālāke āilā gosāñi śuniyā

dui-mālā pāṭhāilā govinda-hāte diyā

*āṭhāranālāke*—Āṭhāranālā; *āilā*—eles chegaram; *gosāñi*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śuniyā*—ao ouvir; *dui-mālā*—duas guirlandas; *pāṭhāilā*—enviou; *govinda-hāte diyā*—pelas mãos de Govinda.

**Quando todos chegaram a uma ponte chamada Āṭhāranālā, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, ao ouvir a notícia de sua chegada, enviou duas guirlandas com Govinda.**
