### CC Madhya 16.51

> রাঢ়ী এক বিপ্র, তেঁহো — নিত্যানন্দ দাস  ।
> মহা-ভাগ্যবান্ তেঁহো, নাম — কৃষ্ণদাস ॥৫১॥

rāḍhī eka vipra, teṅho--nityānanda dāsa

mahā-bhāgyavān teṅho, nāma--kṛṣṇadāsa

*rāḍhī eka vipra*—um brāhmaṇa residente de Rāḍha-deśa (onde o Ganges não corre); *teṅho*—ele; *nityānanda dāsa*—servo do Senhor Nityānanda; *mahā-bhāgyavān*—imensamente afortunado; *teṅho*—ele; *nāma*—chamado; *kṛṣṇadāsa*—Kṛṣṇadāsa.

**Certo brāhmaṇa chamado Kṛṣṇadāsa, que era residente de Rāḍha-deśa e servo do Senhor Nityānanda, era uma pessoa muito afortunada.**
