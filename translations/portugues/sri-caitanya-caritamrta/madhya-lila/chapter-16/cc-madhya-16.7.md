### CC Madhya 16.7

> রামানন্দ, সার্বভৌম, দুইজনা-স্থানে ।\
> তবে যুক্তি করে প্রভু — ‘যাব বৃন্দাবনে’ ॥৭॥

rāmānanda, sārvabhauma, dui-janā-sthāne
tabe yukti kare prabhu--'yāba vṛndāvane'

*rāmānanda*—Rāmānanda; *sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma; *dui-janā-sthāne*—diante das duas pessoas; *tabe*—então; *yukti kare*—consultou; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yāba vṛndāvane*—irei a Vṛndāvana.

**Depois disto, o próprio Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu consultou Rāmānanda Rāya e Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya, dizendo: "Irei a Vṛndāvana."**
