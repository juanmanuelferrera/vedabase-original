### CC Madhya 16.62

> কিবা প্রার্থনা, কিবা আজ্ঞা — কেহ না বুঝিল ।\
> আলিঙ্গন করি’ প্রভু তাঁরে বিদায় দিল ॥৬২॥

kibā prārthanā, kibā ājñā--keha nā bujhila

āliṅgana kari' prabhu tāṅre vidāya dila

*kibā*—o que; *prārthanā*—o pedido; *kibā*—o que; *ājñā*—a ordem; *keha*—quem quer que fosse; *nā bujhila*—não soube; *āliṅgana kari'*—abraçando; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a Ele; *vidāya dila*—despediu-se.

**Ninguém soube o que Advaita Ācārya pediu nem o que o Senhor ordenou. Após abraçar o Ācārya, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu despediu-se Dele.**
