### CC Madhya 16.151

> প্রভু বিদায় দিল, রায় যায় তাঁর সনে ।
> কৃষ্ণকথা রামানন্দ-সনে রাত্রি-দিনে ॥১৫১॥

prabhu vidāya dila, rāya yāya tāṅra sane

kṛṣṇa-kathā rāmānanda-sane rātri-dine

*prabhu vidāya dila*—o Senhor os despediu; *rāya*—Rāmānanda Rāya; *yāya*—vai; *tāṅra sane*—com Ele; *kṛṣṇa-kathā*—discussão sobre os tópicos do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *rāmānanda-sane*—com Rāmānanda; *rātri-dine*—dia e noite.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu despediu os oficiais, e Rāya Rāmānanda continuou com o Senhor. O Senhor falou com Rāmānanda Rāya sobre Śrī Kṛṣṇa dia e noite.**
