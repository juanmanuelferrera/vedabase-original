### CC Madhya 16.125

> স্বগণ-সহিতে প্রভু প্রসাদ অঙ্গীকরি’ ।
> উঠিয়া চলিলা প্রভু বলি’ ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ॥১২৫॥

svagaṇa-sahite prabhu prasāda aṅgīkari'

uṭhiyā calilā prabhu bali' 'hari' 'hari'

*sva-gaṇa-sahite*—com Seus associados pessoais; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *prasāda*—os restos de alimento; *aṅgīkari'*—aceitando; *uṭhiyā*—levantando-se; *calilā*—partiu; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bali'*—proferindo; *hari hari*—Hari, Hari.

**Após aceitar o prasāda, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu levantou-se e começou a partir, cantando os santos nomes: "Hari! Hari!"**
