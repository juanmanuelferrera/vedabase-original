### CC Madhya 16.33

> তাঁর লাগি’ গোপীনাথ ক্ষীর চুরি কৈল  ।
> মহাপ্ৰভুর মুখে আগে এ কথা শুনিল ॥৩৩॥

tāṅra lāgi' gopīnātha kṣīra curi kaila

mahāprabhura mukhe āge e kathā śunila

*tāṅra lāgi'*—por ele (Mādhavendra Purī); *gopīnātha*—a Deidade chamada Gopīnātha; *kṣīra*—leite condensado; *curi*—roubar; *kaila*—realizou; *mahāprabhura mukhe*—da boca de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āge*—anteriormente; *e kathā*—este incidente; *śunila*—ouviram.

**Foi Gopīnātha quem roubou leite condensado em benefício de Mādhavendra Purī. Este incidente havia sido relatado anteriormente pelo próprio Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
