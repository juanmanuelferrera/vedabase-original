### CC Madhya 16.24

> আচার্যরত্ন-সঙ্গে চলে তাঁহার গৃহিণী ।
> তাঁহার প্রেমের কথা কহিতে না জানি ॥২৪॥

ācāryaratna-saṅge cale tāṅhāra gṛhiṇī

tāṅhāra premera kathā kahite nā jāni

*ācāryaratna-saṅge*—com Candraśekhara; *cale*—vai; *tāṅhāra*—sua; *gṛhiṇī*—esposa; *tāṅhāra*—seu; *premera kathā*—a descrição do amor extático; *kahite*—falar; *nā jāni*—não sei como.

**A esposa de Candraśekhara [Ācāryaratna] também foi. Não sei como descrever a grandeza do amor de Candraśekhara pelo Senhor.**
