### CC Madhya 16.39

> দুই মালা গোবিন্দ দুইজনে পরাইল ।
> অদ্বৈত, অবধূত-গোসাঞি বড় সুখ পাইল ॥৩৯॥

dui mālā govinda dui-jane parāila

advaita, avadhūta-gosāñi baḍa sukha pāila

*dui mālā*—as duas guirlandas; *govinda*—Govinda; *dui-jane parāila*—colocou no pescoço de duas personalidades; *advaita*—Advaita Ācārya; *avadhūta-gosāñi*—Nityānanda Prabhu; *baḍa sukha pāila*—ficaram muito felizes.

**Govinda ofereceu as duas guirlandas a Advaita Ācārya e a Nityānanda Prabhu, e Ambos ficaram muito felizes.**
