### CC Madhya 16.23

> শিবানন্দের বালক, নাম — চৈতন্য-দাস ।\
> তেঁহো চলিয়াছে প্রভুরে দেখিতে উল্লাস ॥২৩॥

śivānandera bālaka, nāma--caitanya-dāsa

teṅho caliyāche prabhure dekhite ullāsa

*śivānandera bālaka*—o filho de Śivānanda; *nāma*—chamado; *caitanya-dāsa*—Caitanya dāsa; *teṅho*—ele; *caliyāche*—ia; *prabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhite*—para ver; *ullāsa*—jubilosamente.

**Caitanya dāsa, o filho de Śivānanda Sena, também os acompanhou jubilosamente enquanto iam ver o Senhor.**
