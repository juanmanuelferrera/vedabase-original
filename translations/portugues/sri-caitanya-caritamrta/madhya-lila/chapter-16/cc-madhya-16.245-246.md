### CC Madhya 16.245-246

> ইহাঁ প্রভু একত্র করি’ সব ভক্তগণ ।
> অদ্বৈত-নিত্যানন্দাদি যত ভক্তজন ॥২৪৫॥
> সবা আলিঙ্গন করি’ কহেন গোসাঞি ।
> সবে আজ্ঞা দেহ’ — আমি নীলাচলে যাই ॥২৪৬॥

ihāṅ prabhu ekatra kari' saba bhakta-gaṇa

advaita-nityānandādi yata bhakta-jana

sabā āliṅgana kari' kahena gosāñi

sabe ājñā deha'--āmi nīlācale yāi

*ihāṅ*—aqui (em Śāntipura); *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ekatra kari'*—reunindo num só lugar; *saba bhakta-gaṇa*—todos os devotos; *advaita-nityānanda-ādi*—encabeçados por Advaita Ācārya e Nityānanda Prabhu; *yata bhakta-jana*—todos os devotos; *sabā āliṅgana kari'*—abraçando a cada um deles; *kahena gosāñi*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse; *sabe*—todos vós; *ājñā deha'*—dai-Me permissão; *āmi*—Eu; *nīlācale*—a Nīlācala, Jagannātha Purī; *yāi*—possa ir.

**Enquanto isso, em Śāntipura, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu reuniu todos os Seus devotos — encabeçados por Advaita Ācārya e Nityānanda Prabhu —, abraçou a todos eles e pediu-lhes permissão para retornar a Jagannātha Purī.**
