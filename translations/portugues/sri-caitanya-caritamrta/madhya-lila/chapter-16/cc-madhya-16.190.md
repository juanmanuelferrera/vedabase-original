### CC Madhya 16.190

> তবে মুকুন্দ দত্ত কহে, — ‘শুন, মহাশয়  ।\
> গঙ্গাতীর যাইতে মহাপ্ৰভুর মন হয় ॥১৯০॥

tabe mukunda datta kahe,--'śuna, mahāśaya

gaṅgā-tīra yāite mahāprabhura mana haya

*tabe*—em seguida; *mukunda datta kahe*—Mukunda Datta, um devoto de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, disse; *śuna mahāśaya*—meu caro senhor, escuta-me, por favor; *gaṅgā-tīra yāite*—ir à margem do Ganges; *mahāprabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mana*—a mente ou o desejo; *haya*—é.

**Mukunda Datta então disse ao governador maometano: "Meu caro senhor, escuta, por favor. Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu deseja ir à margem do Ganges.**
