### CC Madhya 16.87

> তবে প্রভু সার্বভৌম-রামানন্দ-স্থানে ।
> আলিঙ্গন করি’ কহে মধুর বচনে ॥৮৭॥

tabe prabhu sārvabhauma-rāmānanda-sthāne

āliṅgana kari' kahe madhura vacane

*tabe*—então; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sārvabhauma-rāmānanda-sthāne*—diante de Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya e Rāmānanda Rāya; *āliṅgana kari'*—abraçando; *kahe*—diz; *madhura vacane*—doces palavras.

**Então, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu apresentou uma proposta a Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya e Rāmānanda Rāya. Ele os abraçou e proferiu doces palavras.**
