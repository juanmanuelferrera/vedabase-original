### CC Madhya 16.231

> এবে যদি মহাপ্রভু ‘শান্তিপুর’ আইলা  ।
> শুনিয়া পিতারে রঘুনাথ নিবেদিলা ॥২৩১॥

ebe yadi mahāprabhu 'śāntipura' āilā

śuniyā pitāre raghunātha nivedilā

*ebe*—agora; *yadi*—quando; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śāntipura*—a Śāntipura; *āilā*—veio; *śuniyā*—ao ouvir; *pitāre*—a seu pai; *raghunātha*—Raghunātha dāsa; *nivedilā*—fez um pedido.

**Quando Raghunātha dāsa soube que Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu havia chegado a Śāntipura, fez um pedido a seu pai.**
