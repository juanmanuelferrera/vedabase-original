### CC Madhya 16.14-15

> যদ্যপি প্ৰভুর আজ্ঞা গৌড়েতে রহিতে ।\
> নিত্যানন্দ-প্রভুকে প্রেমভক্তি প্রকাশিতে ॥১৪॥\
> তথাপি চলিলা মহাপ্ৰভুরে দেখিতে ।\
> নিত্যানন্দের প্রেম-চেষ্টা কে পারে বুঝিতে ॥১৫॥

yadyapi prabhura ājñā gauḍete rahite
nityānanda-prabhuke prema-bhakti prakāśite
tathāpi calilā mahāprabhure dekhite
nityānandera prema-ceṣṭā ke pāre bujhite

*yadyapi*—embora; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ājñā*—a ordem; *gauḍete rahite*—de permanecer em Bengala; *nityānanda-prabhuke*—a Nityānanda Prabhu; *prema-bhakti*—amor extático a Deus; *prakāśite*—de pregar; *tathāpi*—ainda assim; *calilā*—partiu; *mahāprabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhite*—para ver; *nityānandera*—do Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *prema-ceṣṭā*—as atividades de amor extático; *ke*—quem; *pāre*—é capaz; *bujhite*—de compreender.

**Embora o Senhor tivesse dito a Nityānanda Prabhu que permanecesse em Bengala e difundisse o amor extático a Deus, Nityānanda partiu para ver Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Quem pode compreender o amor extático de Nityānanda Prabhu?**
