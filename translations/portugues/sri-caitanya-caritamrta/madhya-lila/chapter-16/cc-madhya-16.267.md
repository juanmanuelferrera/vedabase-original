### CC Madhya 16.267

> তবু আমি শুনিলুঁ মাত্র, না কৈলুঁ অবধান ।
> প্রাতে চলি’ আইলাঙ ‘কানাইর নাটশালা’-গ্রাম ॥২৬৭॥

tabu āmi śuniluṅ mātra, nā kailuṅ avadhāna

prāte cali' āilāṅa 'kānāira nāṭaśālā'-grāma

*tabu*—ainda assim; *āmi*—Eu; *śuniluṅ*—ouvi; *mātra*—apenas; *nā*—não; *kailuṅ*—prestei; *avadhāna*—nenhuma atenção; *prāte*—pela manhã; *cali' āilāṅa*—caminhei; *kānāira nāṭaśālā*—a Kānāi Nāṭaśālā; *grāma*—o lugar.

**"Embora ouvisse isto, não lhe prestei nenhuma atenção. Pela manhã, contudo, fui ao lugar chamado Kānāi Nāṭaśālā.**
