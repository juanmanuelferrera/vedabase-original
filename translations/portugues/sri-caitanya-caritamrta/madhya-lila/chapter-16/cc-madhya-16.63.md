### CC Madhya 16.63

> নিত্যানন্দে কহে প্রভু, — শুনহ, শ্ৰীপাদ ।
> এই আমি মাগি, তুমি করহ প্রসাদ ॥৬৩॥

nityānande kahe prabhu,--śunaha, śrīpāda

ei āmi māgi, tumi karaha prasāda

*nityānande*—a Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu; *kahe*—diz; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śunaha*—por favor, ouve; *śrī-pāda*—ó santo homem; *ei*—isto; *āmi*—Eu; *māgi*—peço; *tumi*—Tu; *karaha*—por favor, concede; *prasāda*—misericórdia.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse então a Nityānanda Prabhu: "Por favor, ouve-Me, ó santo homem: agora Te peço algo. Concede-Me bondosamente o pedido.**
