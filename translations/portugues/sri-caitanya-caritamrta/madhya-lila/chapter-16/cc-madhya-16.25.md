### CC Madhya 16.25

> সব ঠাকুরাণী মহাপ্রভুকে ভিক্ষা দিতে ।
> প্রভুর নানা প্রিয় দ্রব্য নিল ঘর হৈতে ॥২৫॥

saba ṭhākurāṇī mahāprabhuke bhikṣā dite

prabhura nānā priya dravya nila ghara haite

*saba ṭhākurāṇī*—todas as esposas dos grandes devotos; *mahāprabhuke*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhikṣā dite*—para oferecer alimento; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nānā*—vários; *priya dravya*—alimentos agradáveis; *nila*—levaram; *ghara haite*—de casa.

**Para oferecer a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu diversos tipos de alimento, todas as esposas dos grandes devotos levaram de casa várias coisas que agradavam a Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
