### CC Madhya 16.126

> রামানন্দ, মর্দরাজ, শ্রীহরিচন্দন ।
> সঙ্গে সেবা করি’ চলে এই তিন জন ॥১২৬॥

rāmānanda, mardarāja, śrī-haricandana

saṅge sevā kari' cale ei tina jana

*rāmānanda*—Rāmānanda; *mardarāja*—Mardarāja; *śrī-haricandana*—Śrī Haricandana; *saṅge*—em companhia; *sevā kari'*—prestando serviço; *cale*—iam; *ei tina jana*—estes três cavalheiros.

**Rāmānanda Rāya, Mardarāja e Śrī Haricandana sempre acompanhavam Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e Lhe prestavam diversos serviços.**
