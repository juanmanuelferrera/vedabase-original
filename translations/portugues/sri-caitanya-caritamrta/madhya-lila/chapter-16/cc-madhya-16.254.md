### CC Madhya 16.254

kāśī-miśra, rāmānanda, pradyumna, sārvabhauma

vāṇīnātha, śikhi-ādi yata bhakta-gaṇa

*kāśī-miśra*—Kāśī Miśra; *rāmānanda*—Rāmānanda; *pradyumna*—Pradyumna; *sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma; *vāṇīnātha*—Vāṇīnātha; *śikhi-ādi*—Śikhi Māhiti e outros; *yata bhakta-gaṇa*—todos os devotos.

**Kāśī Miśra, Rāmānanda Rāya, Pradyumna, Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya, Vāṇīnātha Rāya, Śikhi Māhiti e todos os demais devotos encontraram-se com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
