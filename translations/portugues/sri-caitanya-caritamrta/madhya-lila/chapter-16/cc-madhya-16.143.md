### CC Madhya 16.143

> পণ্ডিতে লঞা যাইতে সার্বভৌমে আজ্ঞা দিলা ৷
> ভট্টাচার্য কহে, — “উঠ, ঐছে প্রভুর লীলা ॥১৪৩॥

paṇḍite lañā yāite sārvabhaume ājñā dilā

bhaṭṭācārya kahe,--"uṭha, aiche prabhura līlā

*paṇḍite lañā*—levando o Paṇḍita; *yāite*—para ir; *sārvabhauma*—a Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *ājñā dilā*—deu uma ordem; *bhaṭṭācārya kahe*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya disse; *uṭha*—por favor, levanta-te; *aiche*—assim; *prabhura līlā*—o modo dos passatempos do Senhor.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ordenou a Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya que levasse Gadādhara Paṇḍita consigo. O Bhaṭṭācārya disse a Gadādhara Paṇḍita: "Levanta-te! Assim são os passatempos de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
