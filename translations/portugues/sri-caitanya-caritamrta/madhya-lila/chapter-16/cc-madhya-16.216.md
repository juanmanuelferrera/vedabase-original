### CC Madhya 16.216

> পুনরপি প্রভু যদি ‘শান্তিপুর’ আইলা ।\
> রঘুনাথ-দাস আসি’ প্রভুরে মিলিলা ॥২১৬॥

punarapi prabhu yadi 'śāntipura' āilā

raghunātha-dāsa āsi' prabhure mililā

*punarapi*—novamente; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yadi*—quando; *śāntipura āilā*—veio a Śāntipura; *raghunātha-dāsa*—Raghunātha dāsa; *āsi'*—vindo; *prabhure mililā*—encontrou-se com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu retornou a Śāntipura, Raghunātha dāsa veio encontrar-se com Ele.**
