### CC Madhya 16.135

> আই’কে দেখিতে যাইব, না যাইব তোমা লাগি’ ।
> ‘প্রতিজ্ঞা’-‘সেবা’-ত্যাগ-দোষ, তার আমি ভাগী ।।” ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

āi'ke dekhite yāiba, nā yāiba tomā lāgi'

'pratijñā'-'sevā'-tyāga-doṣa, tāra āmi bhāgī"

*āi'ke*—mãe Śacīdevī; *dekhite*—para ver; *yāiba*—irei; *nā yāiba*—não irei; *tomā lāgi'*—por Tua causa; *pratijñā-sevā*—o voto e o serviço a Gopīnātha; *tyāga-doṣa*—a culpa de abandonar; *tāra*—por isso; *āmi bhāgī*—sou responsável.

**"Irei ver Śacīmātā, mas não irei por Tua causa. Serei responsável pelo abandono de meu voto e do serviço a Gopīnātha."**
