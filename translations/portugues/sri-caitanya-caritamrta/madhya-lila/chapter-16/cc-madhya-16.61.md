### CC Madhya 16.61

> তাঁর মুখ দেখি’ হাসে শচীর নন্দন ।
> অঙ্গীকার জানি’ আচার্য করেন নর্তন ॥৬১॥

tāṅra mukha dekhi' hāse śacīra nandana

aṅgīkāra jāni' ācārya karena nartana

*tāṅra mukha*—Sua face; *dekhi'*—vendo; *hāse*—sorri; *śacīra nandana*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *aṅgīkāra jāni'*—compreendendo a aceitação; *ācārya*—Advaita Ācārya; *karena*—realizou; *nartana*—dança.

**Ao ver a face de Advaita Ācārya, o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu sorriu. Compreendendo que o Senhor aceitara a proposta, Advaita Ācārya começou a dançar.**
