### CC Madhya 17.37

> হেনকালে ব্যাঘ্র তথা আইল পাঁচ-সাত ।
> ব্যাঘ্র-মৃগী মিলি’ চলে মহাপ্রভুর সাথ ॥৩৭॥

hena-kāle vyāghra tathā āila pāṅca-sāta

vyāghra-mṛgī mili' cale mahāprabhura sātha

*hena-kāle*—neste momento; *vyāghra*—tigres; *tathā*—ali; *āila*—vieram; *pāṅca-sāta*—cinco a sete; *vyāghra-mṛgī*—os tigres e as corças; *mili'*—reunindo-se; *cale*—vão; *mahāprabhura sātha*—com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Enquanto Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu atravessava a selva, vieram cinco ou sete tigres. Juntando-se às corças, os tigres começaram a seguir o Senhor.**
