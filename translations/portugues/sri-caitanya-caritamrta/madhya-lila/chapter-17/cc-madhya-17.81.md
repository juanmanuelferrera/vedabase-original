### CC Madhya 17.81

> এইমত বলভদ্র করেন স্তবন ।
> প্রেমসেবা করি’ তুষ্ট কৈল প্রভুর মন ॥৮১॥

ei-mata balabhadra karena stavana

prema-sevā kari' tuṣṭa kaila prabhura mana

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *balabhadra*—Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *karena*—oferece; *stavana*—orações; *prema-sevā kari'*—prestando serviço com amor; *tuṣṭa*—apaziguada; *kaila*—fez; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mana*—a mente.

**Dessa maneira, Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya ofereceu suas orações ao Senhor. Prestando-Lhe serviço com amor extático, ele apaziguou a mente do Senhor.**
