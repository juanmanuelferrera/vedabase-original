### CC Madhya 17.181

> মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে যদি ‘ভিক্ষা’ মাগিল । 
> দৈন্য করি’ সেই বিপ্র কহিতে লাগিল ॥১৮১॥

mahāprabhu tāṅre yadi 'bhikṣā' māgila

dainya kari' sei vipra kahite lāgila

*mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—dele; *yadi*—quando; *bhikṣā māgila*—pediu o almoço; *dainya kari'*—por humildade; *sei vipra*—aquele brāhmaṇa; *kahite lāgila*—começou a falar.

**Por isso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu de bom grado pediu alimento ao brāhmaṇa, e o brāhmaṇa, sentindo uma humildade natural, começou a falar da seguinte maneira.**
