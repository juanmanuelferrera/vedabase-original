### CC Madhya 17.61

> ভট্টাচার্য পাক করে বন্য-ব্যঞ্জন ।
> বন্য-ব্যঞ্জনে প্ৰভুর আনন্দিত মন ॥৬১॥

bhaṭṭācārya pāka kare vanya-vyañjana

vanya-vyañjane prabhura ānandita mana

*bhaṭṭācārya*—Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *pāka kare*—cozinha; *vanya-vyañjana*—toda variedade de vegetais da floresta; *vanya-vyañjane*—por tais vegetais da floresta; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ānandita mana*—a mente fica muito feliz.

**Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya costumava cozinhar toda espécie de vegetais colhidos da floresta, e Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu sentia grande prazer em aceitar essas preparações.**
