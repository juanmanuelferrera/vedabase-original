### CC Madhya 17.191

svayambhu, viśrāma, dīrgha-viṣṇu, bhūteśvara

mahāvidyā, gokarṇādi dekhilā vistara

*svayambhu*—Svayambhu; *viśrāma*—Viśrāma; *dīrgha-viṣṇu*—Dīrgha Viṣṇu; *bhūteśvara*—Bhūteśvara; *mahāvidyā*—Mahāvidyā; *gokarṇa*—Gokarṇa; *ādi*—e assim por diante; *dekhilā*—viu; *vistara*—muitos.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu visitou todos os lugares sagrados às margens do Yamunā, inclusive Svayambhu, Viśrāma-ghāṭa, Dīrgha Viṣṇu, Bhūteśvara, Mahāvidyā e Gokarṇa.**
