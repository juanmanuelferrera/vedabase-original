### CC Madhya 17.219

prabhure mūrcchita dekhi' sei ta brāhmaṇa

bhaṭṭācārya-saṅge kare prabhura santarpaṇa

*prabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mūrcchita*—inconsciente; *dekhi'*—vendo; *sei ta brāhmaṇa*—de fato aquele brāhmaṇa; *bhaṭṭācārya-saṅge*—com o Bhaṭṭācārya; *kare*—faz; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *santarpaṇa*—cuidar.

**Ao ver que Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu estava inconsciente, o brāhmaṇa, juntamente com Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya, cuidou Dele.**
