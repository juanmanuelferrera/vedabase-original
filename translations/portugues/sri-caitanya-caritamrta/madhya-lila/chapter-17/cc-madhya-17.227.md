### CC Madhya 17.227

> সহস্রগুণ প্রেম বাড়ে মথুরা দরশনে ।\
> লক্ষগুণ প্রেম বাড়ে, ভ্রমেন যবে বনে ॥২২৭॥

sahasra-guṇa prema bāḍe mathurā daraśane

lakṣa-guṇa prema bāḍe, bhramena yabe vane

*sahasra-guṇa*—mil vezes; *prema*—amor; *bāḍe*—aumentou; *mathurā*—Mathurā; *daraśane*—ao ver; *lakṣa-guṇa*—cem mil vezes; *prema bāḍe*—o amor aumenta; *bhramena*—vagueia; *yabe*—quando; *vane*—nas florestas de Vṛndāvana.

**O amor extático do Senhor aumentou mil vezes quando Ele visitou Mathurā, mas aumentou cem mil vezes quando Ele vagueou pelas florestas de Vṛndāvana.**
