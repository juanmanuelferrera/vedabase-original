### CC Madhya 17.50

> যদ্যপি প্রভু লোক-সংঘট্টের ত্রাসে  ।\
> প্রেম ‘গুপ্ত’ করেন, বাহিরে না প্রকাশে ॥৫০॥

yadyapi prabhu loka-saṅghaṭṭera trāse

prema 'gupta' karena, bāhire nā prakāśe

*yadyapi*—embora; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *loka-saṅghaṭṭera*—de multidões de pessoas; *trāse*—temendo; *prema*—êxtase; *gupta karena*—mantém oculto; *bāhire*—externamente; *nā*—não; *prakāśe*—manifesta.

**O Senhor nem sempre manifestava Seu êxtase. Temendo uma grande assembleia de pessoas, o Senhor mantinha Seu êxtase oculto.**
