### CC Madhya 17.176

> ভিক্ষা লাগি’ ভট্টাচার্যে করাইলা রন্ধন । \
> তবে মহাপ্রভু হাসি’ বলিলা বচন ॥১৭৬॥

bhikṣā lāgi' bhaṭṭācārye karāilā randhana

tabe mahāprabhu hāsi' balilā vacana

*bhikṣā lāgi'*—para o almoço; *bhaṭṭācārye*—Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *karāilā randhana*—fez cozinhar; *tabe*—naquele momento; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *hāsi'*—sorrindo; *balilā vacana*—disse estas palavras.

**Ele pediu a Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya que cozinhasse o almoço de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Naquele momento, o Senhor, sorrindo, falou da seguinte maneira.**
