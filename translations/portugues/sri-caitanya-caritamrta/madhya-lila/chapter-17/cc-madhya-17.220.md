### CC Madhya 17.220

> আস্তে-ব্যস্তে মহাপ্রভুর লঞা বহিৰ্বাস ।
> জলসেক করে অঙ্গে, বস্ত্রের বাতাস ॥২২০॥

āste-vyaste mahāprabhura lañā bahirvāsa

jala-seka kare aṅge, vastrera vātāsa

*āste-vyaste*—com grande pressa; *mahāprabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *lañā*—pegando; *bahirvāsa*—o pano de cobrir; *jala-seka kare*—aspergem água; *aṅge*—no corpo; *vastrera vātāsa*—abanando com o pano.

**Apressadamente, eles aspergiram água sobre o corpo do Senhor. Em seguida, pegaram Seu pano e começaram a abaná-lo com ele.**
