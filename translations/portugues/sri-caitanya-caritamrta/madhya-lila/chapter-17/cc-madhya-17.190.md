### CC Madhya 17.190

> যমুনার ‘চব্বিশ ঘাটে’ প্রভু কৈল স্নান ।
> সেই বিপ্র প্রভুকে দেখায় তীর্থস্থান ॥১৯০॥

yamunāra 'cabbiśa ghāṭe' prabhu kaila snāna

sei vipra prabhuke dekhāya tīrtha-sthāna

*yamunāra*—do rio Yamunā; *cabbiśa ghāṭe*—nos vinte e quatro ghats, ou locais de banho; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kaila*—realizou; *snāna*—banho; *sei vipra*—aquele brāhmaṇa; *prabhuke*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhāya*—mostra; *tīrtha-sthāna*—os lugares sagrados de peregrinação.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu banhou-se em vinte e quatro ghats ao longo das margens do Yamunā, e o brāhmaṇa mostrou-Lhe todos os lugares de peregrinação.**

Os vinte e quatro ghats (locais de banho) ao longo do Yamunā são (1) Avimukta, (2) Adhirūḍha, (3) Guhya-tīrtha, (4) Prayāga-tīrtha, (5) Kanakhala-tīrtha, (6) Tinduka, (7) Sūrya-tīrtha, (8) Vaṭasvāmī, (9) Dhruva-ghāṭa, (10) Ṛṣi-tīrtha, (11) Mokṣa-tīrtha, (12) Bodha-tīrtha, (13) Gokarṇa, (14) Kṛṣṇagaṅgā, (15) Vaikuṇṭha, (16) Asi-kuṇḍa, (17) Catuḥ-sāmudrika-kūpa, (18) Akrūra-tīrtha, (19) Yājñika-vipra-sthāna, (20) Kubjā-kūpa, (21) Raṅga-sthala, (22) Mañca-sthala, (23) Mallayuddha-sthāna e (24) Daśāśvamedha.
