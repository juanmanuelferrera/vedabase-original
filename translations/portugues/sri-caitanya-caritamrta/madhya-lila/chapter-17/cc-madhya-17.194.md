### CC Madhya 17.194

> পথে গাভীঘটা চরে প্রভুরে দেখিয়া ।\
> প্রভুকে বেড়য় আসি’ হুঙ্কার করিয়া ॥১৯৪॥

pathe gābhī-ghaṭā care prabhure dekhiyā

prabhuke beḍaya āsi' huṅkāra kariyā

*pathe*—no caminho; *gābhī-ghaṭā*—grupos de vacas; *care*—pastam; *prabhure dekhiyā*—após verem o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *prabhuke beḍaya*—cercaram o Senhor; *āsi'*—vindo; *huṅ-kāra kariyā*—emitindo um forte mugido.

**Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu passava por Vṛndāvana, manadas de vacas que pastavam viram-No passar e, cercando-O imediatamente, puseram-se a mugir bem alto.**
