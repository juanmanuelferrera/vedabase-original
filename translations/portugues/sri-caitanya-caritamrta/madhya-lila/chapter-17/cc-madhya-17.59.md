### CC Madhya 17.59

> কেহ অন্ন আনি’ দেয় ভট্টাচার্য-স্থানে ।\
> কেহ দুগ্ধ, দধি, কেহ ঘৃত, খণ্ড আনে ॥৫৯॥

keha anna āni' deya bhaṭṭācārya-sthāne

keha dugdha, dadhi, keha ghṛta, khaṇḍa āne

*keha*—alguém; *anna*—grãos; *āni'*—trazendo; *deya*—entrega; *bhaṭṭācārya-sthāne*—a Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *keha*—alguém; *dugdha*—leite; *dadhi*—iogurte; *keha*—alguém; *ghṛta*—ghee; *khaṇḍa*—açúcar; *āne*—traz.

**Algumas pessoas traziam grãos e os entregavam a Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya. Outras traziam leite e iogurte, e outras ainda traziam ghee e açúcar.**
