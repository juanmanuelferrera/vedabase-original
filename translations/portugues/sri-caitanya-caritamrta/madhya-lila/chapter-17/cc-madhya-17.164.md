### CC Madhya 17.164

> তবে মহাপ্রভু সেই ব্রাহ্মণে লঞা  ।\
> তাঁহারে পুছিলা কিছু নিভৃতে বসিয়া ॥১৬৪॥

tabe mahāprabhu sei brāhmaṇe lañā

tāṅhāre puchilā kichu nibhṛte vasiyā

*tabe*—depois disso; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sei*—aquele; *brāhmaṇe*—brāhmaṇa; *lañā*—tomando; *tāṅhāre*—a ele; *puchilā*—perguntou; *kichu*—algo; *nibhṛte vasiyā*—sentando-se num lugar isolado.

**Depois disso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu chamou o brāhmaṇa à parte. Sentando-se num lugar isolado, o Senhor começou a interrogá-lo.**
