### CC Madhya 17.90

> ভিক্ষা করি’ মহাপ্রভু করিলা শয়ন ।
> মিশ্রপুত্র রঘু করে পাদ-সম্বাহন ॥৯০॥

bhikṣā kari' mahāprabhu karilā śayana

miśra-putra raghu kare pāda-samvāhana

*bhikṣā kari'*—após terminar Seu almoço; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *karilā śayana*—descansou; *miśra-putra*—o filho de Tapana Miśra; *raghu*—Raghu; *kare*—faz; *pāda-samvāhana*—massageando as pernas.

**Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu descansava após o almoço, o filho de Tapana Miśra, chamado Raghu, costumava massagear Suas pernas.**
