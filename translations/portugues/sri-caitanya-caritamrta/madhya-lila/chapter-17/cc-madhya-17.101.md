### CC Madhya 17.101

> মহারাষ্ট্রীয় বিপ্র আইসে প্রভু দেখিবারে ।\
> প্রভুর রূপ-প্রেম দেখি’ হয় চমৎকারে ॥১০১॥

mahārāṣṭrīya vipra āise prabhu dekhibāre

prabhura rūpa-prema dekhi' haya camatkāre

*mahārāṣṭrīya*—pertencente ao estado de Mahārāṣṭra; *vipra*—certo brāhmaṇa; *āise*—vem; *prabhu dekhibāre*—para ver o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rūpa-prema*—a beleza e o amor extático; *dekhi'*—vendo; *haya camatkāre*—fica atônito.

**Em Vārāṇasī havia um brāhmaṇa mahārāṣṭrīyano que costumava vir diariamente ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Esse brāhmaṇa simplesmente se assombrava ao contemplar a beleza pessoal do Senhor e Seu amor extático por Kṛṣṇa.**
