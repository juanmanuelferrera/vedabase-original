### CC Madhya 17.209

> শুক-শারিকা প্রভুর হাতে উড়ি’ পড়ে ।\
> প্রভুকে শুনাঞা কৃষ্ণের গুণ-শ্লোক পড়ে ॥২০৯॥

śuka-śārikā prabhura hāte uḍi' paḍe

prabhuke śunāñā kṛṣṇera guṇa-śloka paḍe

*śuka-śārikā*—os papagaios, macho e fêmea; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *hāte*—na mão; *uḍi'*—voando; *paḍe*—pousam; *prabhuke*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śunāñā*—fazendo ouvir; *kṛṣṇera*—do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *guṇa-śloka paḍe*—recitaram versos sobre as qualidades transcendentais.

**Ambos os papagaios voaram para a mão do Senhor e começaram a cantar as qualidades transcendentais de Kṛṣṇa, e o Senhor os escutou.**
