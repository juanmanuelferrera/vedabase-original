### CC Madhya 17.20

tāṅhāra vacana prabhu aṅgīkāra kaila

balabhadra-bhaṭṭācārye saṅge kari' nila

*tāṅhāra vacana*—suas palavras; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *aṅgīkāra kaila*—aceitou; *balabhadra-bhaṭṭācārye*—Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *saṅge kari' nila*—levou conSigo.

**Assim, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu aceitou o pedido de Svarūpa Dāmodara Paṇḍita e concordou em levar Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya conSigo.**
