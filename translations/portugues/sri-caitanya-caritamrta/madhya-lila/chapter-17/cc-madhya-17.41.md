### CC Madhya 17.41

> নাচে, কুন্দে ব্যাঘ্রগণ মৃগীগণ-সঙ্গে ।
> বলভদ্র-ভট্টাচার্য দেখে অপূর্ব-রঙ্গে ॥৪১॥

nāce, kunde vyāghra-gaṇa mṛgī-gaṇa-saṅge

balabhadra-bhaṭṭācārya dekhe apūrva-raṅge

*nāce*—dançam; *kunde*—saltam; *vyāghra-gaṇa*—os tigres; *mṛgī-gaṇa-saṅge*—com as corças; *balabhadra-bhaṭṭācārya*—Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *dekhe*—vê; *apūrva-raṅge*—com grande assombro.

**Quando todos os tigres e corças dançaram e saltaram, Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya os viu e ficou tomado de assombro.**
