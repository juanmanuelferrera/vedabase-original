### CC Madhya 17.158

> একবিপ্র পড়ে প্রভুর চরণ ধরিয়া ।
> প্রভু-সঙ্গে নৃত্য করে প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা ॥১৫৮॥

eka-vipra paḍe prabhura caraṇa dhariyā

prabhu-saṅge nṛtya kare premāviṣṭa hañā

*eka-vipra*—um brāhmaṇa; *paḍe*—prostra-se; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *caraṇa dhariyā*—segurando os pés de lótus; *prabhu-saṅge*—com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nṛtya kare*—dança; *prema-āviṣṭa hañā*—estando absorto em amor extático.

**Certo brāhmaṇa prostrou-se aos pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e então começou a dançar com Ele em amor extático.**
