### CC Madhya 25.87

> এত বলি’ প্রভুরে লঞা তথায় বসিল ।
> প্রভুরে প্রকাশানন্দ পুছিতে লাগিল ॥৮৭॥

eta bali' prabhure lañā tathāya vasila

prabhure prakāśānanda puchite lāgila

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *prabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *lañā*—levando; *tathāya vasila*—sentou-se ali; *prabhure*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *prakāśānanda*—Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī; *puchite lāgila*—começou a indagar.

**Após dizer isto, Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī sentou-se com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e começou a questionar o Senhor da seguinte maneira.**
