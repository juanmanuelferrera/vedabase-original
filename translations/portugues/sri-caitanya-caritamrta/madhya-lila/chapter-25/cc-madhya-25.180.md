### CC Madhya 25.180

> সবে চাহে প্রভু-সঙ্গে নীলাচল যাইতে ৷
> সবারে বিদায় দিলা প্রভু যত্ন-সহিতে ৷৷ ১৮০ ৷৷ ॥১৮০॥

sabe cāhe prabhu-saṅge nīlācala yāite

sabāre vidāya dilā prabhu yatna-sahite

*sabe cāhe*—todos eles queriam; *prabhu-saṅge*—com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nīlācala yāite*—ir a Jagannātha Purī; *sabāre*—a todos eles; *vidāya dilā*—despediu-se; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yatna-sahite*—com grande atenção.

**Esses cinco desejavam acompanhar Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu até Jagannātha Purī, mas o Senhor atenciosamente despediu-se deles.**
