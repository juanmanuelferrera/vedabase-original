### CC Madhya 25.175

> প্রভু যবে স্নানে যান বিশ্বেশ্বর-দরশনে ৷
> দুইদিকে লোক করে প্রভু-বিলোকনে ৷৷ ১৭৫ ৷৷ ॥১৭৫॥

prabhu yabe snāne yāna viśveśvara-daraśane

dui-dike loka kare prabhu-vilokane

*prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yabe*—quando; *snāne yāna*—vai banhar-se; *viśveśvara-daraśane*—ou ver a Deidade do Senhor Viśveśvara; *dui-dike*—dos dois lados; *loka*—todas as pessoas; *kare*—faziam; *prabhu-vilokane*—ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ia banhar-se no Ganges e ver o templo de Viśveśvara, as pessoas enfileiravam-se dos dois lados para ver o Senhor.**
