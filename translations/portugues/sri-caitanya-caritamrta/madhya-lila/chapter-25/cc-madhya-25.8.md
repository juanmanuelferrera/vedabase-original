### CC Madhya 25.8

> “প্রভুর স্বভাব, — যেবা দেখে সন্নিধানে  ।
> ‘স্বরূপ’ অনুভবি’ তাঁরে ‘ঈশ্বর’ করি’ মানে ॥৮॥

"prabhura svabhāva,-yebā dekhe sannidhāne
'svarūpa' anubhavi' tāṅre 'īśvara' kari' māne

*prabhura sva-bhāva*—as características de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yebā*—todo aquele que; *dekhe*—vê; *sannidhāne*—de perto; *sva-rūpa*—Sua personalidade; *anubhavi'*—percebendo; *tāṅre*—a Ele; *īśvara kari'*—como o Senhor Supremo; *māne*—aceita.

**O brāhmaṇa de Mahārāṣṭra pensou: "Todo aquele que observa de perto as características de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu imediatamente percebe Sua personalidade e O aceita como o Senhor Supremo.**
