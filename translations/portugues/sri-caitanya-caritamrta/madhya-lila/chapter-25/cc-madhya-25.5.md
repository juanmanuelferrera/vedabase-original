### CC Madhya 25.5

> সন্ন্যাসীর গণ প্রভুরে যদি উপেক্ষিল ।\
> ভক্ত-দুঃখ খণ্ডাইতে তারে কৃপা কৈল ॥৫॥

sannyāsīra gaṇa prabhure yadi upekṣila
bhakta-duḥkha khaṇḍāite tāre kṛpā kaila

*sannyāsīra gaṇa*—todos os sannyāsīs; *prabhure*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yadi*—quando; *upekṣila*—criticaram; *bhakta-duḥkha*—a infelicidade dos devotos; *khaṇḍāite*—para dissipar; *tāre*—a eles; *kṛpā kaila*—mostrou Sua misericórdia.

**Quando os sannyāsīs māyāvādīs de Vārāṇasī criticaram Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, os devotos do Senhor ficaram muito abatidos. Para satisfazê-los, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu mostrou Sua misericórdia aos sannyāsīs.**
