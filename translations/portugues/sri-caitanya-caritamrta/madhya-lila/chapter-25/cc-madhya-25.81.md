### CC Madhya 25.81

> প্রকাশানন্দ কহে, — “তুমি সাক্ষাৎ ভগবান্ ।\
> তবু যদি কর তাঁর ‘দাস’-অভিমান ॥৮১॥

prakāśānanda kahe,-"tumi sākṣāt bhagavān

tabu yadi kara tāṅra 'dāsa'-abhimāna

*prakāśānanda kahe*—Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī respondeu; *tumi*—Tu; *sākṣāt*—diretamente; *bhagavān*—a Suprema Personalidade de Deus, Kṛṣṇa; *tabu*—ainda; *yadi*—se; *kara*—Te apresentas; *tāṅra dāsa-abhimāna*—considerando-Te Seu servo.

**Prakāśānanda respondeu: "Tu és a Suprema Personalidade de Deus, o próprio Kṛṣṇa. Não obstante, Te consideras Seu servo eterno.**
