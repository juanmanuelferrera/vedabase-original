### CC Madhya 25.186

> এথা রূপ-গোসাঞি যবে মথুরা আইলা ৷\
> ধ্রুবঘাটে তাঁরে সুবুদ্ধিরায় মিলিলা ৷৷ ১৮৬ ৷৷ ॥১৮৬॥

ethā rūpa-gosāñi yabe mathurā āilā

dhruva-ghāṭe tāṅre subuddhi-rāya mililā

*ethā*—ali; *rūpa-gosāñi*—Rūpa Gosāñi; *yabe*—quando; *mathurā āilā*—chegou a Mathurā; *dhruva-ghāṭe*—na margem do Yamunā conhecida como Dhruva-ghāṭa; *tāṅre*—com ele; *subuddhi-rāya*—um devoto do Senhor Caitanya chamado Subuddhi Rāya; *mililā*—encontrou-se.

**Quando Rūpa Gosvāmī chegou a Mathurā, encontrou-se com Subuddhi Rāya nas margens do Yamunā, num lugar chamado Dhruva-ghāṭa.**
