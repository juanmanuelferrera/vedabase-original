### CC Madhya 25.71

> প্রকাশানন্দের প্রভু বন্দিলা চরণ ।
> প্রকাশানন্দ আসি’ তাঁর ধরিল চরণ ॥৭১॥

prakāśānandera prabhu vandilā caraṇa

prakāśānanda āsi' tāṅra dharila caraṇa

*prakāśānandera*—de Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *vandilā*—orou; *caraṇa*—aos pés; *prakāśānanda*—Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī; *āsi'*—chegando; *tāṅra*—Seus; *dharila caraṇa*—imediatamente agarrou os pés de lótus.

**Após interromper o kīrtana, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, que é um grande exemplo de humildade, ofereceu orações aos pés de Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī. Diante disto, Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī imediatamente avançou e segurou os pés de lótus do Senhor.**
