### CC Madhya 25.3

> এই মত মহাপ্রভু দুই মাস পর্যন্ত ।
> শিখাইলা তাঁরে ভক্তিসিদ্ধান্তের অন্ত ॥৩॥

ei mata mahāprabhu dui māsa paryanta
śikhāilā tāṅre bhakti-siddhāntera anta

*ei mata*—desta maneira; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dui māsa paryanta*—durante dois meses; *śikhāilā*—instruiu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *bhakti-siddhāntera anta*—todas as conclusões do serviço devocional.

**O Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu instruiu Śrī Sanātana Gosvāmī em todas as conclusões do serviço devocional durante dois meses consecutivos.**
