### CC Madhya 25.59

> এ সব বৃত্তান্ত শুনি’ মহারাষ্ট্রীয় ব্রাহ্মণ ।
> প্রভুরে কহিতে সুখে করিলা গমন ॥৫৯॥

e saba vṛttānta śuni' mahārāṣṭrīya brāhmaṇa

prabhure kahite sukhe karilā gamana

*e saba vṛttānta*—todas essas descrições; *śuni'*—ouvindo; *mahārāṣṭrīya brāhmaṇa*—o brāhmaṇa de Mahārāṣṭra; *prabhure*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kahite*—para informar; *sukhe*—muito alegremente; *karilā gamana*—foi.

**Após ouvir todas essas afirmações, o brāhmaṇa de Mahārāṣṭra foi, muito jubilosamente, informar o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
