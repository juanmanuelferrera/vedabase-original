### CC Madhya 25.26

> সূত্র-উপনিষদের মুখ্যার্থ ছাড়িয়া ।
> আচার্য ‘কল্পনা’ করে আগ্রহ করিয়া ॥২৬॥

sūtra-upaniṣadera mukhyārtha chāḍiyā

ācārya 'kalpanā' kare āgraha kariyā

*sūtra-upaniṣadera*—do Vedānta-sūtra e dos Upaniṣads; *mukhya-artha*—o significado direto; *chāḍiyā*—abandonando; *ācārya*—Śaṅkarācārya; *kalpanā*—imaginação; *kare*—faz; *āgraha kariyā*—com grande avidez.

**"Abandonando o significado direto do Vedānta-sūtra e dos Upaniṣads, Śaṅkarācārya imagina alguma outra interpretação.**
