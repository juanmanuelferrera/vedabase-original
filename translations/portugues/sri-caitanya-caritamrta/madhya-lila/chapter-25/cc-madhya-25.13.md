### CC Madhya 25.13

> ভক্ত-দুঃখ দেখি’ প্রভু মনেতে চিন্তিল ।\
> সন্ন্যাসীর মন ফিরাইতে মন হইল ॥১৩॥

bhakta-duḥkha dekhi' prabhu manete cintila
sannyāsīra mana phirāite mana ha-ila

*bhakta-duḥkha dekhi'*—vendo a infelicidade dos devotos; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *manete cintila*—ponderou em Sua mente; *sannyāsīra mana*—as mentes dos sannyāsīs māyāvādīs; *phirāite*—para mudar; *mana ha-ila*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu decidiu.

**Eles apresentaram seu pedido, e Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, vendo a infelicidade de Seus devotos, decidiu mudar as mentes dos sannyāsīs māyāvādīs.**
