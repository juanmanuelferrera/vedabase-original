### CC Madhya 10.31

darśana kari' mahāprabhu calilā bāhire

bhaṭṭācārya ānila tāṅre kāśī-miśra-ghare

*darśana kari'*—vendo o Senhor Jagannātha; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *calilā*—partiu; *bāhire*—para fora; *bhaṭṭācārya*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *ānila*—levou; *tāṅre*—a Ele; *kāśī-miśra-ghare*—à casa de Kāśī Miśra.

**Após ver o Senhor Jagannātha, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu deixou o templo. O Bhaṭṭācārya, então, levou-O à casa de Kāśī Miśra.**
