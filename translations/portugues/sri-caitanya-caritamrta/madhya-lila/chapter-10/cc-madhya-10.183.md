### CC Madhya 10.183

> এত বলি’ ভারতীরে লঞা নিজ-বাসা আইলা ৷
> ভারতী-গোসাঞি প্রভুর নিকটে রহিলা ৷৷ ১৮৩ ৷৷ ॥১৮৩॥

eta bali' bhāratīre lañā nija-vāsā āilā
bhāratī-gosāñi prabhura nikaṭe rahilā

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *bhāratīre*—Brahmānanda Bhāratī; *lañā*—levando consigo; *nija-vāsā āilā*—retornou à Sua própria residência; *bhāratī-gosāñi*—Brahmānanda Bhāratī; *prabhura nikaṭe*—no abrigo de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rahilā*—permaneceu.

**Após dizer isto, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu levou Brahmānanda Bhāratī consigo até Sua residência. A partir desse momento, Brahmānanda Bhāratī permaneceu com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
