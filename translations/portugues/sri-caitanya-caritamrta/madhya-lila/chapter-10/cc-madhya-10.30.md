### CC Madhya 10.30

> মহাপ্রসাদ দিয়া তাহাঁ মিলিলা সেবকগণ ৷\
> মহাপ্রভু সবাকারে কৈল আলিঙ্গন ৷৷ ৩০ ৷৷ ॥৩০॥

mahā-prasāda diyā tāhāṅ mililā sevaka-gaṇa

mahāprabhu sabākāre kaila āliṅgana

*mahā-prasāda*—restos do alimento do Senhor Jagannātha; *diyā*—entregando; *tāhāṅ*—lá; *mililā*—encontraram; *sevaka-gaṇa*—os servos do Senhor Jagannātha; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sabākāre*—a todos eles; *kaila*—fez; *āliṅgana*—abraçar.

**Todos os servos do Senhor Jagannātha entregaram os restos do alimento do Senhor a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Em troca, Caitanya Mahāprabhu abraçou a todos eles.**
