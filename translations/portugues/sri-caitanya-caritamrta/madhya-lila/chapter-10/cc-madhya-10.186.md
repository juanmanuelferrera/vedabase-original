### CC Madhya 10.186

> প্রভুকে লঞা করা’ন ঈশ্বর দরশন ৷
> আগে লোক-ভিড় সব করি’ নিবারণ ৷৷ ১৮৬ ৷৷ ॥১৮৬॥

prabhuke lañā karā'na īśvara daraśana
āge loka-bhiḍa saba kari' nivāraṇa

*prabhuke*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *lañā*—levando; *karā'na*—ajuda em; *īśvara daraśana*—visitar o Senhor Jagannātha; *āge*—à frente de; *loka-bhiḍa*—multidões de pessoas; *saba*—todas; *kari' nivāraṇa*—refreando.

**Kāśīśvara costumava conduzir Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ao templo de Jagannātha. Ele precedia o Senhor através da multidão e impedia que as pessoas O tocassem.**
