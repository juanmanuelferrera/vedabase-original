### CC Madhya 10.120

> উঠাঞা মহাপ্রভু কৈল আলিঙ্গন ৷\
> দুইজনে প্রেমাবেশে হৈল অচেতন ৷৷ ১২০ ৷৷ ॥১২০॥

uṭhāñā mahāprabhu kaila āliṅgana

dui-jane premāveśe haila acetana

*uṭhāñā*—após erguê-lo; *mahāprabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kaila*—fez; *āliṅgana*—abraçar; *dui-jane*—as duas pessoas; *prema-āveśe*—no êxtase do amor; *haila*—ficaram; *acetana*—inconscientes.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ergueu Svarūpa Dāmodara e o abraçou. Ambos ficaram extáticos de amor e caíram inconscientes.**
