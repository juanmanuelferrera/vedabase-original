### CC Madhya 10.29

> আর দিন মহাপ্রভু ভট্টাচার্যের সঙ্গে ৷\
> জগন্নাথ দরশন কৈল মহারঙ্গে ৷৷ ২৯ ৷৷ ॥২৯॥

āra dina mahāprabhu bhaṭṭācāryera saṅge

jagannātha daraśana kaila mahā-raṅge

*āra dina*—no dia seguinte; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhaṭṭācāryera saṅge*—com Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *jagannātha*—do Senhor Jagannātha; *daraśana*—visitando o templo; *kaila*—fez; *mahā-raṅge*—com grande entusiasmo.

**No dia seguinte, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu chegou e foi, com grande entusiasmo, junto com Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya, ver o templo do Senhor Jagannātha.**
