### CC Madhya 10.51

> তবে মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে কৈল আলিঙ্গন ৷
> স্তুতি করি’ কহে রামানন্দ-বিবরণ ৷৷ ৫১ ৷৷ ॥৫১॥

tabe mahāprabhu tāṅre kaila āliṅgana

stuti kari' kahe rāmānanda-vivaraṇa

*tabe*—em seguida; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kaila*—fez; *āliṅgana*—abraço; *stuti kari'*—elogiando muito; *kahe*—disse; *rāmānanda*—de Rāmānanda Rāya; *vivaraṇa*—descrição.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu abraçou Bhavānanda Rāya e, com grande respeito, falou de seu filho Rāmānanda Rāya.**
