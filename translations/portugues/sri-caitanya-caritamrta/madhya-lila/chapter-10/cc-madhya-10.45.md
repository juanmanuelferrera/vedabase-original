### CC Madhya 10.45

> চন্দনেশ্বর, সিংহেশ্বর, মুরারি ব্রাহ্মণ ৷\
> বিষ্ণুদাস, — ইঁহ ধ্যায়ে তোমার চরণ ৷৷ ৪৫ ৷৷ ॥৪৫॥

candaneśvara, siṁheśvara, murāri brāhmaṇa
viṣṇudāsa,—iṅha dhyāye tomāra caraṇa

*candaneśvara*—Candaneśvara; *siṁheśvara*—Siṁheśvara; *murāri brāhmaṇa*—o brāhmaṇa chamado Murāri; *viṣṇudāsa*—Viṣṇudāsa; *iṅha*—todos eles; *dhyāye*—meditam; *tomāra*—Teus; *caraṇa*—nos pés de lótus.

**"Aqui estão Candaneśvara, Siṁheśvara, Murāri Brāhmaṇa e Viṣṇudāsa. Todos eles estão constantemente ocupados em meditar em Teus pés de lótus.**
