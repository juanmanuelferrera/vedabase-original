### CC Madhya 10.60

> এত বলি’ প্রভু তাঁরে কৈল আলিঙ্গন ৷
> তাঁর পুত্র সব শিরে ধরিল চরণ ৷৷ ৬০ ৷৷ ॥৬০॥

eta bali' prabhu tāṅre kaila āliṅgana

tāṅra putra saba śire dharila caraṇa

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kaila*—fez; *āliṅgana*—abraço; *tāṅra putra*—seus filhos; *saba*—todos; *śire*—sobre a cabeça; *dharila*—colocou; *caraṇa*—Seus pés.

**Dizendo isto, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu abraçou Bhavānanda Rāya. O Senhor então tocou as cabeças de seus filhos com Seus pés de lótus.**
