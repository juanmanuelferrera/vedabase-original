### CC Madhya 1.236

> বলভদ্র ভট্টাচার্য, আর পণ্ডিত দামোদর ।\
> দুইজন-সঙ্গে প্রভু আইলা নীলাচল ॥২৩৬॥

balabhadra bhaṭṭācārya, āra paṇḍita dāmodara

dui-jana-saṅge prabhu āilā nīlācala

*balabhadra bhaṭṭācārya*—de nome Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *āra*—e; *paṇḍita dāmodara*—Dāmodara Paṇḍita; *dui-jana*—duas pessoas; *saṅge*—com; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *āilā*—voltou; *nīlācala*—para Jagannātha Purī.

**As duas pessoas que acompanharam Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu a Jagannātha Purī [Nīlācala] foram Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya e Dāmodara Paṇḍita.**
