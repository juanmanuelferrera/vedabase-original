### CC Madhya 1.101

> তবে সার্বভৌমে প্রভু প্রসাদ করিল ।\
> আপন-ঈশ্বরমূর্তি তাঁরে দেখাইল ॥১০১॥

tabe sārvabhaume prabhu prasāda karila

āpana-īśvara-mūrti tāṅre dekhāila

*tabe*—naquele momento; *sārvabhaume*—a Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *prasāda karila*—concedeu misericórdia; *āpana*—Sua própria; *īśvara-mūrti*—forma original como o Senhor; *tāṅre*—a ele; *dekhāila*—mostrou.

**Após este incidente, o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu concedeu Sua misericórdia a Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya, mostrando-lhe Sua forma original como o Senhor.**
