### CC Madhya 1.28

> যদ্যপি আপনি হয়ে প্রভু বলরাম ।
> তথাপি চৈতন্যের করে দাস-অভিমান ॥২৮॥

yadyapi āpani haye prabhu balarāma

tathāpi caitanyera kare dāsa-abhimāna

*yadyapi*—embora; *āpani*—pessoalmente; *haye*—seja; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *balarāma*—Balarāma; *tathāpi*—ainda; *caitanyera*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kare*—aceita; *dāsa-abhimāna*—a concepção de servo eterno.

**Embora Nityānanda Prabhu não seja outro senão o próprio Balarāma, Ele, ainda assim, sempre Se considera o servo eterno do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
