### CC Madhya 1.262

> নিত্যানন্দ-সঙ্গে যুক্তি করিয়া নিভৃতে ।
> তাঁরে পাঠাইলা গৌড়ে প্রেম প্রচারিতে ॥২৬২॥

nityānanda-saṅge yukti kariyā nibhṛte

tāṅre pāṭhāilā gauḍe prema pracārite

*nityānanda-saṅge*—com Nityānanda Prabhu; *yukti*—discussão; *kariyā*—fazendo; *nibhṛte*—em privado; *tāṅre*—a Ele; *pāṭhāilā*—enviou; *gauḍe*—para Bengala; *prema*—amor a Deus; *pracārite*—para pregar.

**Depois de enviar Sanātana Gosvāmī de volta a Vṛndāvana, o Senhor consultou-se em privado com Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu. Então O enviou a Bengala para pregar o amor a Deus.**
