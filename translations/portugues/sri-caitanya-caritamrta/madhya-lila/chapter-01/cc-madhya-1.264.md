### CC Madhya 1.264

> প্রদ্যুম্ন মিশ্রেরে প্রভু রামানন্দ-স্থানে ।
> কৃষ্ণকথা শুনাইল কহি’ তাঁর গুণে ॥২৬৪॥

pradyumna miśrere prabhu rāmānanda-sthāne

kṛṣṇa-kathā śunāila kahi' tāṅra guṇe

*pradyumna miśrere*—de nome Pradyumna Miśra; *prabhu*—o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rāmānanda-sthāne*—ao lugar de Rāmānanda Rāya; *kṛṣṇa-kathā*—tópicos do Senhor Śrī Kṛṣṇa; *śunāila*—fez ouvir; *kahi'*—explicando; *tāṅra*—de Rāmānanda Rāya; *guṇe*—as qualidades transcendentais.

**Após explicar as qualidades transcendentais de Rāmānanda Rāya, o Senhor enviou Pradyumna Miśra à residência de Rāmānanda Rāya, e Pradyumna Miśra aprendeu kṛṣṇa-kathā com ele.**
