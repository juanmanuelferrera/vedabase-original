### CC Madhya 1.183

> অর্ধরাত্রে দুই ভাই আইলা প্রভু-স্থানে ।
> প্রথমে মিলিলা নিত্যানন্দ-হরিদাস সনে ॥১৮৩॥

ardha-rātre dui bhāi āilā prabhu-sthāne

prathame mililā nityānanda-haridāsa sane

*ardha-rātre*—na calada da noite; *dui bhāi*—os dois irmãos; *āilā*—vieram; *prabhu-sthāne*—ao lugar do Senhor Caitanya; *prathame*—primeiro; *mililā*—encontraram-se; *nityānanda-haridāsa*—o Senhor Nityānanda e Haridāsa Ṭhākura; *sane*—com.

**Assim, na calada da noite, os dois irmãos, Dabira Khāsa e Sākara Mallika, foram ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu incógnitos. Primeiro encontraram-se com Nityānanda Prabhu e Haridāsa Ṭhākura.**
