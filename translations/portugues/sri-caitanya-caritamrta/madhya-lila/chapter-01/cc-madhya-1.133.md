### CC Madhya 1.133

> স্নানযাত্রা দেখি’ প্রভু সঙ্গে ভক্তগণ ।
> সবা লঞা কৈলা প্রভু গুণ্ডিচা মার্জন ॥১৩৩॥

snāna-yātrā dekhi' prabhu saṅge bhakta-gaṇa
sabā lañā kailā prabhu guṇḍicā mārjana

*snāna-yātrā*—a cerimônia do banho; *dekhi'*—vendo; *prabhu*—o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *saṅge*—com Ele; *bhakta-gaṇa*—os devotos; *sabā*—todos; *lañā*—levando; *kailā*—fez; *prabhu*—o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *guṇḍicā mārjana*—a lavagem e limpeza do templo de Guṇḍicā.

**Após assistir à cerimônia do banho do Senhor Jagannātha, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu lavou e limpou o templo de Śrī Guṇḍicā com o auxílio de muitos devotos.**
