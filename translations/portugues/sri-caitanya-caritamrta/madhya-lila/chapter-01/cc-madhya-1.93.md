### CC Madhya 1.93

> নিত্যানন্দ প্রভু মহাপ্রভু ভুলাইয়া ।\
> গঙ্গাতীরে লঞা আইলা ‘যমুনা’ বলিয়া ॥৯৩॥

nityānanda prabhu mahāprabhu bhulāiyā
gaṅgā-tīre lañā āilā 'yamunā' baliyā

*nityānanda prabhu*—o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhulāiyā*—desconcertando; *gaṅgā-tīre*—à margem do Ganges; *lañā*—levando; *āilā*—trouxe; *yamunā*—o rio Yamunā; *baliyā*—informando.

**Antes de tudo, o Senhor Nityānanda desconcertou Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, levando-O ao longo das margens do Ganges, dizendo que se tratava do rio Yamunā.**
