### CC Madhya 1.258

> তবে রূপ-গোসাঞির পুনরাগমন ।
> তাঁহার হৃদয়ে কৈল প্রভু শক্তি-সঞ্চারণ ॥২৫৮॥

tabe rūpa-gosāñira punar-āgamana

tāṅhāra hṛdaye kaila prabhu śakti-sañcāraṇa

*tabe*—em seguida; *rūpa-gosāñira*—de Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī; *punaḥ-āgamana*—vindo lá novamente; *tāṅhāra*—dele; *hṛdaye*—no coração; *kaila*—fez; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *śakti-sañcāraṇa*—invocação de poder transcendental.

**Em Jagannātha Purī, Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī encontrou o Senhor novamente, e o Senhor investiu o coração dele de todo poder transcendental.**
