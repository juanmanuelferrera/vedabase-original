### CC Madhya 1.163

> গোসাঞি কুলিয়া হৈতে চলিলা বৃন্দাবন ।\
> সঙ্গে সহস্রেক লোক যত ভক্তগণ ॥১৬৩॥

gosāñi kuliyā haite calilā vṛndāvana
saṅge sahasreka loka yata bhakta-gaṇa

*gosāñi*—o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kuliyā haite*—de Kuliyā; *calilā*—partiu; *vṛndāvana*—rumo a Vṛndāvana; *saṅge*—com Ele; *sahasreka*—milhares; *loka*—de pessoas; *yata*—todas; *bhakta-gaṇa*—os devotos.

**Quando o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu começou a partir de Kuliyā rumo a Vṛndāvana, milhares de homens estavam com Ele, e todos eles eram devotos.**
