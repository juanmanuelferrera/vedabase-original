### CC Madhya 23.126

> প্রভুর উপদেশামৃত শুনে যেই জন ।\
> অচিরাৎ মিলয়ে তাঁরে কৃষ্ণপ্রেমধন ॥১২৬॥

prabhura upadeśāmṛta śune yei jana

acirāt milaye tāṅre kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana

*prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *upadeśa-amṛta*—o néctar das instruções; *śune*—ouve; *yei jana*—todo aquele que; *acirāt*—sem demora; *milaye*—encontra; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana*—o tesouro do amor a Kṛṣṇa.

**Todo aquele que ouvir estas instruções dadas a Sanātana Gosvāmī pelo Senhor muito em breve alcançará o amor a Deus, Kṛṣṇa.**
