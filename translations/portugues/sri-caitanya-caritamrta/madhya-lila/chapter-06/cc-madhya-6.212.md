### CC Madhya 6.212

> তবে ভট্টাচার্যে প্রভু সুস্থির করিল ।
> স্থির হঞা ভট্টাচার্য বহু স্তুতি কৈল ॥২১২॥

tabe bhaṭṭācārye prabhu susthira karila
sthira hañā bhaṭṭācārya bahu stuti kaila

*tabe*—então; *bhaṭṭācārye*—a Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *su-sthira*—apaziguamento; *karila*—fez; *sthira hañā*—estando apaziguado; *bhaṭṭācārya*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *bahu*—muitas; *stuti*—orações; *kaila*—ofereceu.

**Depois disso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu apaziguou o Bhaṭṭācārya e, quando este se acalmou, ofereceu muitas orações ao Senhor.**
