### CC Madhya 6.215

> স্তুতি শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু নিজ বাসা আইলা ।
> ভট্টাচার্য আচার্য-দ্বারে ভিক্ষা করাইলা ॥২১৫॥

stuti śuni' mahāprabhu nija vāsā āilā
bhaṭṭācārya ācārya-dvāre bhikṣā karāilā

*stuti śuni'*—após ouvir as orações; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nija*—própria; *vāsā*—à residência; *āilā*—retornou; *bhaṭṭācārya*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *ācārya-dvāre*—por intermédio de Gopīnātha Ācārya; *bhikṣā*—o almoço; *karāilā*—induziu a aceitar.

**Após ouvir as orações oferecidas por Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu retornou à Sua residência, e o Bhaṭṭācārya, por intermédio de Gopīnātha Ācārya, induziu o Senhor a aceitar o almoço ali.**
