### CC Madhya 6.66

> গোপীনাথ প্রভু লঞা তাহাঁ বাসা দিল ।
> জল, জলপাত্রাদিক সর্ব সমাধান কৈল ॥৬৬॥

gopīnātha prabhu lañā tāhāṅ vāsā dila
jala, jala-pātrādika sarva sāmādhāna kaila

*gopīnātha*—Gopīnātha Ācārya; *prabhu*—o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *lañā*—levando; *tāhāṅ*—lá; *vāsā*—aposento; *dila*—deu; *jala*—água; *jala-pātra-ādika*—potes de água e outros recipientes; *sarva*—todos; *sāmādhāna*—os arranjos; *kaila*—fez.

**Assim, Gopīnātha Ācārya levou o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu aos aposentos residenciais e mostrou-Lhe onde encontrar água, banheiras e potes de água. De fato, ele providenciou tudo.**
