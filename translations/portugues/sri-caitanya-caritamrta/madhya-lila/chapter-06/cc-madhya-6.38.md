### CC Madhya 6.38

> হুঙ্কার করিয়া উঠে ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলি’ ।
> আনন্দে সার্বভৌম তাঁর লৈল পদধূলি ॥৩৮॥

huṅkāra kariyā uṭhe 'hari' 'hari' bali'

ānande sārvabhauma tāṅra laila pada-dhūli

*huṅkāra kariyā*—emitindo um som alto; *uṭhe*—levantou-Se; *hari hari bali'*—cantando Hari, Hari; *ānande*—em prazer; *sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *tāṅra*—Seus; *laila*—tomou; *pada-dhūli*—a poeira dos pés.

**Caitanya Mahāprabhu Se levantou e cantou bem alto: "Hari! Hari!" Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya ficou muito satisfeito ao ver o Senhor recobrar a consciência, e tomou a poeira dos pés de lótus do Senhor.**
