### CC Madhya 6.278

> যেই ভট্টাচার্য পড়ে পড়ায় মায়াবাদে ।
> তাঁর ঐছে বাক্য স্ফুরে চৈতন্য-প্রসাদে ॥২৭৮॥

yei bhaṭṭācārya pade paḍāya māyāvāde

tāṅra aiche vākya sphure caitanya-prasāde

*yei*—aquele; *bhaṭṭācārya*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *paḍe*—lê; *paḍāya*—ensina; *māyāvāde*—a filosofia do impersonalismo māyāvāda; *tāṅra*—sua; *aiche*—tal; *vākya*—explicação; *sphure*—manifestou-se; *caitanya-prasāde*—pela misericórdia do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**De fato, aquela mesma pessoa que estava acostumada a ler e a ensinar a filosofia māyāvāda agora chegava a odiar a palavra "mukti". Isso só foi possível pela misericórdia de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
