### CC Madhya 6.271

> প্রভু কহে, — ‘মুক্তিপদে’র আর অর্থ হয় ।\
> মুক্তিপদ-শব্দে ‘সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর’ কহয় ॥২৭১॥

prabhu kahe,--'mukti-pade'ra āra artha haya
mukti-pada-śabde 'sākṣāt īśvara' kahaya

*prabhu kahe*—o Senhor disse; *mukti-padera*—do termo mukti-pade; *āra*—outro; *artha*—significado; *haya*—há; *mukti-pada-śabde*—pela palavra mukti-pada; *sākṣāt*—diretamente; *īśvara*—a Suprema Personalidade de Deus; *kahaya*—diz-se.

**O Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu respondeu: "A palavra 'mukti-pade' tem outro significado. Mukti-pada refere-se diretamente à Suprema Personalidade de Deus.**
