### CC Madhya 6.257

> সার্বভৌম হৈলা প্রভুর ভক্ত একতান ।\
> মহাপ্রভুর সেবা-বিনা নাহি জানে আন ॥২৫৭॥

sārvabhauma hailā prabhura bhakta ekatāna

mahāprabhura sevā-vinā nāhi jāne āna

*sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *hailā*—tornou-se; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *bhakta*—um devoto; *ekatāna*—sem desvio; *mahāprabhura*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sevā*—serviço; *vinā*—exceto; *nāhi*—não; *jāne*—conhece; *āna*—nada mais.

**De fato, Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya tornou-se um devoto puro de Caitanya Mahāprabhu; ele não conhecia nada além do serviço ao Senhor.**
