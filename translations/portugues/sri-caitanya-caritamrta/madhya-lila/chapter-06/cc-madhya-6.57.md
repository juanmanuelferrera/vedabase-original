### CC Madhya 6.57

> শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু কৈল শ্রীবিষ্ণু স্মরণ ।
> ভট্টাচার্যে কহে কিছু বিনয় বচন ॥৫৭॥

śuni' mahāprabhu kaila śrī-viṣṇu smaraṇa
bhaṭṭācārye kahe kichu vinaya vacana

*śuni'*—ouvindo isso; *mahāprabhu*—o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kaila*—fez; *śrī-viṣṇu smaraṇa*—lembrar-se do Senhor Viṣṇu; *bhaṭṭācārye*—a Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *kahe*—fala; *kichu*—algumas; *vinaya vacana*—palavras muito humildes.

**Assim que Caitanya Mahāprabhu ouviu isso do Bhaṭṭācārya, lembrou-Se imediatamente do Senhor Viṣṇu e começou a falar-lhe humildemente da seguinte maneira.**
