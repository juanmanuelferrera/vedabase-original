### CC Madhya 6.164

> ভূমিরাপোঽনলো বায়ুঃ খং মনো বুদ্ধিরেব চ ।
> অহঙ্কার ইতীয়ং মে ভিন্না প্রকৃতিরষ্টধা ॥১৬৪॥

bhūmir āpo 'nalo vāyuḥ

khaṁ mano buddhir eva ca

ahaṅkāra itīyaṁ me

bhinnā prakṛtir aṣṭadhā

*bhūmiḥ*—terra; *āpaḥ*—água; *analaḥ*—fogo; *vāyuḥ*—ar; *kham*—éter; *manaḥ*—mente; *buddhiḥ*—inteligência; *eva*—certamente; *ca*—e; *ahaṅkāraḥ*—ego falso; *iti*—assim; *iyam*—esta; *me*—Minha; *bhinnā*—separada; *prakṛtiḥ*—energia; *aṣṭadhā*—óctupla.

**" 'Terra, água, fogo, ar, éter, mente, inteligência e ego falso são Minhas oito energias separadas.**
