### CC Madhya 6.63

> প্রভু কহে, — ‘মন্দির ভিতরে না যাইব ।\
> গরুড়ের পাশে রহি’ দর্শন করিব ।।’ ॥ ৬৩ ॥

prabhu kahe,--'mandira bhitare nā yāiba
garuḍera pāśe rahi' darśana kariba'

*prabhu kahe*—Śrī Caitanya respondeu; *mandira*—o templo; *bhitare*—dentro; *nā*—nunca; *yāiba*—entrarei; *garuḍera*—da coluna conhecida como Garuḍa-stambha; *pāśe*—ao lado; *rahi'*—permanecendo; *darśana*—ver; *kariba*—farei.

**O Senhor disse: "Nunca entrarei no templo, mas sempre contemplarei o Senhor do lado do Garuḍa-stambha."**
