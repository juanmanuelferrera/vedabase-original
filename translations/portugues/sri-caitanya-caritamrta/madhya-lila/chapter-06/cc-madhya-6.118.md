### CC Madhya 6.118

> আর দিন মহাপ্ৰভু ভট্টাচার্য-সনে ।\
> আনন্দে করিলা জগন্নাথ দরশনে ॥১১৮॥

āra dina mahāprabhu bhaṭṭācārya-sane
ānande karilā jagannātha daraśane

*āra dina*—no dia seguinte; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhaṭṭācārya-sane*—juntamente com Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *ānande*—com grande prazer; *karilā*—fez; *jagannātha*—ao Senhor Jagannātha; *daraśane*—visita.

**Na manhã seguinte, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya visitaram juntos o templo do Senhor Jagannātha. Ambos estavam de ânimo muito agradável.**
