### CC Madhya 6.2

> জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র জয় নিত্যানন্দ ।
> জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥২॥

jaya jaya gauracandra jaya nityānanda
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

*jaya jaya gauracandra*—todas as glórias ao Senhor Gaurahari; *jaya nityānanda*—todas as glórias a Nityānanda Prabhu; *jaya advaita-candra*—todas as glórias a Advaita Ācārya; *jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda*—todas as glórias aos devotos do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Todas as glórias ao Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu! Todas as glórias ao Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu! Todas as glórias a Advaita Ācārya! E todas as glórias aos devotos do Senhor Caitanya!**
