### CC Madhya 6.201

> আত্মনিন্দা করি’ লৈল প্ৰভুর শরণ ।
> কৃপা করিবারে তবে প্রভুর হৈল মন ॥২০১॥

ātma-nindā kari' laila prabhura śaraṇa
kṛpā karibāre tabe prabhura haila mana

*ātma-nindā*—autocondenação; *kari'*—fazendo; *laila*—tomou; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *śaraṇa*—refúgio; *kṛpā*—misericórdia; *karibāre*—conceder; *tabe*—então; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *haila*—foi; *mana*—a mente.

**Quando Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya denunciou a si mesmo como ofensor e tomou refúgio no Senhor, o Senhor desejou mostrar-lhe misericórdia.**
