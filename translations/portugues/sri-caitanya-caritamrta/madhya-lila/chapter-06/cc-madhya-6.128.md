### CC Madhya 6.128

> ভট্টাচার্য কহে, — না বুঝি’, হেন জ্ঞান যার  ।
> বুঝিবার লাগি’ সেহ পুছে পুনর্বার ॥১২৮॥

bhaṭṭācārya kahe,--nā bujhi', hena jñāna yāra
bujhibāra lāgi' seha puche punarbāra

*bhaṭṭācārya kahe*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya respondeu; *nā bujhi'*—não compreendendo; *hena*—este; *jñāna*—o conhecimento; *yāra*—de alguém; *bujhibāra lāgi'*—apenas para compreender; *seha*—ele também; *puche*—pergunta; *punaḥ-bāra*—de novo.

**Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya respondeu: "Aceito que não compreendes; ainda assim, mesmo aquele que não compreende indaga sobre o assunto.**
