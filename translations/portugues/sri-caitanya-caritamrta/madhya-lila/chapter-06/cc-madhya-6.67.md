### CC Madhya 6.67

> আর দিন গোপীনাথ প্রভু স্থানে গিয়া ।
> শয্যোত্থান দরশন করাইল লঞা ॥৬৭॥

āra dina gopīnātha prabhu sthāne giyā
śayyotthāna daraśana karāila lañā

*āra dina*—no dia seguinte; *gopīnātha*—Gopīnātha Ācārya; *prabhu*—do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sthāne*—ao lugar; *giyā*—indo; *śayyā-utthāna*—o levantar-se da cama do Senhor Jagannātha; *daraśana*—ver; *karāila*—fez; *lañā*—levando-O.

**No dia seguinte, Gopīnātha Ācārya levou o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu para ver o despertar matinal do Senhor Jagannātha.**
