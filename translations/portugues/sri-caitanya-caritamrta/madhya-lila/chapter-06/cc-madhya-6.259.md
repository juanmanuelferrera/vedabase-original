### CC Madhya 6.259

> একদিন সার্বভৌম প্রভু-আগে আইলা ।\
> নমস্কার করি’ শ্লোক পড়িতে লাগিলা ॥২৫৯॥

eka-dina sārvabhauma prabhu-āge āilā

namaskāra kari' śloka paḍite lāgilā

*eka-dina*—certo dia; *sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *prabhu-āge*—diante do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āilā*—veio; *namaskāra kari'*—após oferecer reverências; *śloka*—um verso; *paḍite lāgilā*—começou a recitar.

**Certo dia, Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya veio diante de Caitanya Mahāprabhu e, após oferecer reverências, começou a recitar um verso.**
