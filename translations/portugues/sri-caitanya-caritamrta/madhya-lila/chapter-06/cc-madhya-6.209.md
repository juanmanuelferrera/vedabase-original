### CC Madhya 6.209

> দেখি’ গোপীনাথাচার্য হরষিত-মন ৷\
> ভট্টাচার্যের নৃত্য দেখি’ হাসে প্রভুর গণ ॥২০৯॥

dekhi' gopīnāthācārya haraṣita-mana
bhaṭṭācāryera nṛtya dekhi' hāse prabhura gaṇa

*dekhi'*—vendo isso; *gopīnātha-ācārya*—Gopīnātha Ācārya; *haraṣita-mana*—uma mente satisfeita; *bhaṭṭācāryera*—de Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *nṛtya*—a dança; *dekhi'*—vendo; *hāse*—riem; *prabhura gaṇa*—os associados do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Enquanto Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya se encontrava nesse êxtase, Gopīnātha Ācārya estava muito satisfeito. Todos os associados de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu riram ao ver o Bhaṭṭācārya dançar daquela maneira.**
