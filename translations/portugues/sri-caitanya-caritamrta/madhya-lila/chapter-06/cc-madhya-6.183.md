### CC Madhya 6.183

> শুনি’ ভট্টাচার্য হৈল পরম বিস্মিত ।
> মুখে না নিঃসরে বাণী, হইলা স্তম্ভিত ॥১৮৩॥

śuni' bhaṭṭācārya haila parama vismita

mukhe nā niḥsare vāṇī, ha-ilā stambhita

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *bhaṭṭācārya*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *haila*—ficou; *parama*—muitíssimo; *vismita*—surpreso; *mukhe*—na boca; *nā*—não; *niḥsare*—vibra; *vāṇī*—palavras; *ha-ilā*—ficou; *stambhita*—atônito.

**Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya ficou muitíssimo surpreso ao ouvir isso. Ficou atônito e nada disse.**
