### CC Madhya 15.152

> এত শুনি’ আমি বড় মনে সুখ পাইলুঁ ।
> ইঁহারে উঠাঞা তবে আলিঙ্গন কৈলুঁ ॥১৫২॥

eta śuni' āmi baḍa mane sukha pāiluṅ

iṅhāre uṭhāñā tabe āliṅgana kailuṅ-

*eta śuni'*—ouvindo isto; *āmi*—eu; *baḍa*—muito grande; *mane*—na mente; *sukha*—felicidade; *pāiluṅ*—obtive; *iṅhāre*—a ele; *uṭhāñā*—erguendo; *tabe*—naquele momento; *āliṅgana kailuṅ*—abracei.

**"Ao ouvir isto, fiquei muito feliz. Ergui Murāri Gupta e o abracei.**
