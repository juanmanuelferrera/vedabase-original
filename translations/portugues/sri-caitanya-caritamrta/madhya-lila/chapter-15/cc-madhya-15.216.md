### CC Madhya 15.216

> কাঁজিবড়া, দুগ্ধ-চিড়া, দুগ্ধ-লক্লকী ।\
> আর যত পিঠা কৈল, কহিতে না শকি ॥২১৬॥

kāṅji-baḍā, dugdha-ciḍā, dugdha-laklakī

āra yata piṭhā kaila, kahite nā śaki

*kāṅji-baḍā*—bolos feitos com água azeda de arroz; *dugdha-ciḍā*—arroz-doce misturado com leite; *dugdha-laklakī*—outra preparação de leite e bolos para serem lambidos; *āra*—e; *yata*—vários tipos de; *piṭhā*—bolos; *kaila*—fez; *kahite*—descrever; *nā śaki*—não sou capaz.

**Havia kāṅji-baḍā, dugdha-ciḍā, dugdha-laklakī e vários bolos, que sou incapaz de descrever.**
