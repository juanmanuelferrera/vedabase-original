### CC Madhya 15.186

> প্রভু-পাশ আসি’ সার্বভৌম এক দিন  ।
> যোড়হাত করি’ কিছু কৈল নিবেদন ॥১৮৬॥

prabhu-pāśa āsi' sārvabhauma eka dina

yoḍa-hāta kari' kichu kaila nivedana

*prabhu-pāśa*—à presença de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āsi'*—vindo; *sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *eka dina*—certo dia; *yoḍa-hāta kari'*—de mãos postas; *kichu*—alguma; *kaila*—fez; *nivedana*—súplica.

**Certo dia, Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya apresentou-se diante de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu de mãos postas e fez um pedido.**
