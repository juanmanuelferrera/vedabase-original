### CC Madhya 15.249

> শুনিতেই ভট্টাচার্য উলটি’ চাহিল ।
> তাঁর অবধান দেখি’ অমোঘ পলাইল ॥২৪৯॥

śunitei bhaṭṭācārya ulaṭi' cāhila

tāṅra avadhāna dekhi' amogha palāila

*śunitei*—ouvindo; *bhaṭṭācārya*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *ulaṭi' cāhila*—voltou os olhos para ele; *tāṅra*—sua; *avadhāna*—atenção; *dekhi'*—vendo; *amogha*—Amogha; *palāila*—retirou-se.

**Tão logo Amogha disse isto, Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya voltou os olhos para ele. Ao ver a atitude do Bhaṭṭācārya, Amogha retirou-se imediatamente.**
