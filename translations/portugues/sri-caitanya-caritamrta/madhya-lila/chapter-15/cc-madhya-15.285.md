### CC Madhya 15.285

> অপরাধ’ নাহি, সদা লও কৃষ্ণনাম ।
> এত বলি’ প্রভু আইলা সার্বভৌম-স্থান ॥২৮৫॥

aparādha' nāhi, sadā lao kṛṣṇa-nāma

eta bali' prabhu āilā sārvabhauma-sthāna

*aparādha' nāhi*—não cometas ofensas; *sadā*—sempre; *lao*—canta; *kṛṣṇa-nāma*—o Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra; *eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āilā*—veio; *sārvabhauma-sthāna*—à residência de Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya.

**"Amogha, canta sempre o Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra e não cometas mais nenhuma ofensa." Após dar esta instrução a Amogha, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu dirigiu-se à casa de Sārvabhauma.**
