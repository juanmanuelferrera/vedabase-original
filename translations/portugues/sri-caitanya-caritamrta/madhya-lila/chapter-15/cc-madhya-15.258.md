### CC Madhya 15.258

> এত বলি’ মহাপ্ৰভু চলিলা ভবনে ।\
> ভট্টাচার্য তাঁর ঘরে গেলা তাঁর সনে ॥২৫৮॥

eta bali' mahāprabhu calilā bhavane

bhaṭṭācārya tāṅra ghare gelā tāṅra sane

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *calilā bhavane*—retornou à Sua residência; *bhaṭṭācārya*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *tāṅra ghare*—à Sua residência; *gelā*—foi; *tāṅra sane*—com Ele.

**Após dizer isto, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu partiu e retornou à Sua residência. Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya também O seguiu.**
