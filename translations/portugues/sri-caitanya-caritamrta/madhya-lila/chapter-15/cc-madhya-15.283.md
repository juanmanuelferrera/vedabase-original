### CC Madhya 15.283

> প্রভু আশ্বাসন করে স্পর্শি’ তার গাত্র ।
> সার্বভৌম-সম্বন্ধে তুমি মোর স্নেহপাত্র ॥২৮৩॥

prabhu āśvāsana kare sparśi' tāra gātra

sārvabhauma-sambandhe tumi mora sneha-pātra

*prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āśvāsana kare*—acalma; *sparśi'*—tocando; *tāra*—seu; *gātra*—corpo; *sārvabhauma-sambandhe*—por causa de uma relação com Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *tumi*—tu; *mora*—Meu; *sneha-pātra*—objeto de afeição.

**Depois disso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu acalmou Amogha tocando-lhe o corpo e dizendo: "Tu és o objeto de Minha afeição, pois és o genro de Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya.**
