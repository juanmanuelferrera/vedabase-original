### CC Madhya 15.259

> প্রভু-পদে পড়ি’ বহু আত্মনিন্দা কৈল ।
> তাঁরে শান্ত করি’ প্রভু ঘরে পাঠাইল ॥২৫৯॥

prabhu-pade paḍi' bahu ātma-nindā kaila

tāṅre śānta kari' prabhu ghare pāṭhāila

*prabhu-pade*—aos pés do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *paḍi'*—prostrando-se; *bahu*—muita; *ātma-nindā kaila*—fez autocensura; *tāṅre*—a ele; *śānta kari'*—acalmando; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ghare pāṭhāila*—enviou de volta à sua casa.

**Prostrando-se aos pés do Senhor, Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya disse muitas coisas em autocensura. O Senhor então o acalmou e o enviou de volta à sua casa.**
