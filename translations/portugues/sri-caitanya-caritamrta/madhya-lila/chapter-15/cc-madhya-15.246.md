### CC Madhya 15.246

> ভোজন দেখিতে চাহে, আসিতে না পারে  ।
> লাঠি-হাতে ভট্টাচার্য আছেন দুয়ারে ॥২৪৬॥

bhojana dekhite cāhe, āsite nā pāre

lāṭhī-hāte bhaṭṭācārya āchena duyāre

*bhojana*—o comer; *dekhite cāhe*—ele queria ver; *āsite nā pāre*—não podia entrar; *lāṭhī-hāte*—com um bastão na mão; *bhaṭṭācārya*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *āchena*—estava; *duyāre*—no umbral.

**Amogha queria ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu comer, mas não lhe era permitido entrar. De fato, o Bhaṭṭācārya guardava o umbral de sua casa com um bastão na mão.**
