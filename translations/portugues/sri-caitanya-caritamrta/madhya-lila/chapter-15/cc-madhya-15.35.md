### CC Madhya 15.35

> গোসাঞির আবেশ দেখি’ লোকে চমত্কার ।\
> সর্বলোক ‘জয়’ ‘জয়’ বলে বার বার ॥৩৫॥

gosāñira āveśa dekhi' loke camatkāra

sarva-loka jaya' jaya' bale bāra bāra

*gosāñira*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āveśa*—êxtase; *dekhi'*—ao ver; *loke*—todas as pessoas; *camatkāra*—atônitas; *sarva-loka*—todas as pessoas; *jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *bale*—dizem; *bāra bāra*—repetidas vezes.

**Todos ficaram muito atônitos ao ver o êxtase emocional de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, e todos começaram a cantar: "Todas as glórias! Todas as glórias!", repetidas vezes.**
