### CC Madhya 15.290

> প্রভু-পদ ধরি’ ভট্ট কহিতে লাগিলা ।\
> মরিত’ অমোঘ, তারে কেনে জীয়াইলা ॥২৯০॥

prabhu-pada dhari' bhaṭṭa kahite lāgilā

marita' amogha, tāre kene jīyāilā

*prabhu-pada*—os pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dhari'*—segurando; *bhaṭṭa*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *kahite lāgilā*—começou a falar; *marita' amogha*—Amogha teria morrido; *tāre*—a ele; *kene*—por que; *jīyāilā*—trouxeste de volta à vida.

**Segurando os pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, o Bhaṭṭācārya disse: "Por que trouxeste Amogha de volta à vida? Teria sido melhor que ele tivesse morrido."**
