### CC Madhya 15.133

> সার্বভৌম, বিদ্যাবাচস্পতি, — দুই ভাই  ।
> দুইজনে কৃপা করি’ কহেন গোসাঞি ॥১৩৩॥

sārvabhauma, vidyā-vācaspati,--dui bhāi

dui-jane kṛpā kari' kahena gosāñi

*sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *vidyā-vācaspati*—Vidyā-vācaspati; *dui bhāi*—dois irmãos; *dui-jane*—aos dois; *kṛpā kari'*—por Sua misericórdia imotivada; *kahena*—diz; *gosāñi*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Por Sua misericórdia imotivada, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu deu as seguintes instruções aos irmãos Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya e Vidyā-vācaspati.**
