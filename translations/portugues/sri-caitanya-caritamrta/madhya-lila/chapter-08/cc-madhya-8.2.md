### CC Madhya 8.2

> জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷\
> জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ৷৷ ২ ৷৷ ॥২॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

*jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *śrī-caitanya*—ao Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jaya*—todas as glórias; *nityānanda*—ao Senhor Nityānanda; *jaya advaita-candra*—todas as glórias a Advaita Ācārya; *jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda*—todas as glórias aos devotos de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Todas as glórias ao Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu! Todas as glórias ao Senhor Nityānanda! Todas as glórias a Advaita Ācārya! E todas as glórias a todos os devotos do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu!**
