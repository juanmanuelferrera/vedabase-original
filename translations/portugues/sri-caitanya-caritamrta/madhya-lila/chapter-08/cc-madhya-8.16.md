### CC Madhya 8.16

> প্রভু তাঁরে দেখি’ জানিল — এই রামরায় ৷
> তাঁহারে মিলিতে প্রভুর মন উঠি’ ধায় ৷৷ ১৬ ৷৷ ॥১৬॥

prabhu tāṅre dekhi' jānila--ei rāma-rāya

tāṅhāre milite prabhura mana uṭhi' dhāya

*prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—ele; *dekhi'*—vendo; *jānila*—pôde compreender; *ei*—este; *rāma-rāya*—Śrīla Rāmānanda Rāya; *tāṅhāre*—ele; *milite*—encontrar; *prabhura*—do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mana*—mente; *uṭhi'*—erguendo-se; *dhāya*—corre atrás.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu pôde compreender que a pessoa que viera banhar-se no rio era Rāmānanda Rāya. O Senhor desejava tanto encontrá-lo que Sua mente imediatamente começou a correr atrás dele.**
