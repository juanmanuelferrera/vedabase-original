### CC Madhya 8.17

> তথাপি ধৈর্য ধরি’ প্রভু রহিলা বসিয়া ৷
> রামানন্দ আইলা অপূর্ব সন্ন্যাসী দেখিয়া ৷৷ ১৭ ৷৷ ॥১৭॥

tathāpi dhairya dhari' prabhu rahilā vasiyā

rāmānanda āilā apūrva sannyāsī dekhiyā

*tathāpi*—ainda assim; *dhairya dhari'*—mantendo a paciência; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rahilā*—permaneceu; *vasiyā*—sentado; *rāmānanda*—Śrīla Rāmānanda Rāya; *āilā*—chegou; *apūrva*—maravilhoso; *sannyāsī*—renunciante; *dekhiyā*—vendo.

**Embora Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu corresse atrás dele mentalmente, Ele permaneceu pacientemente sentado. Rāmānanda Rāya, vendo o maravilhoso sannyāsī, dirigiu-se então a Ele.**
