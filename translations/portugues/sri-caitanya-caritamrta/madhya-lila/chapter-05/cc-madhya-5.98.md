### CC Madhya 5.98

> উলটিয়া আমা তুমি না করিহ দরশনে ।
> আমাকে দেখিলে, আমি রহিব সেই স্থানে ॥৯৮॥

ulaṭiyā āmā tumi nā kariha daraśane

āmāke dekhile, āmi rahiba sei sthāne

*ulaṭiyā*—voltando teu rosto; *āmā*—a Mim; *tumi*—tu; *nā*—não; *kariha*—faças; *daraśane*—ver; *āmāke*—a Mim; *dekhile*—se Me vires; *āmi*—Eu; *rahiba*—ficarei; *sei sthāne*—nesse exato lugar.

**O Senhor prosseguiu: "Não tentes ver-Me virando-te para trás. Logo que Me vires, ficarei imóvel nesse exato lugar.**
