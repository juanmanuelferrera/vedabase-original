### CC Madhya 5.79

> বড়বিপ্রের মনে, — ‘কৃষ্ণ বড় দয়াবান্ ।\
> অবশ্য মোর বাক্য তেঁহো করিবে প্রমাণ ।।’ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

baḍa-viprera mane,--'kṛṣṇa baḍa dayāvān

avaśya mora vākya teṅho karibe pramāṇa'

*baḍa-viprera mane*—na mente do brāhmaṇa idoso; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *baḍa*—muito; *dayāvān*—misericordioso; *avaśya*—certamente; *mora*—minha; *vākya*—palavra; *teṅho*—Ele; *karibe*—fará; *pramāṇa*—prova.

**O brāhmaṇa idoso pensou: "Visto que o Senhor Kṛṣṇa é muito misericordioso, Ele certamente virá comprovar minha declaração."**
