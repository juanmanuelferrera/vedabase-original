### CC Madhya 20.327

> ব্রহ্মসাবর্ণ্যে ‘বিষ্বক্সেন’, ‘ধর্মসেতু’ ধর্মসাবর্ণ্যে ।\
> রুদ্রসাবর্ণ্যে ‘সুধামা’, ‘যোগেশ্বর’ দেবসাবর্ণ্যে ॥৩২৭॥

brahma-sāvarṇye 'viṣvaksena', 'dharmasetu' dharma-sāvarṇye

rudra-sāvarṇye 'sudhāmā', 'yogeśvara' deva-sāvarṇye

*brahma-sāvarṇye*—no Brahma-sāvarṇya-manvantara; *viṣvaksena*—o avatāra chamado Viṣvaksena; *dharmasetu*—o avatāra chamado Dharmasetu; *dharma-sāvarṇye*—no Dharma-sāvarṇya-manvantara; *rudra-sāvarṇye*—no Rudra-sāvarṇya-manvantara; *sudhāmā*—o avatāra chamado Sudhāmā; *yogeśvara*—o avatāra chamado Yogeśvara; *deva-sāvarṇye*—no Deva-sāvarṇya-manvantara.

**"No Brahma-sāvarṇya-manvantara, o avatāra chamava-se Viṣvaksena, e no Dharma-sāvarṇya, chamava-se Dharmasetu. No Rudra-sāvarṇya chamava-se Sudhāmā, e no Deva-sāvarṇya, chamava-se Yogeśvara.**
