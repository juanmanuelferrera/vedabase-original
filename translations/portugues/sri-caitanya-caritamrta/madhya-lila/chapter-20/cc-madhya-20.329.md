### CC Madhya 20.329

> যুগাবতার এবে শুন, সনাতন ।
> সত্য-ত্রেতা-দ্বাপর-কলি-যুগের গণন ॥৩২৯॥

yugāvatāra ebe śuna, sanātana

satya-tretā-dvāpara-kali-yugera gaṇana

*yuga-avatāra*—encarnação dos milênios; *ebe*—agora; *śuna*—ouve; *sanātana*—ó Sanātana Gosvāmī; *satya-tretā-dvāpara-kali-yugera*—de Satya-yuga, Tretā-yuga, Dvāpara-yuga e Kali-yuga; *gaṇana*—a ordem cronológica.

**"Ó Sanātana, ouve agora de Mim a respeito dos yuga-avatāras, as encarnações para os milênios. Antes de tudo, há quatro yugas – Satya-yuga, Tretā-yuga, Dvāpara-yuga e Kali-yuga.**
