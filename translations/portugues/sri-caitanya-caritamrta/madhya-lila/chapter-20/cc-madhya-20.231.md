### CC Madhya 20.231

> শ্রীধর — পদ্মচক্রগদাশঙ্খকর ।\
> হৃষীকেশ — গদাচক্রপদ্মশঙ্খধর ॥২৩১॥

śrīdhara--padma-cakra-gadā-śaṅkha-kara

hṛṣīkeśa--gadā-cakra-padma-śaṅkha-dhara

*śrīdhara*—o Senhor Śrīdhara; *padma*—lótus; *cakra*—disco; *gadā*—maça; *śaṅkha*—búzio; *kara*—nas mãos; *hṛṣīkeśa*—o Senhor Hṛṣīkeśa; *gadā*—maça; *cakra*—disco; *padma*—lótus; *śaṅkha*—búzio; *dhara*—segurando.

**"O Senhor Śrīdhara segura o lótus, o disco, a maça e o búzio. O Senhor Hṛṣīkeśa segura a maça, o disco, o lótus e o búzio.**
