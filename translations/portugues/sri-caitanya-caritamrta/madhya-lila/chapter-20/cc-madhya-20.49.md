### CC Madhya 20.49

> তেঁহো কহে, — এক ‘দরবেশ’ আছে দ্বারে ।
> ‘তাঁরে আন’ প্রভুর বাক্যে কহিল তাঁহারে ॥৪৯॥

teṅho kahe,--eka 'daraveśa' āche dvāre

'tāṅre āna' prabhura vākye kahila tāṅhāre

*teṅho kahe*—ele respondeu; *eka daraveśa*—um mendicante muçulmano; *āche*—há; *dvāre*—à porta; *tāṅre āna*—traze-o; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *vākye*—a ordem; *kahila*—disse; *tāṅhāre*—a ele.

**Candraśekhara respondeu: "Há um mendicante muçulmano." Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu imediatamente disse: "Por favor, traze-o aqui." Candraśekhara então falou a Sanātana Gosvāmī, que ainda estava sentado junto à porta.**
