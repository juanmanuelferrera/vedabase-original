### CC Madhya 20.56

> প্রভু কহে, — “তোমা স্পর্শি আত্ম পবিত্রিতে ।\
> ভক্তি-বলে পার তুমি ব্রহ্মাণ্ড শোধিতে ॥৫৬॥

prabhu kahe,--"tomā sparśi ātma pavitrite

bhakti-bale pāra tumi brahmāṇḍa śodhite

*prabhu kahe*—o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu respondeu; *tomā sparśi*—toco-te; *ātma pavitrite*—para purificar-Me; *bhakti-bale*—a força de teu serviço devocional; *pāra*—és capaz; *tumi*—tu; *brahmāṇḍa*—o universo inteiro; *śodhite*—de purificar.

**O Senhor respondeu: "Toco-te apenas para purificar-Me, pois, pela força de teu serviço devocional, tu podes purificar o universo inteiro.**
