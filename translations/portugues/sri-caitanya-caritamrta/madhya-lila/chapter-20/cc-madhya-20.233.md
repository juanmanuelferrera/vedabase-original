### CC Madhya 20.233

> পুরুষোত্তম — চক্রপদ্মশঙ্খগদাধর ।
> শ্রীঅচ্যুত — গদাপদ্মচক্রশঙ্খধর ॥২৩৩॥

puruṣottama--cakra-padma-śaṅkha-gadā-dhara

śrī-acyuta--gadā-padma-cakra-śaṅkha-dhara

*puruṣottama*—o Senhor Puruṣottama; *cakra*—disco; *padma*—lótus; *śaṅkha*—búzio; *gadā*—maça; *dhara*—segurando; *śrī-acyuta*—o Senhor Acyuta; *gadā*—maça; *padma*—lótus; *cakra*—disco; *śaṅkha*—búzio; *dhara*—segurando.

**"O Senhor Puruṣottama segura o disco, o lótus, o búzio e a maça. O Senhor Acyuta segura a maça, o lótus, o disco e o búzio.**
