### CC Madhya 20.44

> যত্ন করি’ তেঁহো এক ভোটকম্বল দিল ।
> গঙ্গা পার করি’ দিল — গোসাঞি চলিল ॥৪৪॥

yatna kari' teṅho eka bhoṭa-kambala dila

gaṅgā pāra kari' dila--gosāñi calila

*yatna kari'*—com grande cuidado; *teṅho*—ele (Śrīkānta); *eka*—um; *bhoṭa-kambala*—cobertor de lã; *dila*—deu; *gaṅgā pāra kari' dila*—fê-lo atravessar o rio Ganges; *gosāñi calila*—Sanātana Gosvāmī partiu.

**Com grande cuidado, Śrīkānta deu-lhe um cobertor de lã e ajudou-o a atravessar o Ganges. Assim, Sanātana Gosvāmī partiu novamente.**
