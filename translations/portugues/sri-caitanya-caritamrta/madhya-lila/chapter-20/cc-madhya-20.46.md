### CC Madhya 20.46

> চন্দ্রশেখরের ঘরে আসি’ দ্বারেতে বসিলা  ।
> মহাপ্রভু জানি’ চন্দ্রশেখরে কহিলা ॥৪৬॥

candraśekharera ghare āsi' dvārete vasilā

mahāprabhu jāni' candraśekhare kahilā

*candraśekharera ghare*—à casa de Candraśekhara; *āsi'*—indo; *dvārete*—à porta; *vasilā*—sentou-se; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jāni'*—sabendo; *candraśekhare*—a Candraśekhara; *kahilā*—disse.

**Sanātana Gosvāmī então foi à casa de Candraśekhara e sentou-se junto à porta. Compreendendo o que se passava, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu falou a Candraśekhara.**
