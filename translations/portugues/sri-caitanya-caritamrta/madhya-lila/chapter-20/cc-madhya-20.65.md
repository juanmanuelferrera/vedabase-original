### CC Madhya 20.65

> ‘কেমনে ছুটিলা’ বলি প্রভু প্রশ্ন কৈলা ।
> আদ্যোপান্ত সব কথা তেঁহো শুনাইলা ॥৬৫॥

'kemane chuṭilā' bali prabhu praśna kailā

ādyopānta saba kathā teṅho śunāilā

*kemane chuṭilā*—como foste libertado; *bali*—dizendo; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *praśna kailā*—perguntou; *ādya-upānta*—do começo ao fim; *saba*—toda; *kathā*—a narração; *teṅho*—ele; *śunāilā*—descreveu.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu então perguntou a Sanātana Gosvāmī: "Como foste libertado da prisão?" Sanātana descreveu então toda a história, do começo ao fim.**
