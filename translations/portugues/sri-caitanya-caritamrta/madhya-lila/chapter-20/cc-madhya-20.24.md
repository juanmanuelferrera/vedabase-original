### CC Madhya 20.24

> ‘তোমার ঠাঞি জানি কিছু দ্রব্য আছয়’  ।
> ঈশান কহে, — ‘মোর ঠাঞি সাত মোহর হয়’ ॥২৪॥

'tomāra ṭhāñi jāni kichu dravya āchaya'

īśāna kahe,--'mora ṭhāñi sāta mohara haya'

*tomāra ṭhāñi*—em tua posse; *jāni*—compreendo; *kichu*—algum; *dravya*—objeto valioso; *āchaya*—há; *īśāna kahe*—Īśāna respondeu; *mora ṭhāñi*—em minha posse; *sāta mohara*—sete moedas de ouro; *haya*—há.

**Sanātana perguntou a seu servo: "Īśāna, creio que tens contigo alguns objetos valiosos." Īśāna respondeu: "Sim, tenho sete moedas de ouro."**
