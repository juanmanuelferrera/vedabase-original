### CC Madhya 20.67

tapana-miśrere āra candraśekharere

prabhu-ājñāya sanātana mililā doṅhāre

*tapana-miśrere*—a Tapana Miśra; *āra*—e; *candraśekharere*—a Candraśekhara; *prabhu-ājñāya*—pela ordem de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sanātana*—Sanātana; *mililā*—encontrou; *doṅhāre*—ambos.

**Pela ordem de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Sanātana Gosvāmī encontrou tanto Tapana Miśra quanto Candraśekhara.**
