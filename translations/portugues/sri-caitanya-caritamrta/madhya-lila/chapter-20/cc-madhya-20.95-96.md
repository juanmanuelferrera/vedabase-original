### CC Madhya 20.95-96

> পূর্বে যৈছে রায়-পাশে প্রভু প্রশ্ন কৈলা ।\
> তাঁর শক্ত্যে রামানন্দ তাঁর উত্তর দিলা ॥৯৫॥\
> ইহাঁ প্রভুর শক্ত্যে প্রশ্ন করে সনাতন ।\
> আপনে মহাপ্রভু করে ‘তত্ত্ব’-নিরূপণ ॥৯৬॥

pūrve yaiche rāya-pāśe prabhu praśna kailā

tāṅra śaktye rāmānanda tāṅra uttara dilā

ihāṅ prabhura śaktye praśna kare sanātana

āpane mahāprabhu kare 'tattva'-nirūpaṇa

*pūrve*—antigamente; *yaiche*—como; *rāya-pāśe*—a Rāmānanda Rāya; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *praśna kailā*—indagou; *tāṅra śaktye*—somente por Sua misericórdia; *rāmānanda*—Rāmānanda Rāya; *tāṅra*—suas; *uttara*—respostas; *dilā*—deu; *ihāṅ*—aqui; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śaktye*—pela força; *praśna*—perguntas; *kare*—faz; *sanātana*—Sanātana Gosvāmī; *āpane*—pessoalmente; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kare*—faz; *tattva*—a verdade; *nirūpaṇa*—discernindo.

**Antigamente, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu fez perguntas espirituais a Rāmānanda Rāya, e, pela misericórdia imotivada do Senhor, Rāmānanda Rāya pôde responder de modo adequado. Agora, pela misericórdia do Senhor, Sanātana Gosvāmī interrogou o Senhor, e Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu pessoalmente forneceu a verdade.**
