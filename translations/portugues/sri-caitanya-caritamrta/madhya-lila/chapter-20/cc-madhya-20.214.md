### CC Madhya 20.214

> এক ‘কৃষ্ণলোক’ হয় ত্রিবিধপ্রকার ।
> গোকুলাখ্য, মথুরাখ্য, দ্বারকাখ্য আর ॥২১৪॥

eka 'kṛṣṇaloka' haya trividha-prakāra

gokulākhya, mathurākhya, dvārakākhya āra

*eka*—um; *kṛṣṇa-loka*—o planeta conhecido como Kṛṣṇaloka; *haya*—há; *tri-vidha-prakāra*—em três divisões diferentes; *gokula-ākhya*—Gokula; *mathurā-ākhya*—Mathurā; *dvārakā-ākhya*—Dvārakā; *āra*—e.

**"O planeta de Kṛṣṇaloka divide-se em três seções: Gokula, Mathurā e Dvārakā.**
