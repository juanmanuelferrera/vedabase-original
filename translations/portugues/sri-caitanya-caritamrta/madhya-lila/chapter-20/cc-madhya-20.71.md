### CC Madhya 20.71

> সেই বস্ত্র সনাতন না কৈল অঙ্গীকার ।
> শুনিয়া প্রভুর মনে আনন্দ অপার ॥৭১॥

sei vastra sanātana nā kaila aṅgīkāra

śuniyā prabhura mane ānanda apāra

*sei vastra*—aquela roupa nova; *sanātana*—Sanātana Gosvāmī; *nā kaila*—não fez; *aṅgīkāra*—aceitação; *śuniyā*—ao ouvir; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mane*—na mente; *ānanda apāra*—felicidade ilimitada.

**Candraśekhara ofereceu um conjunto de roupas novas a Sanātana Gosvāmī, mas Sanātana não as aceitou. Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ouviu a notícia disso, ficou ilimitadamente feliz.**
