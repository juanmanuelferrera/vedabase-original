### CC Madhya 20.79

> মহারাষ্ট্রীয় দ্বিজে প্রভু মিলাইলা সনাতনে  ।
> সেই বিপ্র তাঁরে কৈল মহা-নিমন্ত্রণে ॥৭৯॥

mahārāṣṭrīya dvije prabhu milāilā sanātane

sei vipra tāṅre kaila mahā-nimantraṇe

*mahā-rāṣṭrīya*—de Mahārāṣṭra; *dvije*—o brāhmaṇa; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *milāilā*—apresentou; *sanātane*—a Sanātana Gosvāmī; *sei*—aquele; *vipra*—brāhmaṇa; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kaila*—fez; *mahā*—completo; *nimantraṇe*—convite.

**Quando Caitanya Mahāprabhu apresentou o brāhmaṇa mahārāṣṭrīya a Sanātana, o brāhmaṇa imediatamente convidou Sanātana Gosvāmī para refeições completas.**
