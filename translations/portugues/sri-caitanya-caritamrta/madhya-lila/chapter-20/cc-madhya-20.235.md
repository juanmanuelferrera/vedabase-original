### CC Madhya 20.235

> শ্রীহরি — শঙ্খচক্রপদ্মগদাকর ।\
> শ্রীকৃষ্ণ — শঙ্খগদাপদ্মচক্রকর ॥২৩৫॥

śrī-hari--śaṅkha-cakra-padma-gadā-kara

śrī-kṛṣṇa--śaṅkha-gadā-padma-cakra-kara

*śrī-hari*—o Senhor Hari; *śaṅkha*—búzio; *cakra*—disco; *padma*—lótus; *gadā*—maça; *kara*—na mão; *śrī-kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *śaṅkha*—búzio; *gadā*—maça; *padma*—lótus; *cakra*—disco; *kara*—nas mãos.

**"Śrī Hari segura o búzio, o disco, o lótus e a maça. O Senhor Śrī Kṛṣṇa segura o búzio, a maça, o lótus e o disco.**
