### CC Madhya 20.75

> ভিক্ষা করি’ মহাপ্রভু বিশ্রাম করিল ।
> মিশ্র প্রভুর শেষপাত্র সনাতনে দিল ॥৭৫॥

bhikṣā kari' mahāprabhu viśrāma karila

miśra prabhura śeṣa-pātra sanātane dila

*bhikṣā kari'*—após fazer Seu almoço; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *viśrāma karila*—descansou; *miśra*—Tapana Miśra; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śeṣa-pātra*—o prato de restos; *sanātane dila*—entregou a Sanātana.

**Após comer, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu descansou por algum tempo. Tapana Miśra deu então a Sanātana Gosvāmī os restos do alimento deixado por Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
