### CC Madhya 20.55

> শ্রীহস্তে করেন তাঁর অঙ্গ সম্মার্জন ।\
> তেঁহো কহে, — ‘মোরে, প্রভু, না কর স্পর্শন’ ॥৫৫॥

śrī-haste karena tāṅra aṅga sammārjana

teṅho kahe,--'more, prabhu, nā kara sparśana'

*śrī-haste*—com a mão espiritual; *karena*—faz; *tāṅra aṅga*—de seu corpo; *sammārjana*—a limpeza; *teṅho kahe*—ele disse; *more*—em mim; *prabhu*—meu Senhor; *nā kara sparśana*—não toques.

**Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu começou a limpar o corpo de Sanātana Gosvāmī com Sua própria mão transcendental, Sanātana Gosvāmī disse: "Ó meu Senhor, por favor, não me toques."**
