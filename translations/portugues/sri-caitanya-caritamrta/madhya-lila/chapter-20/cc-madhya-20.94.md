### CC Madhya 20.94

> প্রসন্ন হঞা প্রভু তাঁরে কৃপা কৈল ।\
> তাঁর কৃপায় প্রশ্ন করিতে তাঁর শক্তি হৈল ॥৯৪॥

prasanna hañā prabhu tāṅre kṛpā kaila

tāṅra kṛpāya praśna karite tāṅra śakti haila

*prasanna hañā*—estando muito satisfeito; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kṛpā kaila*—outorgou Sua misericórdia imotivada; *tāṅra kṛpāya*—por Sua misericórdia; *praśna karite*—indagar; *tāṅra*—dele; *śakti haila*—houve força.

**Estando satisfeito com Sanātana Gosvāmī, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu outorgou-lhe Sua misericórdia imotivada. Pela misericórdia do Senhor, Sanātana Gosvāmī recebeu a força espiritual para indagar dEle.**
