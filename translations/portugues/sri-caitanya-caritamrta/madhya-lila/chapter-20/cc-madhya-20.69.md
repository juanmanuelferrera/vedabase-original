### CC Madhya 20.69

> চন্দ্রশেখরেরে প্রভু কহে বোলাঞা ।
> ‘এই বেষ দূর কর, যাহ ইঁহারে লঞা’ ॥৬৯॥

candraśekharere prabhu kahe bolāñā

'ei veṣa dūra kara, yāha iṅhāre lañā'

*candraśekharere*—a Candraśekhara; *prabhu kahe*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse; *bolāñā*—chamando; *ei veṣa*—este tipo de roupa; *dūra kara*—tira; *yāha*—vai; *iṅhāre lañā*—levando-o contigo.

**Depois disso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu chamou Candraśekhara e pediu-lhe que levasse Sanātana Gosvāmī consigo. Também lhe pediu que retirasse a roupa que Sanātana usava no momento.**
