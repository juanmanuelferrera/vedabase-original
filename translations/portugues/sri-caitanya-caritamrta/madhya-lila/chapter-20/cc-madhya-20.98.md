### CC Madhya 20.98

> তবে সনাতন প্রভুর চরণে ধরিয়া ।\
> দৈন্য বিনতি করে দন্তে তৃণ লঞা ॥৯৮॥

tabe sanātana prabhura caraṇe dhariyā

dainya vinati kare dante tṛṇa lañā

*tabe*—em seguida; *sanātana*—Sanātana Gosvāmī; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *caraṇe*—os pés de lótus; *dhariyā*—agarrando; *dainya*—humildade; *vinati*—curvando-se; *kare*—faz; *dante*—nos dentes; *tṛṇa*—uma palha; *lañā*—tomando.

**Pondo uma palha na boca e prostrando-se, Sanātana Gosvāmī agarrou os pés de lótus de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e falou humildemente como segue.**
