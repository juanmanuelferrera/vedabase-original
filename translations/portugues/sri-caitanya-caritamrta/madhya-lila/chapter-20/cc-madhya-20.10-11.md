### CC Madhya 20.10-11

> সনাতন কহে, — “তুমি না কর রাজ-ভয় ।
> দক্ষিণ গিয়াছে যদি লেউটি’ আওয়য় ॥১০॥
> তাঁহারে কহিও — সেই বাহ্যকৃত্যে গেল ।
> গঙ্গার নিকট গঙ্গা দেখি’ ঝাঁপ দিল ॥১১॥

sanātana kahe,--"tumi nā kara rāja-bhaya
dakṣiṇa giyāche yadi leuṭi' āoyaya
tāṅhāre kahio--sei bāhya-kṛtye gela
gaṅgāra nikaṭa gaṅgā dekhi' jhāṅpa dila

*sanātana kahe*—Sanātana respondeu; *tumi*—tu; *nā*—não; *kara*—faze; *rāja-bhaya*—medo do governo; *dakṣiṇa*—para o sul; *giyāche*—foi; *yadi*—se; *leuṭi'*—retornando; *āoyaya*—vier; *tāṅhāre*—a ele; *kahio*—dize; *sei*—ele; *bāhya-kṛtye*—para evacuar; *gela*—foi; *gaṅgāra nikaṭa*—perto da margem do Ganges; *gaṅgā dekhi'*—vendo o Ganges; *jhāṅpa dila*—pulou.

**Sanātana respondeu: "Não há perigo. O Nawab foi para o sul. Se ele retornar, dizei-lhe que Sanātana foi defecar perto da margem do Ganges e que, tão logo viu o Ganges, pulou dentro dele.**
