### CC Madhya 20.89

> প্রভু কহে, — ‘তোমার ভোটকম্বল কোথা গেল ?’\
> প্রভুপদে সব কথা গোসাঞি কহিল ॥৮৯॥

prabhu kahe,--'tomāra bhoṭa-kambala kothā gela?'

prabhu-pade saba kathā gosāñi kahila

*prabhu kahe*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse; *tomāra*—teu; *bhoṭa-kambala*—cobertor de lã; *kothā gela*—para onde foi; *prabhu-pade*—aos pés de lótus do Senhor Caitanya; *saba*—toda; *kathā*—narração; *gosāñi*—Sanātana Gosvāmī; *kahila*—contou.

**Quando Sanātana Gosvāmī retornou, o Senhor perguntou: "Onde está teu cobertor de lã?" Sanātana Gosvāmī então narrou toda a história ao Senhor.**
