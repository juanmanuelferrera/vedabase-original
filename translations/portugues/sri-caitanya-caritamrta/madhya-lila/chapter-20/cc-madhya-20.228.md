### CC Madhya 20.228

> শ্ৰীমাধব — গদাচক্রশঙ্খপদ্মকর ।
> শ্রীগোবিন্দ — চক্রগদাপদ্মশঙ্খধর ॥২২৮॥

śrī-mādhava--gadā-cakra-śaṅkha-padma-kara

śrī-govinda--cakra-gadā-padma-śaṅkha-dhara

*śrī-mādhava*—o Senhor Mādhava; *gadā*—maça; *cakra*—disco; *śaṅkha*—búzio; *padma*—lótus; *kara*—nas mãos; *śrī-govinda*—o Senhor Govinda; *cakra*—disco; *gadā*—maça; *padma*—lótus; *śaṅkha*—búzio; *dhara*—segurando.

**"O Senhor Mādhava segura a maça, o disco, o búzio e o lótus. O Senhor Govinda segura o disco, a maça, o lótus e o búzio.**
