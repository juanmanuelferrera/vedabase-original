### CC Madhya 19.97

> ‘আগে কহ’ — প্রভু-বাক্যে উপাধ্যায় কহিল ।\
> রঘুপতি উপাধ্যায় নমস্কার কৈল ॥৯৭॥

'āge kaha'--prabhu-vākye upādhyāya kahila

raghupati upādhyāya namaskāra kaila

*āge kaha*—por favor, fala mais; *prabhu-vākye*—a pedido de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *upādhyāya*—Raghupati Upādhyāya; *kahila*—disse; *raghupati upādhyāya*—Raghupati Upādhyāya; *namaskāra kaila*—ofereceu reverências a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Quando o Senhor pediu a Raghupati Upādhyāya que recitasse mais, ele imediatamente ofereceu seus respeitos ao Senhor e atendeu a Seu pedido.**
