### CC Madhya 19.253

> মহারাষ্ট্রীয় বিপ্র আসি’ তাঁহারে মিলিলা ।\
> প্রভু তাঁরে স্নেহ করি’ কৃপা প্রকাশিলা ॥২৫৩॥

mahārāṣṭrīya vipra āsi' tāṅhāre mililā

prabhu tāṅre sneha kari' kṛpā prakāśilā

*mahārāṣṭrīya vipra*—o brāhmaṇa mahārāṣṭrīya; *āsi'*—vindo; *tāṅhāre*—ele; *mililā*—encontrou; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *sneha kari'*—demonstrando Sua afeição; *kṛpā prakāśilā*—distribuiu Sua misericórdia.

**O brāhmaṇa mahārāṣṭrīya veio, e o Senhor o encontrou. Por afeição, o Senhor concedeu-lhe Sua misericórdia.**
