### CC Madhya 19.78

> যমুনার জল দেখি’ চিক্কণ শ্যামল ।
> প্রেমাবেশে মহাপ্রভু হইলা বিহ্বল ॥৭৮॥

yamunāra jala dekhi' cikkaṇa śyāmala

premāveśe mahāprabhu ha-ilā vihvala

*yamunāra*—do rio Yamunā; *jala*—a água; *dekhi'*—vendo; *cikkaṇa*—lustrosa; *śyāmala*—enegrecida; *prema-āveśe*—em amor extático; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ha-ilā*—ficou; *vihvala*—perturbado.

**Ao atravessar o rio Yamunā, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu viu a água negra e lustrosa e imediatamente ficou perturbado de amor extático.**
