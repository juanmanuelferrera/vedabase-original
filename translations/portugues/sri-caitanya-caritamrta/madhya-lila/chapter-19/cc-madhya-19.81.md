### CC Madhya 19.81

> মহাপ্রভুর ভরে নৌকা করে টলমল ।\
> ডুবিতে লাগিল নৌকা, ঝলকে ভরে জল ॥৮১॥

mahāprabhura bhare naukā kare ṭalamala

ḍubite lāgila naukā, jhalake bhare jala

*mahāprabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhare*—devido ao peso; *naukā*—o barco; *kare*—faz; *ṭalamala*—inclinar-se; *ḍubite*—a afundar; *lāgila*—começou; *naukā*—o barco; *jhalake*—em jorros; *bhare*—enche; *jala*—água.

**Devido ao grande peso do Senhor, o barco começou a inclinar-se. Começou a encher-se de água e estava prestes a afundar.**
