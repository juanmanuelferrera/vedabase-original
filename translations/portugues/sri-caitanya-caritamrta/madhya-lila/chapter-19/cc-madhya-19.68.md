### CC Madhya 19.68

> ভট্টের বিস্ময় হৈল, প্রভুর হর্ষ মন ।\
> ভট্টেরে কহিলা প্রভু তাঁর বিবরণ ॥৬৮॥

bhaṭṭera vismaya haila, prabhura harṣa mana

bhaṭṭere kahilā prabhu tāṅra vivaraṇa

*bhaṭṭera*—de Vallabha Bhaṭṭācārya; *vismaya haila*—houve surpresa; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *harṣa*—muito feliz; *mana*—a mente; *bhaṭṭere kahilā*—disse a Vallabha Bhaṭṭācārya; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅra vivaraṇa*—descrição de Rūpa Gosvāmī.

**Vallabha Bhaṭṭācārya ficou muito surpreso com isso. Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, contudo, ficou muito satisfeito e, por isso, fez-lhe esta descrição de Rūpa Gosvāmī.**
