### CC Madhya 19.109

> প্রভু দেখিবারে গ্রামের সব-লোক আইল ।\
> প্রভু-দরশনে সবে ‘কৃষ্ণভক্ত’ হইল ॥১০৯॥

prabhu dekhibāre grāmera saba-loka āila

prabhu-daraśane sabe 'kṛṣṇa-bhakta' ha-ila

*prabhu dekhibāre*—para ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *grāmera*—da vila; *saba-loka*—todas as pessoas; *āila*—vieram; *prabhu-daraśane*—simplesmente por ver Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sabe*—todas elas; *kṛṣṇa-bhakta ha-ila*—tornaram-se devotos do Senhor Kṛṣṇa.

**Ao saberem que Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu havia chegado, todos os habitantes da vila foram vê-Lo. Simplesmente por vê-Lo, todos se tornaram devotos de Kṛṣṇa.**
