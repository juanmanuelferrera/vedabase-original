### CC Madhya 19.113

> গঙ্গা-পথে মহাপ্ৰভুরে নৌকাতে বসাঞা ।\
> প্রয়াগে আইলা ভট্ট গোসাঞিরে লইয়া ॥১১৩॥

gaṅgā-pathe mahāprabhure naukāte vasāñā

prayāge āilā bhaṭṭa gosāñire lañā

*gaṅgā-pathe*—pelo Ganges; *mahāprabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *naukāte vasāñā*—fazendo-O sentar-se no barco; *prayāge āilā*—foi a Prayāga; *bhaṭṭa*—Vallabha Bhaṭṭa; *gosāñire lañā*—com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Vallabha Bhaṭṭācārya evitou o rio Yamunā. Colocando o Senhor num barco no rio Ganges, foi com Ele a Prayāga.**
