### CC Madhya 19.245

> রাত্রে তেঁহো স্বপ্ন দেখে, — প্রভু আইলা ঘরে ।\
> প্রাতঃকালে আসি’ রহে গ্রামের বাহিরে ॥২৪৫॥

rātre teṅho svapna dekhe,--prabhu āilā ghare

prātaḥ-kāle āsi' rahe grāmera bāhire

*rātre*—à noite; *teṅho*—ele (Candraśekhara); *svapna*—um sonho; *dekhe*—viu; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āilā*—chegou; *ghare*—à sua casa; *prātaḥ-kāle*—pela manhã; *āsi'*—vindo; *rahe*—permaneceu; *grāmera bāhire*—fora da cidade.

**Num sonho, Candraśekhara vira que o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu chegara à sua casa; por isso, pela manhã, Candraśekhara saiu da cidade para receber o Senhor.**
