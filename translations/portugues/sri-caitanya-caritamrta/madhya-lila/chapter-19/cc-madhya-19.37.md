### CC Madhya 19.37

> তাঁহা লঞা রূপ-গোসাঞি প্রয়াগে আইলা ।\
> মহাপ্রভু তাহাঁ শুনি’ আনন্দিত হৈলা ॥৩৭॥

tāṅhā lañā rūpa-gosāñi prayāge āilā

mahāprabhu tāhāṅ śuni' ānandita hailā

*tāṅhā lañā*—levando-o consigo; *rūpa-gosāñi*—Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī; *prayāge*—a Prayāga; *āilā*—chegou; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāhāṅ*—lá; *śuni'*—ouvindo; *ānandita hailā*—ficaram muito satisfeitos.

**Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī e Anupama Mallika foram a Prayāga, e ficaram muito satisfeitos ao ouvir a notícia de que Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu se encontrava ali.**
