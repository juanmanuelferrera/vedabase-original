### CC Madhya 19.110

> ব্রাহ্মণসকল করেন প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণ ।\
> বল্লভ-ভট্ট তাঁ-সবারে করেন নিবারণ ॥১১০॥

brāhmaṇa-sakala karena prabhura nimantraṇa

vallabha-bhaṭṭa tāṅ-sabāre karena nivāraṇa

*brāhmaṇa-sakala*—todos os brāhmaṇas daquela vila; *karena*—fazem; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nimantraṇa*—convites; *vallabha-bhaṭṭa*—Vallabha Bhaṭṭācārya; *tāṅ-sabāre*—a todos eles; *karena*—faz; *nivāraṇa*—proibição.

**Todos os brāhmaṇas da vila estavam ansiosos por estender convites ao Senhor, mas Vallabha Bhaṭṭācārya os proibiu de fazê-lo.**
