### CC Madhya 19.43

> প্রভুর মহিমা দেখি’ লোকে চমৎকার ।
> প্রয়াগে প্রভুর লীলা নারি বর্ণিবার ॥৪৩॥

prabhura mahimā dekhi' loke camatkāra

prayāge prabhura līlā nāri varṇibāra

*prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mahimā*—a grandeza; *dekhi'*—vendo; *loke*—em todas as pessoas; *camatkāra*—espanto; *prayāge*—em Prayāga; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *līlā*—os passatempos; *nāri*—não sou capaz; *varṇibāra*—de descrever.

**Todos ficavam atônitos ao ver a grandeza de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. De fato, não sou capaz de descrever apropriadamente os passatempos do Senhor em Prayāga.**
