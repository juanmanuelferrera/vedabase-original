### CC Madhya 19.201

> কৃষ্ণ যদি রুক্মিণীরে কৈলা পরিহাস ।
> ‘কৃষ্ণ ছাড়িবেন’ — জানি’ রুক্মিণীর হৈল ত্রাস ॥২০১॥

kṛṣṇa yadi rukmiṇīre kailā parihāsa

'kṛṣṇa chāḍibena'--jāni' rukmiṇīra haila trāsa

*kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *yadi*—embora; *rukmiṇīre*—a Rukmiṇī, a primeira rainha; *kailā*—fez; *parihāsa*—brincadeira; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *chāḍibena*—vai me abandonar; *jāni'*—pensando; *rukmiṇīra*—de Rukmiṇī; *haila*—houve; *trāsa*—choque.

**"Embora Kṛṣṇa estivesse brincando com a rainha Rukmiṇī, ela pensava que Ele iria abandonar sua companhia e, por isso, ficou chocada.**
