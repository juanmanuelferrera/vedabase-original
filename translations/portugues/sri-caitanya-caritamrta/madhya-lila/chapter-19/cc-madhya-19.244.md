### CC Madhya 19.244

> মহাপ্রভু চলি’ চলি’ আইলা বারাণসী ।
> চন্দ্রশেখর মিলিলা গ্রামের বাহিরে আসি’ ॥২৪৪॥

mahāprabhu cali' cali' āilā vārāṇasī

candraśekhara mililā grāmera bāhire āsi'

*mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *cali' cali'*—caminhando e caminhando; *āilā*—chegou; *vārāṇasī*—a Vārāṇasī; *candraśekhara*—Candraśekhara; *mililā*—encontrou; *grāmera*—da cidade; *bāhire*—fora; *āsi'*—vindo.

**Após caminhar e caminhar, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu finalmente chegou a Vārāṇasī, onde encontrou Candraśekhara, que estava saindo da cidade.**
