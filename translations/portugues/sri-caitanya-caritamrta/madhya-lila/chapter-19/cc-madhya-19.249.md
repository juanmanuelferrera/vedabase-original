### CC Madhya 19.249

> ভিক্ষা করাঞা মিশ্র কহে প্রভু-পায় ধরি’ ।\
> এক ভিক্ষা মাগি, মোরে দেহ’ কৃপা করি’ ॥২৪৯॥

bhikṣā karāñā miśra kahe prabhu-pāya dhari'

eka bhikṣā māgi, more deha' kṛpā kari'

*bhikṣā karāñā*—depois de oferecer o almoço; *miśra*—Tapana Miśra; *kahe*—disse; *prabhu*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *pāya*—os pés de lótus; *dhari'*—tocando; *eka bhikṣā*—um favor; *māgi*—suplico; *more*—a mim; *deha'*—por favor, concede; *kṛpā kari'*—por Tua misericórdia imotivada.

**Após oferecer o almoço a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Tapana Miśra suplicou um favor ao Senhor e pediu-Lhe que o agraciasse com Sua misericórdia.**
