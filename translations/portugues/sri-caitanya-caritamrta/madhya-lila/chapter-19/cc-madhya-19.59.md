### CC Madhya 19.59

> ভট্টাচার্য দুই ভাইয়ে নিমন্ত্রণ কৈল ।\
> প্রভুর শেষ প্রসাদ-পাত্র দুইভাই পাইল ॥৫৯॥

bhaṭṭācārya dui bhāiye nimantraṇa kaila

prabhura śeṣa prasāda-pātra dui-bhāi pāila

*bhaṭṭācārya*—Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *dui bhāiye*—os dois irmãos; *nimantraṇa kaila*—convidou para o almoço; *prabhura śeṣa prasāda-pātra*—os restos do prato de alimento oferecido a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dui-bhāi pāila*—os dois irmãos obtiveram.

**Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya convidou os dois irmãos a também participarem do almoço. Os restos de alimento do prato de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu foram-lhes oferecidos.**
