### CC Madhya 19.112

> যাঁর ইচ্ছা, প্ৰয়াগে যাঞা করিবে নিমন্ত্রণ’ ।
> এত বলি’ প্রভু লঞা করিল গমন ॥১১২॥

yāṅra icchā, prayāge yāñā karibe nimantraṇa'

eta bali' prabhu lañā karila gamana

*yāṅra*—de quem; *icchā*—há desejo; *prayāge yāñā*—indo a Prayāga; *karibe*—poderá fazer; *nimantraṇa*—convites; *eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *prabhu lañā*—com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *karila gamana*—partiu para Prayāga.

**Vallabha Bhaṭṭa disse: "Se alguém quiser, poderá ir a Prayāga e estender convites ao Senhor." Dessa maneira, levou o Senhor consigo e partiu para Prayāga.**
