### CC Madhya 19.104

> রসগণ-মধ্যে তুমি শ্রেষ্ঠ মান’ কায় ?\
> ‘আদ্য এব পরো রসঃ’ — কহে উপাধ্যায় ॥১০৪॥

rasa-gaṇa-madhye tumi śreṣṭha māna' kāya?

'ādya eva paro rasaḥ'--kahe upādhyāya

*rasa-gaṇa-madhye*—entre todas as doçuras; *tumi*—tu; *śreṣṭha*—como suprema; *māna'*—aceitas; *kāya*—qual; *ādyaḥ*—o amor conjugal; *eva*—certamente; *paraḥ rasaḥ*—a melhor de todas as doçuras; *kahe*—respondeu; *upādhyāya*—Raghupati Upādhyāya.

**Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu perguntou: "Entre todas as doçuras, qual consideras a melhor?", Raghupati Upādhyāya respondeu: "A doçura do amor conjugal é a suprema."**
