### CC Madhya 19.248

> নিজ ঘরে লঞা প্রভুরে ভিক্ষা করাইল ।\
> ভট্টাচার্যে চন্দ্রশেখর নিমন্ত্রণ কৈল ॥২৪৮॥

nija ghare lañā prabhure bhikṣā karāila

bhaṭṭācārye candraśekhara nimantraṇa kaila

*nija ghare*—à sua própria casa; *lañā*—levando; *prabhure*—ao Senhor; *bhikṣā karāila*—ofereceu o almoço; *bhaṭṭācārye*—a Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *candraśekhara*—Candraśekhara; *nimantraṇa*—convite; *kaila*—fez.

**Tapana Miśra levou Caitanya Mahāprabhu à sua própria casa e Lhe ofereceu o almoço. Candraśekhara convidou Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya para almoçar em sua casa.**
