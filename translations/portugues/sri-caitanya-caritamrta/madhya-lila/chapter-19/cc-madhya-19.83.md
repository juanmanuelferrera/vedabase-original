### CC Madhya 19.83

> দেশ-পাত্র দেখি’ মহাপ্রভু ধৈর্য হইল ।\
> আড়াইলের ঘাটে নৌকা আসি’ উত্তরিল ॥৮৩॥

deśa-pātra dekhi' mahāprabhu dhairya ha-ila

āḍāilera ghāṭe naukā āsi' uttarila

*deśa-pātra dekhi'*—vendo as circunstâncias; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dhairya ha-ila*—ficou calmo; *āḍāilera ghāṭe*—na margem da aldeia de Āḍāila; *naukā*—o barco; *āsi'*—chegando; *uttarila*—aportou.

**Vendo as circunstâncias, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu finalmente ficou calmo, de modo que o barco pôde alcançar a margem de Āḍāila e ali aportar.**
