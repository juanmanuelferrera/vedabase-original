### CC Madhya 3.137

> প্রভাতে আচার্যরত্ন দোলায় চড়াঞা ।\
> ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে আইলা শচীমাতা লঞা ॥১৩৭॥

prabhāte ācāryaratna dolāya caḍāñā

bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge āilā śacīmātā lañā

*prabhāte*—pela manhã; *ācāryaratna*—Candraśekhara; *dolāya*—num palanquim; *caḍāñā*—sentando; *bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge*—com os devotos; *āilā*—veio; *śacī-mātā*—mãe Śacī; *lañā*—trazendo.

**Pela manhã, Candraśekhara trouxe Śacīmātā de sua casa, com muitos devotos, e a sentou num palanquim.**
