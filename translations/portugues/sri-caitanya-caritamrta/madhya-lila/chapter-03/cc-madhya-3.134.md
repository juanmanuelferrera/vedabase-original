### CC Madhya 3.134

> তবু ত’ না জানে শ্রম প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা ।
> নিত্যানন্দ মহাপ্ৰভুকে রাখিল ধরিঞা ॥১৩৪॥

tabu ta' nā jāne śrama premāviṣṭa hañā

nityānanda mahāprabhuke rākhila dhariñā

*tabu*—ainda; *ta'*—certamente; *nā jāne*—não conhece; *śrama*—fadiga; *prema-āviṣṭa*—absorto em amor; *hañā*—estando; *nityānanda*—o Senhor Nityānanda; *mahāprabhuke*—o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rākhila*—deteve; *dhariñā*—segurando.

**Estando plenamente absorto em amor a Deus, Ele não percebia Sua fadiga. Mas Nityānanda Prabhu, segurando-O, deteve Sua dança.**
