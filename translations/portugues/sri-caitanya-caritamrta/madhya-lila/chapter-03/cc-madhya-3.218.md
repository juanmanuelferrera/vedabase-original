### CC Madhya 3.218

> অদ্বৈত-গৃহে প্রভুর বিলাস শুনে যেই জন ।
> অচিরে মিলয়ে তাঁরে কৃষ্ণপ্রেম-ধন ॥২১৮॥

advaita-gṛhe prabhura vilāsa śune yei jana
acire milaye tāṅre kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana

*advaita-gṛhe*—na casa de Advaita Ācārya; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *vilāsa*—os passatempos; *śune*—ouve; *yei*—aquele que; *jana*—pessoa; *acire*—muito em breve; *milaye*—obtém; *tāṅre*—ele; *kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana*—a riqueza do amor a Deus.

**Aquele que ouvir as atividades do Senhor na casa de Advaita Ācārya certamente obterá muito em breve a riqueza do amor a Kṛṣṇa.**
