### CC Madhya 3.135

> আচার্য-গোসাঞি তবে রাখিল কীর্তন ।\
> নানা সেবা করি’ প্রভুকে করাইল শয়ন ॥১৩৫॥

ācārya-gosāñi tabe rākhila kīrtana

nānā sevā kari' prabhuke karāila śayana

*ācārya-gosāñi*—Advaita Ācārya; *tabe*—então; *rākhila*—suspendeu; *kīrtana*—o cantar; *nānā*—vários; *sevā*—serviços; *kari'*—realizando; *prabhuke*—ao Senhor; *karāila*—fez; *śayana*—deitar-se.

**Embora o Senhor estivesse fatigado, Nityānanda Prabhu manteve-O firme, segurando-O. Naquele momento, Advaita Ācārya suspendeu o cantar e, prestando vários serviços ao Senhor, fê-lo deitar-se para descansar.**
