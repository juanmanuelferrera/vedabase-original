### CC Madhya 3.83

> নিত্যানন্দ বলে — যবে কৈলে নিমন্ত্রণ ৷
> তত দিতে চাহ, যত করিয়ে ভোজন ॥৮৩॥

nityānanda bale--yabe kaile nimantraṇa

tata dite cāha, yata kariye bhojana

*nityānanda bale*—o Senhor Nityānanda disse; *yabe*—quando; *kaile*—fizeste; *nimantraṇa*—convite; *tata*—tanto; *dite cāha*—deves fornecer; *yata*—quanto; *kariye bhojana*—eu possa comer.

**O Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu respondeu: "Seja como for, Tu Me convidaste. Portanto, deves fornecer tanto quanto Eu queira comer."**
