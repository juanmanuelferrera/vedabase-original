### CC Madhya 3.24

> প্রভু কহে, — শ্রীপাদ, তোমার কোথাকে গমন ।\
> শ্রীপাদ কহে, তোমার সঙ্গে যাব বৃন্দাবন ॥২৪॥

prabhu kahe,--śrīpāda, tomāra kothāke gamana

śrīpāda kahe, tomāra saṅge yāba vṛndāvana

*prabhu kahe*—o Senhor perguntou; *śrīpāda*—senhor; *tomāra*—d'Ele; *kothāke*—para onde; *gamana*—ides; *śrīpāda kahe*—Nityānanda Prabhu respondeu; *tomāra*—contigo; *saṅge*—junto; *yāba*—irei; *vṛndāvana*—rumo a Vṛndāvana.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu estava em êxtase, e perguntou aonde Nityānanda Prabhu estava indo. Nityānanda respondeu que estava indo com Ele rumo a Vṛndāvana.**
