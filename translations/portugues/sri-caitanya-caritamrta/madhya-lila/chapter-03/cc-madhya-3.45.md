### CC Madhya 3.45

> সার্দ্রক, বাস্তুক-শাক বিবিধ প্রকার ।
> পটোল, কুষ্মাণ্ড-বড়ি, মানকচু আর ॥৪৫॥

sārdraka, vāstuka-śāka vividha prakāra
paṭola, kuṣmāṇḍa-baḍi, mānakacu āra

*sārdraka*—potes com pratos de gengibre; *vāstuka-śāka*—espinafre; *vividha*—várias; *prakāra*—espécies; *paṭola*—um tipo de vegetal; *kuṣmāṇḍa*—abóbora; *baḍi*—com dāl partido; *mānakacu*—a raiz de um vegetal chamado kacu; *āra*—e.

**Entre os vegetais cozidos havia paṭolas, abóbora, mānakacu e uma salada feita com pedaços de gengibre e vários tipos de espinafre.**
