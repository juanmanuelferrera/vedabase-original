### CC Madhya 3.35

> আচার্য কহে, মিথ্যা নহে শ্রীপাদ-বচন ।
> যমুনাতে স্নান তুমি করিলা এখন ॥৩৫॥

ācārya kahe, mithyā nahe śrīpāda-vacana
yamunāte snāna tumi karilā ekhana

*ācārya kahe*—Advaita Ācārya respondeu; *mithyā nahe*—isto não é falso; *śrīpāda-vacana*—as palavras de Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu; *yamunāte*—no rio Yamunā; *snāna*—banho; *tumi*—Tu; *karilā*—realizaste; *ekhana*—agora mesmo.

**Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu acusou Nityānanda de tê-lO enganado, Śrīla Advaita Ācārya disse: "O que quer que Nityānanda Prabhu Te tenha dito não é falso. De fato, acabaste de tomar Teu banho no rio Yamunā."**
