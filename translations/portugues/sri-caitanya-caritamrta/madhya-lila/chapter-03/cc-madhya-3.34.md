### CC Madhya 3.34

> প্রভু কহে, — নিত্যানন্দ আমারে বঞ্চিলা ।
> গঙ্গাকে আনিয়া মোরে যমুনা কহিলা ॥৩৪॥

prabhu kahe,--nityānanda āmāre vañcilā
gaṅgāke āniyā more yamunā kahilā

*prabhu kahe*—o Senhor respondeu; *nityānanda*—o Senhor Nityānanda; *āmāre*—a Mim; *vañcilā*—enganou; *gaṅgāke*—à margem do Ganges; *āniyā*—trazendo; *more*—a Mim; *yamunā*—o rio Yamunā; *kahilā*—informou.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse então: "Nityānanda Me enganou. Trouxe-Me à margem do Ganges e disse-Me que era o Yamunā."**
