### CC Madhya 11.202

> স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি প্রভুকে কৈল নিবেদন ৷\
> তুমি না বসিলে কেহ না করে ভোজন ৷৷ ২০২ ৷৷ ॥২০২॥

svarūpa-gosāñi prabhuke kaila nivedana
tumi nā vasile keha nā kare bhojana

*svarūpa-gosāñi*—Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosāñi; *prabhuke*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kaila*—fez; *nivedana*—submissão; *tumi*—Tu; *nā vasile*—se não Te sentares; *keha*—ninguém; *nā*—não; *kare*—faz; *bhojana*—comer.

**Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī então informou a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu: "A menos que Te sentes e tomes prasāda, ninguém o aceitará.**
