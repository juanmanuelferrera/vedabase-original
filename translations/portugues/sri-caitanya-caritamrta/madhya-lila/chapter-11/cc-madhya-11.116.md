### CC Madhya 11.116

> পূর্বে প্রভু মোরে প্রসাদ-অন্ন আনি’ দিল ৷\
> প্রাতে শয্যায় বসি’ আমি সে অন্ন খাইল ৷৷ ১১৬ ৷৷ ॥১১৬॥

pūrve prabhu more prasāda-anna āni' dila
prāte śayyāya vasi' āmi se anna khāila

*pūrve*—antes disto; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *more*—a mim; *prasāda-anna*—arroz mahā-prasāda; *āni'*—trazendo; *dila*—entregou; *prāte*—de manhã cedo; *śayyāya*—em minha cama; *vasi'*—sentado; *āmi*—eu; *se*—aquele; *anna*—arroz; *khāila*—comi.

**"Anteriormente, o Senhor deu-me arroz mahā-prasāda certa manhã, e eu o comi sentado em minha cama, sem sequer ter lavado a boca.**
