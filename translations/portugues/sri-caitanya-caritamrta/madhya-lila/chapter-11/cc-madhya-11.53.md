### CC Madhya 11.53

> তথাপি কহিয়ে আমি এক উপায় ৷
> এই উপায় কর’ প্রভু দেখিবে যাহায় ৷৷ ৫৩ ৷৷ ॥৫৩॥

tathāpi kahiye āmi eka upāya
ei upāya kara' prabhu dekhibe yāhāya

*tathāpi*—ainda assim; *kahiye*—digo; *āmi*—eu; *eka upāya*—um meio; *ei upāya*—este meio; *kara'*—procura adotar; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhibe*—te verá; *yāhāya*—por esse meio.

**Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya então sugeriu: "Há um meio pelo qual podes vê-lo diretamente.**
