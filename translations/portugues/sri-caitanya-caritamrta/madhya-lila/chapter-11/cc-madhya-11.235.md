### CC Madhya 11.235

> মহানৃত্য, মহাপ্রেম, মহাসংকীর্তন ৷
> দেখি’ প্রেমাবেশে ভাসে নীলাচল-জন ৷৷ ২৩৫ ৷৷ ॥২৩৫॥

mahā-nṛtya, mahā-prema, mahā-saṅkīrtana
dekhi' premāveśe bhāse nīlācala-jana

*mahā-nṛtya*—grande dança; *mahā-prema*—grande amor; *mahā-saṅkīrtana*—grande canto congregacional; *dekhi'*—vendo; *prema-āveśe*—em amor extático; *bhāse*—flutuam; *nīlācala-jana*—todos os habitantes de Jagannātha Purī.

**Ao verem a grande dança, o grande amor e o grande saṅkīrtana, todas as pessoas de Jagannātha Purī flutuavam num oceano extático de amor.**
