### CC Madhya 11.212

> হেনকালে রামানন্দ আইলা প্রভু-স্থানে ৷\
> প্রভু মিলাইল তাঁরে সব বৈষ্ণবগণে ৷৷ ২১২ ৷৷ ॥২১২॥

hena-kāle rāmānanda āilā prabhu-sthāne

prabhu milāila tāṅre saba vaiṣṇava-gaṇe

*hena-kāle*—nesse momento; *rāmānanda*—Rāmānanda; *āilā*—veio; *prabhu-sthāne*—ao lugar de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *milāila*—fez encontrar-se; *tāṅre*—a ele (Śrī Rāmānanda Rāya); *saba*—todos; *vaiṣṇava-gaṇe*—os devotos do Senhor.

**Nesse momento Rāmānanda Rāya também veio encontrar-se com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, e o Senhor aproveitou a oportunidade para apresentar todos os vaiṣṇavas a ele.**
