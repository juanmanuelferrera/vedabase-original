### CC Madhya 11.70

> মহাপ্রভুর গণ যত আইলা গৌড় হৈতে ৷
> ভট্টাচার্য, একে একে দেখাহ আমাতে ৷৷ ৭০ ৷৷ ॥৭০॥

mahāprabhura gaṇa yata āila gauḍa haite

bhaṭṭācārya, eke eke dekhāha āmāte

*mahāprabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *gaṇa*—associados; *yata*—todos; *āila*—que vieram; *gauḍa haite*—de Bengala; *bhaṭṭācārya*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *eke eke*—um após outro; *dekhāha*—mostra, por favor; *āmāte*—a mim.

**"Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya, mostra-me, por favor, um após outro, todos os devotos de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu que estão vindo de Bengala."**
