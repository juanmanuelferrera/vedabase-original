### CC Madhya 11.206

> তবে প্রভু প্রসাদান্ন গোবিন্দ-হাতে দিলা ৷\
> যত্ন করি’ হরিদাস-ঠাকুরে পাঠাইলা ৷৷ ২০৬ ৷৷ ॥২০৬॥

tabe prabhu prasādānna govinda-hāte dilā

yatna kari' haridāsa-ṭhākure pāṭhāilā

*tabe*—em seguida; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *prasāda-anna*—restos do alimento de Jagannātha; *govinda-hāte*—na mão de Govinda; *dilā*—entregou; *yatna kari'*—com grande atenção; *haridāsa-ṭhākure*—a Haridāsa Ṭhākura; *pāṭhāilā*—enviou.

**Depois disso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu cuidadosamente entregou algum prasāda nas mãos de Govinda para ser dado a Haridāsa Ṭhākura.**
