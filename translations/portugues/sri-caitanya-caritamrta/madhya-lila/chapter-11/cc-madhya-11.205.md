### CC Madhya 11.205

> নিত্যানন্দ লঞা ভিক্ষা করিতে বৈস তুমি ৷
> বৈষ্ণবের পরিবেশন করিতেছি আমি ৷৷ ২০৫ ৷৷ ॥২০৫॥

nityānanda lañā bhikṣā karite vaisa tumi

vaiṣṇavera pariveśana karitechi āmi

*nityānanda lañā*—levando junto Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu; *bhikṣā*—almoço; *karite*—tomar; *vaisa*—senta-Te; *tumi*—Tu; *vaiṣṇavera*—a todos os devotos; *pariveśana*—distribuição de prasāda; *karitechi*—estou fazendo; *āmi*—eu.

**"Podes sentar-Te e aceitar o almoço com Nityānanda Prabhu, e eu distribuirei o prasāda a todos os vaiṣṇavas."**
