### CC Madhya 11.164

> ভক্ত সব ধাঞা আইল হরিদাসে নিতে ৷
> প্রভু তোমায় মিলিতে চাহে, চলহ ত্বরিতে ৷৷ ১৬৪ ৷৷ ॥১৬৪॥

bhakta saba dhāñā āila haridāse nite
prabhu tomāya milite cāhe, calaha tvarite

*bhakta*—devotos; *saba*—todos; *dhāñā*—correndo; *āila*—vieram; *haridāse*—Haridāsa; *nite*—para levar; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tomāya*—a ti; *milite*—encontrar-se; *cāhe*—deseja; *calaha*—vem; *tvarite*—prontamente.

**Todos os devotos então foram até Haridāsa Ṭhākura, dizendo: "O Senhor deseja encontrar-se contigo. Por favor, vem imediatamente."**
