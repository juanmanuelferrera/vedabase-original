### CC Madhya 11.185

> মহাপ্রভু আইলা তবে হরিদাস-মিলনে ৷
> হরিদাস করে প্রেমে নাম-সংকীর্তনে ৷৷ ১৮৫ ৷৷ ॥১৮৫॥

mahāprabhu āilā tabe haridāsa-milane
haridāsa kare preme nāma-saṅkīrtane

*mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āilā*—veio; *tabe*—depois disso; *haridāsa-milane*—encontrar-se com Ṭhākura Haridāsa; *haridāsa*—Ṭhākura Haridāsa; *kare*—faz; *preme*—em amor extático; *nāma-saṅkīrtane*—o cantar do santo nome.

**Depois disto, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu foi encontrar-Se com Haridāsa Ṭhākura, e viu-o ocupado em cantar o mahā-mantra com amor extático. Haridāsa cantava: "Hare Kṛṣṇa, Hare Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa, Hare Hare / Hare Rāma, Hare Rāma, Rāma Rāma, Hare Hare."**
