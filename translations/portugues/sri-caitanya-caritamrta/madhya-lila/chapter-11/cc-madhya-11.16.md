### CC Madhya 11.16

> রায় প্রণতি কৈল, প্রভু কৈল আলিঙ্গন ৷\
> দুই জনে প্রেমাবেশে করেন ক্রন্দন ৷৷ ১৬ ৷৷ ॥১৬॥

rāya praṇati kaila, prabhu kaila āliṅgana
dui jane premāveśe karena krandana

*rāya praṇati kaila*—Rāmānanda Rāya ofereceu suas reverências; *prabhu*—o Senhor; *kaila*—fez; *āliṅgana*—abraço; *dui jane*—ambos; *prema-āveśe*—em amor extático; *karena*—faziam; *krandana*—prantear.

**Ao encontrar-se com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Rāmānanda Rāya ofereceu suas reverências. O Senhor abraçou-o, e ambos começaram a chorar no grande êxtase do amor.**
