### CC Madhya 11.64

> পাছে প্রভুর নিকট আইলা ভক্তগণ ৷
> গৌড় হৈতে ভক্ত আইসে, — কৈল নিবেদন ৷৷ ৬৪ ৷৷ ॥৬৪॥

pāche prabhura nikaṭa āilā bhakta-gaṇa

gauḍa haite bhakta āise,-kaila nivedana

*pāche*—atrás; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nikaṭa*—na presença; *āilā*—vieram; *bhakta-gaṇa*—os devotos; *gauḍa haite*—de Bengala; *bhakta*—devotos; *āise*—vêm; *kaila nivedana*—submeteram.

**Os devotos que haviam seguido o Senhor vieram à Sua presença e pediram-Lhe que regressasse a Purī. Informaram que os devotos de Bengala estavam a caminho de Puruṣottama-kṣetra.**
