### CC Madhya 11.231

> চারি জনের নৃত্য দেখিতে প্রভুর অভিলাষ ৷\
> সেই অভিলাষে করে ঐশ্বর্য প্রকাশ ৷৷ ২৩১ ৷৷ ॥২৩১॥

cāri janera nṛtya dekhite prabhura abhilāṣa
sei abhilāṣe kare aiśvarya prakāśa

*cāri janera*—das quatro pessoas; *nṛtya*—dança; *dekhite*—ver; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *abhilāṣa*—desejo; *sei abhilāṣe*—para esse fim; *kare*—faz; *aiśvarya prakāśa*—exibição de um milagre.

**Desejando ver a dança das quatro grandes personalidades, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu manifestou-Se de tal maneira que parecia estar vendo a todos.**
