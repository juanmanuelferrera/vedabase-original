### CC Madhya 11.207

> আপনে বসিলা সব সন্ন্যাসীরে লঞা ৷
> পরিবেশন করে আচার্য হরষিত হঞা ৷৷ ২০৭ ৷৷ ॥২০৭॥

āpane vasilā saba sannyāsīre lañā

pariveśana kare ācārya haraṣita hañā

*āpane*—pessoalmente; *vasilā*—sentou-Se; *saba*—todos; *sannyāsīre lañā*—levando consigo os sannyāsīs; *pariveśana kare*—distribui; *ācārya*—Gopīnātha Ācārya; *haraṣita hañā*—com grande prazer.

**Então Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu pessoalmente sentou-Se para aceitar o almoço com os demais sannyāsīs, e Gopīnātha Ācārya começou a distribuir o prasāda com grande prazer.**
