### CC Madhya 11.225

> চারিদিকে চারি সম্প্রদায় উচ্চৈঃস্বরে গায় ৷\
> মধ্যে তাণ্ডব-নৃত্য করে গৌররায় ৷৷ ২২৫ ৷৷ ॥২২৫॥

cāri-dike cāri sampradāya uccaiḥsvare gāya
madhye tāṇḍava-nṛtya kare gaurarāya

*cāri-dike*—nos quatro lados; *cāri sampradāya*—os quatro grupos; *uccaiḥ-svare*—em voz muito alta; *gāya*—cantam; *madhye*—no meio; *tāṇḍava-nṛtya*—saltar e dançar; *kare*—realiza; *gaurarāya*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Nos quatro lados, os quatro grupos de saṅkīrtana cantavam em voz muito alta, e no meio Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu dançava, saltando alto.**
