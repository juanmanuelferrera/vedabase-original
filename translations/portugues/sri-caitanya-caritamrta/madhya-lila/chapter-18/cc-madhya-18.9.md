### CC Madhya 18.9

> যেই কুণ্ডে নিত্য কৃষ্ণ রাধিকার সঙ্গে ।
> জলে জলকেলি করে, তীরে রাস-রঙ্গে ॥৯॥

yei kuṇḍe nitya kṛṣṇa rādhikāra saṅge
jale jala-keli kare, tīre rāsa-raṅge

*yei kuṇḍe*—em cujo lago; *nitya*—diariamente; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *rādhikāra saṅge*—acompanhado por Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; *jale*—na água; *jala-keli*—brincando na água; *kare*—realiza; *tīre*—na margem; *rāsa-raṅge*—Sua dança da rāsa.

**"Naquele lago, o Senhor Kṛṣṇa e Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī costumavam brincar diariamente na água e realizar a dança da rāsa na margem.**
