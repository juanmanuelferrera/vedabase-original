### CC Madhya 18.187

> ‘অদ্বৈত-ব্রহ্মবাদ’ সেই করিল স্থাপন ।
> তার শাস্ত্রযুক্ত্যে তারে প্রভু কৈলা খণ্ডন ॥১৮৭॥

'advaita-brahma-vāda' sei karila sthāpana

tāra śāstra-yuktye tāre prabhu kailā khaṇḍana

*advaita-brahma-vāda*—a concepção do Brahman impessoal; *sei*—aquela pessoa santa; *karila sthāpana*—estabeleceu; *tāra śāstra-yuktye*—segundo a lógica de sua escritura; *tāre*—a ele; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kailā*—fez; *khaṇḍana*—refutação.

**Quando aquela pessoa estabeleceu a concepção do Brahman impessoal da Verdade Absoluta com base no Alcorão, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu refutou seu raciocínio.**
