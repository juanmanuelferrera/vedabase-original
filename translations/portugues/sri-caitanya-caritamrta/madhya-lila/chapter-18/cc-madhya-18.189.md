### CC Madhya 18.189

> প্রভু কহে, — তোমার শাস্ত্র স্থাপে ‘নির্বিশেষে’ ।\
> তাহা খণ্ডি’ ‘সবিশেষ’ স্থাপিয়াছে শেষে ॥১৮৯॥

prabhu kahe,--tomāra śāstra sthāpe 'nirviśeṣe'

tāhā khaṇḍi' 'saviśeṣa' sthāpiyāche śeṣe

*prabhu kahe*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu continuou a falar; *tomāra śāstra*—tua escritura (o Alcorão); *sthāpe*—estabelece; *nirviśeṣe*—o impersonalismo; *tāhā khaṇḍi'*—refutando isso; *sa-viśeṣa*—Deus pessoal; *sthāpiyāche*—estabelece; *śeṣe*—no final.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu disse: "O Alcorão certamente estabeleceu o impersonalismo, mas, no final, refuta esse impersonalismo e estabelece o Deus pessoal.**
