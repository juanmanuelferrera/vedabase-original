### CC Madhya 18.164

> প্রভুরে দেখিয়া ম্লেচ্ছ করয়ে বিচার ।
> এই যতি-পাশ ছিল সুবর্ণ অপার ॥১৬৪॥

prabhure dekhiyā mleccha karaye vicāra

ei yati-pāśa chila suvarṇa apāra

*prabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhiyā*—vendo; *mleccha*—os maometanos; *karaye vicāra*—consideraram; *ei yati-pāśa*—em posse deste sannyāsī; *chila*—havia; *suvarṇa apāra*—uma grande quantidade de ouro.

**Vendo o Senhor inconsciente, os soldados pensaram: "Este sannyāsī deve ter possuído uma grande quantidade de ouro.**
