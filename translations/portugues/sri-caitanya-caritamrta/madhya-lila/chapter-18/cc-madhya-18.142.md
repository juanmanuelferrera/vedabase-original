### CC Madhya 18.142

> বৃন্দাবন হৈতে যদি প্রভুরে কাড়িয়ে ।
> তবে মঙ্গল হয়, — এই ভাল যুক্তি হয়ে ॥১৪২॥

vṛndāvana haite yadi prabhure kāḍiye

tabe maṅgala haya,--ei bhāla yukti haye

*vṛndāvana haite*—de Vṛndāvana; *yadi*—se; *prabhure*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kāḍiye*—eu levar; *tabe*—então; *maṅgala haya*—há auspiciosidade; *ei*—este; *bhāla*—bom; *yukti*—plano; *haye*—é.

**"Seria bom se pudéssemos tirar Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu de Vṛndāvana. Esta é a minha conclusão final."**
