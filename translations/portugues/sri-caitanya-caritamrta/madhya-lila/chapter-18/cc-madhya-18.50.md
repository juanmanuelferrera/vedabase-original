### CC Madhya 18.50

bhūgarbha-gosāñi, āra śrī-jīva-gosāñi

śrī-yādava-ācārya, āra govinda gosāñi

*bhūgarbha-gosāñi*—Bhūgarbha Gosāñi; *āra*—e; *śrī-jīva-gosāñi*—Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī; *śrī-yādava-ācārya*—Śrī Yādava Ācārya; *āra*—e; *govinda gosāñi*—Govinda Gosvāmī.

**Bhūgarbha Gosvāmī, Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī, Śrī Yādava Ācārya e Govinda Gosvāmī também acompanharam Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī.**
