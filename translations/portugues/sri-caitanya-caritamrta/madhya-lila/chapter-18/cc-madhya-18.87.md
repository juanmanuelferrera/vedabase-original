### CC Madhya 18.87

> কিন্তু আজি এক মুঞি ‘স্বপ্ন’ দেখিনু ।\
> সেই স্বপ্ন পরতেক তোমা আসি’ পাইনু ॥৮৭॥

kintu āji eka muñi 'svapna' dekhinu

sei svapna parateka tomā āsi' pāinu

*kintu*—mas; *āji*—hoje; *eka*—um; *muñi*—eu; *svapna*—sonho; *dekhinu*—tive; *sei svapna*—aquele sonho; *parateka*—de acordo com; *tomā*—a Ti; *āsi'*—vindo; *pāinu*—encontrei.

**"Hoje tive um sonho, e, de acordo com esse sonho, vim até aqui e Te encontrei."**
