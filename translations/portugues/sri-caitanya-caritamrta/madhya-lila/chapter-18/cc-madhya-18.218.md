### CC Madhya 18.218

> শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু ঈষৎ হাসিতে লাগিলা ।\
> সেই দুইজন প্রভুর সঙ্গে চলি’ আইলা ॥২১৮॥

śuni' mahāprabhu īṣat hāsite lāgilā

sei dui-jana prabhura saṅge cali' āilā

*śuni'*—ouvindo; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *īṣat*—suavemente; *hāsite lāgilā*—pôs-se a sorrir; *sei*—aquelas; *dui-jana*—duas pessoas; *prabhura saṅge*—com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *cali' āilā*—vieram.

**Ao ouvir isso, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu aceitou a proposta deles sorrindo suavemente. Assim, aquelas duas pessoas continuaram a acompanhá-Lo.**
