### CC Madhya 18.156

> বাহ্য বিকার নাহি, প্রেমাবিষ্ট মন ।\
> ভট্টাচার্য কহে, — চল, যাই মহাবন ॥১৫৬॥

bāhya vikāra nāhi, premāviṣṭa mana

bhaṭṭācārya kahe,--cala, yāi mahāvana

*bāhya*—externos; *vikāra*—sintomas; *nāhi*—não havia; *prema-āviṣṭa mana*—a mente estava cheia de amor extático; *bhaṭṭācārya kahe*—Bhaṭṭācārya disse; *cala*—vamos; *yāi mahāvana*—vamos a Mahāvana.

**Embora o Senhor não exibisse nenhum sintoma externo, Sua mente estava repleta de amor extático. Naquele momento, Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya disse: "Vamos a Mahāvana [Gokula]."**
