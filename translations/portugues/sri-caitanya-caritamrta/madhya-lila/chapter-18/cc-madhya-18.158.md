### CC Madhya 18.158

> প্রেমী কৃষ্ণদাস, আর সেইত ব্রাহ্মণ ।\
> গঙ্গাতীর-পথে যাইবার বিজ্ঞ দুইজন ॥১৫৮॥

premī kṛṣṇadāsa, āra seita brāhmaṇa

gaṅgā-tīra-pathe yāibāra vijña dui-jana

*premī kṛṣṇadāsa*—o devoto Rājaputa Kṛṣṇadāsa; *āra*—e; *seita brāhmaṇa*—aquele brāhmaṇa Sanoḍiyā; *gaṅgā-tīra-pathe*—o caminho pela margem do Ganges; *yāibāra*—para ir; *vijña*—experientes; *dui-jana*—duas pessoas.

**Tanto Rājaputa Kṛṣṇadāsa quanto o brāhmaṇa Sanoḍiyā conheciam muito bem o caminho ao longo da margem do Ganges.**
