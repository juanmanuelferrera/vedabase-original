### CC Madhya 18.69

> ‘গোকুল’ দেখিয়া আইলা ‘মথুরা’-নগরে ।
> ‘জন্মস্থান’ দেখি’ রহে সেই বিপ্র-ঘরে ॥৬৯॥

'gokula' dekhiyā āilā 'mathurā'-nagare

janma-sthāna' dekhi' rahe sei vipra-ghare

*gokula dekhiyā*—vendo Gokula; *āilā*—veio; *mathurā-nagare*—à cidade de Mathurā; *janma-sthāna*—o local de nascimento do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *dekhi'*—vendo; *rahe*—permanece; *sei vipra-ghare*—na casa do brāhmaṇa Sanoḍiyā.

**Após ver Gokula, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu retornou a Mathurā, onde viu o local de nascimento do Senhor. Enquanto ali esteve, Ele se hospedou na casa do brāhmaṇa Sanoḍiyā.**
