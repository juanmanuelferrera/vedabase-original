### CC Madhya 18.22

> সে-রাত্রি রহিলা হরিদেবের মন্দিরে  ।\
> রাত্রে মহাপ্রভু করে মনেতে বিচারে ॥২২॥

se-rātri rahilā haridevera maṇḍire

rātre mahāprabhu kare manete vicāre

*se-rātri*—naquela noite; *rahilā*—permaneceu; *hari-devera*—de Harideva; *mandire*—no templo; *rātre*—à noite; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kare*—faz; *manete*—na mente; *vicāre*—reflexão.

**Naquela noite, o Senhor permaneceu no templo de Harideva e, durante a noite, começou a refletir.**
