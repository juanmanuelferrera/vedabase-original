### CC Madhya 18.21

> ভট্টাচার্য ‘ব্রহ্মকুণ্ডে’ পাক যাঞা কৈল ।\
> ব্রহ্মকুণ্ডে স্নান করি’ প্রভু ভিক্ষা কৈল ॥২১॥

bhaṭṭācārya 'brahma-kuṇḍe' pāka yāñā kaila

brahma-kuṇḍe snāna kari' prabhu bhikṣā kaila

*bhaṭṭācārya*—Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *brahma-kuṇḍe*—no lago chamado Brahma-kuṇḍa; *pāka*—cozinhar; *yāñā*—indo até lá; *kaila*—realizou; *brahma-kuṇḍe*—em Brahma-kuṇḍa; *snāna kari'*—tomando banho; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhikṣā kaila*—aceitou o almoço.

**Em Brahma-kuṇḍa, Bhaṭṭācārya cozinhou alimentos, e o Senhor, após tomar Seu banho em Brahma-kuṇḍa, aceitou Seu almoço.**
