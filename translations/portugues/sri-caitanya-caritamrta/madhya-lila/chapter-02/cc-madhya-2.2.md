### CC Madhya 2.2

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

*jaya jaya śrī-caitanya*—todas as glórias a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jaya nityānanda*—todas as glórias ao Senhor Nityānanda; *jaya advaitacandra*—todas as glórias a Advaita Prabhu; *jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda*—todas as glórias aos devotos do Senhor.

**Todas as glórias a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu! Todas as glórias ao Senhor Nityānanda! Todas as glórias a Advaitacandra! Todas as glórias a todos os devotos do Senhor!**
