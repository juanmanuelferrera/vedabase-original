### CC Madhya 14.19

> তবে মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে ঐশ্বর্য দেখাইল ।\
> ‘কারেহ না কহিবে’ এই নিষেধ করিল ॥১৯॥

tabe mahāprabhu tāṅre aiśvarya dekhāila

'kāreha nā kahibe' ei niṣedha karila

*tabe*—naquele momento; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—ao rei; *aiśvarya*—poder divino; *dekhāila*—mostrou; *kāreha nā kahibe*—não fales a ninguém; *ei*—isto; *niṣedha karila*—proibiu.

**Naquele momento, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu mostrou ao rei algumas de Suas opulências divinas e proibiu-o de revelar isso a alguém.**
