### CC Madhya 14.160

> গোপীগণ-মধ্যে শ্রেষ্ঠা রাধা-ঠাকুরাণী ৷\
> নির্মল-উজ্জ্বল-রস-প্রেম-রত্নখনি ॥১৬০॥

gopī-gaṇa-madhye śreṣṭhā rādhā-ṭhākurāṇī

nirmala-ujjvala-rasa-prema-ratna-khani

*gopī-gaṇa-madhye*—de todas as gopīs; *śreṣṭhā*—a principal; *rādhā-ṭhākurāṇī*—Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; *nirmala*—purificadas; *ujjvala*—brilhantes; *rasa*—em doçuras; *prema*—de amor extático; *ratna-khani*—a mina de joias.

**"De todas as gopīs, Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī é a principal. Ela é uma mina de joias de amor extático e a fonte de todas as doçuras conjugais transcendentais purificadas.**
