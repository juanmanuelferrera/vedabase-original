### CC Madhya 14.245

> পূর্ববৎ কৈল প্রভু লঞা ভক্তগণ ।
> পরম আনন্দে করেন নর্তন-কীর্তন ॥২৪৫॥

pūrvavat kaila prabhu lañā bhakta-gaṇa

parama ānande karena nartana-kīrtana

*pūrva-vat*—como anteriormente; *kaila*—fez; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *lañā*—levando; *bhakta-gaṇa*—todos os devotos; *parama ānande*—com grande prazer; *karena*—realiza; *nartana-kīrtana*—cantar e dançar.

**Como anteriormente, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e Seus devotos novamente cantaram e dançaram com grande prazer.**
