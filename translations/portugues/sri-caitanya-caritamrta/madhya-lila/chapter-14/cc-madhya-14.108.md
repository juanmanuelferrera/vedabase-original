### CC Madhya 14.108

> মহোত্সব কর তৈছে বিশেষ সম্ভার ।\
> দেখি’ মহাপ্রভুর যৈছে হয় চমৎকার ॥১০৮॥

mahotsava kara taiche viśeṣa sambhāra

dekhi' mahāprabhura yaiche haya camatkāra

*mahotsava*—o festival; *kara*—realiza; *taiche*—de tal maneira; *viśeṣa sambhāra*—com grande suntuosidade; *dekhi'*—após ver; *mahāprabhura*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *yaiche*—de modo que; *haya*—haja; *camatkāra*—espanto.

**O rei Pratāparudra disse: "Realiza este festival de maneira tão suntuosa que, ao vê-lo, Caitanya Mahāprabhu fique plenamente satisfeito e maravilhado.**
