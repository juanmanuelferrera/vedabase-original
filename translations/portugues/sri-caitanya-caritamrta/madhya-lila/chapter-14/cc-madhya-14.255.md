### CC Madhya 14.255

> এইমত ভক্তগণে যাত্রা দেখাইল ।\
> ভক্তগণ লঞা বৃন্দাবন-কেলি কৈল ॥২৫৫॥

ei-mata bhakta-gaṇe yātrā dekhāila

bhakta-gaṇa lañā vṛndāvana-keli kaila

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *bhakta-gaṇe*—a todos os devotos; *yātrā*—o festival de Ratha-yātrā; *dekhāila*—mostrou; *bhakta-gaṇa*—os devotos; *lañā*—com; *vṛndāvana-keli*—passatempos de Vṛndāvana; *kaila*—realizou.

**Dessa maneira, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu mostrou a cerimônia de Ratha-yātrā a Seus devotos e realizou com eles os passatempos de Vṛndāvana.**
