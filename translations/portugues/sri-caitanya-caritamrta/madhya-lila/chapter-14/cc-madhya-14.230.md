### CC Madhya 14.230

> রাধার শুদ্ধরস প্রভু আবেশে শুনিল ।
> সেই রসাবেশে প্রভু নৃত্য আরম্ভিল ॥২৩০॥

rādhāra śuddha-rasa prabhu āveśe śunila

sei rasāveśe prabhu nṛtya ārambhila

*rādhāra*—de Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; *śuddha-rasa*—doçuras transcendentais puras; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *āveśe śunila*—ouviu com grande êxtase; *sei*—essa; *rasa-āveśe*—em absorção no amor extático; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nṛtya ārambhila*—começou a dançar.

**Foi assim que Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ouviu essas conversas sobre a doçura transcendental pura de Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī. Absorto em êxtase transcendental, o Senhor começou a dançar.**
