### CC Madhya 14.2

> জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ।
> জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ জয়াদ্বৈত ধন্য ॥২॥

jaya jaya gauracandra śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya
jaya jaya nityānanda jayādvaita dhanya

*jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *gauracandra*—a Gauracandra; *śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *nityānanda*—a Nityānanda Prabhu; *jaya*—todas as glórias; *advaita*—a Advaita Ācārya; *dhanya*—exaltado.

**Todas as glórias a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, conhecido como Gauracandra! Todas as glórias ao Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu! Todas as glórias a Advaita Ācārya, que é tão exaltado!**
