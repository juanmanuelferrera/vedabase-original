### CC Madhya 14.100

> তবে বক্রেশ্বরে প্রভু কহিলা নাচিতে ।
> বক্রেশ্বর নাচে, প্রভু লাগিলা গাইতে ॥১০০॥

tabe vakreśvare prabhu kahilā nācite

vakreśvara nāce, prabhu lāgilā gāite

*tabe*—em seguida; *vakreśvare*—a Vakreśvara Paṇḍita; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kahilā*—ordenou; *nācite*—dançar; *vakreśvara nāce*—Vakreśvara Paṇḍita começou a dançar; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *lāgilā*—começou; *gāite*—a cantar.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu então ordenou a Vakreśvara Paṇḍita que dançasse e, assim que ele começou a dançar, o Senhor começou a cantar.**
