### CC Madhya 14.164

> এত শুনি’ বাড়ে প্রভুর আনন্দ-সাগর ।
> ‘কহ, কহ’ কহে প্রভু, বলে দামোদর ॥১৬৪॥

eta śuni' bāḍe prabhura ānanda-sāgara

'kaha, kaha' kahe prabhu, bale dāmodara

*eta śuni'*—ao ouvir isto; *bāḍe*—aumentou; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ānanda-sāgara*—o oceano de bem-aventurança transcendental; *kaha kaha*—continua a falar; *kahe prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu continuava a pedir; *bale dāmodara*—Dāmodara Gosvāmī continuava a responder.

**Quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ouviu estas conversas, Seu oceano de bem-aventurança transcendental aumentou. Por isso, Ele disse a Svarūpa Dāmodara: "Continua a falar, continua a falar." E assim Svarūpa Dāmodara prosseguiu.**
