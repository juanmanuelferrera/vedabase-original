### CC Madhya 14.70

> প্রাতঃকালে স্নান করি’ দেখি’ জগন্নাথ ।
> সংকীর্তনে নৃত্য করে ভক্তগণ সাথ ॥৭০॥

prātaḥ-kāle snāna kari' dekhi' jagannātha

saṅkīrtane nṛtya kare bhakta-gaṇa sātha

*prātaḥ-kāle*—pela manhã; *snāna kari'*—tomando banho; *dekhi'*—após ver; *jagannātha*—o Senhor Jagannātha; *saṅkīrtane*—na execução do saṅkīrtana; *nṛtya kare*—dança; *bhakta-gaṇa sātha*—com os devotos.

**Após tomar Seu banho de manhã cedo, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ia ver o Senhor Jagannātha no templo. Então Ele executava o saṅkīrtana com Seus devotos.**
