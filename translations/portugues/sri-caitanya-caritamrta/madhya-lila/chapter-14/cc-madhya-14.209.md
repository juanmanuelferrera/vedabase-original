### CC Madhya 14.209

> এত বলি’ মহালক্ষ্মীর সব দাসীগণে  ।
> কটি-বস্ত্রে বান্ধি’ আনে প্রভুর নিজগণে ॥২০৯॥

eta bali' mahā-lakṣmīra saba dāsī-gaṇe

kaṭi-vastre bāndhi' āne prabhura nija-gaṇe

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *mahā-lakṣmīra*—da deusa da fortuna; *saba*—todas; *dāsī-gaṇe*—as criadas; *kaṭi-vastre*—por seus panos da cintura; *bāndhi'*—amarrando; *āne*—trazem; *prabhura*—de Jagannātha; *nija-gaṇe*—os servos pessoais.

**"Dessa maneira, todas as criadas da deusa da fortuna prenderam os servos de Jagannātha, amarraram-nos pela cintura e os trouxeram diante da deusa da fortuna.**
