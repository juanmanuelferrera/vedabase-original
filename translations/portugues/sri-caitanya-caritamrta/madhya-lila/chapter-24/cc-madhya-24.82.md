### CC Madhya 24.82

> ‘ব্রহ্ম-আত্মা’-শব্দে যদি কৃষ্ণেরে কহয় ।\
> ‘রূঢ়িবৃত্ত্যে’ নির্বিশেষ অন্তর্যামী কয় ॥৮২॥

'brahma-ātmā'-śabde yadi kṛṣṇere kahaya

'rūḍhi-vṛttye' nirviśeṣa antaryāmī kaya

*brahma-ātmā*—brahma e ātmā; *śabde*—por estas palavras; *yadi*—se; *kṛṣṇere kahaya*—indica-se Kṛṣṇa; *rūḍhi-vṛttye*—pelo significado direto; *nirviśeṣa*—impessoal; *antaryāmī*—a Superalma; *kaya*—diz-se.

**"Embora as palavras brahma e ātmā indiquem Kṛṣṇa, seu significado direto refere-se apenas ao Brahman impessoal e à Superalma.**
