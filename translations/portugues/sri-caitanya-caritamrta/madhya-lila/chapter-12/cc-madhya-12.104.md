### CC Madhya 12.104

> নিজ-বস্ত্রে কৈল প্রভু গৃহ সংমার্জন ।
> মহাপ্রভু নিজ-বস্ত্রে মাজিল সিংহাসন ॥১০৪॥

nija-vastre kaila prabhu gṛha sammārjana

mahāprabhu nija-vastre mājila siṁhāsana

*nija-vastre*—com Sua própria veste; *kaila*—fez; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *gṛha*—sala; *sammārjana*—esfregação; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nija-vastre*—com Sua própria veste; *mājila*—poliu; *siṁhāsana*—o trono.

**O Senhor esfregou as salas com Suas próprias roupas, e com elas também poliu o trono.**
