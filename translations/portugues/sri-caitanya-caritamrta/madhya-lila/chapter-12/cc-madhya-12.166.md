### CC Madhya 12.166

> যদ্যপি প্রেমাবেশে প্রভু হৈলা অস্থির ।\
> সময় বুঝিয়া প্রভু হৈলা কিছু ধীর ॥১৬৬॥

yadyapi premāveśe prabhu hailā asthira

samaya bujhiyā prabhu hailā kichu dhīra

*yadyapi*—embora; *prema-āveśe*—em amor extático; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *hailā*—ficou; *asthira*—agitado; *samaya bujhiyā*—compreendendo o tempo e as circunstâncias; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *hailā*—ficou; *kichu*—um tanto; *dhīra*—paciente.

**Ao simplesmente lembrar os passatempos do Senhor Śrī Kṛṣṇa, o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ficava agitado pelo amor extático. No entanto, considerando o tempo e as circunstâncias, mantinha-Se com alguma paciência.**
