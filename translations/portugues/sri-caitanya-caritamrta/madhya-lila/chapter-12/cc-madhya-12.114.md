### CC Madhya 12.114

> প্রেমাবেশে প্রভু কহে ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’-নাম ।\
> একলে প্রেমাবেশে করে শতজনের কাম ॥১১৪॥

premāveśe prabhu kahe 'kṛṣṇa' 'kṛṣṇa'-nāma

ekale premāveśe kare śata-janera kāma

*prema-āveśe*—em amor extático; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kahe*—diz; *kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa-nāma*—o santo nome do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *ekale*—sozinho; *prema-āveśe*—em amor extático; *kare*—faz; *śata-janera kāma*—o trabalho de centenas de homens.

**Enquanto Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu vibrava o santo nome de Kṛṣṇa em amor extático, Ele próprio realizava o trabalho de centenas de homens.**
