### CC Madhya 12.71

> এইমত নানা রঙ্গে দিন কত গেল ।\
> জগন্নাথের রথযাত্রা নিকট হইল ॥৭১॥

ei-mata nānā raṅge dina kata gela

jagannāthera ratha-yātrā nikaṭa ha-ila

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *nānā raṅge*—em grande júbilo; *dina kata*—alguns dias; *gela*—passaram; *jagannāthera*—do Senhor Śrī Jagannātha; *ratha-yātrā*—o festival dos carros; *nikaṭa ha-ila*—aproximou-se.

**Dessa maneira, o Senhor passou alguns dias em grande júbilo. Então o festival dos carros do Senhor Jagannātha se aproximou.**
