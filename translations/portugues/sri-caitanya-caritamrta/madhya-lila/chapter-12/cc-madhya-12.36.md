### CC Madhya 12.36

> তবে নিত্যানন্দ-গোসাঞি গোবিন্দের পাশ ।
> মাগিয়া লইল প্রভুর এক বহির্বাস ॥৩৬॥

tabe nityānanda-gosāñi govindera pāśa
māgiyā la-ila prabhura eka bahirvāsa

*tabe*—naquele momento; *nityānanda-gosāñi*—o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *govindera pāśa*—de Govinda, o servidor pessoal de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *māgiyā*—solicitando; *la-ila*—tomou; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *eka*—uma; *bahirvāsa*—vestimenta externa.

**O Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu obteve então uma vestimenta externa usada pelo Senhor, solicitando-a a Govinda.**
