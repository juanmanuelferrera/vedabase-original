### CC Madhya 12.112

> ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহি’ করে ঘটের প্রার্থন ।
> ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহি’ করে ঘট সমর্পণ ॥১১২॥

'kṛṣṇa' 'kṛṣṇa' kahi' kare ghaṭera prārthana

'kṛṣṇa' 'kṛṣṇa' kahi' kare ghaṭa samarpaṇa

*kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa kahi'*—enquanto cantava "Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa"; *kare*—faz; *ghaṭera*—pelos potes de água; *prārthana*—súplica; *kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa*—o santo nome do Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *kahi'*—cantando; *kare*—faz; *ghaṭa*—dos potes de água; *samarpaṇa*—entrega.

**Uma pessoa suplicava por um pote de água cantando os santos nomes "Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa", e outra entregava um pote enquanto cantava "Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa".**
