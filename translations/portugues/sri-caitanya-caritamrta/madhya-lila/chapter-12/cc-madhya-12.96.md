### CC Madhya 12.96

> ‘জল আন’ বলি’ যবে মহাপ্রভু কহিল ।
> তবে শত ঘট আনি’ প্রভু-আগে দিল ॥৯৬॥

'jala āna' bali' yabe mahāprabhu kahila

tabe śata ghaṭa āni' prabhu-āge dila

*jala āna*—trazei água; *bali'*—dizendo; *yabe*—quando; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kahila*—ordenou; *tabe*—nesse momento; *śata ghaṭa*—cem potes; *āni'*—trazendo; *prabhu-āge*—diante do Senhor; *dila*—entregaram.

**Tão logo Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu pediu água, todos os homens imediatamente trouxeram os cem potes, que estavam completamente cheios, e os entregaram diante do Senhor.**
