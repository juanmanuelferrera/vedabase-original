### CC Madhya 12.142

> এইমত কতক্ষণ নৃত্য যে করিয়া ।
> বিশ্রাম করিলা প্রভু সময় বুঝিয়া ॥১৪২॥

ei-mata kata-kṣaṇa nṛtya ye kariyā

viśrāma karilā prabhu samaya bujhiyā

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *kata-kṣaṇa*—por algum tempo; *nṛtya*—dança; *ye*—essa; *kariyā*—após realizar; *viśrāma karilā*—descansou; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *samaya bujhiyā*—compreendendo a hora.

**O Senhor cantou e dançou assim por algum tempo. Por fim, compreendendo as circunstâncias, parou.**
