### CC Madhya 12.45

> উত্কণ্ঠাতে প্ৰতাপরুদ্র নারে রহিবারে ।
> রামানন্দ সাধিলেন প্ৰভুরে মিলিবারে ॥৪৫॥

utkaṇṭhāte pratāparudra nāre rahibāre
rāmānanda sādhilena prabhure milibāre

*utkaṇṭhāte*—em grande ansiedade; *pratāparudra*—o rei Pratāparudra; *nāre rahibāre*—não conseguia permanecer; *rāmānanda*—Śrī Rāmānanda Rāya; *sādhilena*—solicitou; *prabhure*—a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *milibāre*—encontrar-se.

**Mahārāja Pratāparudra, em grande ansiedade, não suportava ficar sem ver o Senhor; portanto, Śrī Rāmānanda Rāya, por sua diplomacia, providenciou um encontro do rei com o Senhor.**
