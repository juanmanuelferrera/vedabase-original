### CC Madhya 12.69

> এইমত মহাপ্রভু ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে ।
> নিরন্তর ক্রীড়া করে সংকীর্তন-রঙ্গে ॥৬৯॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge

nirantara krīḍā kare saṅkīrtana-raṅge

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge*—na companhia de Seus devotos puros; *nirantara*—constantemente; *krīḍā kare*—realiza passatempos; *saṅkīrtana-raṅge*—no decorrer de Seu movimento de saṅkīrtana.

**Assim, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu age na companhia de Seus devotos puros, realizando Seus passatempos e difundindo o movimento de saṅkīrtana.**
