### CC Madhya 12.220

> প্রাতঃকালে রথযাত্রা হবেক জানিয়া ।
> সেবক লাগায় ভোগ দ্বিগুণ করিয়া ॥২২০॥

prātaḥ-kāle ratha-yātrā habeka jāniyā
sevaka lāgāya bhoga dviguṇa kariyā

*prātaḥ-kāle*—pela manhã; *ratha-yātrā*—o festival das quadrigas; *habeka*—ocorreria; *jāniyā*—sabendo; *sevaka*—os servos sacerdotais do Senhor; *lāgāya*—oferecem; *bhoga*—alimento; *dvi-guṇa kariyā*—dobrando a quantidade.

**Sabendo que o festival das quadrigas ocorreria pela manhã, todos os servos do Senhor Jagannātha dobravam suas oferendas de alimento.**
