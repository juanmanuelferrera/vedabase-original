### CC Madhya 12.171

> পুনরপি সেই দ্রব্য করে নিরীক্ষণ ।
> তাঁর ভয়ে প্রভু কিছু করেন ভক্ষণ ॥১৭১॥

punarapi sei dravya kare nirīkṣaṇa

tāṅra bhaye prabhu kichu karena bhakṣaṇa

*punarapi*—novamente; *sei dravya*—justamente aquilo; *kare nirīkṣaṇa*—observa cuidadosamente; *tāṅra bhaye*—por temor a Jagadānanda; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kichu*—algo; *karena*—faz; *bhakṣaṇa*—comer.

**Quando o alimento era assim entregue, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu o observava por algum tempo. Por temor a Jagadānanda, Ele finalmente comia algo.**
