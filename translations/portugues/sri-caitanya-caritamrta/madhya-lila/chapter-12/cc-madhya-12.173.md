### CC Madhya 12.173

> স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি ভাল মিষ্টপ্রসাদ লঞা  ।\
> প্রভুকে নিবেদন করে আগে দাণ্ডাঞা ॥১৭৩॥

svarūpa-gosāñi bhāla miṣṭa-prasāda lañā

prabhuke nivedana kare āge dāṇḍāñā

*svarūpa-gosāñi*—Svarūpa Dāmodara; *bhāla*—de primeira classe; *miṣṭa-prasāda*—doces; *lañā*—tomando; *prabhuke*—ao Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nivedana kare*—ofereceu; *āge*—diante Dele; *dāṇḍāñā*—de pé.

**Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī trouxe então alguns doces excelentes e, de pé diante do Senhor, ofereceu-os a Ele.**
