### CC Madhya 22.2

jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya nityānanda
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

*jaya jaya*—todas as glórias; *śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya nityānanda*—a Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu e Nityānanda Prabhu; *jaya*—todas as glórias; *advaita-candra*—a Advaita Prabhu; *jaya*—todas as glórias; *gaura-bhakta-vṛnda*—aos devotos de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Todas as glórias a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu! Todas as glórias a Nityānanda Prabhu! Todas as glórias a Advaitacandra! Todas as glórias a todos os devotos de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu!**
