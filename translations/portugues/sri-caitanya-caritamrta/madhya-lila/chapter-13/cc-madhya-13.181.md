### CC Madhya 13.181

> সম্ভ্রমে প্রতাপরুদ্র প্রভুকে ধরিল ৷ তাঁহাকে দেখিতে প্ৰভুর বাহ্যজ্ঞান হইল ॥১৮১॥

sambhrame pratāparudra prabhuke dharila

tāṅhāke dekhite prabhura bāhya-jñāna ha-ila

*sambhrame*—com grande respeito; *pratāparudra*—o rei Pratāparudra; *prabhuke*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dharila*—ergueu; *tāṅhāke*—a ele; *dekhite*—ao ver; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bāhya-jñāna*—consciência externa; *ha-ila*—houve.

**Mahārāja Pratāparudra ergueu o Senhor com grande respeito, mas, ao ver o rei, o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu recobrou Seus sentidos externos.**
