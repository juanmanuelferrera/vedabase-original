### CC Madhya 13.30

> পরমানন্দ পুরী, আর ভারতী ব্রহ্মানন্দ ।\
> শ্রীহস্তে চন্দন পাঞা বাড়িল আনন্দ ॥৩০॥

paramānanda purī, āra bhāratī brahmānanda

śrī-haste candana pāñā bāḍila ānanda

*paramānanda purī*—Paramānanda Purī; *āra*—e; *bhāratī brahmānanda*—Brahmānanda Bhāratī; *śrī-haste*—pela mão do Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *candana*—polpa de sândalo; *pāñā*—recebendo; *bāḍila*—aumentou; *ānanda*—bem-aventurança transcendental.

**Paramānanda Purī e Brahmānanda Bhāratī receberam pessoalmente guirlandas e polpa de sândalo das próprias mãos de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Isso aumentou-lhes o prazer transcendental.**
