### CC Madhya 13.186

> প্রভুর বচনে রাজার মনে হৈল ভয় ।
> সার্বভৌম কহে, — তুমি না কর সংশয় ॥১৮৬॥

prabhura vacane rājāra mane haila bhaya

sārvabhauma kahe,-tumi nā kaya saṁśaya

*prabhura vacane*—pelas palavras de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *rājāra*—do rei; *mane*—na mente; *haila*—houve; *bhaya*—temor; *sārvabhauma kahe*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya disse; *tumi*—tu (o rei); *nā kaya saṁśaya*—não te preocupes.

**O rei Pratāparudra ficou amedrontado quando o Senhor Caitanya manifestou ira externa, mas Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya disse ao rei: "Não te preocupes."**
