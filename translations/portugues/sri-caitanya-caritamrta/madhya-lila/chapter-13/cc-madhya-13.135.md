### CC Madhya 13.135

> স্বরূপ সঙ্গে যার অর্থ করে আস্বাদন ।\
> নৃত্যমধ্যে সেই শ্লোক করেন পঠন ॥১৩৫॥

svarūpa saṅge yāra artha kare āsvādana

nṛtya-madhye sei śloka karena paṭhana

*svarūpa saṅge*—na companhia de Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; *yāra*—do qual; *artha*—significado; *kare*—faz; *āsvādana*—saboreio; *nṛtya-madhye*—em meio à dança; *sei śloka*—esse verso; *karena paṭhana*—recita.

**Enquanto dançava, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu novamente começou a recitar esse verso, cujo significado Ele saboreava na companhia de Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī.**
