### CC Madhya 13.205

> এই ত’ কহিল প্রভুর মহাসংকীর্তন ।\
> জগন্নাথের আগে যৈছে করিল নর্তন ॥২০৫॥

ei ta' kahila prabhura mahā-saṅkīrtana

jagannāthera āge yaiche karila nartana

*ei ta'*—desta maneira; *kahila*—descrevi; *prabhura*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mahā-saṅkīrtana*—o grande canto congregacional; *jagannāthera āge*—diante do Senhor Jagannātha; *yaiche*—como; *karila*—Ele realizou; *nartana*—a dança.

**Assim, descrevi a grande realização do canto congregacional pelo Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu enquanto Ele dançava diante do Senhor Jagannātha.**
