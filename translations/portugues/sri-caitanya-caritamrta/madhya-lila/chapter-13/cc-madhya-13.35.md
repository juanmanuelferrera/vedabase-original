### CC Madhya 13.35

> নিত্যানন্দ, অদ্বৈত, হরিদাস, বক্রেশ্বরে ৷\
> চারি জনে আজ্ঞা দিল নৃত্য করিবারে ॥৩৫॥

nityānanda, advaita, haridāsa, vakreśvare

cāri jane ājñā dila nṛtya karibāre

*nityānanda*—o Senhor Nityānanda; *advaita*—Advaita Ācārya; *haridāsa*—Haridāsa Ṭhākura; *vakreśvare*—Vakreśvara Paṇḍita; *cāri jane*—a estas quatro pessoas; *ājñā dila*—o Senhor deu uma ordem; *nṛtya karibāre*—para dançar.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ordenou a Nityānanda Prabhu, Advaita Ācārya, Haridāsa Ṭhākura e Vakreśvara Paṇḍita que dançassem em cada um dos quatro grupos respectivos.**
