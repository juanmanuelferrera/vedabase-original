### CC Madhya 13.189

> তবে মহাপ্ৰভু রথ প্রদক্ষিণ করিয়া ।\
> রথ-পাছে যাই’ ঠেলে রথে মাথা দিয়া ॥১৮৯॥

tabe mahāprabhu ratha pradakṣiṇa kariyā

ratha-pāche yāi' ṭhele rathe māthā diyā

*tabe*—nessa ocasião; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ratha*—a quadriga; *pradakṣiṇa*—circum-ambulação; *kariyā*—fazendo; *ratha-pāche*—à parte traseira da quadriga; *yāi'*—indo; *ṭhele*—empurra; *rathe*—na quadriga; *māthā diyā*—pousando a cabeça.

**Após circum-ambular Jagannātha, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu foi para trás da quadriga e começou a empurrá-la com a cabeça.**
