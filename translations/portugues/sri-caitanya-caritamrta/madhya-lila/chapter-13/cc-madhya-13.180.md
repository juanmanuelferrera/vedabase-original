### CC Madhya 13.180

> এইমত প্রভু নৃত্য করিতে ভ্রমিতে  ।\
> প্ৰতাপরুদ্রের আগে লাগিলা পড়িতে ॥১৮০॥

ei-mata prabhu nṛtya karite bhramite

pratāparudrera āge lāgilā paḍite

*ei-mata*—dessa maneira; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nṛtya karite*—dançando; *bhramite*—vagando; *pratāparudrera*—do rei Pratāparudra; *āge*—diante; *lāgilā*—começou; *paḍite*—a cair.

**Enquanto o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu dançava e vagava dessa maneira, Ele caiu diante de Mahārāja Pratāparudra.**
