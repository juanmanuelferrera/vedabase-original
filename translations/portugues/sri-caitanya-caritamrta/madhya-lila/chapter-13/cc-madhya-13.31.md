### CC Madhya 13.31

> অদ্বৈত-আচার্য, আর প্রভু-নিত্যানন্দ ।
> শ্রীহস্ত-স্পর্শে দুঁহার হইল আনন্দ ॥৩১॥

advaita-ācārya, āra prabhu-nityānanda

śrī-hasta-sparśe duṅhāra ha-ila ānanda

*advaita-ācārya*—Advaita Ācārya; *āra*—e; *prabhu-nityānanda*—o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *śrī-hasta-sparśe*—pelo toque da mão transcendental do Senhor Caitanya; *duṅhāra*—de Ambos; *ha-ila*—houve; *ānanda*—bem-aventurança transcendental.

**De modo semelhante, quando Advaita Ācārya e Nityānanda Prabhu sentiram o toque da mão transcendental de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Ambos ficaram muito satisfeitos.**
