### CC Madhya 13.52

> আর এক শক্তি প্রভু করিল প্রকাশ ।\
> এককালে সাত ঠাঞি করিল বিলাস ॥৫২॥

āra eka śakti prabhu karila prakāśa

eka-kāle sāta ṭhāñi karila vilāsa

*āra*—outro; *eka*—um; *śakti*—poder místico; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *karila*—fez; *prakāśa*—manifestar; *eka-kāle*—simultaneamente; *sāta ṭhāñi*—em sete lugares; *karila*—realizou; *vilāsa*—passatempos.

**O Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu manifestou então outro poder místico, realizando passatempos simultaneamente em todos os sete grupos.**
