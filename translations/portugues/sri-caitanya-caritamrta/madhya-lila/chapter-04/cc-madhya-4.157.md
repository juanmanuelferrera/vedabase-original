### CC Madhya 4.157

> সেই রাত্রে দেবালয়ে করিল শয়ন ।
> শেষরাত্রি হৈলে পুরী দেখিল স্বপন ॥১৫৭॥

sei rātre devālaye karila śayana
śeṣa-rātri haile purī dekhila svapana

*sei rātre*—naquela noite; *deva-ālaye*—no templo; *karila*—fez; *śayana*—dormir; *śeṣa-rātri*—ao fim da noite; *haile*—quando era; *purī*—Mādhavendra Purī; *dekhila*—viu; *svapana*—um sonho.

**Mādhavendra Purī descansou naquela noite no templo, mas, ao fim da noite, teve outro sonho.**
