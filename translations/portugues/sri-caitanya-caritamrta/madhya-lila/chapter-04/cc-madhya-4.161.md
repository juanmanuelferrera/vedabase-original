### CC Madhya 4.161

> দ্বিধা না ভাবিহ, না করিহ কিছু মনে ।
> বিশ্বাস করি’ চন্দন দেহ আমার বচনে ॥১৬১॥

dvidhā nā bhāviha, nā kariha kichu mane
viśvāsa kari' candana deha āmāra vacane

*dvidhā nā bhāviha*—não hesites; *nā kariha*—não faças; *kichu*—qualquer coisa; *mane*—na mente; *viśvāsa kari'*—acreditando em Mim; *candana*—sândalo; *deha*—oferece; *āmāra vacane*—sob Minha ordem.

**"Não deves hesitar em agir conforme a Minha ordem. Acreditando em Mim, simplesmente faze o que for necessário."**
