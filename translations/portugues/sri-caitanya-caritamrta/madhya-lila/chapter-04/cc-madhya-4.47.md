### CC Madhya 4.47

> প্রাতঃস্নান করি’ পুরী গ্রামমধ্যে গেলা ।
> সব লোক একত্র করি’ কহিতে লাগিলা ॥৪৭॥

prātaḥ-snāna kari' purī grāma-madhye gelā
saba loka ekatra kari' kahite lāgilā

*prātaḥ-snāna*—o banho matinal; *kari'*—terminando; *purī*—Mādhavendra Purī; *grāma-madhye*—no interior da aldeia; *gelā*—entrou; *saba loka*—todas as pessoas; *ekatra kari'*—reunindo; *kahite lāgilā*—começou a falar.

**Após tomar seu banho matinal, Mādhavendra Purī entrou na aldeia e reuniu todas as pessoas. Então, falou da seguinte maneira.**
