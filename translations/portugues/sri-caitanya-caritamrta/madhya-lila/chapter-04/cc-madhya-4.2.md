### CC Madhya 4.2

> জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র জয় নিত্যানন্দ ।\
> জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌড়ভক্তবৃন্দ ॥২॥

jaya jaya gauracandra jaya nityānanda
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

*jaya jaya gauracandra*—todas as glórias a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jaya nityānanda*—todas as glórias ao Senhor Nityānanda; *jaya advaita-candra*—todas as glórias a Advaita Prabhu; *jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda*—todas as glórias aos devotos do Senhor.

**Todas as glórias ao Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu! Todas as glórias a Nityānanda Prabhu! Todas as glórias a Advaita Prabhu! E todas as glórias a todos os devotos do Senhor Caitanya!**
