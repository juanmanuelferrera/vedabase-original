### CC Madhya 4.209

> নাম-সংকীর্তনে সেই রাত্রি গোঙাইলা ।\
> মঙ্গল-আরতি দেখি’ প্রভাতে চলিলা ॥২০৯॥

nāma-saṅkīrtane sei rātri goṅāilā
maṅgala-ārati dekhi' prabhāte calilā

*nāma-saṅkīrtane*—em cantar congregacional; *sei*—aquela; *rātri*—noite; *goṅāilā*—passou; *maṅgala-ārati*—o ārati matinal; *dekhi'*—após ver; *prabhāte*—pela manhã; *calilā*—partiu.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu passou aquela noite no templo ocupado em cantar congregacional. Pela manhã, após ver a execução do maṅgala-ārati, Ele partiu.**
