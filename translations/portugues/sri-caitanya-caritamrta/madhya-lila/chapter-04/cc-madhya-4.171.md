### CC Madhya 4.171

> প্রভু কহে, — নিত্যানন্দ, করহ বিচার ৷\
> পুরী-সম ভাগ্যবান্ জগতে নাহি আর ॥১৭১॥

prabhu kahe,--nityānanda, karaha vicāra
purī-sama bhāgyavān jagate nāhi āra

*prabhu kahe*—o Senhor disse; *nityānanda*—Nityānanda Prabhu; *karaha vicāra*—considera apenas; *purī-sama*—como Mādhavendra Purī; *bhāgyavān*—afortunado; *jagate*—no mundo; *nāhi*—não há; *āra*—ninguém mais.

**O Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu pediu a Nityānanda Prabhu que julgasse se havia alguém no mundo tão afortunado quanto Mādhavendra Purī.**
