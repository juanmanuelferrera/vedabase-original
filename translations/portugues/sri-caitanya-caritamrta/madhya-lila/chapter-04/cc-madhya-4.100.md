### CC Madhya 4.100

> স্বর্ণ, রৌপ্য, বস্ত্র, গন্ধ, ভক্ষ্য-উপহার ।\
> অসংখ্য আইসে, নিত্য বাড়িল ভাণ্ডার ॥১০০॥

svarṇa, raupya, vastra, gandha, bhakṣya-upahāra
asaṅkhya āise, nitya bāḍila bhāṇḍāra

*svarṇa*—ouro; *raupya*—prata; *vastra*—vestes; *gandha*—perfumes; *bhakṣya-upahāra*—oferendas comestíveis; *asaṅkhya*—incontáveis; *āise*—chegavam; *nitya*—diariamente; *bāḍila*—aumentava; *bhāṇḍāra*—o depósito.

**Assim, chegavam incontáveis oferendas de ouro, prata, vestes, artigos perfumados e comestíveis. O depósito de Gopāla aumentava a cada dia.**
