### CC Madhya 4.39

> বহুদিন তোমার পথ করি নিরীক্ষণ ।\
> কবে আসি’ মাধব আমা করিবে সেবন ॥৩৯॥

bahu-dina tomāra patha kari nirīkṣaṇa
kabe āsi' mādhava āmā karibe sevana

*bahu-dina*—por muitos dias; *tomāra*—de ti; *patha*—o caminho; *kari*—faço; *nirīkṣaṇa*—observação; *kabe*—quando; *āsi'*—vindo; *mādhava*—Mādhavendra Purī; *āmā*—a Mim; *karibe*—prestará; *sevana*—serviço.

**"Por muitos dias venho observando teu caminho, e venho perguntando a Mim mesmo: 'Quando virá Mādhavendra Purī aqui para Me servir?'**
