### CC Madhya 4.165

> গ্রীষ্মকালে গোপীনাথ পরিবে চন্দন ।
> শুনি’ আনন্দিত হৈল সেবকের মন ॥১৬৫॥

grīṣma-kāle gopīnātha paribe candana
śuni' ānandita haila sevakera mana

*grīṣma-kāle*—no verão; *gopīnātha*—o Senhor Gopīnātha; *paribe*—usará; *candana*—polpa de sândalo; *śuni'*—ao ouvir; *ānandita*—satisfeitas; *haila*—ficaram; *sevakera*—dos servos; *mana*—as mentes.

**Os servos de Gopīnātha ficaram muito satisfeitos ao ouvir que, durante o verão, toda a polpa de sândalo seria usada para ungir o corpo de Gopīnātha.**
