### CC Madhya 4.199

> আস্তে-ব্যস্তে কোলে করি’ নিল নিত্যানন্দ ৷
> ক্রন্দন করিয়া তবে উঠে গৌরচন্দ্র ॥১৯৯॥

āste-vyaste kole kari' nila nityānanda
krandana kariyā tabe uṭhe gauracandra

*āste-vyaste*—com grande pressa; *kole*—no colo; *kari'*—fazendo; *nila*—tomou; *nityānanda*—o Senhor Nityānanda Prabhu; *krandana*—chorando; *kariyā*—fazendo; *tabe*—nesse momento; *uṭhe*—levantou-se; *gauracandra*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Quando o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu caiu ao chão em amor extático, o Senhor Nityānanda rapidamente O tomou em Seu colo. Chorando, Caitanya Mahāprabhu então Se levantou novamente.**
