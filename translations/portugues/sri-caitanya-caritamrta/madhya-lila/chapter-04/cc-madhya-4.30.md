### CC Madhya 4.30

> জল নিতে স্ত্রীগণ তোমারে দেখি’ গেল ৷\
> স্ত্রীসব দুগ্ধ দিয়া আমারে পাঠাইল ॥৩০॥

jala nite strī-gaṇa tomāre dekhi' gela

strī-saba dugdha diyā āmāre pāṭhāila

*jala nite*—para buscar água; *strī-gaṇa*—as mulheres; *tomāre*—a ti; *dekhi' gela*—viram-te e foram; *strī-saba*—todas as mulheres; *dugdha*—leite; *diyā*—dando; *āmāre*—a Mim; *pāṭhāila*—enviaram.

**"As mulheres que vêm aqui buscar água te viram e Me forneceram este leite e Me enviaram a ti."**
