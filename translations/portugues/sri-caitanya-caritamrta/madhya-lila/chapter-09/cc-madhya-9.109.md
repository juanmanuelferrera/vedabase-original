### CC Madhya 9.109

> ‘শ্রী-বৈষ্ণব’ ভট্ট সেবে লক্ষ্মী-নারায়ণ ৷\
> তাঁর ভক্তি দেখি’ প্রভুর তুষ্ট হৈল মন ৷৷ ১০৯ ৷৷ ॥১০৯॥

śrī-vaiṣṇava' bhaṭṭa seve lakṣmī-nārāyaṇa
tāṅra bhakti dekhi' prabhura tuṣṭa haila mana

*śrī-vaiṣṇava*—um devoto da Rāmānuja-sampradāya; *bhaṭṭa*—Vyeṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa; *seve*—costumava adorar; *lakṣmī-nārāyaṇa*—as Deidades do Senhor Nārāyaṇa e da deusa da fortuna, Lakṣmī; *tāṅra*—sua; *bhakti*—devoção; *dekhi'*—vendo; *prabhura*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tuṣṭa*—satisfeita; *haila*—ficou; *mana*—a mente.

**Sendo um vaiṣṇava da Rāmānuja-sampradāya, Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa adorava as Deidades de Lakṣmī e Nārāyaṇa. Ao ver sua devoção pura, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ficou muito satisfeito.**
