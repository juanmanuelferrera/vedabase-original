### CC Madhya 9.314

> শূন্যস্থল দেখি’ লোকের হৈল চমৎকার ৷ লোকে কহে, এ সন্ন্যাসী — রাম-অবতার ৷৷ ৩১৪ ৷৷ সশরীরে তাল গেল শ্রীবৈকুণ্ঠ-ধাম ৷ ঐছে শক্তি কার হয়, বিনা এক রাম ৷৷ ৩১৫ ৷৷ ॥৩১৪॥

śūnya-sthala dekhi' lokera haila camatkāra
loke kahe, e sannyāsī--rāma-avatāra

*śūnya-sthala*—o lugar vazio; *dekhi'*—vendo; *lokera*—das pessoas em geral; *haila*—houve; *camatkāra*—assombro; *loke kahe*—todas as pessoas começaram a dizer; *e sannyāsī*—este sannyāsī; *rāma-avatāra*—encarnação do Senhor Rāmacandra.

**Depois que as sete palmeiras partiram para Vaikuṇṭha, todos ficaram assombrados de vê-las desaparecidas. As pessoas então começaram a dizer: "Este sannyāsī chamado Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu deve ser uma encarnação do Senhor Rāmacandra.**
