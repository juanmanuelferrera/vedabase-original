### CC Madhya 9.345

> প্রেমাবেশে সার্বভৌম করিলা রোদনে ৷\
> সবা-সঙ্গে আইলা প্রভু ঈশ্বর-দরশনে ৷৷ ৩৪৫ ৷৷ ॥৩৪৫॥

premāveśe sārvabhauma karilā rodane
sabā-saṅge āilā prabhu īśvara-daraśane

*prema-āveśe*—em amor extático; *sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma; *karilā rodane*—chorou; *sabā-saṅge*—com todos eles; *āilā*—veio; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *īśvara-daraśane*—para ver o templo de Jagannātha.

**Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya chorou com grande amor extático. Então, o Senhor, acompanhado por todos eles, dirigiu-Se ao templo de Jagannātha.**
