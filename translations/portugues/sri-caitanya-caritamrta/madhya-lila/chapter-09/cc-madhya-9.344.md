### CC Madhya 9.344

> সার্বভৌম মহাপ্রভুর পড়িলা চরণে ৷\
> প্রভু তাঁরে উঠাঞা কৈল আলিঙ্গনে ৷৷ ৩৪৪ ৷৷ ॥৩৪৪॥

sārvabhauma mahāprabhura paḍilā caraṇe
prabhu tāṅre uṭhāñā kaila āliṅgane

*sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *mahāprabhura*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *paḍilā*—prostrou-se; *caraṇe*—aos pés; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *uṭhāñā*—levantando-se; *kaila āliṅgane*—abraçou.

**Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya prostrou-se aos pés de lótus do Senhor, e o Senhor o ergueu e o abraçou.**
