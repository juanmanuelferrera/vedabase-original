### CC Madhya 9.171

> প্রভু কহে, — তুমি পুনঃ আইস নীলাচলে ৷
> আমি সেতুবন্ধ হৈতে আসিব অল্পকালে ৷৷ ১৭১ ৷৷ ॥১৭১॥

prabhu kahe,--tumi punaḥ āisa nīlācale
āmi setubandha haite āsiba alpa-kāle

*prabhu kahe*—o Senhor disse; *tumi*—tu; *punaḥ*—novamente; *āisa*—vem; *nīlācale*—a Jagannātha Purī; *āmi*—Eu; *setubandha haite*—de Rāmeśvara; *āsiba*—regressarei; *alpa-kāle*—muito em breve.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu então lhe disse: "Por favor, regressa a Jagannātha Purī, pois Eu retornarei lá muito em breve, vindo de Rāmeśvara [Setubandha].**
