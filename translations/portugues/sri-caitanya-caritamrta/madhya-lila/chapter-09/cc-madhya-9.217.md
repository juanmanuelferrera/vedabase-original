### CC Madhya 9.217

> এত বলি’ সেই বিপ্র সুখে পাক কৈল ৷
> উত্তম প্রকারে প্রভুকে ভিক্ষা করাইল ৷৷ ২১৭ ৷৷ ॥২১৭॥

eta bali' sei vipra sukhe pāka kaila
uttama prakāre prabhuke bhikṣā karāila

*eta bali'*—dizendo isto; *sei vipra*—aquele brāhmaṇa; *sukhe*—em grande felicidade; *pāka kaila*—cozinhou; *uttama prakāre*—muito bem; *prabhuke*—ao Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhikṣā*—almoço; *karāila*—ofereceu.

**Dizendo isto, o brāhmaṇa cozinhou com grande felicidade, e um jantar de primeira classe foi oferecido a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
