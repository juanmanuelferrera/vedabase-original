### CC Madhya 9.343

> সার্বভৌম ভট্টাচার্য আনন্দে চলিলা ৷
> সমুদ্রের তীরে আসি’ প্রভুরে মিলিলা ৷৷ ৩৪৩ ৷৷ ॥৩৪৩॥

sārvabhauma bhaṭṭācārya ānande calilā
samudrera tīre āsi' prabhure mililā

*sārvabhauma bhaṭṭācārya*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *ānande*—com prazer; *calilā*—foi; *samudrera tīre*—na praia junto ao oceano; *āsi'*—chegando; *prabhure mililā*—encontrou o Senhor.

**Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya também foi ver o Senhor com grande prazer, e encontrou-se com Ele na praia junto ao mar.**
