### CC Madhya 9.350

> প্রভু লঞা সার্বভৌম নিজ-ঘরে গেলা ৷
> মোর ঘরে ভিক্ষা বলি’ নিমন্ত্রণ কৈলা ৷৷ ৩৫০ ৷৷ ॥৩৫০॥

prabhu lañā sārvabhauma nija-ghare gelā
mora ghare bhikṣā bali' nimantraṇa kailā

*prabhu lañā*—levando o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *nija-ghare*—à sua própria casa; *gelā*—foi; *mora*—minha; *ghare*—em casa; *bhikṣā*—almoço; *bali'*—dizendo; *nimantraṇa kailā*—convidou.

**Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya então levou o Senhor consigo para sua casa, dizendo: "O almoço de hoje será em minha casa." Dessa forma ele convidou o Senhor.**
