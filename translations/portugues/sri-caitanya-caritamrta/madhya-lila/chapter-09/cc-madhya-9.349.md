### CC Madhya 9.349

> কাশীমিশ্র আসি’ প্রভুর পড়িলা চরণে ৷\
> মান্য করি’ প্রভু তাঁরে কৈল আলিঙ্গনে ৷৷ ৩৪৯ ৷৷ ॥৩৪৯॥

kāśī-miśra āsi' prabhura paḍilā caraṇe
mānya kari' prabhu tāṅre kaila āliṅgane

*kāśī-miśra*—Kāśī Miśra; *āsi'*—chegando; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *paḍilā*—prostrou-se; *caraṇe*—aos pés; *mānya kari'*—com grande respeito; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *kaila*—fez; *āliṅgane*—abraçando.

**Em seguida, Kāśī Miśra veio e prostrou-se aos pés de lótus do Senhor, e o Senhor o abraçou respeitosamente.**
