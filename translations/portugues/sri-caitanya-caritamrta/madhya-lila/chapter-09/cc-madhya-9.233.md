### CC Madhya 9.233

> ভট্টথারি-ঘরে মহা উঠিল ক্রন্দন ৷
> কেশে ধরি’ বিপ্রে লঞা করিল গমন ৷৷ ২৩৩ ৷৷ ॥২৩৩॥

bhaṭṭathāri-ghare mahā uṭhila krandana

keśe dhari' vipre lañā karila gamana

*bhaṭṭathāri-ghare*—no lar dos Bhaṭṭathāris; *mahā*—grande; *uṭhila*—ergueu-se; *krandana*—choro; *keśe dhari'*—segurando pelos cabelos; *vipre*—o brāhmaṇa Kṛṣṇadāsa; *lañā*—levando; *karila*—fez; *gamana*—partida.

**Enquanto havia muita gritaria e muito choro na comunidade dos Bhaṭṭathāris, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu agarrou Kṛṣṇadāsa pelos cabelos e o levou embora.**
