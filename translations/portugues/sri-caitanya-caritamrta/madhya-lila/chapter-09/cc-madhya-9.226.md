### CC Madhya 9.226

> গোসাঞির সঙ্গে রহে কৃষ্ণদাস ব্রাহ্মণ ৷
> ভট্টথারি-সহ তাহাঁ হৈল দরশন ৷৷ ২২৬ ৷৷ ॥২২৬॥

gosāñira saṅge rahe kṛṣṇadāsa brāhmaṇa
bhaṭṭathāri-saha tāhāṅ haila daraśana

*gosāñira*—o Senhor; *saṅge*—com; *rahe*—estava; *kṛṣṇadāsa brāhmaṇa*—um servo brāhmaṇa chamado Kṛṣṇadāsa; *bhaṭṭathāri-saha*—com os Bhaṭṭathāris; *tāhāṅ*—ali; *haila*—houve; *daraśana*—um encontro.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu estava acompanhado de Seu servo, Kṛṣṇadāsa. Ele era um brāhmaṇa, mas encontrou-se com os Bhaṭṭathāris ali.**
