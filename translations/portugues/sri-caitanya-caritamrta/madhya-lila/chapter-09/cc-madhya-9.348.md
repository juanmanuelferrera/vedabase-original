### CC Madhya 9.348

> মালা-প্রসাদ পাঞা প্রভু সুস্থির হইলা ৷\
> জগন্নাথের সেবক সব আনন্দে মিলিলা ৷৷ ৩৪৮ ৷৷ ॥৩৪৮॥

mālā-prasāda pāñā prabhu susthira ha-ilā
jagannāthera sevaka saba ānande mililā

*mālā-prasāda*—a guirlanda e o prasāda; *pāñā*—recebendo; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *su-sthira ha-ilā*—acalmou-se; *jagannāthera*—do Senhor Jagannātha; *sevaka*—servos; *saba*—todos; *ānande mililā*—encontraram-se com Ele com grande prazer.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu acalmou-se após receber a guirlanda e o prasāda do Senhor Jagannātha. Todos os servos do Senhor Jagannātha encontraram-se com Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu com grande prazer.**
