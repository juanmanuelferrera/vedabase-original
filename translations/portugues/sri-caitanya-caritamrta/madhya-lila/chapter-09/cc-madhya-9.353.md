### CC Madhya 9.353

> ভিক্ষা করাঞা তাঁরে করাইল শয়ন ৷\
> আপনে সার্বভৌম করে পাদসম্বাহন ৷৷ ৩৫৩ ৷৷ ॥৩৫৩॥

bhikṣā karāñā tāṅre karāila śayana
āpane sārvabhauma kare pāda-saṁvāhana

*bhikṣā karāñā*—após oferecer o almoço; *tāṅre*—a Ele; *karāila*—fez; *śayana*—deitar-se para descansar; *āpane*—pessoalmente; *sārvabhauma*—Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya; *kare*—faz; *pāda-saṁvāhana*—massageando as pernas.

**Após oferecer o alimento a Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya fê-Lo deitar-se para descansar e, pessoalmente, começou a massagear as pernas do Senhor.**
