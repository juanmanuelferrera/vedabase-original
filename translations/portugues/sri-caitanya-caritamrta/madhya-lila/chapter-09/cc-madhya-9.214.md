### CC Madhya 9.214

> বিপ্র কহে, — তুমি সাক্ষাৎ শ্রীরঘুনন্দন ৷
> সন্ন্যাসীর বেষে মোরে দিলা দরশন ৷৷ ২১৪ ৷৷ ॥২১৪॥

vipra kahe,--tumi sākṣāt śrī-raghunandana
sannyāsīra veṣe more dilā daraśana

*vipra kahe*—o brāhmaṇa disse; *tumi*—Tu; *sākṣāt*—diretamente; *śrī-raghunandana*—o Senhor Śrī Rāmacandra; *sannyāsīra veṣe*—nas vestes de um mendicante; *more*—a mim; *dilā*—deste; *daraśana*—audiência.

**Depois de receber o manuscrito, o brāhmaṇa, muito satisfeito, disse: "Senhor, Tu és o próprio Senhor Rāmacandra e vieste nas vestes de um sannyāsī para me conceder audiência.**
