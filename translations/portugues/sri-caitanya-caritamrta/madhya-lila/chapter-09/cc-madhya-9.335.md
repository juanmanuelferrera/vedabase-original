### CC Madhya 9.335

> তবে মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে আসিতে আজ্ঞা দিয়া ৷\
> নীলাচলে চলিলা প্রভু আনন্দিত হঞা ৷৷ ৩৩৫ ৷৷ ॥৩৩৫॥

tabe mahāprabhu tāṅre āsite ājñā diyā
nīlācale calilā prabhu ānandita hañā

*tabe*—então; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *tāṅre*—a ele; *āsite*—para ir; *ājñā diyā*—dando uma ordem; *nīlācale*—para Jagannātha Purī; *calilā*—partiu; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ānandita hañā*—com grande prazer.

**Dando ordens a Rāmānanda Rāya para que fosse a Nīlācala, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu partiu para Jagannātha Purī com grande prazer.**
