### CC Madhya 9.167

> পরমানন্দপুরী তাহাঁ রহে চতুর্মাস ৷\
> শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু গেলা পুরী-গোসাঞির পাশ ৷৷ ১৬৭ ৷৷ ॥১৬৭॥

paramānanda-purī tāhāṅ rahe catur-māsa
śuni' mahāprabhu gelā purī-gosāñira pāśa

*paramānanda-purī*—Paramānanda Purī; *tāhāṅ*—ali; *rahe*—permanecia; *catur-māsa*—quatro meses; *śuni'*—ouvindo; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *gelā*—foi; *purī*—Paramānanda Purī; *gosāñira*—o mestre espiritual; *pāśa*—perto.

**Paramānanda Purī estava permanecendo na colina Ṛṣabha durante os quatro meses da estação das chuvas e, quando Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ouviu isto, foi imediatamente vê-lo.**
