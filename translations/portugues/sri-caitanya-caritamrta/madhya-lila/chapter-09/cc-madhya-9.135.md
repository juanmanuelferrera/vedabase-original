### CC Madhya 9.135

> গোপজাতি কৃষ্ণ, গোপী — প্রেয়সী তাঁহার ৷\
> দেবী বা অন্য স্ত্রী কৃষ্ণ না করে অঙ্গীকার ৷৷ ১৩৫ ৷৷ ॥১৩৫॥

gopa-jāti kṛṣṇa, gopī--preyasī tāṅhāra
devī vā anya strī kṛṣṇa nā kare aṅgīkāra

*gopa-jāti*—pertencente à comunidade de pastores de vacas; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *gopī*—as donzelas de Vrajabhūmi, as gopīs; *preyasī*—as mais queridas; *tāṅhāra*—Suas; *devī*—as esposas dos semideuses; *vā*—ou; *anya*—outras; *strī*—mulheres; *kṛṣṇa*—o Senhor Kṛṣṇa; *nā*—não; *kare*—faz; *aṅgīkāra*—aceitação.

**"O Senhor Kṛṣṇa pertence à comunidade de pastores de vacas, e as gopīs são as mais queridas amantes de Kṛṣṇa. Embora as esposas dos habitantes dos planetas celestiais sejam as mais opulentas dentro do mundo material, nem elas nem quaisquer outras mulheres do universo material podem obter a associação de Kṛṣṇa.**
