### CC Madhya 9.306

> কৃষ্ণকর্ণামৃত শুনি’ প্রভুর আনন্দ হৈল ৷\
> আগ্রহ করিয়া পুঁথি লেখাঞা লৈল ৷৷ ৩০৬ ৷৷ ॥৩০৬॥

kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta śuni' prabhura ānanda haila
āgraha kariyā puṅthi lekhāñā laila

*kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta śuni'*—após ouvir o Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta; *prabhura*—do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ānanda haila*—houve grande felicidade; *āgraha kariyā*—com grande ânsia; *puṅthi*—o livro; *lekhāñā*—mandando copiar; *laila*—levou.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu ficou muito satisfeito de ouvir o livro Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta e, com grande ânsia, mandou copiá-lo e o levou consigo.**
