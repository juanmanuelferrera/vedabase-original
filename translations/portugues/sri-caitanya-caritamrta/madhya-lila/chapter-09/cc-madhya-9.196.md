### CC Madhya 9.196

> প্রভুর বচনে বিপ্রের হইল বিশ্বাস ৷
> ভোজন করিল, হৈল জীবনের আশ ৷৷ ১৯৬ ৷৷ ॥১৯৬॥

prabhura vacane viprera ha-ila viśvāsa
bhojana karila, haila jīvanera āśa

*prabhura vacane*—nas palavras do Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *viprera*—do brāhmaṇa; *ha-ila*—houve; *viśvāsa*—fé; *bhojana karila*—ele almoçou; *haila*—houve; *jīvanera*—de viver; *āśa*—esperança.

**Embora o brāhmaṇa estivesse jejuando, ele teve fé nas palavras de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu e aceitou alimento. Dessa maneira, sua vida foi salva.**
