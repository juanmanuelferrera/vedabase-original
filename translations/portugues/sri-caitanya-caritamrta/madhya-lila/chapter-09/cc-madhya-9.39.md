### CC Madhya 9.39

> তাহাঁ হৈতে চলি’ আগে গেলা এক গ্রামে ৷
> ব্রাহ্মণ-সমাজ তাহাঁ, করিল বিশ্রামে ৷৷ ৩৯ ৷৷ ॥৩৯॥

tāhāṅ haite cali' āge gelā eka grāme
brāhmaṇa-samāja tāhāṅ, karila viśrāme

*tāhāṅ haite*—de lá; *cali'*—partindo; *āge*—adiante; *gelā*—foi; *eka*—uma; *grāme*—a uma aldeia; *brāhmaṇa-samāja*—assembleia de brāhmaṇas; *tāhāṅ*—ali; *karila viśrāme*—Ele descansou.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu deixou então Vṛddhakāśī e prosseguiu adiante. Em uma vila, Ele viu que a maioria dos residentes era de brāhmaṇas, e ali descansou.**
