### CC Madhya 7.85

> মধ্যাহ্ন করিয়া আইলা দেবতা-মন্দিরে ৷\
> নিজগণ প্ৰবেশি’ কপাট দিল বহির্দ্বারে ৷৷ ৮৫ ৷৷ ॥৮৫॥

madhyāhna kariyā āilā devatā-mandire

nija-gaṇa praveśi' kapāṭa dila bahir-dvāre

*madhyāhna kariyā*—realizando o banho etc.; *āilā*—voltou; *devatā-mandire*—ao templo do Senhor; *nija-gaṇa praveśi'*—permitindo a entrada de Seus próprios homens; *kapāṭa dila*—fechou; *bahir-dvāre*—a porta externa.

**Após terminarem Seus banhos, voltaram ao meio-dia para o templo. Admitindo Seus próprios homens, Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu fechou a porta externa.**
