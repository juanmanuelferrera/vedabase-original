### CC Madhya 7.77

> প্রেমাবেশে নৃত্যগীত কৈল কতক্ষণ ৷
> দেখিতে আইলা তাহাঁ বৈসে যত জন ৷৷ ৭৭ ৷৷ ॥৭৭॥

premāveśe nṛtya-gīta kaila kata-kṣaṇa

dekhite āilā tāhāṅ vaise yata jana

*prema-āveśe*—no grande êxtase do amor a Deus; *nṛtya-gīta*—dançando e cantando; *kaila*—realizou; *kata-kṣaṇa*—por algum tempo; *dekhite*—para ver; *āilā*—vieram; *tāhāṅ*—ali; *vaise*—que vivem; *yata jana*—todos os homens.

**Em grande êxtase, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu dançou e cantou por algum tempo. De fato, todos os vizinhos vieram vê-lo.**
