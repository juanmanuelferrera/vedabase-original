### CC Madhya 7.86

> তবে গোপীনাথ দুইপ্রভুরে ভিক্ষা করাইল ৷\
> প্রভুর শেষ প্রসাদান্ন সবে বাঁটি’ খাইল ৷৷ ৮৬ ৷৷ ॥৮৬॥

tabe gopīnātha dui-prabhure bhikṣā karāila

prabhura śeṣa prasādānna sabe bāṅṭi' khāila

*tabe*—em seguida; *gopīnātha*—Gopīnātha Ācārya; *dui-prabhure*—aos dois Senhores, Caitanya Mahāprabhu e Nityānanda Prabhu; *bhikṣā karāila*—deu prasāda para comer; *prabhura*—do Senhor; *śeṣa*—os restos; *prasāda-anna*—alimento; *sabe*—todos eles; *bāṅṭi'*—partilhando; *khāila*—comeram.

**Gopīnātha Ācārya trouxe então *prasādam* para os dois Senhores comerem, e depois que comeram, os remanescentes da comida foram distribuídos a todos os devotos.**
