### CC Madhya 7.106

> যেই গ্রামে রহি’ ভিক্ষা করেন যাঁর ঘরে ৷
> সেই গ্রামের যত লোক আইসে দেখিবারে ৷৷ ১০৬ ৷৷ ॥১০৬॥

yei grāme rahi' bhikṣā karena yāṅra ghare

sei grāmera yata loka āise dekhibāre

*yei grāme*—em qualquer aldeia; *rahi'*—permanecendo; *bhikṣā*—esmolas; *karena*—aceita; *yāṅra*—de quem; *ghare*—em casa; *sei*—aquela; *grāmera*—da aldeia; *yata loka*—todas as pessoas; *āise*—vinham; *dekhibāre*—para ver.

**Em qualquer aldeia em que Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu permanecesse para aceitar esmolas, muitas pessoas vinham vê-Lo.**
