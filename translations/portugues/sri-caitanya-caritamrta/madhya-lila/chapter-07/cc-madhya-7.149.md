### CC Madhya 7.149

> এতেক কহিয়া প্রভু কৈল অন্তর্ধানে ৷\
> দুই বিপ্র গলাগলি কান্দে প্রভুর গুণে ৷৷ ১৪৯ ৷৷ ॥১৪৯॥

eteka kahiyā prabhu kaila antardhāne

dui vipra galāgali kānde prabhura guṇe

*eteka*—isto tudo; *kahiyā*—dizendo; *prabhu*—o Senhor Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *kaila*—realizou; *antardhāne*—desaparecimento; *dui vipra*—os dois brāhmaṇas, Kūrma e Vāsudeva; *galāgali*—abraçando um ao outro; *kānde*—choram; *prabhura guṇe*—devido à misericórdia de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Após instruir dessa forma o *brāhmaṇa* Vāsudeva, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu desapareceu daquele lugar. Então os dois *brāhmaṇas*, Kūrma e Vāsudeva, abraçaram-se mutuamente e começaram a chorar, lembrando-se das qualidades transcendentais de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
