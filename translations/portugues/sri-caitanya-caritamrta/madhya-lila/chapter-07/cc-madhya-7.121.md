### CC Madhya 7.121

> ‘কূর্ম’-নামে সেই গ্রামে বৈদিক ব্রাহ্মণ ৷
> বহু শ্রদ্ধা-ভক্ত্যে কৈল প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণ ৷৷ ১২১ ৷৷ ॥১২১॥

'kūrma'-nāme sei grāme vaidika brāhmaṇa
bahu śraddhā-bhaktye kaila prabhura nimantraṇa

*kūrma-nāme*—de nome Kūrma; *sei*—aquela; *grāme*—na aldeia; *vaidika brāhmaṇa*—um brāhmaṇa védico; *bahu*—muita; *śraddhā-bhaktye*—com fé e devoção; *kaila*—fez; *prabhura*—de Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nimantraṇa*—o convite.

**Numa aldeia havia um brāhmaṇa védico chamado Kūrma. Ele convidou o Senhor Caitanya Mahāprabhu a Seu lar com grande respeito e devoção.**
