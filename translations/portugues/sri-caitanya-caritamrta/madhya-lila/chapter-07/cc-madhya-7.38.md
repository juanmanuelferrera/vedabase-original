### CC Madhya 7.38

> প্রেমাবেশে পথে তুমি হবে অচেতন ৷\
> এ-সব সামগ্রী তোমার কে করে রক্ষণ ৷৷ ৩৮ ৷৷ ॥৩৮॥

premāveśe pathe tumi habe acetana

e-saba sāmagrī tomāra ke kare rakṣaṇa

*prema-āveśe*—em amor extático a Deus; *pathe*—pelo caminho; *tumi*—Tu; *habe*—ficarás; *acetana*—inconsciente; *e-saba*—tudo isso; *sāmagrī*—parafernália; *tomāra*—Tua; *ke*—quem; *kare*—faz; *rakṣaṇa*—proteção.

**"Quando, pelo caminho, caíres inconsciente em amor extático a Deus, quem protegerá Teus pertences — o pote de água, as vestes e o restante?"**
