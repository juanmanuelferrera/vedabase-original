### SB 3.10.25

> कङ्कगृध्रबकश्येन भासभल्लूकबर्हिणः ।\
> हंससारसचक्राह्व काकोलूकादयः खगाः ॥२५॥

*kaṅka*-gṛdhra-baka-śyena-

*bhāsa*-bhallūka-barhiṇaḥ

*haṁsa*-sārasa-cakrāhva-

kākolūkādayaḥ khagāḥ

*kaṅka*—a garça; *gṛdhra*—o abutre; *baka*—o guindaste; *śyena*—o falcão; *bhāsa*—o bhāsa; *bhallūka*—o bhallūka; *barhiṇaḥ*—o pavão; *haṁsa*—o cisne; *sārasa*—o sārasa; *cakrāhva*—o cakravāka; *kāka*—o corvo; *ulūka*—a coruja; *ādayaḥ*—e outros; *khagāḥ*—as aves.

**A garça, o abutre, o guindaste, o falcão, o bhāsa, o bhallūka, o pavão, o cisne, o sārasa, o cakravāka, o corvo, a coruja e outros são as aves.**
