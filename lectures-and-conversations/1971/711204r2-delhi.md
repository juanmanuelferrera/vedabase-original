# Conversation

*Type: Conversation Dated: December 4th 1971 Location: Delhi Audio file: 711204R2-DELHI.mp3*

**Śyāmasundara: **…the last called, Acyutānanda. Bhavānanda and Girirāja were out making Life Members.

**Prabhupāda: **Hmm.

**Śyāmasundara: **The story goes that the beach *paṇḍāl* is out of the question now; they can't do it. So there's one good plot of land called the Congress Grounds in the center, just like Connaught Circle. But it's owned by the Congress Party. And they won't know 'til Saturday if they can have it for sure. So meanwhile, they can't advertise or do anything or raise funds even, until they know for sure if they can get the grounds.

**Prabhupāda: **Oh, the land is not yet secure?

**Śyāmasundara: **Not secure until Saturday.

**Prabhupāda: **That is also doubtful?

**Śyāmasundara: **That is shaky, but... I know Acyutānanda; it's not the same as talking with Bhavānanda or Girirāja. Sometimes Acyutānanda is negative about things, when there may be other things we don't know. So I've asked him to call..., to have Bhavānanda call… [break]

**Prabhupāda: **…otherwise they shall not like us.

**Śyāmasundara: **Yes. Bhavānanda will give us the real picture, because he knows.

**Prabhupāda: **Hmm?

**Śyāmasundara: **I'm sure Bhavānanda and Girirāja are doing all the work on the thing. They can give us a clearer picture. [break] [begins reading a letter that Prabhupāda wrote] It says, "My dear Jayapatāka, Please accept my blessings. I am sending Bihārīlal Prabhu to Calcutta for the purpose of collecting four first-class *khols*, ten *karatālas* and a few thousand magazines for taking with him for our Madras program.

We distributed twelve thousand magazines here in Delhi, so I think twelve thousand magazines should be sent to Madras. For payment for the other things, Tamāla Kṛṣṇa will pay. You may please assist our Madras party by arranging these items for Bihārīlal to bring.

"I am also sending Maṇibhaṇḍa Prabhu to Calcutta to relieve you of export business. I think now we may only export drums, *karatālas*, *japa-mālas* and *ārātrika* articles. You may now devote your full time to preaching work and making members. Karāndhara has sent me one telegram inquiring about *Kṛṣṇa* book, whether you have sent the shipping documents to him.

"You will be pleased to know that we are trying here in Delhi for permits for a large India party to be in Mayapur for Lord Caitanya's Appearance Day. I think it may not be necessary to have a second *paṇḍāl* program in Calcutta in January. What do you think? I hope this meets you in good health." [break] …so it is not a very good position.

**Prabhupāda: **Whether this, he will be able to bring.

**Śyāmasundara: **Bihārīlal? Well with this letter, Tamāla…

**Prabhupāda: **No, then. It's going to take time?

**Śyāmasundara: **Oh, yes. Bihārīlal can arrange these items, because he worked with Jayapatāka on that… [break] [indistinct]

**Indian** d**evotee: **No.

But this internal strife… Somebody said that he does not [indistinct], that I don't like to handle the complaints. Then things become very difficult. For this purpose… [break]
