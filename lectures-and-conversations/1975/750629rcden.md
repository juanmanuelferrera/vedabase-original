# Conversation in Car after Morning Walk

*Type: Conversation Dated: June 29th 1975 Location: Denver Audio file: 750629RC-DENVER-Mis.mp3*

(in car)

**Prabhupāda:** . . . and busy foolish is the last class man. At the present moment they are "busy foolish."

**Tamal Kṛṣṇa:** That's worse than lazy foolish.

**Prabhupāda:** Huh?

**Tamal Kṛṣṇa:** They're worse than lazy foolish.

**Prabhupāda:** Yes. Lazy foolish is foolish but he's lazy, he'll not do so harm. But the busy foolish he'll simply create harm. At the present the population is busy foolish. So we are creating lazy intelligent. An intelligent man must be lazy, otherwise how he can act intelligently, sober. "All right, let me consider." You cannot hope that the intelligent man will give his decision easily. 

**Tamal Kṛṣṇa:** He can be called lazy, but that's not *tama-guṇa*.

**Prabhupāda:** That is sobriety. Modern tendency is to create "busy foolish." The communists are busy foolish.
