# Room Conversation

*Type: Conversation Dated: January 16th 1977 Location: Calcutta Audio file: 770116R3.CAL.mp3*

Prabhupāda: We are discussing some subject matter from *Reader's Digest* about the next two hundred years, how things will happen. The scientist proposing that they'll control the laws of nature, and they will produce food from garbage---so many fantastic ideas. But our suggestion, according to Caitanya Mahāprabhu,

> harer nāma harer nāma harer nāma iva kevalam
> kalau nāsty eva nāsty eva nāsty eva gatir anyathā

Cc. Ādi 17.21
