# Room Conversation

*Type: Conversation Dated: December 14th 1970 Location: Indore Audio file: 701214R2-INDORE.mp3*

**Prabhupāda: **[to Indian man] Where is your son? [Hindi conversation with man] This is material conception. [Hindi conversation]

**Indian man:** [Hindi] [to Yamuna] You understand it?

**Yamuna:** You're talking about the books? They translate them in English?

**Indian man:** Yes. They have got them all in Hindi. So they can read them. Why bother to read the translation?

**Prabhupāda: **It is not bothering for reading. It is helping the institution. We have not come to bother you with the books. But we have come to your door for begging something to support this institution.

**Indian man:** [Hindi]

**Prabhupāda: **Working that way, we require money. So [Hindi] How you can expect? [Hindi] I would have liked that you would have introduced another four members. Because you are influential. So anyway, I..., at least one member, as you have selected. So you can become. [Hindi conversation with Indians] [break] [end]
