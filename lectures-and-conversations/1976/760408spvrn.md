# Lecture, partially recorded (Hindi)

*Type: Lecture Dated: April 8th 1976 Location: Vrndavana Audio file: 760408SP-VRNDAVAN-Hindi-mono.mp3*

(Translated from Hindi into English)

**Prabhupāda:** What? Just now said that by seeing whom one remembers the name of Kṛṣṇa, he is an exalted Vaiṣṇava. At one time I was going from London to Africa and at twelve midnight that plane landed in Athens. Since we had to wait there for 45 minutes so we all got off and went to the resting area. We were three or four of us, and the moment they saw us the many youth waiting there said, "Hare Kṛṣṇa" at twelve midnight and at Athens airport. Just like you said, we are known as "Hare Kṛṣṇa people" all over the world. Just on seeing us everyone says "Hare Kṛṣṇa". This is not we . . . (indistinct)

And sometimes there is cartoon in the newspaper. One cartoon came out, one old lady is telling her husband forcefully, Chant Chant Chant! And the old man says (aside: the English comes out and laughs) Can't Can't Can't! (laughter) The old lady says Chant Chant Chant! And he says Can't Can't Can't! (laughter) So this is our position.

So I thank you all very much. All these westerners have come here spending a lot of money, each ticket costs at least 10000 rupees. They are around 400 - 500 of them and the government earns in thousands and crores of rupees. There is no loss for the government from their coming here but still it is unfortunate that when their visa expires they are repeatedly asked to get out. 

There are a lot of obstacles, although devotional service is *apratihatā*, they have to go back and again come back and again spend 10000 rupees. So kindly all of you Vṛndāvana residents it behooves you to provide all kinds of facilities to these people who come from outside India and more will come. *Jaya jaya vrindavana vasi jata jana.* 

So this is Indian philosophy, *gṛham śatrum api prāptaṁ viśvastam akuto 'bhayam*, even if an enemy comes to our home he should be treated with all respect, so much so that he forgets that he is an enemy. This is our philosophy. And if one comes to Vṛndāvana and you all hate them that they are *yavana, mleccha*, this or that, we will not eat food touched by them, then how will things progress.

So this aspect has to be, it is my request to all of you to understand this position, *vaiṣṇave jāti-buddhiḥ nārakī buddhiḥ saḥ*, give up this nasty attitude. That is my request, nothing else. *arcye viṣṇau śilā-dhīr guruṣu nara-matir vaiṣṇave jāti-buddhiḥ* (Padma Purāṇa). So these people who come from far to take *darśana* of the Lord, everyone know it is made of stone. So do they come to see the stone? Spending so much money if someone asks what stone is Kṛṣṇa made of? O foolish person, have they come to see the stone after spending so much money? So such foolishness should not happen, *vaiṣṇave jāti-buddhiḥ*. Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu Himself says: 

> *yei bhaje sei baḍa, abhakta — hīna,*
> *chāra kṛṣṇa-bhajane nāhi jāti-kulādi-vicāra *
> (CC Antya 4.67)

There is no question of considering a Vaiṣṇava in terms of birth. And the Lord Himself says: 

> *māṁ hi pārtha vyapāśritya*
> *ye 'pi syuḥ pāpa-yonayaḥ *
> (BG 9.32)

*pāpa-yoni, te 'pi yānti parāṁ gatim* (BG 9.32), so on who is going to reach the supreme destination is he a *mlechha* or *yavana*? 

So my only request is to cleanse this environment of having a *nārakī buddhiḥ*, give up this attitude and they have all come to learn from you, teach them, enlighten them. This is the only request. There is no more time now, *ārati* is going to be done. Thank you all very much. 

Hare Kṛṣṇa.

**Devotees:** *Jaya* Śrīla Prabhupāda. (end)

तो आप लोगों को धन्यवाद। ये परदेशी भक्त लोग इधर बहुत खर्च करके आए हैं कि एक एक टिकट लगता है कम से कम 10,000 रूपया। ये चार सौ-पांच सौ हैं, और हजारों, करोड़ों, गवर्नमेंट का आमदनी होती है, और इसमें गवर्नमेंट का कोई नुकसान नहीं है इनके आने से, लेकिन फिर भी बहुत अन्याय है कि जब इनका वीसा ख़तम हो जाता है तो बार-बार उनको लौट जाने के लिए कहते हैं।  फिर आना पड़ता है। 
हलाकि भक्ति अहैतुकी अप्रतिहता है, बहुत सी बाधाएं हैं। उनको जाना पड़ता है, फिर आना पड़ता है दस हज़ार रूपया खर्च करके। तो आप लोग सब वृन्दावन वासी कृपा किजिए जो परदेश से वृन्दावन में आना शुरू किया है, और भी आएंगे, उनको सब सुविधा दीजिये। जय जय वृन्दावन वासी जत जन। 
तो ये भारतीय दर्शनशास्र है। "गृहं शत्रुं अपि प्राप्तं विश्वस्तं अकुटो 'भयम्"। हमारा घर में अगर कोई दुश्मन भी आये उसको भी खातिर करना चाहिए इतना ज़्यादा ताकि वो भूल जाये की वो दुश्मन है। ये हमारा दर्शनशास्र है। और वृंदावन में आए और आपने यदि गिला की ये जर्मन है, म्लेच्छा है, यवन है, ये या वो, इसका छूने से हमलोग खायेगा नहीं, तो कैसे काम बनेगा।  

इसलिए इस पहलू पर ध्यान देना होगा, मेरा आप सभी से अनुरोध है कि इस स्थिति को समझें। "वैष्णवे जाति-बुद्धिः नारकी बुद्धिः सः" इस घिनौने रवैये को त्याग दो। यही मेरा अनुरोध है, इसके अलावा कुछ नहीं। "अर्चये विष्णौ शिला-धीर गुरुषु नर-मतिर वैष्णवे जाति-बुद्धिर" (पद्मा पुराण)। तो ये लोग जो भगवान के दर्शन के लिए दूर-दूर से आते हैं, वे सभी जानते हैं कि यह पत्थर से बना है। तो क्या वे पत्थर देखने आते हैं? इतना पैसा खर्च करके कोई पूछे कि कृष्ण किस पत्थर से बने हैं? अरे मूर्ख व्यक्ति, क्या वे इतना पैसा खर्च करके पत्थर देखने आए हैं? ये ऐसी बेवकूफी नहीं होनी चाहिए की वैष्णवे जाति-बुद्धिर। चैतन्य महाप्रभु खुद कहते हैं। 

 
"येई भजे सेई बड़ा, अभक्त-हिना,
