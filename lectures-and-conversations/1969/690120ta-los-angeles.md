*Type: Conversation Dated: January 20th 1969 Location: Los Angeles Audio file: 690120TA-LOS-ANGELES.mp3*

**Prabhupāda:** ...ornament Kṛṣṇa, the more you become rich. The reflection, Kṛṣṇa being richly dressed, richly fed, it will be reflected on you. Kṛṣṇa is not in necessity, but we should dress Kṛṣṇa with the first-quality ornaments. In India the Deities, They are given very, very valuable jewelry. The Muhammadans were attracted for these jewelries, [laughter] and they came to India to plunder the temples to get the jewelries.

Still, temples there are millions of dollars of jewelries. Any such temple. In Jagannātha temple there is a valuable jewel. Just here, it is kept, here, in a pocket. So Deity should be very nicely dressed. That is the temple worship. At the same time should be careful also. Chant. [end]
