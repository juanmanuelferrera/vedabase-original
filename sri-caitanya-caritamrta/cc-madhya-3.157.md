### CC Madhya 3.157

> yata loka āila mahāprabhuke dekhite
> nānā-grāma haite, āra navadvīpa haite

*yataloka*—all the people; *āila*—came; *mahāprabhuke*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhite*—to see; *nānā-grāma haite*—from various villages; *āra*—and; *navadvīpa haite*—from Navadvīpa.

**People came to see Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu from various other villages nearby, as well as Navadvīpa.**
