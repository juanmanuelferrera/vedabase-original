### CC Adi 17.196

> tabe sei yavanere āmi ta' puchila
> hindu 'hari' bale, tāra svabhāva jānila

*tabe*—then; *sei*—that; *yavanere*—from the meat-eaters; *āmi*—I; *ta'*—certainly; *puchila*—inquired; *hindu*—the Hindu; *haribale*—says Hari; *tara*—his; *svabhāva*—nature; *jānila*—I know.

**"I then inquired from these yavanas, 'I know that these Hindus by nature chant "Hari, Hari."**
