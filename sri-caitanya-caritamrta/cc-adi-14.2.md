### CC Adi 14.2

> jaya jaya śrī-caitanya, jaya nityānanda
> jayādvaitacandra, jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

*jayajaya*—all glories; *śrī-caitanya*—to Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jaya*—all glories; *nityānanda*—to Nityānanda Prabhu; *jayaadvaita-candra*—all glories to Advaita Ācārya; *jaya*—all glories; *gaura-bhakta-vṛnda*—to all the devotees of the Lord.

**All glories to Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Nityānanda Prabhu, Advaita Prabhu and all the devotees of Lord Caitanya!**
