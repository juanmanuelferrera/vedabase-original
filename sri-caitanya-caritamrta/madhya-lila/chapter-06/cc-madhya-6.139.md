### CC Madhya 6.139

> বেদ-পুরাণে কহে ব্রহ্ম-নিরূপণ ।\
> সেই ব্রহ্ম — বৃহদ্বস্তু, ঈশ্বর-লক্ষণ ॥১৩৯॥

> veda-purāṇe kahe brahma-nirūpaṇa\
> sei brahma—bṛhad-vastu, īśvara-lakṣaṇa

*veda-purāṇe*—in the *Vedas* and the *Purāṇas*; *kahe*—it is stated; *brahma-nirūpaṇa*—explaining the Supreme; *sei brahma*—that Supreme; *bṛhat-vastu*—the greatest; *īśvara-lakṣaṇa*—feature of the Supreme Personality.

**"All the Vedas and literature that strictly follows the Vedic principles explain that the Supreme Brahman is the Absolute Truth, the greatest of all, and a feature of the Supreme Lord.**

The greatest of everything is Śrī Kṛṣṇa. Lord Kṛṣṇa states in the *Bhagavad-gītā* [[bg/15/15|(Bg. 15.15)]], *vedaiś ca sarvair aham eva vedyaḥ:* "By all the *Vedas,* I am to be known.") In *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* it is said that the Absolute Truth is understood in three phases-namely, Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān, the Supreme Personality of Godhead (*brahmeti paramātmeti bhagavān iti śabdyate*). Thus the Supreme Personality of Godhead is the last word in understanding the Absolute Truth, Brahman.
