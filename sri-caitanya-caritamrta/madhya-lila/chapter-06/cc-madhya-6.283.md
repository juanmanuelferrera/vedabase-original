### CC Madhya 6.283

> যৈছে পরিপাটী করে ভিক্ষা-নিৰ্বাহন ।\
> বিস্তারিয়া আগে তাহা করিব বর্ণন ॥২৮৩॥

> yaiche paripāṭī kare bhikṣā-nirvāhana\
> vistāriyā āge tāhā kariba varṇana

*yaiche*—how; *paripāṭī*—perfectly; *kare*—does; *bhikṣā*—of offering alms; *nirvāhana*—the execution; *vistāriyā*—in full detail; *āge*—later; *tāhā*—that; *karibavarṇana*—I shall describe.

**I shall also describe in full detail how Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya perfectly rendered service to Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu by offering Him alms.**
