### CC Madhya 6.21

> mukunda kahe,—prabhura ihāṅ haila āgamane
> āmi-saba āsiyāchi mahāprabhura sane

*mukundakahe*—Mukunda replies; *prabhura*—of Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *ihāṅ*—here; *haila*—there was; *āgamane*—coming; *āmi-saba*—all of us; *āsiyāchi*—have come; *mahāprabhura*—Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sane*—with.

**Mukunda Datta replied, "The Lord has already arrived here. We have come with Him."**
