### CC Madhya 6.15

> তাঁহা শুনে লোকে কহে অন্যোন্যে বাত়্  ।\
> এক সন্ন্যাসী আসি’ দেখি’ জগন্নাথ ॥১৫॥

> tāṅhā śune loke kahe anyonye bāt\
> eka sannyāsī āsi' dekhi' jagannātha

*tāṅhā*—at that place; *śune*—they hear; *loke*—the people in general; *kahe*—talk; *anyonye*—among themselves; *vāt*—topics; *eka*—one; *sannyāsī*—mendicant; *āsi'*—coming there; *dekhi'*—seeing; *jagannātha*—the Deity of Lord Jagannātha.

**There the devotees heard the people talking about a mendicant who had come to Jagannātha Purī and seen the Deity of Jagannātha.**
