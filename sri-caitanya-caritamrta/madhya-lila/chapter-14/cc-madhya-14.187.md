### CC Madhya 14.187

> গতিস্থানাসনাদীনাং মুখনেত্রাদিকর্মণাম্ ।\
> তাৎকালিকন্তু বৈশিষ্ট্যং বিলাসঃ প্রিয়সঙ্গজম্ ॥১৮৭॥

> gati-sthānāsanādīnāṁ\
> mukha-netrādi-karmaṇām\
> tātkālikaṁ tu vaiśiṣṭyaṁ\
> vilāsaḥ priya-saṅgajam

*gati*—moving; *sthāna*—standing; *āsana-ādīnām*—and of sitting and so on; *mukha*—of the face; *netra*—of the eyes; *ādi*—and so on; *karmaṇām*—of the activities; *tāt-kālikam*—relating to that time; *tu*—then; *vaiśiṣṭyam*—various symptoms; *vilāsaḥ*—of the name *vilāsa*; *priya-saṅga-jam*—produced from meeting her beloved.

**“ 'The various symptoms manifested in a woman's face, eyes, and the other parts of her body and the way she moves, stands or sits when she meets her beloved are called vilāsa.' "**
