### CC Madhya 11.130

> একে একে সর্বভক্তে কৈল সম্ভাষণ ।
> সবা লঞা অভ্যন্তরে করিলা গমন ॥১৩০॥

> eke eke sarva-bhakte kaila sambhāṣaṇa
> sabā lañā abhyantare karilā gamana

*ekeeke*—one after another; *sarva-bhakte*—to every devotee; *kaila*—did; *sambhāṣaṇa*—address; *sabā lañā*—taking all of them; *abhyantare*—inside; *karilā gamana*—entered.

**The Lord addressed all the devotees one after another and took all of them with Him into the house.**
