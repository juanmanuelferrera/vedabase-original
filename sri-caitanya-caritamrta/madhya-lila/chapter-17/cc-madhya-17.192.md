### CC Madhya 17.192

> 'vana' dekhibāre yadi prabhura mana haila
> sei ta brāhmaṇe prabhu saṅgete la-ila

*vana*—the forests; *dekhibāre*—to see; *yadi*—when; *prabhura*—of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *mana*—mind; *haila*—was; *sei ta*—indeed that; *brāhmaṇe*—*brāhmaṇa*; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *saṅgete la-ila*—took along.

**When Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu wanted to see the various forests of Vṛndāvana, He took the brāhmaṇa with Him.**
