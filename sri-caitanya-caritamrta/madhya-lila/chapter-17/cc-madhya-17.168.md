### CC Madhya 17.168

> gopāla prakaṭa kari' sevā kaila 'mahāśaya'
> adyāpiha tāṅhāra sevā 'govardhane' haya

*gopāla*—the Deity Gopāla; *prakaṭa kari'*—installing; *sevā*—service; *kaila*—did; *mahāśaya*—that great personality; *adyāpiha*—still now; *tāṅhāra*—of that Deity Gopāla; *sevā*—the service; *govardhane*—on the Govardhana Hill; *haya*—is conducted.

**"After installing the Deity Gopāla, Śrīla Mādhavendra Purī rendered Him service. That very Deity is still being worshiped at Govardhana Hill."**
