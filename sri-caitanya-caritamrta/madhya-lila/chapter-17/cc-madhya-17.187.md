### CC Madhya 17.187

> তবে সেই বিপ্র প্রভুকে ভিক্ষা করাইল ।\
> মধুপুরীর লোক সব প্রভুকে দেখিতে আইল ॥১৮৭॥

> tabe sei vipra prabhuke bhikṣā karāila\
> madhu-purīra loka saba prabhuke dekhite āila

*tabe*—after that; *sei vipra*—that *brāhmaṇa*; *prabhuke*—unto Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *bhikṣā karāila*—gave lunch; *madhu-purīra*—of Mathurā; *loka*—people in general; *saba*—all; *prabhuke*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dekhite āila*—came to see.

**After this discussion, the brāhmaṇa gave lunch to Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Then all the people residing in Mathurā came to see the Lord.**
