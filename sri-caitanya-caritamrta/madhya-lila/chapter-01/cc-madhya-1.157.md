### CC Madhya 1.157

> পথে দুই দিকে পুষ্পবকুলের শ্রেণী ।\
> মধ্যে মধ্যে দুইপাশে দিব্য পুষ্করিণী ॥১৫৭॥

> pathe dui dike puṣpa-bakulera śreṇī\
> madhye madhye dui-pāśe divya puṣkariṇī

*pathe*—on the road; *duidike*—on both sides; *puṣpa-bakulera*—of *bakula* flower trees; *śreṇī*—rows; *madhyemadhye*—in the middle; *dui-pāśe*—on both sides; *divya*—transcendental; *puṣkariṇī*—lakes.

**He mentally decorated both sides of the road with bakula flower trees, and at intervals on both sides he placed lakes of a transcendental nature.**
