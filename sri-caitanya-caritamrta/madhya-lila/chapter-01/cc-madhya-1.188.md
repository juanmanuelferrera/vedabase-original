### CC Madhya 1.188

> jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya dayā-maya
> patita-pāvana jaya, jaya mahāśaya

*jayajaya*—all glories; *śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya*—unto Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *dayā-maya*—the most merciful; *patita-pāvana*—the savior of the fallen souls; *jaya*—glories; *jaya*—glories; *mahāśaya*—to the great personality.

**"All glories to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu, the most merciful savior of the fallen souls! All glories to the Supreme Personality!**
