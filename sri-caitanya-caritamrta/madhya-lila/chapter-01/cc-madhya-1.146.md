### CC Madhya 1.146

> কৃষ্ণজন্ম-যাত্রাতে প্রভু গোপবেশ হৈলা ।\
> দধিভার বহি’ তবে লগুড় ফিরাইলা ॥১৪৬॥

> kṛṣṇa-janma-yātrāte prabhu gopa-veśa hailā\
> dadhi-bhāra vahi' tabe laguḍa phirāilā

*kṛṣṇa-janma-yātrāte*—on the birthday ceremony of Lord Kṛṣṇa; *prabhu*—Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *gopa-veśa*—dressed like a cowherd boy; *hailā*—was; *dadhi-bhāra*—a balance for pots of yogurt; *vahi'*—carrying; *tabe*—at that time; *laguḍa*—a log; *phirāilā*—encircled.

**On Janmāṣṭamī, Lord Kṛṣṇa's birthday, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu dressed Himself as a cowherd boy and, carrying a balance with pots of yogurt, encircled a log.**
