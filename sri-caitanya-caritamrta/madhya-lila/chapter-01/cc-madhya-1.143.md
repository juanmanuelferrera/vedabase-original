### CC Madhya 1.143

> সবা লঞা কৈল গুণ্ডিচা-গৃহ-সংমার্জন ।\
> রথযাত্রা-দরশনে প্রভুর নর্তন ॥১৪৩॥

> sabā lañā kaila guṇḍicā-gṛha-sammārjana\
> ratha-yātrā-daraśane prabhura nartana

*sabālañā*—taking all of them; *kaila*—performed; *guṇḍicā-gṛha-sammārjana*—washing of the Guṇḍicā temple; *ratha-yātrā*—the car festival; *daraśane*—in seeing; *prabhura*—of the Lord; *nartana*—dancing.

**First the Lord washed the temple of Guṇḍicā very thoroughly. Then everyone saw the Ratha-yātrā festival and the Lord's dancing before the car.**
