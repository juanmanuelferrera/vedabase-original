### CC Madhya 1.73

> প্রভু কহে, — তারে আমি সন্তুষ্ট হঞা ।\
> আলিঙ্গন কৈলু সর্বশক্তি সঞ্চারিয়া ॥৭৩॥

> prabhu kahe,—tāre āmi santuṣṭa hañā\
> āliṅgana kailu sarva-śakti sañcāriyā

*prabhukahe*—the Lord said; *tāre*—him; *āmi*—I; *santuṣṭa hañā*—being very much satisfied; *āliṅgana kailu*—embraced; *sarva-śakti*—all potencies; *sañcāriyā*—bestowing.

**The Lord said, "I was so much pleased with Rūpa Gosvāmī that I embraced him and bestowed upon him all necessary potencies for preaching the bhakti cult.**
