### CC Madhya 4.42

> শৈল-উপরি হৈতে আমা কুঞ্জে লুকাঞা ।\
> ম্লেচ্ছ-ভয়ে সেবক মোর গেল পলাঞা ॥৪২॥

> śaila-upari haite āmā kuñje lukāñā\
> mleccha-bhaye sevaka mora gela palāñā

*śaila-upari*—the top of the hill; *haite*—from; *āmā*—Me; *kuñje*—in the bushes; *lukāñā*—concealing; *mleccha-bhaye*—from fear of the Mohammedans; *sevaka*—servant; *mora*—My; *gela*—went; *palāñā*—running away.

**"When the Mohammedans attacked, the priest who was serving Me hid Me in this bush in the jungle. Then he ran away out of fear of the attack.**
