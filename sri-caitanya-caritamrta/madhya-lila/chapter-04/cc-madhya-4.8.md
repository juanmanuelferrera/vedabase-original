### CC Madhya 4.8

> তাঁর সূত্রে আছে, তেঁহ না কৈল বর্ণন ।
> যথাকথঞ্চিত্ করি’ যে লীলা কথন ॥৮॥

> tāṅra sūtre āche, teṅha nā kaila varṇana
> yathā-kathañcit kari' se līlā kathana

*tāṅra*—his; *sūtre*—in the codes; *āche*—there are; *teṅha*—he; *nā kaila varṇana*—did not describe; *yathā-kathañcit*—something of them; *kari'*—doing; *se*—these; *līlā*—of pastimes; *kathana*—narration.

**Some of the incidents in his codes he did not describe elaborately, and so I shall try to describe them in this book.**
