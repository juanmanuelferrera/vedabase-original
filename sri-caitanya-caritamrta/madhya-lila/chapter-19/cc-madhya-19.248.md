### CC Madhya 19.248

> নিজ ঘরে লঞা প্রভুরে ভিক্ষা করাইল ।
> ভট্টাচার্যে চন্দ্রশেখর নিমন্ত্রণ কৈল ॥২৪৮॥

> nija ghare lañā prabhure bhikṣā karāila
> bhaṭṭācārye candraśekhara nimantraṇa kaila

*nija ghare*—to his own place; *lañā*—taking; *prabhure*—to the Lord; *bhiksā karāila*—offered lunch; *bhaṭṭācārye*—unto Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya; *candraśekhara*—Candraśekhara; *nimantraṇa*—invitation; *kaila*—made.

**Tapana Miśra took Caitanya Mahāprabhu to his own house and gave Him lunch. Candraśekhara invited Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya to take lunch at his home.**
