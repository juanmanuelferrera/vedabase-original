### CC Madhya 3.107

> তবে ত’ আচার্য সঙ্গে লঞা দুই জনে ।\
> করিল ইচ্ছায় ভোজন, যে আছিল মনে ॥১০৭॥

> tabe ta' ācārya saṅge lañā dui jane\
> karila icchāya bhojana, ye āchila mane

*tabeta'*—thereafter; *ācārya*—Advaita Ācārya; *saṅge*—with; *lañā*—taking; *dui jane*—the two persons, namely Mukunda and Haridāsa; *karila*—did; *icchāya*—according to desire; *bhojana*—eating; *ye āchila mane*—whatever there was in mind.

**Thereupon Advaita Ācārya took prasāda with Mukunda and Haridāsa, and they all wholeheartedly ate as much as they desired.**
