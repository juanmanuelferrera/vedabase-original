### CC Madhya 3.113

> নিত্যানন্দ গোসাঞি বুলে আচার্য ধরিঞা ।\
> হরিদাস পাছে নাচে হরষিত হঞা ॥১১৩॥

> nityānanda gosāñi bule ācārya dhariñā\
> haridāsa pāche nāce haraṣita hañā

*nityānandagosāñi*—Lord Nityānanda Prabhu; *bule*—began to move, dancing; *ācārya dhariñā*—following Advaita Ācārya; *haridāsa*—Haridāsa Ṭhākura; *pāche*—behind; *nāce*—dances; *haraṣita hañā*—being pleased.

**When Advaita Ācārya began to dance, Nityānanda Prabhu began dancing behind Him. Haridāsa Ṭhākura, being very pleased, also began dancing behind Him.**
