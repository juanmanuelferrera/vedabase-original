### CC Madhya 3.45

> সার্দ্রক, বাস্তুক-শাক বিবিধ প্রকার ।\
> পটোল, কুষ্মাণ্ড-বড়ি, মানকচু আর ॥৪৫॥

> sārdraka, vāstuka-śāka vividha prakāra\
> paṭola, kuṣmāṇḍa-baḍi, mānakacu āra

*sārdraka*—pots with ginger dishes; *vāstuka-śāka*—spinach; *vividha*—various; *prakāra*—kinds; *paṭola*—a kind of fruit; *kuṣmāṇḍa*—squash; *baḍi*—with split dahl; *mānakacu*—the root of a vegetable tree called *kacu*; *āra*—and.

**Among the cooked vegetables were paṭolas, squash, mānakacu and a salad made with pieces of ginger and various types of spinach.**
