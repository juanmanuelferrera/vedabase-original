### CC Madhya 3.26

> এত বলি’ আনিল তাঁরে গঙ্গা-সন্নিধানে ।\
> আবেশে প্রভুর হৈল গঙ্গারে যমুনা-জ্ঞানে ॥২৬॥

> eta bali' ānila tāṅre gaṅgā-sannidhāne\
> āveśe prabhura haila gaṅgāre yamunā-jñāne

*etabali'*—saying this; *ānila*—He brought; *tāṅre*—Him; *gaṅgā-sannidhāne*—near the Ganges; *āveśe*—in ecstasy; *prabhura*—of the Lord; *haila*—there was; *gaṅgāre*—of the river Ganges; *yamunā-jñāne*—acceptance as the river Yamunā.

**Saying this, Nityānanda Prabhu took Caitanya Mahāprabhu near the Ganges, and the Lord, in His ecstasy, accepted the river Ganges as the river Yamunā.**
