### CC Madhya 3.47

> কোমল নিম্বপত্র সহ ভাজা বার্তাকী ।\
> পটোল-ফুলবড়ি-ভাজা, কুষ্মাণ্ড-মানচাকি ॥৪৭॥

> komala nimba-patra saha bhājā vārtākī\
> paṭola-phula-baḍi-bhājā, kuṣmāṇḍa-mānacāki

*komala*—newly grown; *nimba-patra*—*nimba* leaves; *saha*—with; *bhājā*—fried; *vārtākī*—eggplant; *paṭola*—with *paṭola* fruit; *phula-baḍi*—a preparation of dahl; *bhājā*—fried; *kuṣmāṇḍa*—squashes; *mānacāki*—the foodstuff called *mānacāki.*

**Amongst the various vegetables were newly grown leaves of nimba trees fried with eggplant. The fruit known as paṭola was fried with phulabaḍi, a kind of dahl preparation first mashed and then dried in the sun. There was also a preparation known as kuṣmāṇḍa-mānacāki.**

We request our editors of cook books to add all these nice preparations described by the experienced author Śrīla Kavirāja Gosvāmī.
