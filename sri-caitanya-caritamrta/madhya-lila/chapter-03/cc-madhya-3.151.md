### CC Madhya 3.151

> একে একে মিলিল প্রভু সব ভক্তগণ ।\
> সবার মুখ দেখি’ করে দৃঢ় আলিঙ্গন ॥১৫১॥

> eke eke milila prabhu saba bhakta-gaṇa\
> sabāra mukha dekhi' kare dṛḍha āliṅgana

*ekeeke*—one after another; *milila*—met; *prabhu*—the Lord; *saba*—all; *bhakta-gaṇa*—the devotees; *sabāra*—of everyone; *mukha*—face; *dekhi'*—seeing; *kare*—does; *dṛḍha*—tightly; *āliṅgana*—embracing.

**The Lord met all the devotees one after the other, and looking at everyone's face individually, He embraced them tightly.**
