### CC Madhya 3.34

> প্রভু কহে, — নিত্যানন্দ আমারে বঞ্চিলা ।\
> গঙ্গাকে আনিয়া মোরে যমুনা কহিলা ॥৩৪॥

> prabhu kahe,—nityānanda āmāre vañcilā\
> gaṅgāke āniyā more yamunā kahilā

*prabhukahe*—the Lord replied; *nityānanda*—Lord Nityānanda; *āmāre*—Me; *vañcilā*—has cheated; *gaṅgāke*—to the bank of the Ganges; *āniyā*—bringing; *more*—Me; *yamunā*—the river Yamunā; *kahilā*—informed.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu then said, "Nityānanda has cheated Me. He has brought Me to the bank of the Ganges and told Me that it was the Yamunā."**
