### CC Madhya 3.69

> আচার্য কহে — বৈস দোঁহে পিঁড়ির উপরে ।
> এত বলি’ হাতে ধরি’ বসাইল দুঁহারে ॥৬৯॥

> ācārya kahe—vaisa doṅhe piṅḍira upare
> eta bali' hāte dhari' vasāila duṅhāre

*ācāryakahe*—Advaita Ācārya said; *vaisa*—sit down; *doṅhe*—You two; *piṅḍira upare*—on the planks of wood; *eta bali'*—saying this; *hāte dhari'*—catching Their hands; *vasāila duṅhāre*—sat the two Lords down.

**Advaita Ācārya said, "Just sit down here on these seats." Catching Their hands, He sat Them both down.**
