### CC Madhya 3.60

> গৃহের ভিতরে প্রভু করুন গমন ।
> দুই ভাই আইলা তবে করিতে ভোজন ॥৬০॥

> gṛhera bhitare prabhu karuna gamana
> dui bhāi āilā tabe karite bhojana

*gṛherabhitare*—within the room; *prabhu*—Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *karuna*—kindly do; *gamana*—entering; *dui bhāi*—the two brothers, Caitanya Mahāprabhu and Nityānanda Prabhu; *āilā*—came; *tabe*—then; *karite bhojana*—to partake of the *prasāda.*

**Śrī Advaita Prabhu said, "My dear Lords, kindly enter this room." The two brothers, Caitanya Mahāprabhu and Nityānanda Prabhu, then came forward to take the prasāda.**
