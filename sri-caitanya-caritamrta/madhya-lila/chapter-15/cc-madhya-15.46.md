### CC Madhya 15.46

> তোমার ঘরে কীর্তনে আমি নিত্য নাচিব । তুমি দেখা পাবে, আর কেহ না দেখিব ॥৪৬॥

> tomāra ghare kīrtane āmi nitya nāciba
> tumi dekhā pābe, āra keha nā dekhiba

*tomāraghare*—in your house; *kīrtane*—in the performance of congregational chanting; *āmi*—I; *nitya*—daily; *nāciba*—shall dance; *tumi*—you; *dekhā pābe*—will be able to see; *āra*—else; *keha*—anyone; *nā dekhiba*—will not see.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu requested Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura, "Perform congregational chanting daily, and be assured that I shall also dance in your presence. You will be able to see this dancing, but not others.**
