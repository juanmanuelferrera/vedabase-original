### CC Madhya 15.39

> তবে মহাপ্রভু সব ভক্তে বোলাইল ।
> গৌড়দেশে যাহ সবে বিদায় করিল ॥৩৯॥

> tabe mahāprabhu saba bhakte bolāila
> gauḍa-deśe yāha sabe vidāya karila

*tabemahāprabhu*—thereafter Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *saba*—all; *bhakte*—the devotees; *bolāila*—called; *gauḍa-deśe*—to Bengal; *yāha*—return; *sabe*—all of you; *vidāya karila*—bade farewell.

**Thereafter, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu called for all the devotees and asked them to return to Bengal. In this way, He bade farewell to them.**
