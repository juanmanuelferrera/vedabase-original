### CC Madhya 5.40

> বিপ্র বলে, — “তীর্থ-বাক্য কেমনে করি আন ।\
> যে হউক্, সে হউক, আমি দিব কন্যাদান ।।” ॥ ৪০ ॥

> vipra bale,—"tīrtha-vākya kemane kari āna\
> ye ha-uk, se ha-uka, āmi diba kanyā-dāna"

*vipra bale*—the *brāhmaṇa* says; *tīrtha-vākya*—the promise made on the pilgrimage; *kemane*—how; *kari*—I shall do; *āna*—otherwise; *ye ha-uk*—whatever may be; *se ha-uk*—let it take place; *āmi*—I; *diba*—shall give; *kanyā-dāna*—my daughter in charity.

**The elderly brāhmaṇa said, "How can I undo the promise I made in a holy place while on pilgrimage? Whatever may happen, I must give him my daughter in charity."**
