### CC Madhya 5.147

> চলিতে চলিতে প্রভু আইলা ‘আঠারনালা’ ।\
> তাহাঁ আসি’ প্রভু কিছু বাহ্য প্রকাশিলা ॥১৪৭॥

> calite calite prabhu āilā 'āṭhāranālā'\
> tāhāṅ āsi' prabhu kichu bāhya prakāśilā

*calitecalite*—walking in this way; *prabhu*—the Lord; *āilā*—arrived; *āṭhāranālā*—at a place known as Āṭhāranālā; *tāhāṅ*—there; *āsi'*—coming; *prabhu*—the Lord; *kichu*—some; *bāhya*—external consciousness; *prakāśilā*—expressed.

**Thus walking and walking, the Lord eventually arrived at the place known as Āṭhāranālā. Arriving there, He expressed His external consciousness, speaking to Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu.**

There is a bridge situated at the entrance of Jagannātha Purī called Āṭhāranālā which has eighteen arches. *Āṭhāra* means eighteen.
