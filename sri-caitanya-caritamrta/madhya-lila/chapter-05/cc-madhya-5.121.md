### CC Madhya 5.121

> সেই রাজা জিনি’ নিল তাঁর সিংহাসন ।\
> ‘মাণিক্য-সিংহাসন’ নাম অনেক রতন ॥১২১॥

> sei rājā jini' nila tāṅra siṁhāsana\
> 'māṇikya-siṁhāsana' nāma aneka ratana

*sei rājā*—that King (Mahārāja Puruṣottama); *jini'*—conquering; *nila*—took; *tāṅra*—his; *siṁha-āsana*—the throne; *māṇikya-siṁhāsana*—the throne known as Māṇikya-siṁhāsana; *nāma*—named; *aneka*—various; *ratana*—bedecked with jewels.

**That King was victorious over the King of Vidyānagara, and he took possession of his throne, the Māṇikya-siṁhāsana, which was bedecked with many jewels.**
