### CC Madhya 9.20

> সেই দিন তাঁর ঘরে রহি’ ভিক্ষা করি’ ।\
> তাঁরে কৃপা করি’ আগে চলিলা গৌরহরি ॥২০॥

> sei dina tāṅra ghare rahi' bhikṣā kari'\
> tāṅre kṛpā kari' āge calilā gaurahari

*seidina*—on that day; *tāṅra ghare*—the house of that *brāhmaṇa*; *rahi'*—staying; *bhikṣā kari'*—accepting *prasāda*; *tāṅre*—unto him; *kṛpā kari'*—showing mercy; *āge*—ahead; *calilā*—departed; *gaura-hari*—Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**That day, Lord Caitanya remained there and accepted prasāda at his house. After bestowing mercy upon him in this way, the Lord proceeded ahead.**
