### CC Madhya 9.39

> তাহাঁ হৈতে চলি’ আগে গেলা এক গ্রামে ।\
> ব্রাহ্মণ-সমাজ তাহাঁ, করিল বিশ্রামে ॥৩৯॥

> tāhāṅ haite cali' āge gelā eka grāme\
> brāhmaṇa-samāja tāhāṅ, karila viśrāme

*tāhāṅ haite*—from there; *cali'*—going; *āge*—forward; *gelā*—went; *eka*—one; *grāme*—to a village; *brāhmaṇa-samāja*—assembly of *brāhmaṇas*; *tāhāṅ*—there; *karila viśrāme*—He rested.

**Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu then left Vṛddhakāśī and proceeded further ahead. In one village He saw that most of the residents were brāhmaṇas, and He took His rest there.**
