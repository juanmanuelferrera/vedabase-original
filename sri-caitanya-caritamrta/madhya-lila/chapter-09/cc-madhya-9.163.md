### CC Madhya 9.163

> চাতুর্মাস্য পূর্ণ হৈল, ভট্ট-আজ্ঞা লঞা ।\
> দক্ষিণ চলিলা প্রভু শ্রীরঙ্গ দেখিয়া ॥১৬৩॥

> cāturmāsya pūrṇa haila, bhaṭṭa-ājñā lañā\
> dakṣiṇa calilā prabhu śrī-raṅga dekhiyā

*cāturmāsya*—the period of Cāturmāsya; *pūrṇa haila*—became completed; *bhaṭṭa-ājñā lañā*—taking permission from Vyeṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa; *dakṣiṇa*—south; *calilā*—proceeded; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *śrī-raṅga dekhiyā*—visiting Śrī Raṅga.

**When the period of Cāturmāsya was completed, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu took permission to leave Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa, and after visiting Śrī Raṅga He proceeded further toward southern India.**
