### CC Madhya 9.26

> বাল্যাবধি রামনাম-গ্রহণ আমার ।\
> তোমা দেখি’ কৃষ্ণনাম আইল একবার ॥২৬॥

> bālyāvadhi rāma-nāma-grahaṇa āmāra\
> tomā dekhi' kṛṣṇa-nāma āila eka-bāra

*bālya-avadhi*—since the days of my childhood; *rāma-nāma-grahaṇa*—chanting the holy name of Lord Rāmacandra; *āmāra*—my; *tomā dekhi'*—after seeing You; *kṛṣṇa-nāma*—the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa; *āila*—came; *eka-bāra*—once only.

**"From my childhood I have been chanting the holy name of Lord Rāmacandra, but after seeing You I began to chant the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa just once.**
