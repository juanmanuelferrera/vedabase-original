### CC Madhya 8.155

> আনন্দাংশে ‘হ্লাদিনী’, সদংশে ‘সন্ধিনী’ ।\
> চিদংশে ‘সম্বিৎ’, যারে জ্ঞান করি’ মানি ॥১৫৫॥

> ānandāṁśe 'hlādinī', sad-aṁśe 'sandhinī'\
> cid-aṁśe 'samvit', yāre jñāna kari' māni

*ānanda-aṁśe*—in bliss; *hlādinī*—the pleasure-giving potency; *sat-aṁśe*—in eternity; *sandhinī*—the creative potency; *cit-aṁśe*—in knowledge; *samvit*—samvit; *yāre*—which; *jñāna*—knowledge; *kari'*—taking as; *māni*—I accept.

**"Hlādinī is His aspect of bliss; sandhinī, of eternal existence; and samvit, of cognizance, which is also accepted as knowledge.**
