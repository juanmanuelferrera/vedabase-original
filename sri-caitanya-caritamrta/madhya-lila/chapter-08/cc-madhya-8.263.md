### CC Madhya 8.263

> ‘কৃষ্ণতত্ত্ব’, ‘রাধাতত্ত্ব’, ‘প্রেমতত্ত্বসার’ ।\
> ‘রসতত্ত্ব’ ‘লীলাতত্ত্ব’ বিবিধ প্রকার ॥২৬৩॥

> 'kṛṣṇa-tattva', 'rādhā-tattva', 'prema-tattva-sāra'\
> 'rasa-tattva' 'līlā-tattva' vividha prakāra

*kṛṣṇa-tattva*—the truth about Kṛṣṇa; *rādhā-tattva*—the truth about Rādha; *prema-tattva-sāra*—the essence of Their loving affairs; *rasa-tattva*—the truth about transcendental mellow; *līlā-tattva*—the truth about the pastimes of the Lord; *vividha prakāra*—of different varieties.

**"There is transcendental variety in talks about Kṛṣṇa and Rādhārāṇī and Their transcendental loving affairs, humors and pastimes."**
