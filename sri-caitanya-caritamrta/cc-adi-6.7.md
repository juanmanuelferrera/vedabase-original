### CC Adi 6.7

> mahā-viṣṇu sṛṣṭi karena jagad-ādi kārya
> tāṅra avatāra sākṣāt advaita ācārya

*mahā-viṣṇu*—the original Viṣṇu; *sṛṣṭi*—creation; *karena*—does; *jagat-ādi*—the material world; *kārya*—the occupation; *tāṅra*—His; *avatāra*—incarnation; *sākṣāt*—directly; *advaitaācārya*—Prabhu Advaita Ācārya.

**Mahā-Viṣṇu performs all the functions for the creation of the universes. Śrī Advaita Ācārya is His direct incarnation.**
