### CC Madhya 1.268

> manuṣyera veśa dhari' yātrikera chale
> prabhura darśana kare āsi' nīlācale

*manuṣyera*—of human beings; *veśa dhari'*—dressing themselves; *yātrikerachale*—as if pilgrims; *prabhura*—of Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *darśana kare*—visit; *āsi'*—coming; *nīlācale*—to Jagannātha Purī.

**Dressing like human beings on pilgrimage, they all used to come to Jagannātha Purī to visit Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.**
