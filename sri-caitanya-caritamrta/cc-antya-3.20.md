### CC Antya 3.20

> eteka vicāri' prabhu madhyāhne calilā
> āra dine dāmodare nibhṛte bolāilā

*eteka vicāri'*—considering like this; *prabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *madhyāhne calilā*—went to perform His noon duties; *āradine*—the next day; *dāmodare*—unto Dāmodara Paṇḍita; *nibhṛte*—in a solitary place; *bolāilā*—called.

**Thinking in this way, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu went to perform His noon duties. The next day, He called Dāmodara Paṇḍita to a solitary place.**
