### CC Antya 1.210

> haridāsa kahe,—"tomāra bhāgyera nāhi sīmā
> ye saba varṇilā, ihāra ke jāne mahimā?"

*hari-dāsa kahe*—Haridāsa Ṭhākura says; *tomāra*—your; *bhāgyera*—of fortune; *nāhi sīmā*—there is no limit; *ye*—whatever; *saba*—all; *varṇilā*—you have described; *ihāra*—of this; *kejāne*—who can understand; *mahimā*—the glories.

**Haridāsa Ṭhākura told him: "There is no limit to your good fortune. No one can understand the glories of what you have described."**
