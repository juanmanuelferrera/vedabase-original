### CC Adi 12.78

> শাখা-উপশাখা, তার নাহিক গণন ।\
> কিছুমাত্র কহি’ করি দিগ্দরশন ॥৭৮॥

> śākhā-upaśākhā, tāra nāhika gaṇana\
> kichu-mātra kahi' kari dig-daraśana

*śākhā-upaśākhā*—branches and sub-branches; *tāra*—of them; *nāhika*—there is no; *gaṇana*—counting; *kichu-mātra*—something about them; *kahi'*—describing; *kari*—I am simply giving; *dig-daraśana*—a glimpse of the direction.

**There are multifarious branches and sub-branches of Advaita Ācārya. It is very difficult to enumerate them fully. I have simply given a glimpse of the whole trunk and its branches and sub-branches.**
