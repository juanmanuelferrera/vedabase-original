### CC Adi 7.133

> এই মত প্রতিসূত্রে সহজার্থ ছাড়িয়া ।\
> গৌণার্থ ব্যাখ্যা করে কল্পনা করিয়া ॥১৩৩॥

> ei mata pratisūtre sahajārtha chāḍiyā\
> gauṇārtha vyākhyā kare kalpanā kariyā

*ei mata*—like this; *prati-sūtre*—in every *sūtra,* or code of the *Vedānta*; *sahaja-artha*—the clear, simple meaning; *chāḍiyā*—giving up; *gauṇa-artha*—indirect meaning; *vyākhyā*—explanation; *kare*—he makes; *kalpanākariyā*—by imagination.

**"The Māyāvāda school, giving up the real, easily understood meaning of Vedic literature, has introduced indirect meanings, based on their imaginative powers, to prove their philosophy."**

Unfortunately, the Śaṅkarite interpretation has covered almost the entire world. Therefore there is a great need to present the original, easily understood natural import of the Vedic literature. We have therefore begun by presenting *Bhagavad-gītā As It Is,* and we propose to present all the Vedic literature in terms of the direct meaning of its words.
