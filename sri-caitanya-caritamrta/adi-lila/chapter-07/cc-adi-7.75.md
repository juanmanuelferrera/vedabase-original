### CC Adi 7.75

> eta bali' eka śloka śikhāila more
> kaṇṭhe kari' ei śloka kariha vicāre

*eta bali'*—saying this; *ekaśloka*—one verse; *śikhāila*—taught; *more*—Me; *kaṇṭhe*—in the throat; *kari'*—keeping; *ei*—this; *śloka*—verse; *kariha*—You should do; *vicāre*—in consideration.

**"After describing the potency of the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra, My spiritual master taught Me another verse, advising Me to always keep the name within My throat.**
