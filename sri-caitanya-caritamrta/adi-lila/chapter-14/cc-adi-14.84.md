### CC Adi 14.84

> rātre svapna dekhe,—eka āsi' brahmaṇa
> miśrere kahaye kichu sa-roṣa vacana

*rātre*—at night; *svapnadekhe*—he dreamt; *eka*—one; *āsi'*—coming; *brāhmana*—*brāhmaṇa*; *miśrere*—unto Jagannātha Miśra; *kahaye*—spoke; *kichu*—something; *sa-roṣa*—with anger; *vacana*—words.
