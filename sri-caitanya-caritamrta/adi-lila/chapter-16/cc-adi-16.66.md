### CC Adi 16.66

> ‘বিভবতি’ ক্রিয়ায় বাক্য — সাঙ্গ, পুনঃ বিশেষণ ।\
> ‘অদ্ভুতগুণা’ — এই পুনরাত্ত দূষণ ॥৬৬॥

> 'vibhavati' kriyāya vākya—sāṅga, punaḥ viśeṣaṇa\
> 'adbhuta-guṇā'—ei punar-ātta dūṣaṇa

*vibhavatikriyāya*—by the verb *vibhavati* ("flourishes"); *vākya*—statement; *sāṅga*—complete; *punaḥ*—again; *viśeṣaṇa*—an adjective; *adbhuta-guṇā*—wonderful qualities; *ei*—this; *punar-ātta*—repetition of the same word; *dūṣaṇa*—fault.

**"The statement by the word 'vibhavati' ['flourishes'] is complete. Qualifying it with the adjective 'adbhuta-guṇā' ['wonderful qualities'] creates the fault of redundancy.**
