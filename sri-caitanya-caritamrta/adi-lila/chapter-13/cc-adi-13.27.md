### CC Adi 13.27

> বিবাহ করিলে হৈল নবীন যৌবন ।\
> সর্বত্র লওয়াইল প্রভু নাম-সংকীর্তন ॥২৭॥

> vivāha karile haila navīna yauvana\
> sarvatra laoyāila prabhu nāma-saṅkīrtana

*vivāha karile*—after getting married; *haila*—began; *navīna*—new; *yauvana*—youth; *sarvatra*—everywhere; *laoyāila*—caused to take; *prabhu*—the Lord; *nāma-saṅkīrtana*—*saṅkīrtana* movement.

**After His marriage His youth began, and in His youth He induced everyone to chant the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra anywhere and everywhere.**
