### CC Adi 13.4

> jaya dāmodara-svarūpa jaya murāri gupta
> ei saba candrodaye tamaḥ kaila lupta

*jaya*—all glories; *dāmodara-svarūpa*—of the name Svarūpa Dāmodara; *jaya*—all glories; *murārigupta*—of the name Murāri Gupta; *ei saba*—of all these; *candra-udaye*—such moons having arisen; *tamaḥ*—darkness; *kaila*—made; *lupta*—dissipated.

**All glories to Svarūpa Dāmodara and Murāri Gupta! All these brilliant moons have together dissipated the darkness of this material world.**
