### CC Adi 13.87

> হৈতে হৈতে হৈল গর্ভ ত্রয়োদশ মাস ।
> তথাপি ভূমিষ্ঠ নহে, — মিশ্রের হৈল ত্রাস ॥৮৭॥

> haite haite haila garbha trayodaśa māsa
> tathāpi bhūmiṣṭha nahe,—miśrera haila trāsa

*haitehaite*—thus becoming; *haila*—it so became; *garbha*—pregnancy; *trayodaśa*—thirteenth; *māsa*—month; *tathāpi*—still; *bhūmiṣṭha*—delivery; *nahe*—there was no sign; *miśrera*—of Jagannātha Miśra; *haila*—became; *trāsa*—apprehension.

**In this way the pregnancy approached its thirteenth month, but still there was no sign of the delivery of the child. Thus Jagannātha Miśra became greatly apprehensive.**
