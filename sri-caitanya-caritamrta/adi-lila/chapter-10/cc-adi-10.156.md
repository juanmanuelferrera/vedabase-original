### CC Adi 10.156

> বড় হৈলে নীলাচলে গেলা প্রভুর স্থানে ।
> অষ্টমাস রহিল ভিক্ষা দেন কোন দিনে ॥১৫৬॥

> baḍa haile nīlācale gelā prabhura sthāne
> aṣṭa-māsa rahila bhikṣā dena kona dine

*baḍa haile*—when he grew to be a young man; *nīlācale*—at Jagannātha Purī; *gelā*—went; *prabhura*—of Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *sthāne*—at the place; *aṣṭa-māsa*—eight months; *rahila*—stayed; *bhikṣā*—*prasāda*; *dena*—gave; *konadine*—some days.

**When Raghunātha grew to be a young man, he visited Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu at Jagannātha Purī and stayed there for eight months. Sometimes he offered prasāda to the Lord.**
