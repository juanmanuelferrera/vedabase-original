### CC Adi 10.117

> প্রভুর আজ্ঞায় নিত্যানন্দ গৌড়ে চলিলা ।\
> তাঁর সঙ্গে তিনজন প্রভু-আজ্ঞায় আইলা ॥১১৭॥

> prabhura ājñāya nityānanda gauḍe calilā\
> tāṅra saṅge tina-jana prabhu-ājñāya āilā

*prabhura ājñāya*—under the order of Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nityānanda*—Lord Nityānanda; *gauḍe*—to Bengal; *calilā*—went back; *tāṅra saṅge*—in His company; *tinajana*—three men; *prabhu-ājñāya*—under the order of the Lord; *āilā*—went.

**By the order of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, three devotees accompanied Lord Nityānanda Prabhu when He returned to Bengal to preach.**
