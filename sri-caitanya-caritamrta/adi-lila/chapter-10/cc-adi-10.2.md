### CC Adi 10.2

> জয় জয় শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-নিত্যানন্দ ।
> জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥২॥

> jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-nityānanda
> jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

*jayajaya*—all glories; *śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya*—to Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *nityānanda*—Lord Nityānanda; *jayaadvaita-candra*—all glories to Advaita Prabhu; *jaya*—all glories; *gaura-bhakta-vṛnda*—to the devotees of Lord Caitanya, headed by Śrīvāsa.

**All glories to Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu and Lord Nityānanda! All glories to Advaita Prabhu, and all glories to the devotees of Lord Caitanya, headed by Śrīvāsa!**
