### CC Adi 17.193

> নগরে হিন্দুর ধর্ম বাড়িল অপার ।
> ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ধ্বনি বই নাহি শুনি আর ॥১৯৩॥

> nagare hindura dharma bāḍila apāra
> 'hari' 'hari' dhvani ba-i nāhi śuni āra

*nagare*—in the city; *hindura*—of the Hindus; *dharma*—religion; *bāḍila*—has increased; *apāra*—unlimitedly; *harihari*—of the Lord's name, Hari, Hari; *dhvani*—the vibration; *ba-i*—except; *nāhi*—do not; *śuni*—we hear; *āra*—anything else.

**“ 'The religion of the Hindus will increase unlimitedly. There are always vibrations of "Hari! Hari!" We do not hear anything but this.'**
