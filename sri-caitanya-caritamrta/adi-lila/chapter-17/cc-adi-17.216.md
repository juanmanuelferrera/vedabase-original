### CC Adi 17.216

> eta śuni' mahāprabhu hāsiyā hāsiyā
> kahite lāgilā kichu kājire chuṅiyā

*eta*—this; *śuni'*—hearing; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *hāsiyāhāsiya*—smiling; *kahite*—to speak; *lāgilā*—began; *kichu*—something; *kājire*—unto the Kazi; *chuṅiyā*—touching.

**After hearing the Kazi speak so nicely, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu touched him and smilingly spoke as follows.**
