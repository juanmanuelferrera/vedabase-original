### CC Adi 17.187

> এত বলি’ কাজী নিজ-বুক দেখাইল ।\
> শুনি’ দেখি’ সর্বলোক আশ্চর্য মানিল ॥১৮৭॥

> eta bali' kājī nija-buka dekhāila\
> śuni' dekhi' sarva-loka āścarya mānila

*eta bali'*—saying this; *kājī*—the Kazi; *nija-buka*—his own chest; *dekhāila*—showed; *śuni'*—hearing; *dekhi'*—seeing; *sarva-loka*—everyone; *āścarya*—wonderful incident; *mānila*—accepted.

**After this description, the Kazi showed his chest. Having heard him and seen the marks, all the people there accepted the wonderful incident.**
