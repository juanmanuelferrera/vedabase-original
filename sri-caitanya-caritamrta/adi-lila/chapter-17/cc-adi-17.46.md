### CC Adi 17.46

> সর্বাঙ্গ বেড়িল কীটে, কাটে নিরন্তর ।\
> অসহ্য বেদনা, দুঃখে জ্বলয়ে অন্তর ॥৪৬॥

> sarvāṅga beḍila kīṭe, kāṭe nirantara\
> asahya vedanā, duḥkhe jvalaye antara

*sarvāṅga*—all over the body; *beḍila*—became covered; *kīṭe*—by insects; *kāṭe*—biting; *nirantara*—always; *asahya*—unbearable; *vedanā*—pain; *duḥkhe*—in unhappiness; *jvalaye*—burns; *antara*—without cessation.

**Incessantly covered with germs and insects biting him all over his body, Gopāla Cāpāla felt unbearable pain. His entire body burned in distress.**
