### CC Adi 17.279

> শ্যামসুন্দর, শিখিপিচ্ছ-গুঞ্জা-বিভূষণ ।\
> গোপ-বেশ, ত্রিভঙ্গিম, মুরলী-বদন ॥২৭৯॥

> śyāmasundara, śikhipiccha-guñjā-vibhūṣaṇa\
> gopa-veśa, tri-bhaṅgima, muralī-vadana

*śyāma-sundara*—Lord Kṛṣṇa, who has a bluish color; *śikhi-piccha*—with a peacock feather on the head; *guñjā*—a garland of *guñjā*; *vibhūṣaṇa*—decorations; *gopa-veśa*—with the dress of a cowherd boy; *tri-bhaṅgima*—curved in three places; *muralī-vadana*—with a flute in the mouth.

**He has a bluish complexion, a peacock feather on His head, a guñjā garland and the decorations of a cowherd boy. His body is curved in three places, and He holds a flute in His mouth.**
