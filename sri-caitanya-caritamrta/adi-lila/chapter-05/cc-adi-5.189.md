### CC Adi 5.189

> প্রেমে মত্ত অঙ্গ ডাহিনে-বামে দোলে ।
> ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ বলিয়া গম্ভীর বোল বলে ॥১৮৯॥

> preme matta aṅga ḍāhine-vāme dole
> 'kṛṣṇa' 'kṛṣṇa' baliyā gambhīra bola bale

*preme*—in ecstasy; *matta*—absorbed; *aṅga*—the whole body; *ḍāhine*—to the right side; *vāme*—to the left side; *dole*—moves; *kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa*—Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa; *baliyā*—saying; *gambhīra*—deep; *bola*—words; *bale*—was uttering.

**His body moved to and fro, right and left, for He was absorbed in ecstasy. He chanted "Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa" in a deep voice.**
