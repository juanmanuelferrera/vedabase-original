### CC Adi 5.163

> মহাপ্রেমময় তিঁহো বসিলা অঙ্গনে ।
> সকল বৈষ্ণব তাঁর বন্দিলা চরণে ॥১৬৩॥

> mahā-prema-maya tiṅho vasilā aṅgane
> sakala vaiṣṇava tāṅra vandilā caraṇe

*mahā-prema-maya*—absorbed in emotional love; *tiṅho*—he; *vasilā*—sat; *aṅgane*—in the courtyard; *sakalavaiṣṇava*—all other Vaiṣṇavas; *tāṅra*—his; *vandilā*—worshiped; *caraṇe*—lotus feet.

**Absorbed in emotional love, he sat in my courtyard, and all the Vaiṣṇavas bowed down at his feet.**
