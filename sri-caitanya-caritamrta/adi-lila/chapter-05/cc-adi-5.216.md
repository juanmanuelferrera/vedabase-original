### CC Adi 5.216

> নিত্যানন্দ-দয়া মোরে তাঁরে দেখাইল ।
> শ্রীরাধা-মদনমোহনে প্রভু করি’ দিল ॥২১৬॥

> nityānanda-dayā more tāṅre dekhāila
> śrī-rādhā-madana-mohane prabhu kari' dila

*nityānanda-dayā*—the mercy of Lord Nityānanda; *more*—unto me; *tāṅre*—Madanamohana; *dekhāila*—showed; *śrī-rādhā-madana-mohane*—Rādhā-Madanamohana; *prabhu kari' dila*—gave as my Lord and master.

**The mercy of Lord Nityānanda showed me Śrī Madanamohana and gave me Śrī Madanamohana as my Lord and master.**
