### CC Adi 15.2

> jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda
> jayādvaitacandra, jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

*jayajaya*—all glories; *śrī-caitanya*—Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *jaya*—all glories; *nityānanda*—Lord Nityānanda Prabhu; *jaya-advaitacandra*—all glories to Advaita Ācārya; *jayagaura-bhakta-vṛnda*—all glories to the devotees of Lord Caitanya.

**All glories to Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu! All glories to Lord Nityānanda Prabhu! All glories to Advaita Ācārya! And all glories to the devotees of Lord Caitanya!**
