### CC Adi 3.62

> bāhu tuli' hari bali' prema-dṛṣṭye cāya
> kariyā kalmaṣa nāśa premete bhāsāya

*bāhutuli'*—raising the arms; *haribali'*—chanting the holy name; *prema-dṛṣṭye*—with His glance of deep love; *cāya*—He looks; *kariyā*—causing; *kalmaṣa*—to sins; *nāśa*—destruction; *premete*—in love of God; *bhāsāya*—He floods.

**Raising His arms, chanting the holy name and looking upon all with deep love, He drives away all sins and floods everyone with love of Godhead.**
