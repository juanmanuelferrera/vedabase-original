### CC Adi 3.74

> nityānanda gosāñi sākṣāt haladhara
> advaita ācārya gosāñi sākṣāt īśvara

*nityānanda gosāñi*—Lord Nityānanda Gosāñi; *sākṣāt*—directly; *hala-dhara*—Lord Balarāma, the holder of the plow; *advaitaācāryagosāñi*—Śrī Advaita Ācārya Gosāñi; *sākṣāt*—directly; *īśvara*—the Personality of Godhead.

**Śrī Nityānanda Gosāñi is directly Haladhara [Lord Balarāma], and Advaita Ācārya is the Personality of Godhead Himself.**
