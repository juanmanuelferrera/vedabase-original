### CC Madhya 3.120

> vyākula hañā prabhu bhūmite paḍilā
> gosāñi dekhiyā ācārya nṛtya sambarilā

*vyākulahañā*—being too much agitated; *prabhu*—the Lord; *bhūmite*—to the ground; *paḍilā*—fell; *gosāñi*—the Lord; *dekhiyā*—seeing; *ācārya*—Advaita Ācārya; *nṛtya*—the dancing; *sambarilā*—checked.

**Being agitated by the ecstasy, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu suddenly fell on the ground. Seeing this, Advaita Ācārya stopped dancing.**
