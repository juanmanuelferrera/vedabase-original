### CC Adi 17.198

> mleccha kahe,—hindure āmi kari parihāsa
> keha keha—kṛṣṇadāsa, keha—rāmadāsa

*mleccha*—the meat-eater; *kahe*—says; *hindure*—unto a Hindu; *āmi*—I; *kari*—do; *parihāsa*—joking; *kehakeha*—some of them; *kṛṣṇadāsa*—of the name Kṛṣṇadāsa; *keha*—some of them; *rāmadāsa*—of the name Rāmadāsa.

**"The meat-eater replied, 'Sometimes I joke with the Hindus. Some of them are called Kṛṣṇadāsa, and some are called Rāmadāsa.**
