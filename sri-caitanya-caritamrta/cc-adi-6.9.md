### CC Adi 6.9

> icchāya ananta mūrti karena prakāśa
> eka eka mūrte karena brahmāṇḍe praveśa

*icchāya*—by His will; *anantamūrti*—unlimited forms; *karena*—does; *prakāśa*—manifestation; *ekaeka*—each and every; *mūrte*—form; *karena*—does; *brahmāṇḍe*—within the universe; *praveśa*—entrance.

**By His will He manifests Himself in unlimited forms, in which He enters each and every universe.**
