### CC Adi 5.196

> eta bali' prerilā more hātasāni diyā
> antardhāna kaila prabhu nija-gaṇa lañā

*eta bali'*—saying this; *prerilā*—dispatched; *more*—me; *hātasāni*—indication of the hand; *diyā*—giving; *antardhānakaila*—disappeared; *prabhu*—my Lord; *nija-gaṇa lañā*—taking His personal associates.

**After saying this, He directed me toward Vṛndāvana by waving His hand. Then He disappeared with His associates.**
