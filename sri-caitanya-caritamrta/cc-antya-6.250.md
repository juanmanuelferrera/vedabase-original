### CC Antya 6.250

> govardhanera putra teṅho, nāma—'raghunātha'
> nīlācale paricaya āche tomāra sātha?"

*govardhanera*—of Govardhana; *putra*—the son; *teṅho*—he; *nāma*—named; *raghunātha*—Raghunātha dāsa; *nīlācale*—in Nīlācala; *paricayaāche*—is there acquaintance; *tomāra sātha*—with you.

**"That person is Raghunātha dāsa, the son of Govardhana Majumadāra. Did you meet him in Nīlācala?"**
