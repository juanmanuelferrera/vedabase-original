### CC Antya 1.209

> tabe mahāprabhu saba bhakta lañā gelā
> haridāsa-ṭhākura rūpe āliṅgana kailā

*tabe*—at that time; *mahāprabhu*—Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *saba*—all; *bhakta*—devotees; *lañā*—with; *gelā*—departed from the place; *hari-dāsa-ṭhākura*—of the name Haridāsa Ṭhākura; *rūpe*—unto Rūpa Gosvāmī; *āliṅgana kailā*—embraced.

**Then, when Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu left with all of His devotees, Haridāsa Ṭhākura also embraced Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī.**
