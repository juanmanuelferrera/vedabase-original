### CC Antya 6.288

> শঙ্করানন্দ-সরস্বতী বৃন্দাবন হৈতে আইলা ।\
> তেঁহ সেই শিলা-গুঞ্জামালা লঞা গেলা ॥২৮৮॥

> śaṅkarānanda-sarasvatī vṛndāvana haite āilā\
> teṅha sei śilā-guñjā-mālā lañā gelā

*śaṅkarānanda-sarasvatī*—one of the devotees of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; *vṛndāvanahaite*—from Vṛndāvana; *āilā*—came; *teṅha*—he; *sei*—that; *śilā-guñjā-mālā*—stone and the garland of small conchshells; *lañā*—taking; *gelā*—went.

**Previously, when Śaṅkarānanda Sarasvatī had returned from Vṛndāvana, he had brought the piece of stone from Govardhana Hill and also the garland of conchshells.**
