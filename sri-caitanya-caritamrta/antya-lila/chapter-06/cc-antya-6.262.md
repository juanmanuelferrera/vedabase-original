### CC Antya 6.262

> এই ত’ প্রস্তাবে শ্রীকবিকর্ণপূর ।\
> রঘুনাথ-মহিমা গ্রন্থে লিখিলা প্রচুর ॥২৬২॥

> ei ta' prastāve śrī kavi-karṇapūra\
> raghunātha-mahimā granthe likhilā pracura

*ei ta' prastāve*—in this connection; *śrī kavi-karṇapūra*—the poet named Kavi-karṇapūra; *raghunātha-mahimā*—the glories of Raghunātha dāsa; *granthe*—in his book; *likhilā*—wrote; *pracura*—much.

**Describing this incident, the great poet Śrī Kavi-karṇapūra has written extensively about the glorious activities of Raghunātha dāsa in his book.**
