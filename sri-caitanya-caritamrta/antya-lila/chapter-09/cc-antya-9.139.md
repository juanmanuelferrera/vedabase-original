### CC Antya 9.139

> শুদ্ধ কৃপা কর, গোসাঞি, ঘুচাহ ‘বিষয়’ ।\
> নির্বিণ্ণ হইনু, মোতে ‘বিষয়’ না হয় ।।” ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

> śuddha kṛpā kara, gosāñi, ghucāha 'viṣaya'\
> nirviṇṇa ha-inu, mote 'viṣaya' nā haya"

*śuddha kṛpā*—pure mercy; *kara*—kindly bestow; *gosāñi*—my Lord; *ghucāha viṣaya*—let me be free from all these material opulences; *nirviṇṇa*—detached; *ha-inu*—I have become; *mote viṣaya nā haya*—I am no longer interested in material opulences.

**"Kindly bestow upon me Your pure mercy so that I may also become renounced. I am no longer interested in material enjoyment."**
