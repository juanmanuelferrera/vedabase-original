### CC Antya 3.19

> “ইহারে কহিয়ে শুদ্ধপ্রেমের তরঙ্গ  ।
> দামোদর-সম মোর নাহি ‘অন্তরঙ্গ’ ।।” ১৯ ॥১৯॥

> "ihāre kahiye śuddha-premera taraṅga
> dāmodara-sama mora nāhi 'antaraṅga' "

*ihāre*—such behavior; *kahiye*—I can say; *śuddha-premerataraṅga*—waves of pure devotional service; *dāmodara-sama*—like Dāmodara; *mora*—My; *nāhi*—there is not; *antaraṅga*—intimate friend.

**"This impudence is also a sign of pure love for Me. I have no other intimate friend like Dāmodara Paṇḍita."**
