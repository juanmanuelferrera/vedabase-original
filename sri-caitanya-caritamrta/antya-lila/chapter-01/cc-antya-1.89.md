### CC Antya 1.89

> তবে শক্তি সঞ্চারি’ আমি কৈলুঁ উপদেশ ।\
> তুমিহ কহিও ইহাঁয় রসের বিশেষ ।।” ॥ ৮৯ ॥

> tabe śakti sañcāri' āmi kailuṅ upadeśa\
> tumiha kahio ihāṅya rasera viśeṣa"

*tabe*—thereupon; *śakti sañcāri'*—empowering him with My transcendental potency; *āmi*—I; *kailuṅ upadeśa*—gave instruction; *tumiha*—you also; *kahio*—inform; *inhāṅya*—unto him; *rasera viśeṣa*—particular information about transcendental mellows.

**I thereupon also bestowed upon him My transcendental potency. Now you also should give him instructions. In particular, instruct him in transcendental mellows."**
