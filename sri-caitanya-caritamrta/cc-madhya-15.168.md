### CC Madhya 15.168

> asamartha nahe kṛṣṇa, dhare sarva bala
> tomāke vā kene bhuñjāibe pāpa-phala?

*asamarthanahe*—is not unable; *kṛṣṇa*—Lord Kṛṣṇa; *dhare*—possesses; *sarva bala*—all potencies; *tomāke*—*you*; *vā*—then; *kene*—why; *bhuñjāibe*—would cause to suffer; *pāpa-phala*—results of sinful reactions.

**"Kṛṣṇa is not incapable, for He has all potencies. Why would He induce you to suffer the sinful reactions of other living entities?**
